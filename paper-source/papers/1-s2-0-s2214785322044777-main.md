# Experimental study on lightweight concrete using Leca, silica fume, and limestone as aggregates

## Abstract from PDF

This article determines the mix proportions of high-strength structural lightweight concrete (HSSLWC), which uses expanded aggregate clay to decrease the weight of the concrete (LECA). Mineral and chemical admixtures have been used to reduce porosity and enhance strength in order to create HSSLWC. On the specimens, speciﬁc gravity, compressive, indirect tensile, and ﬂexural strengths were determined. By exposing certain specimens to air, the impact of curing on compressive strength was studied. The ﬁndings indicate that by including Leca, a lightweight structural concrete (LWSC) with a dry density of 1,600– 1,960 kg/m3 and a compressive strength of 35–66 MPa may be produced, based on cube specimens with a 100mmside length. When used with lightweight particles, limestone signiﬁcantly improved the mechanical characteristics of concrete. Copyright /C2112022 Elsevier Ltd. All rights reserved.
