# Experimental investigation on lightweight concrete by adding polystyrene beads

## Abstract from PDF

By completely replacing the coarse aggregate in concrete with polystyrene beads, we were able to create a lightweight concrete that was both structurally sound and easy to work with. This enabled us to fulﬁl the project’s speciﬁcations. It is a lightweight substance used in a variety of technological applications to produce non-structural concrete with unit weights ranging from 950 to 1,850 kg/m. In addition, it has been employed in the manufacturing of a substance that has been utilised in a range of technical appli- cations. The outcomes of an experimental investigation into the engineering properties are presented in this paper. This investigation included preliminary tests of the used materials and tests to determine the compressive strength of polystyrene aggregate concrete of varying densities. The purpose of this inves- tigation was to get a clearer understanding of the relationships between these characteristics and engi- neering. An investigation was conducted as a component of an analysis of the being examined engineering properties. As a direct result of the increase in demand for construction materials, A.M.N. has made substantial breakthroughs in the building techniques used for the erection of structures, which has led directly to these advancements. In addition, in order to lessen the amount of weight that must be supported by dead weight in taller structures, we have determined that polystyrene beads should be sub- stituted for coarse particles in the concrete used to form the foundations of these buildings. The major purpose of this research is to identify a proportion of concrete mix that, in the absence of polystyrene beads, produces outcomes equivalent to those of foam concrete. By comparing the performance of the two types of concrete, this objective will be achieved. Copyright /C2112023 Elsevier Ltd. All rights reserved.
