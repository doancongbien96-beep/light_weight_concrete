# Study on the fresh and hardened properties of foam concrete incorporating ﬂy ash

## Abstract from PDF

A study has been undertaken to examine the fresh state properties and hardened state properties of cel- lular light weight concrete i.e., foam concrete with ﬂy ash being used as a substitution. The ﬂy ash is replaced at different levels viz., 25%, 50% and 75% by weight of cement. A total of 20 number of mixes were prepared with varying densities ranging from 800 kg/m 3 to 1600 kg/m 3. For each mix 9 prismatic beam specimens of size 40 mm /C240 mm /C2160 mm were cast and tested. Fresh state properties like con- sistency using mini V-funnel and workability using mini slump cone were determined. The hardened state properties like ﬂexural strength and compressive strength were determined after 7 days and 28 days of age. The consistency i.e., ﬂow time of foam concrete is observed to be decreased with the increase in percentage of ﬂy ash. The workability i.e., slump of foam concrete has increased with the increase in ﬂy ash proportion. The compressive strength and ﬂexural strength have decreased exponentially with the increase in replacement of ﬂy ash. /C2112021 Elsevier Ltd. All rights reserved.
