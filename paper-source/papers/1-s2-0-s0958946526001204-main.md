# Novel ultrafine-porous lightweight concrete enabled by ultrasonic carbonation-induced flocculation

## Abstract from PDF

This proposal aims to develop a novel lightweight concrete through ultrasonic carbonation, revolutionizing lightweight concrete production by stabilizing water within a fortified cementitious network and sequestering CO 2 . Unreacted water is removed after 28 days of hydration to develop ultrafine porous characteristics. After 20 min of ultrasonic carbonation, the water in concrete at a water-to-cement (w/c) ratio of 2.0 can be encapsulated within the flocculation structure, effectively preventing bleeding. The dry density of the hardened matrix at 28 days is below 700 kg/m 3 , with compressive strengths exceeding 2 MPa and a thermal conductivity of approxi - mately 0.13 W ⋅ m -1 ⋅ K -1 . These performances complied with the requirements for A07-grade foamed concrete specified by the Chinese Building Materials Industrial Standard JG/T 266 – 2011. Furthermore, the entire process can capture 8.36% to 9.35% CO 2 per cement unit. The total porosity, as measured by mercury intrusion porosimetry (MIP), exceeded 70% at 28 days. The proportions of pore diameters below 10 μ m exceeded 99%. The critical pore size for ultrasonic carbonation for 20 min was 1403 nm, which is 10 times lower than that of conventional foamed concrete.
