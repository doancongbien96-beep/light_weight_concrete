# Study of light weight composite concrete incorporated with Polypropylene ﬁber and Cenosphere

## Abstract from PDF

The Composite Concrete is used as Supplementary Construction materials to enhance the performance of the construction materials in the current scenario. Also, the rapid growing demand of the concrete has increased the environmental pollution. Hence, there is a need of the supplementary cementitious mate- rials (SCM). Cenophhere is one of the SCM, which is a lightweight, inert, hollow sphere having silica and alumina. The ﬁbers are the reinforcement incorporated in the concrete to improve the mechanical prop- erties. The Polypropylene ﬁber is chosen and the Signiﬁcance of Cenosphere and Polypropylene (PP) ﬁber combination in concrete are explored in this study. The various proportions of cenosphere (0%, 15%, and 30%) are combined with 0.5 % Polypropylene ﬁber to ﬁnd the optimum Mechanical properties for the Composite concrete. The composite Concrete is determined as Light weight concrete with the use of Cenosphere and Polypropylene ﬁber. The Micro structural Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM) and Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR) analysis of Cenosphere shown its hollow nature and polypropylene ﬁber shows that it has non sticky nature which contribute to the light weight for the com- posite concrete.
