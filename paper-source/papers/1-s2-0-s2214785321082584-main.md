# Mechanical properties and development of light weight concrete by using autoclaved aerated concrete (AAC) with aluminum powder

## Abstract from PDF

Autoclaved Aerated Concrete is a lightweight and ﬂexible concrete and they’re frequently utilized as building blocks. The examiner of Autoclaved Aerated Concrete replacing natural sand via way of means of ﬂy ash is investigated. Design AAC having a mix proportion percentage of 1:4 with a water-cement ratio of 0.7. In those specimens the use of lime and without lime, the sand is partially changed via way of means of ﬂy ash, and cement is partially changed via way of means of lime. The gypsum is con- tinuously used inside the specimens. The Aluminum powder is used on a variety of 0.27 g, 0.7 g, 0.77 g, and 1 g of the overall weight of concrete. The characteristics of the mortar, including density, water absorption, and compressive strength during 24-hour steam curing, were determined in this procedure. The paper provides the consequences of the research to gain the complete intake of strong waste and decrease the charges of the project. In this examine natural sand is partially changed via way of means of ﬂy ash and cement is partially and changed via way of means of lime. The mix proportion used 1:4 with a water-cement ratio of 0.7. In this research, the specimen is examined with and without lime. Gypsum is continuously used within side the whole specimens. Different percent of aluminum powder is used 0.03%, 0.06%, 0.077%, 0.2%, and 0.13% of overall weight of concrete.
