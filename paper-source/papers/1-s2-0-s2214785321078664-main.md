# Evaluation of mix ratios of light weight concrete using geopolymer as binder

## Abstract from PDF

Concrete, an old material which is changing its nature rapidly based on supplementary materials used in it. Geopolymer concrete is a sustainable material developed by replacing 100% ﬂyash of cement. To acti- vate the stable ﬂyash particles in concrete, alkaline solutions are added in place of water. Foam is one of the additional materials added in concrete to obtain light weight. These additional materials will affect the mix proportions directly. Light weight Geopolymer Concrete (LWGC) is the combination of two tech- niques which doesn’t have similarity in single prospect, so the mix design methods for nominal concrete and light weight concrete alone will not be useful to develop LWGC. This paper will discuss the mix pro- portions of light weight geopolymer concrete with different materials and different variables viz: foam volume, mass ratio, Solution to binder ratio and density based on available codes of practices and liter- ature. The results indicated 0.6 solution to binder ratio is suitable for 8 M LWGC with good workability and compressive strength. Copyright /C2112022 Elsevier Ltd. All rights reserved.
