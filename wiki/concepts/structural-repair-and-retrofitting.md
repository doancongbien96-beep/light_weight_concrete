# Structural Repair and Retrofitting of Deteriorated Structures with SHCC/ECC

## Summary

An advanced sectional repair method for aged RC structures whose rebars have corroded or
whose cover concrete has spalled through chloride attack, carbonation, or seismic damage:
applying high-tensile-strength, ultra-ductile SHCC/ECC patches restores member flexural
and shear capacity beyond the original design level and permanently secures long-term
durability.

## 1. Limits of conventional repair and the SHCC patch mechanism

- **Problems of conventional methods**:
  - Repairing corroded rebar requires massive demolition back to healthy concrete to
    secure lap-splice bond length ($30D\sim 40D$, i.e. 600–800 mm), at great cost and time.
  - Section restoration with ordinary mortar produces reflective cracking and early
    debonding at the old-new concrete interface from drying shrinkage stress concentration.
- **SHCC sectional repair mechanisms**:
  1. **Ultra-short bond transfer length ($4.0D\sim 5.5D$)**: thanks to high tensile
     strength (~10 MPa) and fiber bridging confinement, rebar stress transfers fully into
     the SHCC patch with only $4.0D$ (80 mm) – $5.5D$ (110 mm) of removal depth.
  2. **Reflective crack blocking and durability shield**: cracks in the substrate disperse
     inside the patch into harmless fine multiple cracks ≤ 50 µm, fundamentally blocking
     moisture and chloride re-ingress.
  3. **Full restoration of capacity and ductility**: members that lost 25–30 % of rebar
     section recover yield and ultimate flexural capacity beyond the original design.

## 2. Composite-section applications

- **Kagome truss and steel-girder composite beams**:
  layered hybrid strengthening placing ultra-ductile composite (HD-FRCC) in the tension
  zone and ultra-high-strength composite (HS-FRCC) in the compression zone maximizes
  flexural capacity and ductility.
- **Steel-concrete composite bridge decks (CBD)**:
  ECC overlay in the hogging-moment zones of continuous bridges prevents harmful cracking
  and extends fatigue life.
