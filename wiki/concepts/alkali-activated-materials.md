# Alkali-Activated Materials (AAM / Geopolymer) and Cement-Free Composites

## 1. Overview and chemical reaction mechanisms

Alkali-activated materials (AAM) are low-carbon cement-free binder systems that use
little or no ordinary Portland cement (OPC) clinker, reacting reactive aluminosilicate
powders (GGBS, fly ash FA, metakaolin MK) with alkaline activators (sodium hydroxide
NaOH, sodium silicate $\text{Na}_2\text{SiO}_3$, sodium carbonate $\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3$,
sodium sulfate $\text{Na}_2\text{SO}_4$, slaked lime $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$) to form hydrated
and geopolymerized solids.

### Principal reaction product structures:
1. **Slag-based (AAS: Alkali-Activated Slag)**:
   - High calcium content forms dense **C-(A)-S-H gel** with lower Ca/Si ratio (1.0–1.3)
     than OPC's C-S-H and a highly aluminium-substitated framework.
2. **Fly ash/metakaolin-based (Geopolymer)**:
   - Three-dimensional amorphous aluminosilicate framework **N-A-S-H gel** (poly-sialate
     chains) conferring extreme refractory and chemical resistance.

## 2. Mechanical and durability characteristics of AAM-ECC / SHGC

- **Eco-efficiency and $CO_2$ reduction**:
  - Excluding clinker calcination ($1450\ ^\circ\text{C}$) and decarbonation cuts $CO_2$
    emissions **60–80 %** relative to Portland cement systems.
- **Interface interaction with hydrophobic PE fibers (aging embrittlement suppression)**:
  - OPC-based composites accumulate calcium hydroxide (CH) over time, over-strengthening
    the hydrophilic-fiber interface so strain capacity collapses after 28 days; AAS/AAM
    matrices form little CH crystallization and suppress chemical bonding to hydrophobic PE
    fibers, fully preserving **6.7–7.5 % ultra-high ductility** beyond 90 days of age.
- **Evolution toward one-part powder systems**:
  - To overcome the hazard of caustic liquid handling and field-workability limits,
    one-part cement-free geopolymer composites that react on water addition after dry
    premixing solid powder activators ($\text{Ca(OH)}_2$, powdered sodium silicate,
    $\text{Na}_2\text{SO}_4$) have reached practical use.
