# Shrinkage and Creep Mechanisms with Stress Relaxation in High Performance Concrete and SHCC

## 1. Overview and classification of shrinkage mechanisms

High performance concrete (HPC), ultra-high performance concrete (UHPC), and strain-
hardening cementitious composites (SHCC/ECC) use very low water-binder ratios
(W/B < 0.30) and high binder contents, making restrained shrinkage cracking the primary
threat to structural integrity and durability across hardening.

### Four principal shrinkage mechanisms:
1. **Autogenous shrinkage**:
   - Volume contraction from chemical binding of water during hydration (chemical
     shrinkage, Le Chatelier contraction) plus self-desiccation capillary tension,
     without external moisture loss; dominant in ultra-low W/B matrices.
2. **Drying shrinkage**:
   - Meniscus capillary tension as free pore water evaporates in a low-humidity
     environment, plus C-S-H interlayer water desorption contraction.
3. **Plastic shrinkage**:
   - Tensile cracking at the surface when evaporation exceeds bleeding rate in the fresh state.
4. **Carbonation shrinkage**:
   - Microstructural collapse contraction when atmospheric $CO_2$ reacts with calcium
     hydroxide and C-S-H gel, replacing them with carbonates.

## 2. Tensile creep and restraint stress relaxation

As shrinkage proceeds in a restrained member, elastic tensile stress accumulates toward
cracking:
$$\sigma(t) = R_r \int_0^t E(t') \left( \frac{d\varepsilon_{sh}}{dt'} - \frac{d\varepsilon_{cr}}{dt'} \right) dt'$$
- **Stress relaxation mechanism**:
  - Alkali-activated slag (AAS) and SHCC matrices exhibit relatively low elastic modulus
    ($E_c \approx 15\sim 35\ \text{GPa}$) and high early tensile creep
    ($40\sim 60\ \mu\varepsilon/\text{MPa}$).
  - This high creep **spontaneously relaxes (Relaxation) 30–50 %** of the accumulated
    restrained tensile stress, substantially delaying early-age cracking.

## 3. Crack control and mix-based mitigation

- **Expansive admixture combined with shrinkage-reducing admixture (SRA)**: ettringite-
  formation expansion pressure of CSA-type expansive agents offsets early autogenous shrinkage.
- **Fine multiple-crack distribution through fiber bridging**: shrinkage deformation is
  absorbed by dispersing into tens to hundreds of 50 µm fine cracks instead of one large crack.
