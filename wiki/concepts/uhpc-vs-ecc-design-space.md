# UHPC vs. ECC Design Space

## Definition

The design space comparing two paradigms of cementitious composite design — the
**UHPC (Ultra-High Performance Concrete)** paradigm maximizing compressive strength
($f_c'$) and the **ECC (Engineered Cementitious Composites)** paradigm maximizing
tensile strain capacity ($\epsilon_{tu}$) and crack control — together with their fusion
domain (UHDCC / UHP-ECC).

## Comparison of mechanical performance and material behavior

| Item | UHPC | ECC | UHDCC / UHP-ECC |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Compressive strength ($f_c'$)** | 120–200+ MPa | 30–70 MPa | 100–150+ MPa |
| **Tensile strength ($\sigma_t$)** | 8–15 MPa | 3–8 MPa | 10–20 MPa |
| **Tensile strain capacity ($\epsilon_{tu}$)** | 0.1–0.5 % (softening or slight hardening) | **2.0–6.0+ %** (saturated hardening) | **2.0–5.0 %** (high-strength PSH) |
| **Principal fibers** | high-strength steel fiber (0.2 mm dia., 1.5–2.5 vol.%) | high-strength PVA/PE fiber (12–40 µm, 1.5–2.0 vol.%) | ultra-high-strength fine PE + hybrid steel |
| **Failure mode** | high-strength single crack or partial multiple cracking | **fine multiple cracking ($w \le 60\ \mu\text{m}$)** | high-stress saturated fine multiple cracking |
| **Core design principle** | densest packing (DSP theory), minimum W/B, defect minimization | micromechanical energy/strength criteria ($J'_b \ge J_{tip}$, $\sigma_0 \ge \sigma_{fc}$) | high-strength matrix toughness control + high-friction interface tailoring |

## The core trade-off of the design space

- **The paradox of matrix toughness ($K_m$)**: densifying the matrix to raise compressive
  strength sharply increases its fracture toughness and crack-tip energy.
- Achieving strain-hardening (ECC behavior) in a high-strength matrix therefore demands
  dramatically higher fiber bridging capacity ($\sigma_0, J'_b$): ultra-high-strength PE
  fibers (tensile strength ≥ 3000 MPa) and optimal interface friction control are essential.
