# NDT and SHM of Cementitious Composites

## Definition

The technology framework of **Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)** and **Structural Health
Monitoring (SHM)** for diagnosing and surveilling crack initiation, damage evolution,
multiple-crack distribution, and stress/strain states of cementitious composites and
structures in real time or periodically, without impairing structural function.

## Principal sensing and monitoring technologies

1. **Acoustic Emission (AE)**:
   - Piezoelectric sensors detect elastic waves emitted by fine-crack initiation, fiber
     debonding, and fiber pullout/rupture inside the material.
   - Signal parameter analysis (amplitude, energy, rise time, RA value, $b$-value)
     quantitatively identifies shear versus tensile failure modes.
2. **Fiber Bragg Grating (FBG) sensors**:
   - Wavelength shifts ($\Delta \lambda_B$) of gratings formed in the optical fiber core
     measure local strain and temperature at ultra-high precision.
   - Embedded in smart tendons or concrete to continuously monitor long-term prestress
     loss and member deformation.
3. **Digital Image Correlation (DIC) and surface vision inspection**:
   - Full-field visualization of surface strain fields and crack openings ($w$) at
     micron resolution during loading, quantifying multiple-cracking saturation in real time.
4. **Self-sensing (piezoresistive) smart composites**:
   - Carbon nanotubes (CNT), carbon fibers, or graphene dispersed in the matrix form
     conductive networks.
   - Changes in electrical resistance ($\Delta R / R_0$) under load and damage let the
     material sense its own strain and cracking without embedded sensors.

## Related parameters

- $b$-value: AE amplitude distribution index (rises with fine-crack bursts; drops sharply
  for macro-cracks)
- $\lambda_B$: FBG Bragg reflected wavelength (nm)
- GF (gauge factor): piezoresistive sensitivity of self-sensing composites
  ($( \Delta R/R_0)/\epsilon$)
