# Advanced Concrete Technology: Constituent Materials (textbook)

## Summary

Volume 1, "Constituent Materials," of the four-volume set edited by John Newman (Imperial College London) and Ban Seng Choo (Napier University) (Butterworth-Heinemann 2003, 280 pp. in PDF). The complete set consists of four volumes — materials / concrete properties / processes / testing and quality — and this PDF contains only the materials volume. An advanced textbook written against the Institute of Concrete Technology ACT course syllabus; not a research paper, recorded as a reference-book entry.

## Main Table of Contents (4 parts, 8 chapters)

- Part 1 Cements: Ch1 Portland cement (clinker chemistry, hydration, cements by EN 197-1 specification, quality control; G. Moir), Ch2 calcium aluminate cements (CAC potential and applications; K. Scrivener)
- Part 2 Cementitious Additions: Ch3 reaction mechanisms of fly ash, natural pozzolans, ggbs, silica fume, metakaolin, and limestone filler and their effects on concrete properties and durability (Lewis, Sear, Wainwright, Ryle)
- Part 3 Admixtures: Ch4 admixtures in full (plasticizers/superplasticizers, retarders, accelerators, air-entrainers, waterproofers, corrosion inhibitors, shrinkage-reducing admixtures (SRA), segregation-resistance agents, etc.; J. Dransfield)
- Part 4 Aggregates: Ch5 geological classification of aggregates and deleterious constituents (Poole & Sims), Ch6 aggregate extraction and processing (Murrin-Earp), Ch7 manufacture of lightweight aggregates (power-station slag, expanded clay, sintered PFA, etc.; Owens & Newman), Ch8 effects of natural aggregates on concrete properties (grading, maximum aggregate size MAS, shape, density, drying shrinkage; Lay)
