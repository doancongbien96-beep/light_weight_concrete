## Summary
The most authoritative review in the field of FRC impact, comprehensively covering the fracture-mechanical response of various fiber-reinforced concretes (FRC: steel fibers, synthetic polymer fibers PVA/PE/PP, carbon fibers, natural fibers, and hybrid fibers) under low-velocity drop-weight impact, medium-velocity split Hopkinson pressure bar (SHPB), and high-velocity projectile impact loading, together with energy absorption density, interfacial friction kinetics, and the impact-resistant behavior of structural members (beams, columns, slabs). The interactions of fiber aspect ratio, volume fraction, geometric shape, and matrix strength on dynamic impact toughness were systematized.

## Key Contributions
- **Comparative analysis of the four standard FRC impact test techniques (ACI 544 drop-weight, instrumented drop-weight impact, SHPB, and projectile gas gun)**: standardization of data interpretation across test apparatuses.
- **Compilation of dynamic energy absorption envelopes by fiber type and volume fraction**: characterization of the ductile multi-crack dissipation of synthetic fibers versus the localized penetration resistance of steel fibers.
- **Establishment of mix design guidelines for next-generation impact-resistant protective composite materials**: presentation of a multi-scale hybrid fiber design method.
