# Handbook of Alkali-Activated Cements, Mortars and Concretes (Textbook)

## Summary

The standard handbook (856 pages) of the alkali-activated materials (AAM) field. An edited textbook covering the raw materials of geopolymers and AAMs (metakaolin, slag, FA), activators (sodium silicate, sodium hydroxide), reaction mechanisms (N-A-S-H/C-A-S-H gels), properties and durability, and one-part (dry-mix) AAMs. Not a research paper — recorded as a reference-type note.
