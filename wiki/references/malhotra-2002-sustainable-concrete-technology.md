# Sustainable Development and Concrete Technology (Malhotra, 2002)

## Summary

A foreword written by V.M. Malhotra, chairman of the ACI Committee on Sustainable Development — a historic declaration warning of the $\text{CO}_2$ emission problem posed by Portland cement clinker manufacture, which accounts for about 7 % of global greenhouse gas emissions, and urging that the concrete industry must secure its environmental sustainability through high-volume fly ash concrete (HVFA) and large-scale industrial by-product replacement technologies.

## Key Themes

- **One tonne of $\text{CO}_2$ emitted per tonne of cement clinker**: quantifying the environmental burden from limestone calcination ($\text{CaCO}_3 \rightarrow \text{CaO} + \text{CO}_2$) and fuel combustion in high-temperature clinkering.
- **The imperative of high-volume mineral admixture (SCM) replacement**: arguing that cement replacement via fly ash, ground granulated blast-furnace slag, and silica fume is the key pathway to simultaneously achieving durability improvement and carbon reduction.
