# Engineered Concrete: Mix Design and Test Methods (Textbook)

## Summary

Irving Kett's textbook on concrete mix design and testing methods (248 pp, CRC Press, 2nd edition). Recorded in the reference-book tier since it is not a research paper. A practice-oriented text covering mix design procedures for ordinary Portland cement concrete and its property testing methods (fresh and hardened concrete).

## Position in the Wiki

- Background reference for the standard test methods cited by ECC/EGC papers (ASTM C39 compression, C78/C1609 flexure, C157 shrinkage, C143 slump, etc.).
- Not a deep-read target.
