## Summary
The most authoritative comprehensive review collating the uniaxial direct tensile test (DTT) methodologies for ECC/SHCC performed by research institutions worldwide, specimen geometries (dogbone, dumbbell, flat rectangular, and prismatic), gripping and end-fixity conditions (pin hinges versus fixed clamps), displacement measurement techniques (LVDT gauge length effects), and strain-hardening envelope interpretation criteria. It quantitatively analyzed the influence of test conditions on measured tensile strength and strain capacity and presented standardization guidelines for direct tension testing.

## Key Contributions
- **Comparative analysis of the four major types of direct tensile specimen geometry and boundary conditions**: deviation ($\pm 25 \%$) in measured strain capacity quantified between rotational pin fixation and fixed clamps.
- **Gauge length effect formulated**: a size effect model established whereby apparent strain capacity decreases gently as gauge length grows due to increasing probability of crack localization.
- **Global ECC tensile property database constructed**: tensile strength-ductility correlation envelope built from some 500 mixes.
