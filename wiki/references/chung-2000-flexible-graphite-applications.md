## Summary

A representative review by Prof. D.D.L. Chung (SUNY Buffalo) synthesizing the material-
mechanical and physicochemical mechanisms behind four key industrial applications of flexible
graphite (Grafoil) — natural flake graphite acid-treated and thermally exfoliated, then roll-
pressed without binder: high-temperature/high-pressure gasket sealing, organic-solvent
adsorption, EMI shielding, and structural vibration damping. It systematizes the loss factor
from graphene-sheet interlayer shear slip ($\tan\delta = 0.08 \sim 0.15$), in-plane thermal
conductivity ($200 W/(m\cdot K)$), electrical conductivity ($10^4 S/cm$), and refractory
resistance ($3000 °C$, non-oxidizing).

## Key Contributions

- **Four-way multifunctionality mapping of flexible graphite**: sealability, adsorption,
  EMI shielding (> 40 dB at 1 GHz), and damping capacity consolidated.
- **Layered-structure analysis of binder-free roll-pressed exfoliated graphite**: mechanical
  interlocking between graphite worm particles elucidated.
- **Structural vibration-damping composite applicability shown**: as interlayer damping in
  metal/polymer laminates the loss coefficient is amplified fivefold.
- **Design guidelines for smart infrastructure and architectural shielding panels**:
  lightweight highly conductive shield structures.

## Methods

- Feedstock: exfoliated graphite worms roll-pressed into sheets 0.1 ~ 1.0 mm thick.
- Measurements: tensile strength, flexural modulus, dynamic loss factor (DMA), shielding
  effectiveness (coaxial holder method, 1 GHz).
