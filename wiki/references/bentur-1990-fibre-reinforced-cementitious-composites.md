# Fibre reinforced cementitious composites textbook (Bentur & Mindess 1990)

## Summary

First edition (1990) of the standard FRC textbook by Arnon Bentur (Technion) and Sidney
Mindess (UBC). This PDF is the Taylor & Francis e-Library 2005 electronic edition (493 pp).
The filename fragment 'bendur_…modern-co…' is a truncated title; bibliographic data were
confirmed from the book's own cover page (Arnon Bentur/Sidney Mindess, Elsevier Applied
Science, © 1990) — recorded as a reference-type entry since it is not a research paper,
hence not a deep-read target. The period's standard reference work spanning the structure
of FRC (Ch2), the fiber-cement interface (Ch3), reinforcing efficiency (Ch4), composite
mechanics (Ch5), testing methods (Ch6), and the fiber-type chapters (steel/glass/asbestos/
synthetic/natural/special systems, Ch7-12).

## Organization

- Part I — Mechanical behavior of fiber-reinforced cement materials: Ch2 material
  structure; Ch3 fiber-cement interaction (stress transfer, bond, pull-out); Ch4 fiber
  reinforcing efficiency (orientation and length efficiency); Ch5 mechanics of FRC
  composites; Ch6 testing methods
- Part II — Cement composites by fiber type: Ch7 steel; Ch8 glass (GRC, AR glass);
  Ch9 asbestos; Ch10 synthetic fibers; Ch11 natural fibers; Ch12 special FRC systems
