## Summary

A pharmacognosy/pharmacology paper elucidating the anti-inflammatory activity and cellular mechanisms of triterpenoid and sesquiterpene constituents isolated from the aerial-part extract of Celastrus orbiculatus. The inhibitory efficacy against lipopolysaccharide-induced nitric oxide (NO) production and NF-$\kappa$B transcription factor activation in the macrophage cell line (RAW 264.7) was quantitatively evaluated. It is classified as external-reference medicinal-plant extract research material within the cement composites research corpus.

## Key Contributions

- Isolation of five active compounds (celastrol, pristimerin, etc.) from Celastrus orbiculatus stems/leaves and elucidation of their chemical structures.
- Demonstration of potent NO-production-inhibiting activity of the celastrol compound in RAW 264.7 macrophages at the $IC_{50} = 0.42 \mu M$ level.
- Confirmation of an NF-$\kappa$B pathway-blocking mechanism via inhibition of $I\kappa B-\alpha$ degradation.
