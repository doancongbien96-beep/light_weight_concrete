## Summary

A comprehensive technical report compiled under the Texas Department of Transportation (TxDOT Project 0-7030) that consolidates raw-material procurement criteria, micromechanics-based mix design, quality-control test specifications, and field application guidelines for standardizing the construction of high-ductility ECC for pavement overlays, bridge deck link slabs, expansion joint replacements, and pier repair. Drawing on Texas local fly ash, local silica sand, and PVA and PE fibers, it develops field-cast ECC mixes suited to ambient and hot environments, and demonstrates through life-cycle cost analysis (LCCA) a threefold extension of bridge maintenance service life together with a 45 % reduction in cost.

## Key Contributions

- **Establishment of a standard specification for roadway ECC in transportation infrastructure**: specifies minimum tensile strain capacity $\epsilon_u \ge 2.5 \%$, 28 d compressive strength $f_c \ge 35 MPa$, and crack width $w \le 0.05 mm$.
- **Mix design manual built on locally sourced materials**: mix proportions established that replace 100 % of expensive imported silica sand with local manufactured sand.
- **Construction guidelines completed for full-scale bridge deck link slabs and pavement overlays**: standardized procedures for field mixing, pumping, placing, texturing, and curing.
