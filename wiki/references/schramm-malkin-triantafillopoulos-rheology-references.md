# Three Key References on Rheology Measurement and Fundamentals

## Summary

A single unified note consolidating three reference works that cover the measurement and theoretical fundamentals of ECC/cementitious composite rheology research.

1. **Schramm, A Practical Approach to Rheology and Rheometry (2nd ed.)** — practical measurement manual for rotational viscometers and rheometers. Centered on flow curve interpretation and instrument calibration procedures.
2. **Malkin, Rheology Fundamentals (ChemTec 1994)** — introductory textbook on the theory of viscoelasticity and non-Newtonian flow.
3. **Triantafillopoulos, Measurement of Fluid Rheology and Interpretation of Rheograms (2nd ed., Kaltec)** — practical guide for reading flow curves (rheograms).
