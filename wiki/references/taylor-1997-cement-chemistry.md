# Cement Chemistry (Taylor, textbook)

## Summary

Harold F.W. Taylor's Cement Chemistry is the foremost authoritative textbook on cement hydration chemistry. It covers C-S-H gel structure, clinker mineral reactions, and the chemistry of pozzolan and blast-furnace slag blended cements. A fundamental reference for discussions of ECC matrix design (shrinkage mechanisms, interfacial chemistry).
