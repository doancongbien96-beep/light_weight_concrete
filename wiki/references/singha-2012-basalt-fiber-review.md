## Summary

A foundational review organizing the chemical composition, melt-spinning manufacturing process, mechanical/thermal/chemical properties, and composite-material applicability of basalt fiber, an eco-friendly high-performance inorganic fiber made from basalt ore. Through property and manufacturing-cost comparisons against glass fibers (E-glass, S-glass) and carbon fiber, it systematizes basalt fiber's high heat resistance ($-260 \sim +700 °C$), excellent chemical durability (acid/alkali resistance), electromagnetic transparency, and eco-friendly non-toxic character.

## Key Contributions

- **Systematization of basalt fiber chemical composition and spinning process**: organizes the optimal mineral ratios of $SiO_2$ (45–52 %), $Al_2O_3$ (12–16 %), and $CaO, MgO, FeO$, together with the $1450 \sim 1500 °C$ platinum-rhodium nozzle spinning process technology.
- **Physical/mechanical property benchmarking**: tensile strength $3000 \sim 4800 MPa$, elastic modulus $85 \sim 110 GPa$, elongation at break $3.1 \%$, and density $2.75 g/cm3$ — presenting superior specific strength versus E-glass.
- **Mapping of construction and composite application fields**: cement-reinforcing mono-filaments, prestressing tendons (tendons), fire-resistant panels, and civil-engineering fiber ground reinforcement potential.
