# Concrete Technology (Neville & Brooks, textbook)

## Summary

Second edition (460 pp., scanned copy) of the standard concrete engineering textbook by A.M. Neville (author of the classic Properties of Concrete) and J.J. Brooks. Covers cement types and hydration, aggregates, fresh and hardened concrete properties, durability, and testing methods. Not a research paper; recorded as a reference-book entry.
