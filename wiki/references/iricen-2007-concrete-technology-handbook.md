# Concrete Technology Handbook (IRICEN, 2007)

## Summary

A practical concrete technology handbook published by the Indian Railways Institute of Civil Engineering (IRICEN) for engineers of railway and infrastructure structures. A comprehensive guide that systematically covers the chemistry and hydration of cement, aggregate grading and quality, chemical and mineral admixtures (Fly Ash, GGBS, Silica Fume), concrete mix design principles, field quality control, high-performance concrete (HPC) and self-compacting concrete (SCC), and non-destructive testing and durability diagnostic criteria.

## Key Themes

1. **Raw materials and binder chemistry**: cement mineral composition ($\text{C}_3\text{S}, \text{C}_2\text{S}, \text{C}_3\text{A}, \text{C}_4\text{AF}$), heat-of-hydration control, alkali-aggregate reaction (AAR), and sulfate attack mechanisms.
2. **Mix design and construction technology**: strength- and durability-oriented mix design based on IS and international standards (W/C ratio and cover depth requirements), ready-mixed concrete (RMC) production, and placing/curing management.
3. **Special concretes and durability assessment**: rheological evaluation of high-strength/high-performance concrete (HPC) and self-compacting concrete (SCC), and non-destructive inspection techniques including ultrasonic pulse velocity (UPV) and rebound hardness testing.
