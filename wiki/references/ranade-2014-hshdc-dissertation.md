# Dr. Ranade's PhD Dissertation: HSHDC·COR-TUF Development

## Summary

PhD dissertation (419 pp.) written under the supervision of Victor Li. It integrally covers the micromechanical design, material characterization, and impact resistance (including LS-Dyna analysis) of HSHDC (high-strength high-ductility concrete) and COR-TUF (a hooked steel-fiber UHPC-class composite). Since it is the full-length content that extends the journal-paper-form [[ranade-2013-hshdc-composite-properties]], it is recorded as a reference-type note; the single-fiber pullout details (Table 6.1~6.3), the drop-weight impact tests (Chapter 8), and the COR-TUF mix design (Chapter 3) are additional information not found in the paper note.
