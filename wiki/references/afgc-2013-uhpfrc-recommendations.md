# AFGC/SETRA French Design Recommendations for UHPFRC

## Summary

Interim design recommendations (98 pp., in French) for ultra-high performance fiber-reinforced concrete (UHPFRC) issued jointly by the Association Française de Génie Civil (AFGC) and SETRA. A world-first-class UHPFRC design specification covering material classification (compressive strength grades and fiber types), tensile behavior modeling (softening/hardening), flexural and shear design, and durability and construction requirements. Recorded as a reference-book entry.
