# Eurocode 2 Concrete Structure Design Guide

## Summary

A practitioner-oriented design guide to Eurocode 2 (EC2, the European structural concrete design code) issued by the UK concrete and cement industry. Produced for practitioners during the transition period from BS 8110 to EC2, it is a commentary on design standards rather than a research paper. From the ECC/EGC wiki's perspective this is background material on general RC design codes, carrying reference value when contrasted with the structural design of fiber-reinforced cement composites (e.g., the SHCC provisions of fib MC2010).

## Contents (11 chapters)

1. Introduction to the Eurocodes — the family of 10 EC-series codes, the National Annex concept, differences from BS 8110
2. Getting started — design procedure, determination of actions
3. Slabs — slab design
4. Beams — beam design
5. Columns — column design
6. Foundations — foundation design
7. Flat slabs — flat plates
8. Deflection calculations — deflection calculation
9. Retaining walls — retaining walls
10. Detailing — detailing
11. BS 8500 for building structures — durability-related companion standard

## Position in the Wiki

- This document is not directly related to strain-hardening composite research. It is used only as background literature when contrasting general RC member design procedures (flexure, shear, deflection) with ECC/SHCC member design.
