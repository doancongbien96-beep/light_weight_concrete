# Multi-Scale Modeling of Structural Concrete Textbook

## Summary

The standard textbook (670 pp., 2009) on multiscale concrete modeling from the Maekawa group at the University of Tokyo. It links the cement hydration scale (μm) to the structural scale (m) within a single consistent chemo-physics framework, spanning multi-component hydration models, micropore structure and moisture transport, carbonation, chloride ingress, calcium leaching, creep solidification modeling, and the durability mechanics of damaged RC. A background reference for discussions of durability, creep, and damage modeling of ECC/SHCC.

## Main Contents

1. Hydration: multi-component cement mineral and pozzolan hydration models, strength development
2. Pore structure and moisture transport: micropore formation, temperature-dependent equilibrium
3. Carbon dioxide transport and carbonation
4. Calcium ion transport and leaching (including cement-solidified soil)
5. Chloride transport and rebar corrosion
6. Time-dependent mechanics: pore moisture-creep, solidification model, aggregate mechanics
7. Time-dependent behavior of structural concrete: elasto-plastic and fracture models, cyclic damage, post-cracking long-term constitutive models
8. Durability mechanics of damaged structural concrete: corrosion cracks and member performance
