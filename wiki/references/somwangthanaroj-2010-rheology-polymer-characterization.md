## Summary
An educational and technical reference that systematically explains the fundamental principles of rheology for polymeric materials and complex fluids, viscoelasticity, shear flow behavior (shear thinning, yield stress, and shear thixotropy), and dynamic mechanical analysis (DMA) measurement techniques. It covers the constitutive equations of Newtonian and non-Newtonian fluids (Bingham, Herschel-Bulkley, Power-law) and the mechanical definitions of the storage modulus ($G'$), loss modulus ($G''$), and loss factor ($\tan\delta$), and presents principles for the rheological control of polymer melts and cement–polymer composite suspensions.

## Key Contributions
- **Systematization of non-Newtonian fluid rheology models**: interpretation of the physical parameters of the Herschel-Bulkley equation $\tau = \tau_0 + k \dot{\gamma}^n$.
- **Characterization of dynamic viscoelasticity**: analysis of the $G', G''$ crossover point under frequency sweep and of gelation.
- **Provides a foundation for analyzing the dispersion stability of cement–polymer composite fluids**: explains the principles of viscosity control according to molecular weight and chain structure.
