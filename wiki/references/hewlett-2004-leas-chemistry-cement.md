# Lea's Chemistry of Cement and Concrete (Textbook)

## Summary

The authoritative 4th edition (1066 pp., edited by Peter Hewlett) of the standard textbook in cement chemistry. Covers clinker minerals, hydration reactions, C-S-H structure, SCMs (fly ash, slag, silica fume), and chemical admixture mechanisms. It is the fundamental chemistry reference for discussions of ECC/EGC matrix design (toughness reduction, shrinkage control, heat of hydration).
