# An Introduction to Rheology (textbook)

## Summary

The standard introduction to rheology by Barnes, Hutton, and Walters (200 pp): viscosity,
viscoelasticity, shear-thinning, thixotropy, and other rheological fundamentals. A reference
for the theoretical background of fresh cementitious-material rheology
([[yang-2009-rheological-control-in-production-of]], [[zhu-2019-3dp-ecc-ultra-high-ductility]],
[[ranade-2013-hshdc-composite-properties]]).
