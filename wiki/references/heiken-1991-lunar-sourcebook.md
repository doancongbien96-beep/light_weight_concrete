# Lunar Sourcebook (reference book)

## Summary

An encyclopedia of lunar science comprehensively covering data from the Apollo and Luna programs (778 pp., Cambridge UP 1991). It organizes the current knowledge of lunar surface formation and evolution; the chemical and mineralogical composition of lunar rocks and the regolith (weathered layer); and the lunar environment. It serves as a primary reference for research on construction materials for lunar bases (regolith-based concrete, geomers, etc.). From the perspective of space applications of ECC/ultra-high-performance materials, the regolith properties chapter is a key reference.
