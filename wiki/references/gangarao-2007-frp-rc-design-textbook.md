# Reinforced Concrete Design with FRP Composites (Textbook)

## Summary

A CRC Press textbook on the design of concrete structures reinforced/strengthened with FRP (fiber reinforced polymer) composites (400 pages). Not a research paper, so filed as a reference-book note. A standard text for FRP reinforcement and strengthening (rehabilitation) design, useful as background when discussing structural strengthening applications of ECC/EGC (e.g., ECC-FRP hybrid strengthening).
