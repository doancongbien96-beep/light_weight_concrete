# Introduction to the Mechanics of a Continuous Medium (Textbook)

## Summary

L.E. Malvern's classic textbook on continuum mechanics (723 pages). It covers stress and deformation tensors, constitutive equations, and thermodynamic foundations. Serves as a reference for the mathematical underpinnings of micromechanics-based ECC design theory ([[li-wu-1992-psh-conditions]] et al.). Recorded as a reference-book entry.
