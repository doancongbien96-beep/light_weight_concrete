## Summary
A review paper systematizing the physical mechanisms, measurement techniques, and computational dynamics analysis models of material damping (Material Damping, internal-friction loss factor $\eta$) and structural member damping (Member/Joint Damping) for controlling the dynamic vibration response of structures and composite materials (metals, concrete, fiber composites, sandwich panels). It formalizes the complex elastic modulus ($E^* = E(1 + i\eta)$), the strain energy loss ratio, and the frequency/temperature dependence of viscoelastic dampers, and presents seismic and wind-induced vibration control design guidelines.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishment of a classification system for material and structural damping mechanisms**: organizing the contributions of thermoelastic damping, dislocation slip, interfacial Coulomb friction, and viscoelastic relaxation.
- **Formulation of the modal loss factor (Modal Loss Factor) estimation equation**: presenting a strain-energy-weighted damping integral model for composite cross-sections.
- **Compilation of standard damping coefficient values for building and civil engineering structural members**: mapping the inherent damping ratios of RC, steel, and composite structures.
