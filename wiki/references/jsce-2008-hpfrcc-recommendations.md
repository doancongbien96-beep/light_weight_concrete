# JSCE HPFRCC Design and Construction Recommendations (Concrete Engineering Series 82)

## Summary

Performance-based design and construction recommendations for multiple-fine-cracking-type HPFRCC (high performance fiber reinforced cement composites), published in English by the JSCE (Japan Society of Civil Engineers) Concrete Committee in March 2008 (113 pp). Drafting began with a subcommittee formed in September 2005 chaired by Rokugo and proceeded to full-committee approval in December 2006, with international advisors including Victor Li, Maalej, and Kabele providing technical comments through the RILEM TC-HFC route. Its core is a quantitative definition of the target material — specimens prepared per Testing Method 1 whose uniaxial tensile test (Testing Method 2) yields mean ultimate tensile strain > 0.5 % and mean crack width < 0.2 mm (Testing Method 3) — plus a classification framework that separates ordinary FRC, DFRCC (deflection-hardening), HPFRCC (pseudo strain-hardening), and ultra-high-strength FRC on a tensile strain-strength diagram. The body consists of Chapters 1-11 (terminology; design fundamentals and safety factors; design material properties [tensile yield strength, tensile strength, ultimate tensile strain characteristic values, stress-strain curves, fatigue, crack width]; loads; structural analysis; safety verification [flexure, shear, torsion, fatigue]; serviceability verification [allowable crack width, deflection, vibration]; structural details; environmental durability verification [carbonation, chloride attack, freezing-thawing, fire, etc.]; cast-in-place construction [materials, mix proportioning, curing, inspection]; shotcrete) together with Testing Methods 1-4 (compression/tension specimen preparation, uniaxial tension, crack width mean and variability) and appendices (example design properties by product, experimental data for crack-width and durability verification).
