## Summary
The most authoritative comprehensive review covering the physical principles of optical fiber sensing technologies for long-term safety diagnosis of large civil infrastructure such as bridges, tunnels, dams, and super-tall buildings (point sensors FBG, distributed sensors BOTDA/BOTDR, and long-gauge fiber optic sensors), embedding techniques in cement composites and structures, temperature-strain cross-sensitivity compensation, and global real-bridge monitoring application cases. It systematized that optical fiber sensors with immunity to electromagnetic interference, corrosion resistance, and long-distance multiplexing capability are the core technology of smart infrastructure health monitoring (SHM).

## Key Contributions
- **Three measurement schemes of optical fiber sensors (Point, Quasi-distributed, Distributed) systematized**: performance comparison among FBG, Fabry-Perot, and Brillouin scattering.
- **Embedding inside concrete structures and surface-bonding packaging technologies standardized**: interface shear slip correction coefficients derived.
- **Guidelines for building SHM systems on full-scale bridges presented**: real-time measurement protocols established for dynamic strain, crack occurrence, and vibration modal shapes.
