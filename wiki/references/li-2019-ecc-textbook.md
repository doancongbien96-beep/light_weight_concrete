# ECC Textbook (Victor C. Li, Springer)

## Summary

The standard ECC textbook (428 pages) written by ECC inventor Victor Li himself. An authoritative work covering the entire field of ECC, from micromechanics-based design principles through material and structural applications to durability and sustainability. As it is not a research paper it is recorded as a reference-type entry, serving as the consolidated synthesis of the wiki's PSH theory axis ([[li-wu-1992-psh-conditions]] etc.).
