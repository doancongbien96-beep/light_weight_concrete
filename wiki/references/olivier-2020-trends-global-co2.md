# Global CO₂ · Greenhouse Gas Emission Trends Report 2020

## Summary

The annual emissions statistics report (85 pp., based on the EDGAR database) of the PBL Netherlands Environmental Assessment Agency. It organizes world CO₂ emissions and total greenhouse gas trends for 2019, developments in major emitting countries, and the short-term impact of COVID-19 on emissions. The cement industry is treated as a major item of fossil-fuel · industrial-process emissions — a macro-statistics reference for sustainability discussions of ECC/cementitious composites.
