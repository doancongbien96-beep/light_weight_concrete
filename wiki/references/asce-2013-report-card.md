# ASCE 2013 Report Card for America's Infrastructure

## Summary

ASCE's quadrennial assessment of US infrastructure (74 pp). Grades all sectors — roads,
bridges, etc. (GPA D+) — with analysis of the investment gap. Source of the aging-infrastructure
statistics cited by ECC repair papers ([[li-mo-2011-hes-ecc-repair]] and others). Recorded as
a reference-type entry.
