# ASTM C215 Resonant Frequency Test Method

## Summary

Standard test method that measures the fundamental transverse, longitudinal, and torsional resonant frequencies of concrete specimens to derive dynamic properties such as the dynamic modulus of elasticity. The foundational specification for monitoring changes in dynamic properties caused by cracking and deterioration.
