# Conferencia Internacional de Reologia y Extrusion (Campanella lecture)

## Summary

Comprehensive lecture slide deck (158 slides, Spanish) covering the rheology of food and biomaterials together with extrusion processing. Not a research paper, hence recorded as a reference-book entry. Main contents: (1) steady-shear flow curves and rheological models — power-law (consistency index k, flow index n; n<1 shear-thinning, n>1 shear-thickening), Herschel-Bulkley, Casson, Ellis, Carreau, and the yield-stress concept, (2) time-dependent flow (thixotropy — ropy/non-ropy yogurt strain comparison data, full/partial/no recovery), (3) measurement geometries (concentric cylinder, parallel plate, cone-plate, capillary, mixer type), (4) viscoelasticity — storage and loss moduli, phase angle, complex viscosity, Cox-Merz rule, Deborah number, the Maxwell element and the generalized Maxwell model, transient tests (start-up flow, interrupted shear, step-strain relaxation, creep, recoil), temperature dependence (Arrhenius-type), (5) glass transition — Tg measurement (DSC, DMA, vibrational compaction flow OSF), amorphization-driven enhancement of drug solubility, (6) extrusion processing — a glassy-to-rubbery melt transition driven by shear and heating within 1 min as the condition for expansion, corn flake processing, high-loading extrusion of arabinoxylan-modified fiber, gluten-free dough (zein/glutenin disulfide-bond model), (7) food nanotechnology (starch-protein-free-fatty-acid three-component nanocomposites, CLA encapsulation), SANS, molecular dynamics simulation. Concrete is not covered, but the rheological-model and measurement framework is common across materials.

## Position in This Wiki

- Yield-stress fluid models such as Herschel-Bulkley and the oscillatory/transient rheology testing framework rest on the same theoretical foundation as the fresh-state rheology analysis of cementitious materials (ECC printing ink, AAS paste).
- The glass-transition and amorphous-phase concepts do not connect directly to discussions of cement hydrates or geopolymer gel structures, but serve as reference material for the methodology of tracking material state changes during processing through rheology.
- Not a deep-read target. Open the relevant slides whenever a rheological-model definition or test method needs verification.
