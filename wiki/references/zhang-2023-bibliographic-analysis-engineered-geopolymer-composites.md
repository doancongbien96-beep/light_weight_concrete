## Summary
A review paper performing scientometric bibliographic analysis of some 800 SCI papers on strain-hardening geopolymer composites (EGC/SHGC) published from 2010 through 2023 using VOSviewer and CiteSpace. It derived leading researchers, research institutions, country-wise collaboration networks, and core keyword co-occurrence clustering, and systematized the four major research trends of EGC (ambient-cured one-part EGC, ultra-low fiber mix design, fully recycled sustainability, and behavior in special environments of impact and high temperature).

## Key Contributions
- **Global EGC research landscape and knowledge maps drawn**: visualization of research hubs centered on China, Australia, and the United States and the citation networks of core papers.
- **Next-generation research frontiers identified through keyword burst analysis**: analysis of the rapid emergence of one-part geopolymers, 3D printing, and carbon-negative binders.
- **Research gaps and future standardization tasks derived**: recommendation of the need for long-term durability data and standard specifications for full-scale structural members.
