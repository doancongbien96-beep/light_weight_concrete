## Summary
A monumental classic review of the FRC field comprehensively covering the 30-year development history of fiber reinforced concrete, fiber types (steel, glass, synthetic, carbon, and natural fibers), interface bonding mechanisms, flexural toughness test standards (ASTM C1018, JSCE-SF4), and structural application fields. It collated the one-dimensional/three-dimensional bridging mechanics of short fibers, the classification framework of post-crack softening versus hardening behavior, and the evolution of FRC specification standards, and pointed out the development direction toward next-generation high-performance strain-hardening composites (HPFRCC).

## Key Contributions
- **FRC development history and fiber classification framework established**: division of roles between macro and micro fibers systematized.
- **Mechanical meaning of flexural toughness indices ($I_5, I_{10}, I_{20}, I_{30}$) defined**: interpretation via load-deflection curve energy area ratios.
- **Global application fields mapped**: utilization criteria organized for tunnel shotcrete, industrial floor slabs, and precast segments.
