# UHPC State-of-the-Art Report (FHWA)

## Summary

State-of-the-art report on UHPC by the US Federal Highway Administration (FHWA). Synthesizes the body of UHPC research for highway bridge infrastructure accumulated since 2001 under the Bridge of the Future initiative. A reference report covering mix design, mechanics, durability, and bridge application case studies.
