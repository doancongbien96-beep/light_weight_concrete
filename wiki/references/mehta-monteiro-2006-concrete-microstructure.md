# Concrete: Microstructure, Properties, and Materials (Mehta & Monteiro 3rd edition, textbook)

## Summary

Formal third edition (2006, McGraw-Hill, 684 pp.) of the standard concrete materials science textbook co-authored by P. Kumar Mehta and Paulo J. M. Monteiro (UC Berkeley). Known for explaining strength, dimensional stability, and durability from a microstructure perspective, it is the fundamental reference the wiki's ECC/EGC/UHDCC papers cite for ordinary-concrete comparison properties, the ITZ, and the strength-porosity relationship. Filed as a reference-book note.

## Main Table of Contents (3 parts, 13 chapters)

**I. Microstructure and Properties of Hardened Concrete**

1. Introduction — 2. Microstructure of Concrete (three phases: aggregate, paste, ITZ) — 3. Strength (strength-porosity, ITZ effects) — 4. Dimensional Stability (elasticity, creep, shrinkage) — 5. Durability (permeability, chloride, sulfate, ASR, freeze-thaw, corrosion)

**II. Concrete Materials, Mix Proportioning, and Early-Age Properties**

6. Hydraulic Cements — 7. Aggregates — 8. Admixtures — 9. Proportioning Concrete Mixtures — 10. Concrete at Early Age — 11. Nondestructive Methods (**new in the 3rd edition**, p.387)

**III. Recent Advances and Concrete in the Future**

12. Progress in Concrete Technology (lightweight, high-strength, FRC, etc., p.449) — 13. Advances in Concrete Mechanics (p.559)

## Edition Notes

- The wiki separately holds a note on the author-typeset preprint dated 2001-10-20 (239 pp.), [[mehta-monteiro-2001-concrete-microstructure-properties-materials]]. That draft runs a Part II of 10 chapters, whereas this formal 3rd edition extends Part II to 11 chapters and adds Chapter 11, Nondestructive Methods — the organizations differ, so edition-specific notes are kept separate rather than merged into one.
- Section 2.6 (ITZ, pp. 41-47) of Chapter 2 and the strength theory of Chapter 3 are the standard citation sources for interface-oriented and PSH-family research.
