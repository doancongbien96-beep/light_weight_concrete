## Summary
The most authoritative review article comprehensively covering micromechanics-based mix design of ultra-high-performance fiber-reinforced concrete (UHPFRC, $f_c > 150 MPa$, $f_t > 8 MPa$), uniaxial direct tensile behavior (strain-softening versus strain-hardening), flexural-shear-torsional structural member performance, global structural design specifications (AFGC-SNCF of France, JSCE of Japan, K-UHPC of Korea, FHWA of the United States), and field demonstration cases including long-span footbridges and super-high-rise buildings. The review systematized safe structural design criteria accounting for the fiber orientation distribution coefficient ($K$-factor) and size effects.

## Key Contributions
- **Comparative analysis of UHPFRC structural design codes across four countries**: organization of the characteristic value determination equations of the French AFGC, Japanese JSCE, Korean K-UHPC, and FHWA guidelines.
- **Establishment of the flexural and shear resistance mechanisms of tensile strain-hardening UHPFRC**: identification of conditions under which shear reinforcement can be eliminated entirely.
- **Analysis of representative global bridge and building application cases**: analysis of the application technologies of the Peace Bridge, Sebit Island, and long-span precast girders.
