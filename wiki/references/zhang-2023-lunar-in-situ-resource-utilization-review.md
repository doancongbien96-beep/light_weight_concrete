## Summary
The most authoritative comprehensive review covering the latest technology readiness levels (TRL) of the six core lunar in situ resource utilization (ISRU) technologies for permanent human lunar bases and deep space exploration (lunar regolith oxygen/water extraction, metal/semiconductor smelting, microwave/solar melt sintering, alkali geopolymerization solidification, unmanned robotic 3D printing construction, and energy harvesting), the roadmaps of global space agencies (NASA, ESA, CNSA), and verification results under extreme environments (vacuum, cryogenic/high temperature, radiation). It collated construction strategies for composite structural materials using lunar soil at 100 % with no water or minimal moisture.

## Key Contributions
- **Five major manufacturing methods of lunar ISRU construction classified and TRL-evaluated (sintering, geopolymer, sulfur concrete, 3D printing, casting)**: advantages and disadvantages compared per method.
- **Construction scenarios for lunar landing pads, roads, protective domes, and habitat modules established**: step-by-step automated construction protocols formulated.
- **Extreme space durability design criteria for ISRU materials presented**: thermal cycling, vacuum sublimation, and radiation degradation shielding guidelines systematized.
