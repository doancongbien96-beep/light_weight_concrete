## Summary

This original optics paper elucidates the design and output characteristics of a C-band ($1525 \sim 1565 nm$) continuously wavelength-tunable erbium-doped fiber laser (EDFL) light source for smart structural health monitoring (SHM) of structures based on Fiber Bragg Grating (FBG) sensors and for optical communication systems. By applying tuning via a PZT piezoelectric actuator and precision cantilever-beam mechanical tension/compression, the FBG reflection wavelength is tuned linearly without distortion, achieving a wide tuning range of at least 32 nm, a high side-mode suppression ratio (SMSR > 50 dB), and high wavelength stability.

## Key Contributions
- **Design of an FBG-based continuously wavelength-tunable laser cavity**: uniform tension driving by a cantilever bending mechanism realizes linear wavelength scanning without mode hopping.
- **Improved precision of optical strain/temperature sensor interrogators**: $0.1 pm$ wavelength resolution provides the basis for detecting minute concrete strains ($< 0.1 \mu\epsilon$).
- **Securing a high optical signal-to-noise ratio**: maintaining SMSR > 50 dB and optical output power $> 2.0 mW$.
