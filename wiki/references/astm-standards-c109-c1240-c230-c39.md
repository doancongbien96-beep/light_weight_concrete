# Four ASTM standards

## Summary

Four ASTM test standards stored as individual files under sources/, consolidated and recorded as a single reference-book note:

| Standard | Content | Use in ECC/EGC research |
|---|---|---|
| C109/C109M | mortar compressive strength (50 mm cubes) | compressive testing in [[li-mo-2011-hes-ecc-repair]], [[ranade-2013-hshdc-composite-properties]], etc. |
| C1240 | silica fume specification | silica fume quality basis for UHPC and ECC ([[abbas-2016-uhpc-mechanical-performance-durability-sustainability]]) |
| C230/C230M | flow table (flow test apparatus) | fresh ECC flow evaluation |
| C39/C39M | cylindrical concrete compressive strength | testing of ordinary concrete control specimens |

Standards documents are not summary targets; consult the originals when citing the corresponding test methods.
