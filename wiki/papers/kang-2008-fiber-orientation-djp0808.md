# Effect of Fiber Orientation on Mechanical Properties of SHCC

## Summary

This foundational paper by Professor Seung-Tae Kang, Professor Bang-Yeon Lee, Professor Jin-Keun Kim, and Professor Yun-Yong Kim systematically investigates how 3D spatial fiber orientation distributions—governed by casting direction, formwork boundary constraints, and fresh rheology—affect the direct tensile strain-hardening envelope, first cracking strength, ultimate tensile strength, and tensile strain capacity of PVA-SHCC. Using high-resolution flat-section 2D image analysis and uniaxial direct tension testing, the authors demonstrated that when the average fiber orientation aligns parallel to the tensile stress axis ($0°$), the bridging coefficient converges to $\cos\theta = 1.0$. This anisotropic bridging mechanism produced a 2.4-fold increase in direct tensile strength and a **3.8-fold** enhancement in tensile strain capacity compared to perpendicular ($90°$) specimens.

## Key Contributions

- **Establishment of 3D fiber orientation measurement via cross-sectional ellipse fitting**: Inverted 3D spatial inclination angles $\theta$ from the major/minor axis aspect ratios of thousands of fiber cross-sections on polished surfaces.
- **Correlation between fiber orientation coefficient ($\alpha_{\theta} = \int \cos\theta p(\theta) d\theta$) and tensile strain-hardening**: Quantified the expansion of tensile strain capacity from $1.2 \% \rightarrow 4.5\%$ as $\alpha_{\theta}$ increased from 0.45 to 0.82.
- **Theoretical framework for rheology-controlled performance enhancement**: Established casting guidelines for extrusion and unidirectional slip-forming processes.

## Methods

- **Mixture proportions**: Ordinary Portland cement + fly ash ($FA/C = 0.3$), $W/B = 0.45$, silica sand ($d_{max} = 0.25\ \text{mm}$, 0.25 mm).
- **Fiber specifications**: Kuraray REC15 PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0\%$, diameter 39 µm, length 12 mm, tensile strength 1600 MPa).
- **Specimen casting variables**: Parallel flow casting ($0°$), perpendicular flow casting ($90°$), and 3D random casting (Random).
- **Testing**: Direct tension tests on dogbone specimens ($80 \times 30 \times 13\ \text{mm}$, $80 \times 30 \times 13 mm$, displacement rate $0.2\ \text{mm/min}$, 0.2 mm/min), and optical image analysis on epoxy-impregnated polished sections.
