## Summary
A landmark study by Professor Victor Li's research team at the University of Michigan clarifying the micromechanics mix design, single PE fiber interfacial frictional stress ($\tau_0$), and uniaxial direct tensile strain-hardening performance of a sustainable ultra-high strength-ultra-high ductility composite (RHA-UHP-ECC) in which expensive commercial micro silica fume is replaced entirely at 100 % by highly reactive rice husk ash (RHA, amorphous $SiO_2 > 92 \%$, specific surface area $25 m^2/g$), an agricultural waste by-product. Under the condition of 15 wt % RHA replacement, a 28 d compressive strength of **108.5 MPa**, a direct tensile strength of **12.2 MPa**, and a tensile strain capacity of **8.2 %** were achieved while embodied carbon emissions fell by **42 %**.

## Key Contributions
- **Micromechanics mix of an ultra-high-strength composite based on agricultural waste rice husk ash completed**: commercial silica fume replaced 100 % with RHA.
- **Matrix fracture toughness relaxation by the porous nano structure of RHA clarified**: $K_m = 0.38 MPa\sqrt{m}$ secured, achieving a PSH energy index $J_b'/J_{tip} = 8.2$.
- **Interfacial slip-hardening coefficient $\beta = 0.85$ of ultra-high molecular weight PE fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$) derived**: ultra-high ductility of a tensile strain capacity of 8.2 % demonstrated.
- **Standard presented for eco-friendly high value-added upcycling of agricultural by-products**: innovation in sustainable low-carbon construction materials technology.

## Methods
- Binder: OPC (55 %) + RHA (15 %) + GGBS (30 %), $W/B = 0.20$, micro silica sand ($d_{50} = 120 \mu m$).
- Fiber: UHMWPE fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 12 µm, length 12 mm, tensile strength 3000 MPa).
- Tests: dogbone direct tension test ($0.5 mm/min$, DIC strain), 28 d compressive strength (100 mm cubes), SimaPro 9.0 LCA analysis.
