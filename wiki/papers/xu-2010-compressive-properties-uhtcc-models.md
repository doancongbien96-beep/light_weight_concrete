## Summary
A representative original paper by Professor Shi-Lang Xu of Zhejiang University that precisely evaluated the uniaxial compressive behavior, elastic modulus, Poisson's ratio, peak stress and strain, and post-peak compressive softening behavior of ultrahigh toughness cementitious composites (UHTCC/PVA-ECC) through compression tests on cylindrical and prismatic specimens, and established a two-stage nonlinear uniaxial compressive constitutive model. The three-dimensional lateral confinement provided by PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$) suppressed shear sliding of the crushed compression cone, demonstrating that the ultimate strain at compressive failure reached $\epsilon_{cu} = 0.85 \%$, an increase of more than three times relative to ordinary concrete, and that the compressive fracture energy increased 5.2-fold.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishment of a complete stress-strain ($\sigma-\epsilon$) constitutive equation for UHTCC under uniaxial compression**: formulation of a nonlinear ascending branch and a gentle exponential softening descending branch.
- **Quantification of specimen aspect-ratio (cylinder vs prism) and cross-sectional size effects**: establishment of conversion coefficients for compressive strength and elastic modulus.
- **Definition of the compressive toughness index ($W_{comp}$)**: modeling of the compressive plastic energy absorption capacity through integration of the load-displacement curve area.

## Methods
- PVA-UHTCC ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, REC15, $W/B = 0.25$, fly ash 50 wt %).
- Cylindrical specimens ($\phi 100 \times 200 mm$) and prisms ($100 \times 100 \times 300 mm$); displacement-controlled uniaxial compression tests.

## Results
- Compressive properties at 28 d: uniaxial compressive strength $f_c = 56.8 MPa$, elastic modulus $E_c = 22.5 GPa$, peak compressive strain $\epsilon_0 = 0.42 \%$, strain at failure $0.85 \%$.
- Unlike ordinary concrete, explosive brittle fragmentation was excluded entirely, and gradual ductile deformation was maintained.
