# Tension Monitoring of a Prestressing Strand for Concrete Bridge Using In-Tendon FBG Sensors

## Summary

To non-destructively monitor effective prestress losses in prestressed concrete (PSC) bridges in real time during long-term service, Prof. Jae-Min Kim's research group at Chonnam National University and the Korea Expressway Corporation developed a smart strand system by encapsulating optical fiber Bragg grating (FBG) sensors within the center core wire (king wire) of a 7-wire steel strand. Calibration tensile tests and long-term monitoring of actual PSC bridge girders demonstrated high linear responsiveness (> 99.5 %) and high measurement accuracy ($\le \pm 10\ \mu\epsilon$, $\pm 10 \mu\epsilon$) under high tensile stresses exceeding 1500 MPa ($1500 MPa$) via temperature-compensated dual FBG sensor configurations.

## Key Contributions

- **Grooving and packaging of center king wire for optical fiber protection**: Formulated high-temperature epoxy grouting to completely protect the embedded FBG sensor from inter-wire pinching and frictional damage during strand tensioning.
- **Dual-FBG temperature-strain cross-sensitivity compensation**: Placed a stress-free reference FBG adjacent to the sensing FBG to eliminate 100 % of temperature-induced errors across $-20 \sim 60\ ^\circ\text{C}$ ($-20 \sim 60 °C$).
- **Long-term prestress loss tracking on full-scale PSC girders**: Quantified and separated prestress loss stages caused by wedge slip, concrete elastic deformation, drying shrinkage, and creep.

## Methods

- Strand specifications: 15.2 mm diameter 7-wire strand (tensile strength 1860 MPa, modulus 200 GPa).
- Center king wire (diameter 5.2 mm) fabricated with a longitudinal micro-groove (depth 1.0 mm, width 1.0 mm), housing an FBG optical sensor bonded with high-strength elastic epoxy.
- Optical interrogation: 1 pm wavelength resolution interrogator operating at 100 Hz sampling rate.
- Loading: Stepwise cyclic calibration testing from 0 to 200 kN ($0 \sim 200 kN$) on a universal testing machine (UTM).
- Field validation: Smart strand installation and tension monitoring on a full-scale 30 m ($30 m$) PSC I-girder.
