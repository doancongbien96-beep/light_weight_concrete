# Flexural deflection-hardening, multiple cracking, and deflection-ductility mechanisms of green ECC containing high-volume palm oil fuel ash

## Summary

To recycle palm oil fuel ash (POFA), an agricultural byproduct of Southeast Asia and other tropical regions ($\text{SiO}_2$ 66.91 %, fineness $620\ \text{m}^2/\text{kg}$, heat treatment at 450 °C plus ball milling), into a high-value construction material while curbing carbon emissions from cement manufacture, this landmark origin paper on green strain-hardening composites from Prof. Megat Johari's research team at Universiti Sains Malaysia (USM) designed 12 green ECC mixes (Green-ECC, PVA fiber 2.0 vol.%) combining POFA replacement ratios in the binder (POFA/C = 0.0, 0.4, 0.8, 1.2; cement replacement up to 55 %) with water-to-binder ratios (W/B = 0.33, 0.36, 0.38), then systematically analyzed four-point flexural tests (ASTM C78) at 3, 28, and 90 days together with uniaxial direct tension, compressive strength, and micro multiple-cracking dispersion. High-volume unground POFA was found to suppress chemical calcium hydroxide accumulation in the matrix, thereby optimizing the excessive chemical bonding at the hydrophilic PVA fiber interface and moderating fracture toughness ($K_m$); flexural deflection ductility capacity surged by up to **368 %** over the plain cement mix (POFA/C = 0) (midspan deflection 11.53 mm, uniaxial tensile strain >3.5 %). The mixes also delivered a 28-day flexural strength of 10.0–11.9 MPa and a 90-day flexural strength of **13.51 MPa**, and demonstrated formation of **20–30 or more dense saturated fine multiple cracks** versus only 3–5 cracks in the plain cement mix, with crack widths fully constrained below 50 µm.

## Key Contributions

1. **Pozzolanic activation of agricultural-byproduct POFA and achievement of 55 % cement replacement**:
   - Decarbonation heat treatment at $450\ ^\circ\text{C}$ without grinding produced highly active POFA with a specific surface of $620\ \text{m}^2/\text{kg}$ and a median particle size of $2.99\ \mu\text{m}$; replacing 55 % of the cement (POFA/C = 1.2) while stably securing a 28-day compressive strength of 30–35 MPa and a 90-day flexural strength of 13.5 MPa.
2. **Mechanism identified: relieved PVA-fiber interfacial bond drives a 3.6-fold surge in flexural deflection capacity**:
   - Consumption of $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$ by the pozzolanic reaction of POFA relieves the excessive interfacial bond ($\tau_0$, $G_d$) on the PVA fiber surface, preventing fiber rupture and inducing slip pull-out.
   - The 28-day flexural deflection capacity of the POFA/C = 1.2 mix improved dramatically by **243–368 %** over the control (deflection 11.53 mm at W/B 0.38).
3. **Saturated fine multiple cracking and crack-width refinement**:
   - Whereas plain OPC mixes failed locally after only 3–5 cracks, POFA-ECC crack counts rose more than sixfold to **25–32**, crack spacing tightened to 2–3 mm, and individual crack widths were suppressed to 50 µm or less.

## Methods

- **Raw materials and mix design (Tables 1, 2, 3)**:
  - Binder: ordinary Portland cement (Type I, $\text{CaO}$ 62.80 %, $\text{SiO}_2$ 20.90 %) + modified POFA ($\text{SiO}_2$ 66.91 %, $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$ 6.44 %, $\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3$ 5.72 %, fineness $620\ \text{m}^2/\text{kg}$).
  - Fine aggregate: micro silica sand (mean particle size $110\ \mu\text{m}$, maximum particle size $200\ \mu\text{m}$, Sand/Cement = 0.8).
  - Reinforcing fiber: Kuraray REC15 PVA fiber (Japan; diameter $39\ \mu\text{m}$, length 12 mm, tensile strength 1600 MPa, elastic modulus 41 GPa, elongation at break 6.0 %, oil coating 1.2 wt.%, volume fraction $V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.\%}$).
  - Admixtures: polycarboxylate SP (SP/C = 0.015–0.025), thickener HPMC (HPMC/C = 0.001).
  - Experimental matrix: W/B (0.33, 0.36, 0.38) × POFA/C (0.0, 0.4, 0.8, 1.2), 12 mixes total.
- **Equipment and test methods**:
  - Four-point bending: $40\ \text{mm} \times 50\ \text{mm} \times 300\ \text{mm}$ prismatic beams (ASTM C78, clear span 250 mm, constant-moment zone 83 mm, displacement control $0.3\ \text{mm/min}$, Shimadzu AG-X 100 kN).
  - Uniaxial direct tension: $14\ \text{mm} \times 30\ \text{mm} \times 80\ \text{mm}$ dog-bone specimens (rate $0.2\ \text{mm/min}$).
  - Compressive strength: $50\ \text{mm}$ cubes (ASTM C109, 28 days).

### 2. Fracture behavior and PSH micromechanics analysis
- **Improved strain-hardening index**: increasing POFA content lowers matrix fracture toughness ($K_m$), greatly raising the PSH performance index ($J'_b/J_{tip} \ge 3.0$).
- **Induced self-healing of crack widths**: unreacted POFA and residual cement remain within the finely dispersed cracks of 50 µm or less, promoting rapid calcium carbonate reprecipitation and crack self-healing upon moisture exposure.

- **Overall conclusion**: Green-ECC incorporating high volumes of agricultural-byproduct POFA cuts cement use by 55 % while delivering ultra-high flexural strength of the 13.5 MPa class, maximized flexural deflection ductility of 11.5 mm, and control of 50 µm fine cracks, establishing it as an eco-friendly high-ductility structural material.
