# Lateral Confinement Pressure ($f_l$), Axial Strength Gain, Seismic Ductility Enhancement, and Corner Rounding Optimization of Ferrocement Composite Jacketed RC Columns: A Global Review

## Summary

To overcome the brittle debonding, high cost, and low fire resistance of traditional fiber-reinforced polymer (FRP) and steel jacketing methods, Dr. A. B. M. A. Kaish and co-workers at Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM) reviewed ferrocement composite confining jackets for strengthening RC columns. The authors synthesized global experimental datasets covering wire mesh types (woven mesh, welded wire mesh: WWM, expanded metal), matrix formulations (high-flow/non-shrink mortar), jacket construction methods (cast in-situ, precast segmental), cross-section geometries (circular, square, rectangular), corner rounding radii ($R$), and lateral confinement pressure models ($f_l$). Ferrocement jacketing reduces material and installation costs by 50~70 % compared to FRP while providing monolithic cementitious bonding with 0 % debonding failure. It increases column axial compressive capacity by 30~85 % in circular columns and displacement ductility by 2.5-fold, while enhancing square column axial capacity by 30~55 % and shear resistance by 60~70 %.

## Key Contributions

1. **Systematization of lateral confinement pressure ($f_l$) and nonlinear mechanics in ferrocement jackets**:
   - Formulated how high volume fractions of uniformly distributed fine wire mesh suppress lateral Poisson expansion in 3D, creating triaxial compression in the concrete core.
2. **Identification of 6 key advantages of ferrocement over FRP and steel jackets**:
   - Demonstrated low material cost, semi-skilled constructability, monolithic adhesive-free bonding (no epoxy debonding), high in-plane shear resistance, exceptional fire resistance, and large ductility gains.
3. **Quantification of effective confinement coefficient ($k_e$) across cross-section shapes**:
   - Analyzed unconfined arching zones in square/rectangular sections and formulated strength recovery equations as a function of corner rounding radius ($R$).
4. **Global comparative validation of axial compression and shear models**:
   - Benchmarked 10 global confinement constitutive models (Mander, Balaguru, Kaushik, etc.) to establish practical engineering design guidelines.

## Methods

- **Ferrocement composite specifications and parameters (Sections 2–3 & Tables 1–3)**:
  - Matrix: Cement-to-sand ratio 1:1.5 ~ 1:2.0, W/C = 0.35~0.45, high-flow non-shrink mortar.
  - Reinforcing mesh: Woven wire mesh (wire diameter $0.5\sim 1.2\ \text{mm}$, opening $6\sim 15\ \text{mm}$), welded wire mesh (WWM, wire diameter $1.0\sim 2.5\ \text{mm}$), expanded metal.
  - Layer count and volume fraction: 2, 4, 6, 8 layers ($V_r = 1.0\sim 5.0\ \text{vol.\%}$).
  - Construction methods: Cast in-situ, integrally cast, precast jacket segments.
- **Confinement analytical models (Section 4)**:
  - Lateral confinement pressure: $f_l = \frac{2 V_r f_y}{3}$ (circular sections).
  - Confined compressive strength: $f_{cc}' = f_{co}' \left( 1 + k_1 \frac{f_l'}{f_{co}'} \right)$ ($k_1 = 2.0\sim 4.1$, $f_l' = k_e f_l$).
  - Effective confinement area factor: Square columns $k_e = \left( 1 - \frac{\sum w_i^2}{6 b d} \right) \left( 1 - \frac{s'}{2 b} \right) \left( 1 - \frac{s'}{2 d} \right)$.

### 2. Confinement mechanics and seismic ductility
- **Crack bridging and spalling prevention**: High specific surface area of fine wire meshes restricted substrate crack widths below 0.05 mm and prevented cover spalling, expanding ultimate compressive strain 4-fold from $\varepsilon_{cu} = 0.0035 \rightarrow 0.015$.
- **Crucial role of corner rounding**: Sharp 90° corners concentrated stresses and caused premature mesh rupture; chamfering/rounding corners to $R \ge 20\ \text{mm}$ raised effective confinement area from 53 % to 84 %, boosting strengthening efficiency by over 25 %.

- **Overall conclusion**: This review established comprehensive analytical models and design guidelines for strengthening concrete columns using ferrocement composite jackets.
