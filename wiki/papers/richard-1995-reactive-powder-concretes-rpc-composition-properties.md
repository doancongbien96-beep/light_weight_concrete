# Mixing Principles and Microstructural Mechanisms of Reactive Powder Concretes (RPC 200/800), the Origin of Modern Ultra-High-Performance Concrete (UHPC): The Immortal Supreme Classic That Achieved a Compressive Strength of $200\sim 810\ \text{MPa}$, a Flexural Strength of $140\ \text{MPa}$, and a Fracture Energy of $40,000\ \text{J/m}^2$

## Summary

To overcome the long-standing strength limit of concrete ($<100\ \text{MPa}$) and its brittle fragility, and to achieve ultra-high strength and ultra-high toughness rivaling cast iron and structural steel, **Reactive Powder Concretes (Reactive Powder Concretes: RPC 200 and RPC 800) were created, organically combining 1) maximization of material homogeneity (Homogeneity) through the complete elimination of coarse aggregate, 2) optimal particle-size dense packing (Packing Density) of micro silica fume (particle size $0.1\sim 0.2\ \mu\text{m}$) and quartz powder, 3) expulsion of excess water and entrapped air through high-pressure press forming at casting ($50\ \text{MPa}$) ($W/C = 0.15\sim 0.19$), 4) crystalline xonotlite (Xonotlite) phase transformation through superhigh-temperature hydrothermal curing at $250\sim 400\ ^\circ\text{C}$, and 5) a three-dimensional bridging network of ultra-high-strength micro steel fibers ($V_f = 2.0\sim 10.0\ \text{vol.}\%$)**, demonstrating for the first time in the world **a compressive strength of $200\sim 810\ \text{MPa}$, a flexural failure strength of $30\sim 140\ \text{MPa}$, a fracture energy of $1200\sim 40,000\ \text{J/m}^2$ (400 times that of ordinary concrete), and complete impermeability to radiation and chemical substances** — the greatest immortal landmark classic paper in the history of construction materials, created in Cement and Concrete Research in 1995 by **Pierre Richard and Dr. Marcel Cheyrezy of the central research laboratory of Bouygues Construction (France)**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Historic founding of modern ultra-high-performance concrete (UHPC / UHPFRC) and establishment of the four great mix design principles (historic origin)**:
   - Established the four principles of limiting the maximum aggregate size to $600\ \mu\text{m}$, optimizing silica fume packing, post-hydration heat treatment, and high-strength micro steel fiber reinforcement.
2. **Achievement of a compressive strength of $810\ \text{MPa}$ and a flexural strength of $140\ \text{MPa}$ (the highest in the history of world concrete)**:
   - Developed RPC 800 combining steel-particle powder aggregate, pressing at $50\ \text{MPa}$, and post-set heat treatment at $400\ ^\circ\text{C}$, realizing metal-level strength.
3. **Elucidation of the crystalline xonotlite ($\text{C}_6\text{S}_6\text{H}$) phase transformation under high-temperature hydrothermal curing ($250\sim 400\ ^\circ\text{C}$)**:
   - Demonstrated that amorphous C-S-H gel transforms into highly crystalline tobermorite and xonotlite at superhigh temperatures, removing matrix defects and micropores at their source.
4. **Ductile metallization of brittle concrete secured by a fracture energy of $40,000\ \text{J/m}^2$**:
   - Established completely ductile failure behavior without sudden collapse at fracture, through massive fiber pull-out and plastic deformation energy.

## Methods

- **RPC mix and process technology system (Sections 2~4 & Tables 1~4 & Figs. 1~4)**:
  - RPC 200 mix: Type I cement + silica fume (25 wt.%) + fine quartz sand ($d = 150\sim 400\ \mu\text{m}$) + superplasticizer ($W/C = 0.16\sim 0.19$) + straight steel fibers ($V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$, $L_f = 13\ \text{mm}, d_f = 0.15\ \text{mm}$). Steam curing at $90\ ^\circ\text{C}$.
  - RPC 800 mix: RPC 200 matrix with fine steel-powder aggregate substituted for quartz sand + hydrostatic press forming at $50\ \text{MPa}$ during casting + dry/hydrothermal curing at $250\sim 400\ ^\circ\text{C}$ + high-density steel fibers ($V_f = 5.0\sim 10.0\ \text{vol.}\%$).
- **Mechanical and physical property measurement (Sections 4~6 & Figs. 5~8)**:
  - Uniaxial compressive strength, three-point/four-point flexural strength, Charpy impact energy, mercury intrusion porosimetry (MIP).

### 2. Microstructural homogenization and xonotlite crystal interpretation
- **Complete disappearance of the interfacial transition zone (ITZ)**: without coarse aggregate, the porous ITZ around aggregate fundamentally does not exist, and the matrix and fine quartz sand are perfectly unified.
- **Matrix reinforcement by acicular xonotlite crystals**: $\text{C}_6\text{S}_6\text{H}$ xonotlite acicular crystals formed under hydrothermal conditions at $300\ ^\circ\text{C}$ block the opening of microcracks at the nanoscale.

- **Overall conclusion**: In this paper, Pierre Richard and Marcel Cheyrezy created reactive powder concrete (RPC) and demonstrated a compressive strength of 810 MPa and a fracture energy of 40,000 J/m², opening the era of modern UHPC and next-generation ultra-high-performance composites — the greatest immortal landmark classic paper in the history of construction materials.
