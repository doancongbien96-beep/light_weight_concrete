# Mix Design and Mechanical Tensile Performance Evaluation of Strain-Hardening Fiber Low-Cement Composites (SHFLC)

## Summary

To reduce the massive $\\text{CO}_2$ greenhouse-gas emissions (about 7 % of global anthropogenic emissions) and toxic substances generated by cement manufacturing and to secure eco-friendly sustainability, this study, jointly authored by Prof. Cho Chang-Geon (Chosun University) and Prof. Bang Yeon Lee (Chonnam National University), designed five strain-hardening fiber low-cement composite (SHFLC) mixes in which 60 % of ordinary Portland cement (OPC) was heavily replaced with ground granulated blast-furnace slag (GGBS), alkali-activated slag (AAS), or fly ash, and characterized flowability, strength, and tensile strain-hardening and multiple-cracking behavior through slump-flow, 28-day compressive strength, and uniaxial direct-tension tests (dogbone specimens). The mixes with 60 % GGBS replacement (L2, L3) developed high strengths of **81.5–82.4 MPa** at 28 days and tensile strengths of 4.52–4.70 MPa thanks to the latent hydraulicity of slag and densification of the microstructure, while the mix with 60 % fly ash replacement (L5), although its compressive strength was low at 23.2 MPa, exhibited a tensile strain capacity of **4.79 %** with saturated multiple fine cracking at residual crack widths of 28–37 µm through the mitigating effect on the PVA fiber–matrix interfacial bond, demonstrating ultra-high ductility more than twice that of the control (L1, 2.32 %).

## Key Contributions

1. **Demonstration of an eco-friendly strain-hardening composite (SHFLC) with heavy 60 % cement replacement**:
   - While cutting OPC usage drastically to 40 %, the addition of PVA fiber at 2.0 vol.% realized distinct strain-hardening behavior with tensile strains in the **2.18–4.79 %** range stably in every replacement mix, without brittle failure.
2. **Clarification of how mechanical properties and ductility mechanisms differ by replacement mineral**:
   - **GGBS 60 % replacement (L2, L3)**: dense pozzolanic/hydraulic reactions of slag particles raised matrix fracture toughness and initial crack strength ($f_i = 3.57\\sim 3.72\\ \\text{MPa}$) by 21–26 % relative to the control (2.94 MPa) and achieved a compressive strength of 82.4 MPa.
   - **Combined AAS replacement (L4)**: the addition of the alkali activators $\\text{Ca(OH)}_2 + \\text{Na}_2\\text{SO}_4$ accelerated early-age hydration reactions, securing a compressive strength of 62.0 MPa and a tensile strength of 4.29 MPa.
   - **Fly ash 60 % replacement (L5)**: the ball-bearing effect of spherical particles and the reduction of chemical adhesion ($G_d$) suppressed fiber rupture and induced clean frictional pullout, achieving the highest ductility with a tensile strain of **4.79 %** (+106 % versus L1).
3. **Control of saturated fine multiple cracking with harmful crack widths kept at or below 30 µm**:
   - Residual crack widths measured under release after the end of direct-tension testing remained at the 28–37 µm level, confirming the contribution to suppressing the penetration of harmful substances and maximizing structural durability.

## Methods

- **Materials and binder system**:
  - Ordinary Portland cement (OPC), ground granulated blast-furnace slag (GGBS), fly ash (Class F).
  - Alkali activators: $\\text{Ca(OH)}_2 + \\text{Na}_2\\text{SO}_4$ (10.5 mass % of the GGBS).
  - Silica sand (Sand, S/B 0.40), high-range water reducer (SP), methylcellulose (MC), defoamer.
  - Reinforcing fiber: oil-coated hydrophilic PVA fiber (diameter 39 µm, length 8 mm, tensile strength 1700 MPa, elastic modulus 29.4 GPa, 2.0 vol.%).
- **Mix design (five mixes)**:
  - **L1 (control)**: OPC 100 %, W/B 0.40, S/B 0.40.
  - **L2**: OPC 40 % + GGBS 60 %, W/B 0.35, S/B 0.40.
  - **L3**: OPC 40 % + GGBS 60 %, W/B 0.40, S/B 0.40.
  - **L4 (AAS)**: OPC 40 % + AAS 60 %, W/B 0.40, S/B 0.40.
  - **L5 (FA)**: OPC 40 % + FA 60 %, W/B 0.40, S/B 0.40.
- **Mixing and curing**: Hobart vertical mixer (dry mixing 3 min $\\rightarrow$ 3 min after water addition $\\rightarrow$ admixtures and fibers added and mixed 10–15 min); 1 day of air curing followed by 28 days of water curing (23 ± 3 °C).
- **Test items and equipment**:
  - Slump flow: ASTM C1611 (cone and acrylic plate, average of two runs).
  - Compressive strength: ASTM C109 ($50\\ \\text{mm} \\times 50\\ \\text{mm} \\times 50\\ \\text{mm}$ cubic specimens, average of 3 cubes).
  - Uniaxial direct-tension test: dogbone specimen (mid-section $35\\ \\text{mm} \\times 20\\ \\text{mm}$), displacement-controlled uniaxial tension machine, LVDT gauge instrumentation.

## Results

### 1. Flowability and compressive strength by mix

| Mix | Binder composition (mass ratio) | W/B | S/B | Admixture (SP) | Slump flow (mm) | 28-day compressive strength (MPa) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L1 (control)** | OPC 1.0 | 0.40 | 0.40 | 0.003 | 670 | 72.5 |
| **L2 (GGBS)** | OPC 0.4 + GGBS 0.6 | 0.35 | 0.40 | 0.002 | 610 | **82.4 (+14 %)** |
| **L3 (GGBS)** | OPC 0.4 + GGBS 0.6 | 0.40 | 0.40 | 0.002 | 645 | **81.5 (+12 %)** |
| **L4 (AAS)** | OPC 0.4 + AAS 0.6 | 0.40 | 0.40 | 0.003 | 520 | 62.0 |
| **L5 (FA)** | OPC 0.4 + FA 0.6 | 0.40 | 0.40 | 0.003 | 605 | 23.2 |

### 2. Direct-tension behavior and crack-control characteristics

| Mix | First-cracking strength $f_i$ (MPa) | Ultimate tensile strength $f_t$ (MPa) | Tensile strain capacity $\\varepsilon_u$ (%) | Mean residual crack width (µm) | Strain-hardening behavior characteristics |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L1** | 2.94 | 3.99 | 2.32 | 35 | Standard strain-hardening, moderate crack count |
| **L2** | 3.72 (+26 %) | 4.70 (+18 %) | 2.22 | 32 | High-strength-type strain-hardening, tendency toward early localization |
| **L3** | 3.57 (+21 %) | 4.52 (+13 %) | 3.05 (+31 %) | 30 | Balanced high-strength, high-ductility type |
| **L4** | 3.77 (+28 %) | 4.29 (+8 %) | 2.18 | 37 | Alkali-activation microstructure densification |
| **L5** | 2.31 (−21 %) | 3.30 (−17 %) | **4.79 (+106 %)** | **28** | **Ultra-high-ductility saturated multiple cracking** |

- **Tensile strain capacity**: the L5 mix recorded a maximized strain capacity of 4.79 %, markedly exceeding the standard PVA-ECC of Li et al. (2.63 %).
- **Cracking characteristics**: fine multiple cracks occurred evenly distributed in every mix; L5 in particular exhibited the tightest crack spacing and a fine crack width of 28 µm.
