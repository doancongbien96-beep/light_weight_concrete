# Flexural Behavior of RC Beams Strengthened with High-Strength Reinforcing Steel (HSRS, 600 MPa) and Strain-Hardening Composite (SHCC) Overlays: 65 % Flexural Capacity Amplification, Crack Restriction to 0.08 mm, and Delamination Prevention

## Summary

To substantially upgrade the flexural load capacity and stiffness of deteriorated reinforced concrete (RC) beams while suppressing excessive crack opening and brittle failure associated with high-strength rebars, Prof. Yun Yong Kim, Prof. Bang Yeon Lee, and Prof. Chang-Geun Cho (corresponding author) designed a hybrid strengthening system. The method pairs **high-strength reinforcing steel (HSRS, yield strength $f_y = 600.0\ \text{MPa}$) with a 2.0 vol.% PVA strain-hardening cementitious composite overlay (SHCC: 28 d compressive strength $45.0\ \text{MPa}$, tensile strain capacity $3.20\%$, tensile strength $4.50\ \text{MPa}$) applied to the tension soffit of full-scale RC beams ($2000 \times 150 \times 250\ \text{mm}$)**. Four-point flexural testing, full-field crack tracking, and interfacial shear evaluations demonstrated a **72 % increase in flexural yield load, 65 % amplification of ultimate flexural capacity ($92.5\ \text{kN} \rightarrow 152.5\ \text{kN}$), strict serviceability crack width restriction below $0.08\ \text{mm}$, and complete prevention of premature interfacial delamination via mechanical shear anchors** compared to unreinforced RC control beams.

## Key Contributions

1. **First structural implementation of HSRS (600 MPa) and SHCC flexural retrofitting synergy**:
   - Enabled practical application of 600 MPa high-strength rebars—normally restricted by code crack width limits—by integrating them with the multiple microcracking capability of SHCC.
2. **Demonstration of 65 % ultimate capacity and 72 % yield load amplification**:
   - The combined tension-bearing action of HSRS bars and fiber-bridging SHCC overlays significantly upgraded beam flexural stiffness and moment capacity.
3. **Strict serviceability crack width restriction below $0.08\ \text{mm}$**:
   - Subdivided large macrocracks ($> 0.35\ \text{mm}$) into over 30 tight microcracks ($60\sim 80\ \mu\text{m}$), extending structural service life against chloride corrosion by 4-fold.
4. **Interfacial design guidelines against premature debonding via surface chipping and shear anchors**:
   - Surface roughening combined with mechanical connectors ensured full composite action without interlayer delamination up to ultimate failure.

## Methods

- **Specimen design and material specifications (Section 2 & Tables 1–3 & Figs. 1–5)**:
  - Base RC beams: $2000 \times 150 \times 250\ \text{mm}$ (span $1800\ \text{mm}$, pure bending region $600\ \text{mm}$, base concrete $f_c = 30.0\ \text{MPa}$).
  - Strengthening configurations (4 full-scale beams):
    1. `RC-Control`: Plain unreinforced RC reference beam.
    2. `SHCC-0`: 40 mm SHCC overlay without additional rebars.
    3. `SHCC-3`: 40 mm SHCC overlay + 3-D10 HSRS bars ($f_y = 600\ \text{MPa}$).
    4. `SHCC-5`: 40 mm SHCC overlay + 5-D10 HSRS bars ($f_y = 600\ \text{MPa}$).
  - SHCC mixture: Cement + GGBS + FA (W/B = 0.45, 2.0 vol.% Kuraray REC15 PVA fibers).
- **Flexural testing program (Sections 2.3–2.5 & Figs. 6–8)**:
  - Four-point bending tests (displacement control at 1.0 mm/min).
  - Instrumentation: 5 LVDTs, rebar and concrete strain gauges, interfacial relative slip sensors.

### 2. High-strength rebar stress transfer and crack dispersion mechanisms
- **Full utilization of HSRS rebar capacity**: The ductile SHCC matrix prevented rebar bond-splitting failures, sustaining steady load transfer until HSRS bars yielded at $600\ \text{MPa}$ and developed plastic strains.
- **Durability enhancement via $0.08\ \text{mm}$ crack restriction**: Restricting crack widths below $80\ \mu\text{m}$ effectively blocks moisture and aggressive ion ingress, maximizing service life.

- **Overall conclusion**: This landmark paper demonstrated that retrofitting RC beams with SHCC overlays and high-strength rebars (HSRS) increases flexural load capacity by 65 % while maintaining crack widths below 0.08 mm.
