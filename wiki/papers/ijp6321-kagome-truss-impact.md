# Impact Resistant Properties of Kagome Truss Reinforced Composite Panels

## Summary

This paper investigates the impact resistance of ultra-high-performance mortar (UHPM) composite panels (Chonnam National University, Bang Yeon Lee corresponding author). Three panel configurations (U-F with steel fibers at 0.3 vol %, U-K with Kagome truss, and U-F-K combining both; dimensions 200×200×40 mm) were tested under high-velocity projectile impact (66.14 g projectile, 217 m/s, kinetic energy 1557 J) to evaluate failure modes, penetration depth, damaged area, and mass loss. Kagome truss reinforcement alone provided impact resistance equal to or greater than steel fiber reinforcement, and the truss-fiber combination produced synergistic protection, preventing complete perforation so that U-K and U-F-K exhibited penetration only, whereas U-F suffered perforation.

## Key Contributions

1. **Impact resistance of Kagome truss equivalent to fiber reinforcement**: While steel fiber only U-F suffered perforation with spalling and scabbing, truss-only U-K sustained penetration only. Measured penetration depth in U-K was 11.3 mm versus 15.3 mm predicted by the modified NDRC formula (26.1 % lower due to truss resistance).
2. **Synergistic performance of combined truss and fibers**: U-F-K exhibited the smallest values across all metrics: penetration depth of 9.9 mm (14 % lower than U-K, -33.1 % versus NDRC prediction), rear face main damaged area of 11.6 % (-59.7 % relative to U-F), and mass loss of 1.52 % (-71.2 % compared to 5.28 % in U-F). Reducing mass loss is practically critical to mitigating rear-face scabbing fragments.
3. **Failure mode transition mechanics**: Perforation occurs when impact shear force exceeds member shear capacity, whereas penetration occurs when impact energy is fully dissipated through front-face spalling and rear-face cracking. The short member length and high buckling resistance of the Kagome triangular grid enhance shear capacity, arresting the projectile without wire rupture.
4. **Superiority over NDRC empirical formulas**: The modified NDRC scabbing limit thickness was approximately 73 mm (1.8 times the 40 mm panel thickness). Perforation occurring only in U-F quantitatively confirms that Kagome truss reinforcement provides resistance mechanisms beyond standard empirical equations.

## Methods

- **Mixture proportions (UHPM, binder mass ratios)**: Cement 0.8 + silica fume 0.2, W/B 0.19, silica filler (average diameter 2.2 µm) 0.19, silica sand (0.5 mm) 0.24, expansive agent 0.06, shrinkage-reducing agent 0.008, defoamer 0.0006, SP 0.023. Silica fume BET surface area 7.05 m²/g, SiO₂ ≥96 wt %. Target compressive strength > 120 MPa.
- **Material properties**: Compressive strength 122±4 MPa (unreinforced) and 139±6 MPa (fiber-reinforced, ASTM C109). Direct tensile strength increased from 7.20±1.12 to 8.69±0.95 MPa (+20.7 %, JSCE). Steel fibers: length 16.5 mm, diameter 200 µm, tensile strength 2500 MPa. Kagome truss: wire diameter 0.9 mm (yield strength 400 MPa), pitch 10 mm, 2 layers.
- **Specimens**: 3 variables × 2 replicates = 6 panels. Air cured at 23±3 °C and 60±5 % RH for 48 h, then water cured for 28 d.
- **Impact testing**: High-velocity projectile testing system. Hemispherical-nosed steel projectile (mass 66.14 g, diameter 25 mm, velocity 217 m/s, kinetic energy 1557 J). 4-edge clamped boundary condition.
- **Measurements**: Penetration depth (section measurement), damaged area (image processing pixel ratio), mass loss (pre/post weighing).

## Results

| Specimen | Failure Pattern | Penetration Depth (mm) | Rear Damaged Area (%) | Mass Loss (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| U-F (Fiber only) | Perforation + Spalling/Scabbing | — (Perforation) | 28.8±1.7 | 5.28±0.43 |
| U-K (Truss only) | Penetration + Spalling/Scabbing | 11.3±3.6 | 25.1±4.6 | 2.50±0.79 |
| U-F-K (Hybrid) | Penetration + Spalling/Scabbing | 9.9±0.7 | 11.6±3.8 | 1.52±0.90 |

- U-F-K showed a 59.7 % reduction in rear damaged area and a 71.2 % reduction in mass loss relative to U-F.
- Kagome truss core wires remained unruptured in U-K and U-F-K, arresting the projectile inside the panel.
