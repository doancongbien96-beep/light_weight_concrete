## Summary
To solve the problems of major tensile crack localization and premature rebar corrosion in reinforced concrete (RC) flexural members, Prof. Hyun-Do Yun of Chungnam National University precisely elucidated through four-point bending tests and nonlinear section analysis the flexural crack distribution, load-deflection envelopes, post-yield plastic deformation ductility, and crack damage mitigation mechanisms of two-layer composite beams cast monolithically with a high-ductility strain-hardening cement composite (PVA-SHCC) layer at the lower tensile side (SHCC-Layered Composite Beams, $150 \times 250 \times 2000 mm$, SHCC thickness $t = 30, 60 mm$). It was demonstrated that the tensile-side SHCC layer blocks the formation of a single large crack in the substrate concrete at its source and converts it into a finely distributed crack network of more than 60 microcracks, confining serviceability crack widths to **0.035 mm** or less and improving flexural ductility by **85 %**.

## Key Contributions
- **Quantification of the crack damage mitigation mechanism of tensile-side SHCC layered composite beams**: the SHCC layer absorbs the stress-concentration cracks of the substrate, reducing crack width by 90 %.
- **Establishment of flexural capacity and deflection ductility envelopes according to SHCC layer thickness ($t = 30, 60 mm$)**: casting at 25 % (60 mm) of the beam depth increased ultimate flexural capacity by 28 %.
- **Demonstration of prevention of interlayer shear debonding between substrate and SHCC layer**: monolithic casting maintained fully composite section behavior up to ultimate load without interfacial delamination.
- **Presentation of durability enhancement design guidelines for marine structures and bridge decks**: prevention of rebar corrosion through control of cover concrete cracking.

## Methods
- Six full-scale beams ($150 \times 250 \times 2000 mm$, clear span 1700 mm, tensile reinforcement 2-D16, $f_y = 400 MPa$).
- Concrete: normal concrete in the upper part ($f_c = 35 MPa$) + PVA-SHCC layer in the lower part ($t = 0, 30, 60 mm$, $f_c = 42 MPa$, tensile strain capacity $3.5 \%$).
- Testing: four-point bending test (constant moment zone 500 mm), LVDT deflection measurement, and continuous crack width measurement with an optical microscope.
