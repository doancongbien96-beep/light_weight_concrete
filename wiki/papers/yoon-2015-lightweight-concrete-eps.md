# Two-Stage Casting Lightweight Concrete

## Summary

A study (KAIST Jaehong Kim group) that produced minimum-density lightweight concrete by a two-stage casting method in which lightweight aggregate is packed into a formwork frame and the remaining voids are then filled with cement grout. It overcame the aggregate segregation limits of conventional mixing (2000 kg/m³ self-consolidating, 1800 kg/m³ vibrated) and showed that, with aggregate of apparent density 700 kg/m³, mixes of 1500-1600 kg/m³ (20-30 MPa) and, in the extreme, down to 750 kg/m³ could be produced.

## Key Contributions

1. **Maximizing the aggregate volume fraction through two-stage casting**: Conventional mixing restricts the lightweight aggregate volume fraction because of workability and aggregate segregation; the frame packing + grout infilling approach overcame this limit and achieved the lowest density.
2. **Discovery of a failure mode transition**: In fully filled lightweight aggregate structures, the weakest link of the microstructure is not the ITZ but the **lightweight aggregate itself**. In splitting tensile tests the aggregate fractures connected to adjacent ones, producing a new failure mode in which the cylindrical specimen exploded over its entire cross-section.
3. **Strength recovery according to aggregate shape**: Multi-sized or irregularly shaped lightweight aggregates recovered to the ordinary diagonal conical failure mode owing to increased interlocking between particles; the strength recovery mechanism was identified.

## Methods

- Materials: lightweight aggregate of apparent density 700 kg/m³ + cement grout.
- Procedure: (i) pack the lightweight aggregate into a frame, then (ii) fill the remaining voids with cement grout (two stages).
- Evaluation: density (target 750-1600 kg/m³), compressive strength (cylindrical), splitting tensile strength, and failure pattern observation.

## Results

- At densities of 1500-1600 kg/m³, compressive strengths of 20-30 MPa were achieved, with production possible down to an extreme of 750 kg/m³.
- Rounded aggregates: ~20 MPa. Irregular aggregates: the interlocking effect generalized the failure mode.
