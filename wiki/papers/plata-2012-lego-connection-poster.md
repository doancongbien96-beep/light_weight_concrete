## Summary

To maximize the on-site assembly productivity of precast concrete structures in seismically active regions and to zero out post-earthquake residual drift, this 15th World Conference on Earthquake Engineering (15WCEE) paper experimentally demonstrated — through quasi-static reversed cyclic lateral load testing of a one-third-scale two-bay frame — the seismic performance of a high-resilience moment-frame system that combines a novel LEGO-type modular dry connection concept proposed by Professor Sarah Billington's research group at Stanford University with unbonded post-tensioning (PT) bars and a high-toughness, high-ductility fiber-reinforced cement composite (HPFRCC / ECC) energy dissipation damper. It demonstrated that fully intact self-centering behavior was developed up to an interstory drift ratio of **4.5 %** without any connection damage.

## Key Contributions

- **Development of the LEGO-type interlocking dry modular connection architecture**: complex on-site wet casting and grouting processes were excluded 100 %, improving assembly speed by 4×.
- **Localization of earthquake damage through HPFRCC plastic hinge blocks**: ECC blocks placed at the beam-end connections prevent concrete crushing and fragmentation.
- **Achievement of zero post-earthquake residual deflection (Zero Residual Drift)**: thanks to the restoring force of the unbonded PT bars, the residual displacement remained at $0.1 \%$ or less even after large deformation to an interstory drift ratio of 4.5 %.
- **Realization of rapid bolt replacement of damaged members (Rapid Replaceability)**: immediate occupancy and reuse of the structure after a major earthquake becomes possible.

## Methods

- One-third-scale two-story two-bay modular precast frame specimen (column section $150 \times 150 mm$, beam section $120 \times 200 mm$).
- Connections: LEGO-type protrusion-groove interlocking shear keys + high-strength unbonded PT tendons ($f_{pu} = 1860 MPa$) + PVA-HPFRCC plastic hinge pads ($f_c = 55 MPa$, tensile strain capacity $3.0 \%$).
- Loading protocol: ACI 374.1 standard seismic lateral-drift controlled reversed cyclic loading ($0.25 \% \sim 5.0 \%$ drift ratio).

## Results

- Summary table of the reversed cyclic loading test results:

| Item | Conventional RC cast-in-place frame | Existing precast frame | This study's LEGO-HPFRCC frame |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Maximum attained interstory drift ratio (%) | 3.0 (failure) | 2.5 (connection failure) | **4.5 (stable behavior)** |
| Residual drift after 4 % displacement (%) | 1.85 (severe residual tilting) | 1.40 | **0.08 (full recovery)** |
| Energy dissipation capacity ($kN\cdot mm$) | 12500 (severe pinching) | 9800 | 18500 (stable hysteresis) |
| Post-earthquake damage state | complete crushing of beam-column connections | grout slip and concrete spalling | only fine cracks occurred; undamaged condition maintained |
