# Hydration Heat Reduction and a $62.8\%$ Carbon Emission Cut in Eco-Friendly Alkali-Activated Slag (AAS) Composites Replaced with Ground Waste Expanded Perlite (WEP): Mechanisms

## Summary

To mitigate the steep early-age reaction heat and drying-shrinkage cracking risk of cement-free alkali-activated slag (AAS: Alkali-Activated Slag) composites and to upcycle insulation waste as a high-value resource, this paper — a Journal of Cleaner Production 2019 landmark origin paper by the research team of **Prof. Wu-Jian Long and Prof. Feng Xing of Shenzhen University** — developed eco-friendly composites in which **volcanic glassy ground waste expanded perlite (Ground Waste Expanded Perlite, WEP, $\text{SiO}_2 + \text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 > 85\%$) replaces 10 vol.%, 30 vol.%, and 50 vol.% of the slag binder**, and comprehensively evaluated isothermal calorimetry reaction heat, 3–28 day compressive and flexural strengths, XRD, TGA, SEM-EDS microstructure of the hydration gel (C-A-S-H), and whole-process $\text{CO}_2$ emissions and economics. The study demonstrated that at 30 vol.% WEP replacement the composites **secure excellent structural strength of **28-day compressive strength $52.5\ \text{MPa}$ and flexural strength $8.2\ \text{MPa}$**, while reducing the total early-age reaction heat release by $15.5\%$ and slashing $\text{CO}_2$ emissions by $62.8\%$ versus ordinary Portland cement (OPC)**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Clarification of the pozzolanic reactivity of ground waste expanded perlite (WEP) as a supplementary cementitious material (SCM)**:
   - Proving that porous volcanic glassy WEP releases active silica and alumina under alkaline stimulation, forming dense three-dimensional aluminosilicate C-A-S-H gels together with hydrotalcite alongside the slag.
2. **28 % mitigation of the early-age hydration heat peak of AAS composites and control of thermal cracking**:
   - WEP buffers the rapid dissolution reaction of slag and suppresses the early heat release rate, preventing hydration-heat cracking at its source during mass concrete placement.
3. **Securing structural-grade strength (28-day compressive strength $45.8\sim 58.2\ \text{MPa}$)**:
   - Maintaining strengths above 50 MPa up to a 30 % WEP replacement ratio, exceeding ordinary structural concrete requirements.
4. **Demonstrating a $62.8\%$ $\text{CO}_2$ emission reduction and an $18.5\%$ cost saving versus OPC**:
   - Establishing a low-carbon circular-economy binder system that cuts landfill disposal costs while dramatically reducing carbon emissions.

## Methods

- **Materials and mix design (Section 2.1–2.2 & Tables 1–2)**:
  - Binder: GGBS (ground granulated blast-furnace slag, specific surface area $425\ \text{m}^2/\text{kg}$).
  - Pozzolanic SCM: ground waste expanded perlite (WEP: $d_{50} = 15.2\ \mu\text{m}$, $\text{SiO}_2\ 72.5\%$, $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3\ 13.2\%$).
  - Alkaline activator: mixed solution of liquid sodium silicate + NaOH (alkali equivalent $\text{Na}_2\text{O} = 6.0\ \text{wt.}\%$, silica modulus $M_s = 1.2$).
  - Mix composition: `AAS-Ref` (WEP 0%), `AAS-WEP10` (10%), `AAS-WEP30` (30%), `AAS-WEP50` (50%).
- **Testing and analysis techniques (Section 2.3–2.8 & Fig. 1)**:
  - Reaction hydration heat: TAM Air 8-channel isothermal calorimeter ($25\ ^\circ\text{C}$, continuous measurement for 72 h).
  - Strength tests: $40 \times 40 \times 40\ \text{mm}$ cubes (compression) and $40 \times 40 \times 160\ \text{mm}$ prisms (flexure).
  - Microstructure: PANalytical quantitative XRD analysis, TGA-DTG ($20\sim 1000\ ^\circ\text{C}$), Quanta FEG-SEM/EDS.

### 2. Interpretation of hydration gel formation and the alkali activation mechanism

- **C-(A)-S-H and hydrotalcite gel matrix**: The abundant aluminum component of WEP substitutes Si in the C-S-H gel skeleton to form Si-O-Al bridging networks, and binds with magnesium ions to form a hydrotalcite phase resistant to chemical erosion.
- **Delayed pozzolanic reaction and long-term strength recovery**: At early ages (3 days) strength is slightly lower owing to the relatively slow dissolution of WEP, but by 28 days the active silica reacts further, fully recovering the high strength above 50 MPa.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper — which recycles ground waste expanded perlite (WEP) as an eco-friendly supplementary binder in alkali-activated slag, reducing hydration heat release by $15\sim 32\%$ and carbon emissions by $62.8\%$ while securing structural-grade strength of 52.5 MPa — is a landmark origin paper in low-carbon sustainable concrete.
