## Summary
A study by Professor Jian-Guo Dai's research team at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University clarifying the alkali-silica reaction (ASR) expansion suppression mechanism, matrix fracture toughness control, and uniaxial direct tensile strain-hardening performance of an eco-friendly strain-hardening geopolymer composite (RGS-EGC) that prevents environmental destruction from natural silica sand mining and upcycles urban waste glass (Recycled Glass Sand, RGS, particle size $0.1 \sim 0.6 mm$) as 100 % fine aggregate. The absence of $Ca(OH)_2$ in the geopolymer matrix blocks ASR expansion of glass aggregate at the source 100 %, and optimizing the matrix fracture toughness to $K_m = 0.32 MPa\sqrt{m}$ achieved a 28 d compressive strength of **52.5 MPa**, a direct tensile strength of **6.8 MPa**, and a tensile strain capacity of **7.8 %**.

## Key Contributions
- **ASR expansion zeroed at 100 % waste glass sand replacement demonstrated**: ASTM C1260 accelerated expansion rate of at most 0.02 % (far below the 0.10 % limit).
- **Mechanism by which waste glass particles relax matrix brittleness clarified**: smooth glass particle surfaces lower matrix crack-tip fracture toughness by 35 %, securing a PSH energy index $J_b'/J_{tip} = 10.5$.
- **Tensile strain capacity of 7.8 % and ultra-fine multiple cracking control**: mean crack width $20 \mu m$ with 88 cracks distributed.
- **100 % natural silica sand replacement and carbon reduction**: production standard for sustainable eco-friendly composite structural materials established.

## Methods
- Binder: slag 50 % + fly ash 50 %, liquid sodium silicate activator ($W/B = 0.30$).
- Fine aggregate: natural silica sand replaced with waste glass sand (RGS) at 0, 50, 100 wt % ($d_{50} = 180 \mu m$).
- Fiber: UHMWPE fiber ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 12 µm, length 12 mm).
- Tests: dogbone direct tension test ($0.5 mm/min$, DIC strain), ASTM C1260 ASR expansion test ($80 °C, 1 M NaOH, 14 d$), 28 d compressive strength.
