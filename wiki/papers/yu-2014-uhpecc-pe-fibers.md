## Summary
An early monumental paper in which Dr. Kequan Yu of Tongji University and Professor Surendra Shah's research team at Northwestern University clarified the micromechanics design principles and uniaxial direct tensile, flexural, and compressive mechanical behavior of ultra-high performance ductile cement composites (UHP-ECC) developed by combining ultra-high-molecular-weight PE fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, tensile strength 3000 MPa) with a nano silica/silica fume densified composite matrix, in order to eliminate brittle failure in an ultra-high-strength compression matrix ($f_c > 120 MPa$) and realize ultra-high ductility with uniaxial direct tensile strain capacity above **8.0 %**. Formulating the frictional slip-hardening mechanism under single fiber pullout, a room-temperature 28 d compressive strength of **124.5 MPa**, a direct tensile strength of **13.8 MPa**, and a tensile strain capacity of **8.2 %** were achieved.

## Key Contributions
- **Mix principle for simultaneous ultra-high strength-ultra-high ductility established**: hydration-product nano-densification secures a compressive strength of 120 MPa while maintaining the matrix fracture toughness at $K_m \le 0.65 MPa\sqrt{m}$, satisfying the PSH energy criterion.
- **Single PE fiber interfacial friction slip characteristics formulated**: $\tau_0 = 1.8 \sim 2.2 MPa$ and slip-hardening coefficient $\beta = 0.85$ derived, securing high-strength tensile bridging force.
- **Ultra-dense multiple fine crack network formed**: more than 85 fine cracks distributed across the entire gauge length (mean crack width $25 \mu m$).
- **Ultra-high tensile energy dissipation density demonstrated**: tensile fracture energy density of $780 kJ/m^3$ achieved, demonstrating feasibility for seismic and blast-resistant structural applications.

## Methods
- Matrix: OPC + silica fume (15 wt %) + ground granulated blast furnace slag (30 wt %), $W/B = 0.18$, micro silica sand ($d_{50} = 120 \mu m$).
- Fiber: UHMWPE fiber ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 12 µm, length 12 mm, tensile strength 3000 MPa, elastic modulus 100 GPa).
- Test equipment: dogbone direct tension test ($0.2 mm/min$), four-point bending test ($100 \times 100 \times 400 mm$), single fiber pullout test, 28 d compressive strength.

## Results
- Measured 28 d mechanical properties of UHP-ECC:

| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 28 d uniaxial compressive strength (MPa) | 35.0 | 45.0 | **124.5** |
| First-crack tensile strength (MPa) | 3.2 | 3.5 | **7.2** |
| Ultimate direct tensile strength (MPa) | 3.2 (brittle) | 4.8 | **13.8** |
| Ultimate tensile strain capacity (%) | 0.015 | 3.20 | **8.20** |
