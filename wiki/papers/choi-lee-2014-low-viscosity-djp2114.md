## Summary

A Korea Concrete Institute conference paper in which Dr. Jeong-Il Choi and Prof. Bang Yeon Lee of Chonnam National University established the mix design and the rheology-tensile deformation performance control technology for a low-viscosity, low-yield-stress, cementless high-ductility composite reinforced with PVA fibers ($V_f = 1.0, 1.3 vol \%$), using ground granulated blast-furnace slag (GGBS) powder together with powdered slaked lime $Ca(OH)_2$ and sodium sulfate $Na_2SO_4$ as binders to maximize pumpability for high-rise buildings and underground large-volume structures. At water-to-binder ratio $W/B = 0.40$ with a high-range water-reducing admixture (HRWRA) dosage of 0.36 %, the mix secured excellent flowability with a mini V-funnel efflux time of 5 s or less while achieving strain-hardening with a direct tensile strength of **2.26 MPa** and a tensile strain capacity of **2.38 %**.

## Key Contributions

- **Uniform fiber dispersion technology in a low-viscosity matrix**: blocked fiber clumping (ball effect) through the optimal combination of VMA and HRWRA proportions.
- **Confirmation of the multiple fine-crack control mechanism of the low-viscosity AAS-PVA composite**: sufficient fiber-bridging interfacial friction developed despite the reduced matrix viscosity.
- **Reduced environmental footprint through a zero-cement binder**: $CO_2$ emissions cut by 70 % relative to conventional Portland cement.
- **Verification of suitability for on-site pumping and spray placement**: constructability and durability secured simultaneously.

## Methods

- Binder: GGBS + powdered activators ($Ca(OH)_2 7.5 \% + Na_2SO_4 3.0 \%$), $W/B = 0.40, 0.45$.
- Fiber: monofilament PVA with 0.6 % oil coating (length 8 mm, diameter 40 µm, tensile strength 1600 MPa, $V_f = 1.0, 1.3 \%$).
- Tests: mini slump flow, mini V-funnel efflux time test, uniaxial direct tension test ($0.2 mm/min$).
