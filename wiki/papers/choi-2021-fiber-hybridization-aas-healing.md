# Effects of fiber hybridization on the mechanics and autogenous healing of AAS composites

## Summary

A paper (corresponding author Bang Yeon Lee, Chonnam National University, with Ranade of
SUNY Buffalo) quantifying how PE-PVA hybrid reinforcement changes the mechanics and autogenous
healing of alkali-activated slag cement-free FRC (CF-FRC) versus PE-only — the first reported
strain-hardening hybrid-fiber CF-FRC in the literature. Three mixes at equal total fiber volume:
PE 1.5 % (E1.5-V0), PE 1.5 %+PVA 0.25 % (E1.5-V0.25), PE 1.75 % (E1.75-V0); tests covered
compression, uniaxial tension, healing (crack-width reduction, resonant-frequency stiffness
recovery, post-healing re-tension), and SEM/EDS. Compression (~38 MPa) and first-cracking
strength (~3.0 MPa) were equal across mixes; tension rose to 9.06 MPa in E1.75-V0 (+15.1 %
over E1.5-V0) while the hybrid matched E1.5-V0 (low-strength PVA ruptures early). All three
reached strain capacity 7.3–7.8 %, with the hybrid +6.5 % and E1.75-V0 +5.8 %. Healing was best
in the hybrid: crack-width ratio r_h fell 18.3 %p below E1.5-V0 and 3.5 %p below E1.75-V0, with
the widest full-healing threshold width (42.1 µm). After healing, hybrid and PE-rich mixes
regained or exceeded pre-damage strength (fts-h 8.51 / 10.19 MPa), and EDS showed crack-zone
Ca/Si ratios of 256–2536 in the hybrid versus 1.61–5.73 in PE-only — confirming CaCO3-dominated
healing products. Conclusion: a mere 0.25 % hydrophilic PVA substantively transforms self-healing
and healing chemistry.

## Key Contributions

1. **First report of strain-hardening hybrid-fiber CF-FRC**: prior hybrid studies targeted ECC
   (cement-based); AAS/geopolymer was unreported. Division-of-labor mechanism proven: high-
   strength hydrophobic PE (2700 MPa, 88 GPa) carries peak bridging stress while hydrophilic PVA
   (hydroxyl surface) nucleates crystalline healing products. Quantitatively PSHS (σ0/σcr) = 2.62
   meets the strength criterion; energy criterion met via prior PSHE 8.9 (AAS-PE).
2. **Hybrid beats PE-only for healing at equal total fiber volume**: at total 1.75 %,
   r_h lower by 18.3 %p/3.5 %p; full-healing threshold widths 42.1 µm (hybrid) vs 32.0 µm
   (E1.75-V0) vs 33.3 µm (E1.5-V0) — PVA widens the healable crack-width window itself.
   Healing rank: E1.5-V0.25 > E1.75-V0 > E1.5-V0.
3. **Asymmetric effect of small PVA addition on tension**: no strength change (PVA's low tensile
   strength and chemical bond cause early rupture, negligible bridging contribution) but strain
   capacity +6.5 % (promoted multiple cracking). Raising PE (1.5→1.75 %) instead lifts strength
   15.1 % per micromechanics but reduces crack count (124 → ~112).
4. **Resonant-frequency stiffness recovery quantified**: ASTM C215 RF tracked every 7 days.
   Post-preload normalized RF was 2.5–4.7 %p lower for E1.5-V0 (largest pre-crack width, mean
   80.10 µm); after 28-day healing, RFr recovery was +3.3 %p over E1.5-V0 and +2.7 %p over
   E1.75-V0 for the hybrid. Fast early recovery then gradual rise in all mixes.
5. **Crack independence confirmed on post-healing reloading**: re-tensioning produced new cracks
   rather than reopening healed ones (Figs. 16–17) — healed zones are structurally robust under
   subsequent load. fts-h exceeded pre-damage values for hybrid/PE-rich mixes (8.51 MPa /
   10.19 MPa; continued hydration raises interfacial frictional bond); only E1.5-V0 fell short
   of 100 % (5.96 MPa) — total polymer fiber content (1.75 vs 1.5 %) governs performance recovery.
6. **Healing-product chemistry depends on PVA (EDS)**: crack-zone Ca/Si at S1/S2 reached 256/2536
   in the hybrid versus 2.15/1.61 (E1.5-V0) and 3.30/5.73 (E1.75-V0) — CaCO3-dominated products
   in the hybrid, CaCO3 plus minor C-A-S-H in PE-only. That 0.25 % PVA changes healing chemistry
   means fiber combination is an independent design variable from matrix hydration.

## Methods

- **Binder**: GGBS (specific surface 4320 cm²/g, density 2.91 g/cm³, XRF: CaO 56.8 %, SiO₂ 17.5 %,
  Al₂O₃ 8.5 %, Na₂O 7.8 % etc.) + slaked lime Ca(OH)₂ (≥95 % purity, 10 % of binder weight) as
  activator. W/B 0.3.
- **Admixtures**: polycarboxylate SP, methylcellulose VMA, mineral defoamer — tuned per-mix to
  paste rheology (SP 0.4–0.6 %, VMA 0.01–0.05 %).
- **Fibers**: PE — length 18 mm, diameter 12 µm, tensile strength 2700 MPa, modulus 88 GPa,
  density 0.97 g/cm³. PVA — length 8 mm, diameter 40 µm, strength 1560 MPa, modulus 41 GPa,
  density 1.30 g/cm³, 1.2 % oil coating by weight (reducing chemical bond).
- **Mixes** (weight ratios beyond binder):

| Mix | GGBS | Ca(OH)₂ | Water | SP | VMA | Defoamer | PE (vol %) | PVA (vol %) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1.5-V0 | 0.9 | 0.1 | 0.3 | 0.4 % | 0.01 % | 0.1 % | 1.5 | - |
| E1.5-V0.25 | 0.9 | 0.1 | 0.3 | 0.6 % | 0.02 % | 0.1 % | 1.5 | 0.25 |
| E1.75-V0 | 0.9 | 0.1 | 0.3 | 0.6 % | 0.05 % | 0.1 % | 1.75 | - |

- **Mixing**: GGBS+lime dry 2 min → water+admixtures 3 min → fibers gradually, 270 rpm for 5 min.
- **Specimens**: three 50 mm cubes (density/compression), nine JSCE dog-bones per mix (5 tension,
  3 healing, 1 chemistry). Curing: sealed ambient 48 h (23 ± 3 °C, RH 60 ± 5 %) → water 28 d →
  preload to 3 % strain → 28-day water healing.
- **Tension test**: universal machine (20 kN), displacement control 0.1 mm/min, gauge length
  80 mm, gauge section 30×13 mm (390 mm²), two LVDTs. Compression per ASTM C109 (300 kN).
  Density within 0.5 % of theoretical — no air entrapment.
- **Crack measurement**: gauge-zone cracks counted with magnifier; at peak, mean width =
  LVDT extension / remaining cracks. Healing assessed via digital microscope at 84x: residual
  width Wc measured before and after 28 days on 24 cracks per mix (8 × 3 dog-bones).
- **Stiffness recovery**: ASTM C215 resonant frequency every 7 days, normalized to undamaged RF;
  RFr = RF28 − RF0.
- **Microanalysis**: 1 cm³ healed sample cut → palladium vacuum coating → SEM (1000x) + EDS
  (15 kV) for atomic composition at crack positions S1/S2 and matrix positions S3/S4.

## Results

**Mechanical summary** (E1.5-V0 tension back-calculated from the E1.75-V0 9.06 MPa / +15.1 % relation):

| Mix | Compression | First cracking | Tension | Strain capacity | PSHS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1.5-V0 | ~38 MPa | ~3.0 MPa | ~7.87 MPa (derived) | baseline (7.3–7.8 % range) | 2.72 |
| E1.5-V0.25 | ~38 MPa | ~3.0 MPa | similar to E1.5-V0 | baseline +6.5 % | 2.62 |
| E1.75-V0 | ~38 MPa | ~3.0 MPa | 9.06 MPa (+15.1 %) | baseline +5.8 % | 2.83 |

First-cracking/compression ratios of 7.6/7.7/8.3 % are ordinary-concrete levels. Mean crack width
at peak stayed below 56 µm in all mixes (~51 µm derived for E1.5-V0, 55.1 µm E1.75-V0,
56.0 µm E1.5-V0.25) — favorable for healing. Crack counts were highest in E1.5-V0 at 124
(+8.60 % over the hybrid, +9.95 % over E1.75-V0).

**Preload crack patterns** (healing specimens, Table 4):

| Mix | Crack count | Mean width (µm) | Spacing (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1.5-V0 | 30.00 ± 1.08 | 80.10 ± 2.82 | 2.67 ± 0.09 |
| E1.5-V0.25 | 36.30 ± 1.70 | 66.20 ± 3.18 | 2.21 ± 0.11 |
| E1.75-V0 | 40.80 ± 0.47 | 58.78 ± 0.67 | 1.96 ± 0.02 |

**Self-healing performance**: within preloaded Wc of 20–80 µm, smaller cracks carried greater
healing potential. Full healing occurred in E1.5-V0.25 and E1.75-V0; only partial in E1.5-V0.
Post-healing normalized RF was +5.8 %p above E1.5-V0 and +0.9 %p above E1.75-V0 for the hybrid.

**Post-healing re-tension properties** (Table 5; ε_h sums preload + reload strain):

| Mix | fts-h (MPa) | ε_h (%) |
|---|---|---|
| E1.5-V0 | 5.96 ± 0.12 | 5.83 ± 0.55 |
| E1.5-V0.25 | 8.51 ± 0.87 | 8.27 ± 0.40 |
| E1.75-V0 | 10.19 ± 0.57 | 7.98 ± 0.24 |

**EDS atomic composition summary** (crack-zone Ca/Si): E1.5-V0 S1 2.15, S2 1.61 /
E1.5-V0.25 S1 256, S2 2536 / E1.75-V0 S1 3.30, S2 5.73. Matrix positions (S3/S4) were similar
across mixes. SEM showed incomplete healing with deep voids in E1.5-V0 versus dense plate-like +
crystalline products in hybrid and PE-rich mixes.
