## Summary
A 2023 Cement and Concrete Composites paper clarifying the shear transfer mechanism acting across crack faces in seismic and shear-dominated structures by performing push-off shear tests on a ductile geopolymer composite (PVA-EGC, $f_c = 52 MPa$, PVA fibers $V_f = 2.0 \%$), characterizing the shear stress-slip ($\tau-s$) envelopes, crack dilatancy, and fiber bridging shear resistance as functions of the initial crack width ($w_0 = 0.1 \sim 1.0 mm$). It was demonstrated that the multidirectional flexural-shear dowel bridging force of PVA fibers combines with matrix frictional interlock to exhibit an ultimate shear transfer strength of **7.85 MPa** even at an initial crack width of 0.5 mm.

## Key Contributions
- **Integrated mechanics model of EGC crack-face shear transfer established**: separation and quantification of the aggregate interlock shear component ($\tau_{agg}$) and fiber bridging shear component ($\tau_{fib}$).
- **Shear strength decay formula versus initial crack width ($w_0$) derived**: model $\tau_u = 9.5 (1 - 0.35 w_0)$ established ($R^2 = 0.96$).
- **Crack opening dilatancy restraint mechanism clarified**: fiber bridging tensile force clamps the crack faces tightly, preserving 80 % of residual friction.
- **Shear design criteria presented for seismic shear walls and precast joints**: shear reinforcement reduced by 40 %.

## Methods
- Specimens: standard push-off specimens ($150 \times 250 \times 500 mm$, shear plane $150 \times 200 mm$, with initial-crack-inducing notches).
- Materials: ordinary concrete (NC 45 MPa) versus PVA-EGC ($f_c = 52 MPa$, $V_f = 2.0 \%$).
- Tests: precision servo actuator shear loading; simultaneous continuous measurement of shear slip ($s$) and crack opening width ($w$) via LVDTs.
