# Effect of Fiber Surface Treatment on Tensile Performance of PE-ECC

## Summary

To overcome weak chemical and frictional bonding ($\tau_0 < 0.5\ \text{MPa}$, $\tau_0 < 0.5 MPa$) between hydrophobic ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene (UHMWPE) fibers and cementitious matrices, this study investigates the tensile behavior of ductile PE-ECC modified via oxygen plasma treatment and chemical oxidative etching. Introducing polar oxygen-containing functional groups (-COOH, -OH) and nanoscale surface roughness increased interfacial frictional shear stress ($\tau_0$) to an optimal range ($1.0 \sim 1.8\ \text{MPa}$, $1.0 \sim 1.8 MPa$). This maximized the complementary energy ratio ($J_b'/J_{tip}$), yielding ultra-ductile PE-ECC with tensile strength of $8.5 \sim 12.0\ \text{MPa}$ ($8.5 \sim 12.0 MPa$) and tensile strain capacity of $6.0 \sim 8.5\%$ ($6.0 \sim 8.5 \%$).

## Key Contributions

- **Polar functionalization and enhanced frictional bonding**: Plasma treatment improved surface wettability, reducing water contact angles from $95^\circ$ ($95°$) down to $52^\circ$ ($52°$).
- **Optimization of micromechanical PSH criteria**: Increased interfacial shear strength $\tau_0$ by 3-fold while preventing fiber rupture below tensile strength ($\sigma_{fu} \approx 3000\ \text{MPa}$, $\sigma_{fu} \approx 3000 MPa$), achieving energy performance indices $J_b'/J_{tip} > 6.0$.
- **Demonstration of ultra-high tensile ductility ($> 8.0\%$, $> 8.0 \%$)**: Suppressed crack localization, promoting dense multiple microcracking with crack spacings of $1.5 \sim 2.5\ \text{mm}$ ($1.5 \sim 2.5 mm$).

## Methods

- **Material specifications**:
  - Fibers: UHMWPE fibers (diameter 12 µm, length 12 mm, elastic modulus 100 GPa, tensile strength 3000 MPa).
  - Surface treatment: Low-temperature oxygen plasma discharge (exposure durations of 1 ~ 5 min).
  - Matrix: $W/B = 0.22$, OPC + fly ash (FA/C = 3.0), silica fume 5 %.
- **Testing program**: Single-fiber pullout tests, contact angle measurements, XPS surface chemistry, and dogbone direct tensile testing.

## Results

- **Tensile performance metrics**:
  - Untreated PE-ECC: Tensile strength $5.2\ \text{MPa}$ ($5.2 MPa$), strain capacity $3.8\%$ ($3.8 \%$), interfacial friction $\tau_0 = 0.45\ \text{MPa}$ ($\tau_0 = 0.45 MPa$).
  - Plasma-treated (3 min) PE-ECC: Tensile strength $9.8\ \text{MPa}$ ($9.8 MPa$, 88 % increase), strain capacity $8.2\%$ ($8.2 \%$, 115 % increase), $\tau_0 = 1.35\ \text{MPa}$ ($\tau_0 = 1.35 MPa$).
  - Excessive treatment (> 5 min): Caused surface damage to PE fibers, lowering strain capacity to $4.5\%$ ($4.5 \%$) and highlighting the necessity of optimized treatment exposure.
