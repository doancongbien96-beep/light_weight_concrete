## Summary

A landmark space-construction study which, toward building the protective dome shell (Protective Dome Shell) of a 4-person permanent lunar outpost habitat module (Lunar Outpost Dome) planned near Shackleton crater at the lunar south pole under European Space Agency (ESA) support using 100 % in-situ lunar soil (ISRU), comprehensively demonstrated the D-Shape binder-jetting 3D printing process, the chemical hardening reaction between lunar soil simulant (DNA-1) and an inorganic binder (liquid magnesium-chloride binder), real-scale 1.5 ton printing of dome catenary vault blocks, and vacuum/thermal-cycle durability. The printed 3D-printing regolith blocks achieved a compressive strength of 24.5 MPa and a tensile strength of 3.2 MPa, demonstrating complete structural adequacy as a shielding structure against space radiation, micrometeorites, and extreme thermal cycles ($-150 \sim +120 °C$).

## Key Contributions

- **Successful real-scale (1.5 ton) 3D printing of lunar-base blocks based on D-Shape binder jetting**: reached technology readiness level (TRL 5) for resource-on-site 3D-printed space construction.
- **Elucidation of the Sorel cement hardening mechanism between lunar soil simulant (DNA-1) and $MgCl_2$ aqueous solution**: established a closed-loop recyclable reaction system.
- **Bio-inspired catenary shell (Catenary Shell) design for the naturally protective dome**: stacking a hollow honeycomb-type (Cellular Core) regolith wall over an inflatable habitat (Inflatable Habitat) blocks 99 % of radiation.
- **Material stability verification under thermal-vacuum environment**: inside a vacuum chamber ($10^{-5} mbar$), binder sublimation rate below 0.05 % and strength retention of 92 % were confirmed.

## Methods

- Raw material: lunar soil simulant DNA-1 based on Italian volcanic ash ($SiO_2 45 \%$, $Al_2O_3 18 \%$, $CaO 10 \%$, $Fe_2O_3 11 \%$, particle size $10 \sim 200 \mu m$).
- Printer: D-Shape multi-nozzle large-format 3D printer (printing bed $6 \times 6 m$, layer thickness 5 mm, inkjet binder jetting pressure 0.5 bar).
- Binder: saturated magnesium aqueous-solution binder (liquid/solid ratio $L/S = 0.22$).
- Tests: uniaxial compressive strength (50 mm cubes), three-point bend test, thermal conductivity measurement, vacuum outgassing test.
