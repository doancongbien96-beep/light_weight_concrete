# Rheology, Buildability, and Strain-Hardening Behavior of Cementless Alkali-Activated Slag (AAS) 3D-Printable Strain-Hardening Cementitious Composites (3DP-SHCC) with Recycled Selvage Fibers

## Summary

To eliminate greenhouse gas emissions from cement clinker and overcome crack vulnerability in 3D concrete printing (3DCP) automated construction, Professor Bang Yeon Lee and Jung-Hwan Hyun at Chonnam National University developed a cementless 3D-printable strain-hardening cementitious composite (3DP-SHCC). The material combines a 100 % Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC)-free ground granulated blast-furnace slag (GGBS, Blaine fineness $4320\ \text{cm}^2/\text{g}$) matrix with recycled selvage composite fibers (upcycled weaving byproduct: PE 81 %, PET 12 %, Glass 7 %; length 15 mm, dosage $V_f = 1.0\ \text{vol.\%}$). The authors systematically investigated the effects of alkaline activator types (Type-A single $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$ vs Type-B multi-component calcium-based composite activator) and viscosity-modifying admixtures (VMA, EVCL) on **extrudability, buildability, open time, and uniaxial tensile strain-hardening (tensile strength 3.2 MPa, strain capacity 2.1 %)**. Using a screw-extrusion 3-axis gantry printer (nozzle diameter 15 mm, layer height 8 mm, print speed 30 mm/s), 8-layer hollow cylinders were printed and tested. Formulations with Type-B multi-component activator regulated rapid slag setting, maintaining an open-time printable flow ($\ge 120\ \text{mm}$) for **120 min**, while bridging stresses ($\sigma_0 = 3.8\ \text{MPa} > f_t = 2.4\ \text{MPa}$) and micromechanical energy criteria ($J_b'/J_{tip} = 2.8 > 1.0$) delivered a **2.1 % uniaxial tensile strain capacity** with tight microcracks ($w < 80\ \mu\text{m}$).

## Key Contributions

1. **First 100 % cementless alkali-activated slag (AAS) 3D-printable SHCC mixture design**:
   - Eliminated clinker cement entirely, formulating an aggregate-free paste with 100 % GGBS (W/B = 0.30) to avoid nozzle clogging.
2. **Attainment of a 120 min printable open time using Type-B multi-component activator**:
   - Retarded rapid alkali-slag flash setting, ensuring consistent slump flow for over 2 h of continuous printing.
3. **Upcycling of industrial waste selvage composite fibers ($V_f = 1.0\%$)**:
   - Utilized recycled PE/PET/GF composite fibers to achieve 3.2 MPa tensile strength, 2.1 % tensile strain capacity, and average crack widths below $80\ \mu\text{m}$.
4. **8-layer hollow cylindrical printing without slump or collapse**:
   - Successfully printed self-supporting 8-layer hollow specimens (15 mm nozzle, 8 mm layer, 1.0 min layer interval) with zero buckling deformation.

## Methods

- **Materials and mixture proportions (Tables 1–4, mass ratios)**:
  - Binder: GGBS (Blaine $4320\ \text{cm}^2/\text{g}$, density $2.91\ \text{g/cm}^3$, $\text{CaO}$ 44.5 %, $\text{SiO}_2$ 34.2 %, $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$ 14.8 %).
  - Activators (2 types):
    1. Type-A: Powder calcium hydroxide ($\text{Ca(OH)}_2$, $5\sim 10\ \text{wt.\%}$ of slag).
    2. Type-B: Multi-component calcium-based composite activator (13 wt.% of slag, open-time controlled).
  - Admixtures: Polycarboxylate SP (0.31 wt.%), VMA (0.20 wt.%), EVCL buildability enhancer ($0.05\sim 0.15\ \text{wt.\%}$).
  - Water-to-binder ratio: W/B = 0.30 (aggregate-free paste).
  - Reinforcing fibers: Recycled selvage yarn (length 15 mm, $V_f = 1.0\ \text{vol.\%}$, composition: PE 81 %, PET 12 %, Glass 7 %; PE density $0.97\ \text{g/cm}^3$, tensile strength 2700 MPa, modulus 88 GPa).
- **Testing protocols (Figures 4–6)**:
  - Fluidity and open time: KS L 5105 flow table (measured at 30 min intervals up to 150 min, threshold flow 120 mm).
  - Gantry 3D printing: Screw extrusion 3-axis printer ($\phi 15\ \text{mm}$ nozzle, layer height 8 mm, speed 30 mm/s, printing 85 mm OD 8-layer cylinders).
  - Uniaxial tension: Dogbone specimens cut directly from printed structures ($330\ \text{mm} \times 60\ \text{mm} \times 13\ \text{mm}$, 250 kN UTM, $0.5\ \text{mm/min}$, gauge length 80 mm).

### 2. Bridging micromechanics and buildability
- **Micromechanical criteria satisfaction**: Single-fiber bridging analysis yielded a maximum bridging stress $\sigma_0 = 3.8\ \text{MPa}$ exceeding matrix cracking strength $f_t = 2.4\ \text{MPa}$ ($\sigma_0/f_t = 1.58 > 1.0$), with energy index $J_b'/J_{tip} = 2.8 > 1.0$, securing multiple microcracking in printed layers.
- **Shape retention**: Adding 0.10 wt.% EVCL raised the static yield stress immediately after extrusion, printing 8 self-supporting layers with zero side bulging.

- **Overall conclusion**: This work demonstrated that pairing cementless alkali-activated slag with recycled selvage composite fibers achieves a 2 h printing open time, buildability, and 2.1 % tensile strain-hardening ductility.
