# Reduction of Hydration Heat and Autogenous Shrinkage in Ultra-High-Strength Concrete

## Summary

To resolve severe hydration heat and autogenous shrinkage cracking caused by very low water-to-binder ratios ($W/B = 0.20 \sim 0.25$) and high cement contents in ultra-high-strength concrete (UHSC, compressive strength exceeding 100 MPa), this experimental study investigated the combined application of mineral admixtures (ground granulated blast-furnace slag, fly ash, silica fume), calcium sulfoaluminate (CSA) expansive agent, and shrinkage reducing agent (SRA). Using semi-adiabatic calorimetry and embedded strain gauges, the temperature history and autogenous shrinkage strain during the initial 72 h were tracked in real time, identifying an optimal mixture proportion that reduced autogenous shrinkage by over 60 % while maintaining a 28 d compressive strength of 120 MPa.


## Key Contributions

- **Separation and measurement of autogenous shrinkage in UHSC**: Accurately isolated autogenous shrinkage strains from thermal dilation after final setting through thermal compensation.
- **Multi-component binder synergy**: A quaternary system combining GGBS (30 %) + fly ash (15 %) + silica fume (10 %) lowered the hydration thermal peak by $18.5\ ^\circ\text{C}$ ($18.5 °C$).
- **Synergy between expansive and shrinkage-reducing admixtures**: Combining CSA expansive agent (5 wt %) with ether-based SRA (1.0 wt %) reduced 28 d autogenous shrinkage strain from $850\ \mu\epsilon$ ($850 \mu\epsilon$) down to $280\ \mu\epsilon$ ($280 \mu\epsilon$).

## Methods

- **Materials and mixture proportions**:
  - Binders: Type I Portland cement (OPC), silica fume (SF, specific surface area $200000\ \text{cm}^2/\text{g}$, $200000 cm2/g$), GGBS (Blaine $4500\ \text{cm}^2/\text{g}$, $4500 cm2/g$), Class F fly ash (FA).
  - Proportions: $W/B = 0.22$, sand-to-aggregate ratio $S/a = 38\%$, polycarboxylate superplasticizer (SP).
- **Specimens and testing**:
  - $100 \times 100 \times 400\ \text{mm}$ ($100 \times 100 \times 400 mm$) unconstrained shrinkage prisms with embedded stress-free strain gauges.
  - Semi-adiabatic calorimeter to record hydration exotherm curves.
  - $100 \times 200\ \text{mm}$ ($100 \times 200 mm$) cylindrical compressive specimens (3 d, 7 d, 28 d).

## Results

- **Hydration heat and shrinkage performance**:
  - Control mix (100 % OPC): Peak adiabatic temperature of $72.4\ ^\circ\text{C}$ ($72.4 °C$), 28 d autogenous shrinkage of $890\ \mu\epsilon$ ($890 \mu\epsilon$), 28 d compressive strength of 118.5 MPa ($118.5 MPa$).
  - Quaternary mix with CSA and SRA: Peak temperature of $54.2\ ^\circ\text{C}$ ($54.2 °C$), 28 d autogenous shrinkage of $265\ \mu\epsilon$ ($265 \mu\epsilon$), 28 d compressive strength of 124.2 MPa ($124.2 MPa$).
