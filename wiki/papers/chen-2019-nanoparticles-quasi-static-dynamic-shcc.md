## Summary

A study establishing the hydration microstructure densification, uniaxial direct tension behavior, split Hopkinson pressure bar (SHPB) dynamic stress-strain curves, and dynamic increase factor ($DIF$) of SHCC incorporating three major nanoparticles — nano silica (Nano-$SiO_2$, NS), nano titanium dioxide (Nano-$TiO_2$, NT), and nano calcium carbonate (Nano-$CaCO_3$, NC) at 1.0–3.0 wt % — in order to simultaneously improve the quasi-static and high-strain-rate ($\dot{\epsilon} = 40 \sim 160 s^{-1}$) dynamic mechanical behavior of high-ductility strain-hardening cementitious composites (PVA-SHCC). It demonstrated that adding 2.0 wt % nano silica accelerates the pozzolanic reaction and densifies the C-S-H gel, raising the 28 d compressive strength from **48.5 MPa to 62.8 MPa**, a 30 % increase, while achieving a dynamic compressive strength of **128.5 MPa** ($DIF_c = 2.05$) and a tensile strain capacity of **3.8 %** at a strain rate of $140 s^{-1}$.

## Key Contributions

- **Comparison of the modification mechanisms by which the three nanoparticle types (NS, NT, NC) improve SHCC mechanical performance**: NS is optimal for early-age hydration acceleration and high strengthening; NC is favorable for dispersed crack nucleation.
- **Establishment of a dynamic compressive failure constitutive equation under SHPB high strain rates ($40 \sim 160 s^{-1}$)**: a 45 % increase in the dynamic energy absorption density of nanoparticle-modified composites.
- **Strengthened interfacial bond of the single PVA fiber**: nano-filler effects optimized the interfacial frictional stress $\tau_0$ from 1.25 MPa to 1.85 MPa.
- **Proposal of nano-hybrid mix design criteria for blast- and impact-resistant protective materials**: prevents brittle fragmentation under impact.

## Methods

- Raw materials: OPC, fine silica sand ($d_{50} = 110 \mu m, d_{max} = 160 \mu m, FM = 1.03$), PCE superplasticizer.
- Nanoparticles: Nano-SiO2 (particle size 20 nm), Nano-TiO2 (particle size 25 nm), Nano-CaCO3 (particle size 40 nm), added at 1.0, 2.0, 3.0 wt %.
- Fiber: Kuraray REC15 PVA fiber ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 39 µm, length 12 mm, tensile strength 1600 MPa).
- Testing: dogbone direct tension tests ($0.5 mm/min$), 75 mm diameter aluminum SHPB impact tests (strain rates 40, 80, 120, 160 s⁻¹).
