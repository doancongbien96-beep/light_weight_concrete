## Summary

To improve the machining precision of machine tool bases and precision vibration-isolation structures, this representative CCR paper by Professor S. Orak of Dokuz Eylul University, Turkey elucidated the vibration damping capacity, logarithmic decrement ($\delta$), loss factor ($\eta$), and compressive/flexural strength of polymer concrete (PC) that composites unsaturated polyester resin (Polyester Resin, 10 ~ 25 wt %) with quartz sand/fine-powder aggregate, through free-vibration ring-down testing and frequency response analysis. It quantitatively demonstrated that the material damping capacity of polymer concrete develops $\zeta = 3.5 \sim 5.2 \%$ — **4 ~ 7 times** superior to cast iron (Cast Iron) and **3 times** superior to ordinary Portland cement concrete.

## Key Contributions

- **Elucidation of the ultra-high vibration damping mechanism of polyester polymer concrete**: rapid absorption of vibration energy through molecular friction of the viscoelastic polymer chains of the polymer matrix.
- **Establishment of the damping ratio–strength correlation curve as a function of resin content (10 ~ 25 wt %)**: an optimal balance derived at 15 wt % resin, with a compressive strength of 85 MPa and a damping ratio of $\zeta = 4.2 \%$.
- **Demonstration of an 80 % reduction in vibration ringing time versus cast iron**: source-level suppression of precision machine-tool chatter vibration and a 2-times improvement in surface finish.
- **Presentation of a design standard for machine foundations and vibration-isolation structures**: commercialization of lightweight, high-stiffness, high-damping composite structural materials.

## Methods

- Matrix: unsaturated polyester resin (hardener MEKP, accelerator cobalt octoate), resin contents of 10, 15, 20, 25 wt %.
- Aggregate: silica sand ($d = 0.1 \sim 5.0 mm$, optimum Fuller packing gradation).
- Tests: cantilever-beam impact-excited free-vibration test (accelerometer, FFT-spectrum logarithmic decrement measurement), uniaxial compressive strength, and 3-point flexural strength.
