# Reliability Verification of the Half-Cell Potential (HCP) Technique for Determining Corrosion Initiation of Rebar inside Reinforced Concrete (RC) under Chloride Ingress: ASTM C876 Criterion Error Correction, Cover Thickness, and Galvanic Current Reversal Mechanisms

## Summary

To non-destructively and precisely diagnose the onset of chloride-induced rebar corrosion initiation / depassivation (Chloride-Induced Corrosion Initiation / Depassivation), which governs the durability service life of reinforced concrete (RC) structures under marine environments and deicing salt application, **ASTM G109 standard beam specimens (cover thicknesses $5.0, 7.5, 15.0\ \text{mm}$; ordinary Portland cement OPC, Portland pozzolana cement PPC, slag cement PSC) were fabricated combining saturated calomel electrode (SCE)-based half-cell potential (Half-Cell Potential: HCP), galvanic coupling current ($I_g$), linear polarization resistance (LPR corrosion current density $I_{corr}$), and electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS)**, and harsh wet-dry cyclic exposure experiments (3-day immersion + 11-day drying) using a $3.0\%\ \text{NaCl}$ aqueous solution were carried out — an ASCE Journal of Materials in Civil Engineering 2009 highest-authority landmark primary paper by the research team of **Professor Bulu Pradhan and Chair Professor B. Bhattacharjee of the Indian Institutes of Technology (IIT Delhi / IIT Kharagpur)** that demonstrated **that the single absolute potential criterion of $-275\ \text{mV (vs. SCE, i.e., -350 mV vs. CSE)}$ prescribed by ASTM C876 leads to serious misjudgment depending on concrete moisture saturation, cover thickness, and blended cement type, and established the sharp negative-potential inflection point of the half-cell potential ($\Delta V \ge -150\ \text{mV}$) together with the moment at which the galvanic corrosion current between top- and bottom-layer rebars undergoes anodic reversal (Current Reversal) as the decisive indicator of true rebar passive film breakdown (Depassivation)**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Elucidation of the fatal limitations of the ASTM C876 absolute potential criterion and establishment of correction guidelines (historical primary source)**:
   - Scientifically proves the false negative (False Negative) error in which the potential drops below $-275\ \text{mV}$ even though corrosion has not started, depending on moisture state and mineral admixtures (fly ash/slag).
2. **Establishment of a combined 'potential inflection point ($\Delta V$) + galvanic current reversal' criterion for corrosion initiation determination**:
   - Perfectly demonstrates that the moment the top rebar switches from cathode to anode and the galvanic current direction reverses ($I_g > 0$) coincides with the sharp-drop inflection point of the HCP.
3. **Quantification of the critical chloride arrival time by cover thickness ($5\sim 15\ \text{mm}$) and binder type**:
   - Elucidates that slag cement (PSC) and fly ash (PPC) delay the corrosion initiation time by 2.8× and 2.2× respectively compared with OPC.
4. **Establishment of a standardized diagnostic algorithm for non-destructive corrosion monitoring of global civil infrastructure**:
   - Provides a unified interpretation protocol that excludes more than $90\%$ of false alarms during precision safety diagnosis of bridges and port structures.

## Methods

- **Test specimens and mix design (Section 2 & Tables 1~3 & Figs. 1~3)**:
  - Binders: OPC (ordinary Portland), PPC (fly ash 25%), PSC (ground granulated blast-furnace slag 45%), $W/B = 0.40, 0.50$.
  - Rebar: thermomechanically treated high-strength rebar (TMT, $\phi = 16\ \text{mm}$).
  - Specimen dimensions: ASTM G109 beam ($280 \times 150 \times 115\ \text{mm}$, top cover $7.5\ \text{mm}, 15.0\ \text{mm}$, bottom two-rebar cover $25\ \text{mm}$).
- **Electrochemical measurement program (Sections 2.2~3.3 & Figs. 4~7)**:
  - HCP: periodic measurement with the saturated calomel electrode (SCE: Saturated Calomel Electrode).
  - Galvanic current: measurement of the current ($I_g$) flowing through a $100\ \Omega$ resistor between the top and bottom rebars.
  - LPR corrosion rate: polarization resistance $R_p \implies I_{corr} = B / R_p$.

### 2. Interpretation of depassivation mechanisms and wet-dry cycle behavior
- **Potential recovery upon drying and potential plunge upon wetting**: During repeated wet-dry cycles, oxygen diffusion limitation caused by moisture ingress and resistance changes make the HCP oscillate by $\pm 100\ \text{mV}$; it was elucidated that measurements taken just before the completion of drying are the most reliable.
- **Densification effect of slag replacement**: At $45\%$ ground granulated blast-furnace slag replacement, micropore blockage by secondary C-A-S-H formation extends the time to reach the critical chloride concentration ($0.4\%\ \text{by binder}$) more than 3-fold.

- **Overall conclusion**: This is the highest-level landmark primary paper in the field of concrete durability and electrochemical non-destructive safety diagnosis engineering, in which Professor Bulu Pradhan and Professor B. Bhattacharjee of the Indian Institutes of Technology elucidated the scientific error factors of the half-cell potential (HCP) measurement technique for reinforced concrete and established 100% reliability of corrosion initiation determination through combination with galvanic current reversal.
