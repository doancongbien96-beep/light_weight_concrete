## Summary
To overcome the weak dynamic tensile brittle failure of eco-friendly alkali-activated geopolymer composites and maximize impact energy absorption, this study experimentally characterized in detail the drop-weight impact resistance (ACI 544 standard), first-crack impact count ($N_1$), final failure impact count ($N_2$), and ductility index ($I_d = N_2/N_1$) of hybrid geopolymer composites reinforced with macro hooked steel fiber (Steel fiber, length 30 mm, diameter 0.5 mm) and micro polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) fiber (length 12 mm, diameter 39 µm), each alone and in hybrid combination (total $V_f = 0.5 \sim 1.5 \%$). Through the synergy of micro PVA fibers delaying initial microcracking and macro steel fibers bridging large deflections, the optimal hybrid mix (Steel 1.0 % + PVA 0.5 %) increased the failure impact count more than 18-fold over plain geopolymer and absorbed an impact energy of 3450 J.

## Key Contributions
- **Demonstration of multiscale hybrid fiber bridging synergy in geopolymer composites**: micro fibers capture shear microcracks while macro steel fibers disperse splitting impact waves.
- **18-fold increase in drop-impact failure count $N_2$**: converted the brittle splitting failure of plain geopolymer ($N_2 = 15$) into a highly ductile energy-dispersive failure mode ($N_2 = 275$).
- **Impact ductility index ($I_d$) improved 3.8-fold**: secured buffering protection performance that sustains load for a long duration without fracturing even after initial cracking.
- **Eco-friendly mix derived for protective structural members and blast-proof panels**: established the basis for fabricating high-impact-resistant precast elements with no cement use.

## Methods
- Binder: Class F fly ash 70 % + GGBS 30 %, alkali activator ($NaOH 10 M$ + sodium silicate solution $Ms = 2.0$), $W/B = 0.38$.
- Fiber combinations: Plain; Steel alone (0.5, 1.0, 1.5 %); PVA alone (0.5, 1.0 %); Hybrid (Steel 1.0 % + PVA 0.5 %).
- Impact test: ACI 544.2R standard drop-weight impact tester (disk specimen diameter 152 mm, thickness 64 mm, hammer mass 4.45 kg, drop height 457 mm).
