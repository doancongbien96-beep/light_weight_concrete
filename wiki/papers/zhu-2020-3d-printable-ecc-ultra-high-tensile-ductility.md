# Development of an ultra-high ductility composite (3DP-ECC) for 3D concrete printing (3DCP): 30-layer formwork-free stackability, uniaxial tensile elongation of $8.20\%$, and the mechanism maintaining interlayer interface strength at $92.5\%$

## Summary

To overcome unreinforced brittle failure, the most critical problem facing 3D concrete printing (3DCP) structures, and premature shear delamination defects at inter-layer stacking interfaces, a **3D-printing-dedicated ultra-high ductility cement composite (3DP-ECC)** fusing ultra-high-strength PE micro fibers ($V_f = 1.5$ and $2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$) with attapulgite nanoclay (ANC) for thixotropy control and HPMC thickener was developed; nozzle extrudability, vertical buildability of more than 30 layers, uniaxial direct tensile stress-strain curves by printed layering direction, and interfacial shear bond strength were evaluated precisely, demonstrating **a 28-day compressive strength of $48.5\ \text{MPa}$, a uniaxial ultimate tensile strength of $7.85\ \text{MPa}$ along the nozzle travel direction, an ultimate tensile strain of $8.20\%$ (more than 40 saturated multiple fine cracks), and a high interlayer bond strength of $92.5\%$ relative to mold casting even under continuous nozzle extrusion and 30-layer self-standing stacking** in this 2020 Materials & Design landmark origin paper by Professor Jinlong Pan's team at Southeast University, China and Professor Jay Sanjayan's team at Swinburne University of Technology, Australia.

## Key Contributions

1. **Highest uniaxial tensile elongation ($\varepsilon_u = 8.20\%$) in the history of 3D printed concrete achieved**:
   - A smart digital construction material realizing giant tensile plastic deformation capacity above $8\%$ purely from 3D-printed stacked filaments without steel reinforcing bars.
2. **One-dimensional automatic alignment (fiber alignment) of PE fibers by nozzle shear flow demonstrated**:
   - The shear gradient arising when passing through a $15\ \text{mm}$ square nozzle aligned PE fibers along the stacking length direction by at least $85\%$, maximizing tensile bridging efficiency.
3. **Rapid recovery of static yield stress via nanoclay (ANC) securing 30-layer buildability**:
   - During pumping the mix flows smoothly at low viscosity, while the reversible floc structure of the nanoclay restores within 5 seconds immediately after deposition, completely preventing buckling collapse of lower layers during 30-layer stacking.
4. **Overcoming the vulnerability of stacking interfaces (interlayers)**:
   - Within the stacking time interval (open time of 10 minutes), PE fibers bridge across adjacent layer interfaces, maintaining interlayer shear strength at $92.5\%$ relative to mold casting.

## Methods

- **Materials and 3DP-ECC mix design (Section 2 & Tables 1~3)**:
  - Binder: P·O 42.5 cement + Class F fly ash (FA/C = 2.0) + super-rapid-hardening sulphoaluminate cement (SAC 5 wt.%).
  - Rheology control agents: attapulgite nanoclay (0.5 wt.%) + HPMC (0.1 wt.%) + polycarboxylate water reducer.
  - Reinforcing fiber: ultra-high molecular weight PE fibers ($V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}$, $d_f = 24\ \mu\text{m}$, $\sigma_f = 3000\ \text{MPa}$, $E_f = 100\ \text{GPa}$).
- **Printing and mechanical testing (Sections 2.2~2.5 & Figs. 1~4)**:
  - 3D printer: three-axis gantry concrete printer (nozzle orifice $30 \times 15\ \text{mm}$, printing speed 50 mm/s).
  - Uniaxial tension: dogbone specimens cut in three directions (X-axis: printing direction; Y-axis: transverse; Z-axis: vertical stacking direction).
  - 28-day compression and interlayer direct shear tests.

### 2. Interpretation of nozzle flow shear orientation and thixotropic stacking mechanisms
- **Fiber alignment by nozzle shear flow**: when passing through the narrow square nozzle, the velocity gradient forces PE fibers into alignment along the tensile action axis (X-axis) by at least $85\%$, maximizing tensile elongation at $8.20\%$.
- **Instantaneous reversible structuring of the nanoclay**: the electrostatic three-dimensional card-house structure of attapulgite particles recovers immediately upon deposition, preventing subsidence of underlying layers, while micro PE fibers cross-embed across layer boundaries suppressing interface delamination.

- **Overall conclusion**: this paper is the most authoritative landmark origin paper in the smart digital construction field, in which Professors Jinlong Pan and Jay Sanjayan developed a 3D-printing-dedicated ultra-high ductility composite 3DP-ECC and demonstrated formwork-free 30-layer buildability, world-class tensile elongation of $8.20\%$, and an interlayer bond strength of $92.5\%$.
