# Rheological Control and Mechanical Properties of Eco-Friendly Self-Compacting Concrete (SCC) Incorporating High Volumes of Limestone Quarry Waste Powder (QLP)

## Summary

To establish high-volume recycling routes for quarry waste limestone powder (QLP: $\text{CaCO}_3$ 95.4 %, Blaine fineness $443\ \text{m}^2/\text{kg}$) generated during aggregate crushing and reduce cement consumption, Professor Burak Felekoglu at Dokuz Eylul University compared paste rheology (mini-slump flow, mini V-funnel) between QLP and commercially processed limestone powder (PLP, $538\ \text{m}^2/\text{kg}$) and prepared eight self-compacting concrete (SCC, W/P = 0.28~0.33) mixes replacing 0 % to 40 wt.% of cement with QLP. Fresh properties (slump flow, $T_{500}$ time, V-funnel flow time, L-box passing ratio $H_2/H_1$, segregation resistance) and compressive strengths at 1, 7, and 28 days were evaluated. The results demonstrated that SCC mixes with 20~30 wt.% QLP reduced superplasticizer (SP) dosage by 15 % while achieving slump flows of **$680\sim 730\ \text{mm}$** and L-box ratios of **$0.85\sim 0.92$**. Furthermore, 28 d compressive strengths reached **$55.4\sim 62.1\ \text{MPa}$**, confirming that waste QLP possesses engineering and rheological value equivalent to commercial limestone powders.

## Key Contributions

1. **Demonstration of viscosity-enhancing capabilities of quarry waste powder (QLP)**:
   - Fine QLP particles ($443\ \text{m}^2/\text{kg}$) retained free water, increasing paste plastic viscosity and preventing bleeding and segregation without costly viscosity-modifying admixtures (VMA).
2. **Elucidation of micro-filler effect and superplasticizer demand reduction**:
   - QLP particles packed the voids between cement grains via ball-bearing effects, releasing entrapped water to extend slump flow to **over $700\ \text{mm}$** and cut SP demand.
3. **Formulation of high-strength eco-friendly SCC (28 d compressive strength 55~62 MPa)**:
   - Replacing 30 wt.% of cement with quarry dust yielded a 28 d compressive strength of **55.4 MPa**, lowering unit cement content and cutting carbon emissions by 30 %.

## Methods

- **Raw material properties and chemical analysis (Tables 1–3)**:
  - Cement: CEM I 42.5 (density $3.12\ \text{g/cm}^3$, Blaine fineness $369\ \text{m}^2/\text{kg}$).
  - Limestone powders (two types compared):
    - QLP (Quarry Waste Limestone Powder): filter-collected waste dust, $\text{CaCO}_3$ 95.4 %, density $2.58\ \text{g/cm}^3$, fineness $443\ \text{m}^2/\text{kg}$.
    - PLP (Processed Limestone Powder): commercial pulverized powder, $\text{CaCO}_3$ 97.0 %, density $2.65\ \text{g/cm}^3$, fineness $538\ \text{m}^2/\text{kg}$.
  - Aggregates: Crushed limestone coarse aggregate (maximum aggregate size 16 mm), crushed limestone sand (0~4 mm).
  - Admixture: Polycarboxylate superplasticizer (PCE-SP, 40 % solids).
  - Concrete mix matrix: 8 mixes across W/P ratios of 0.28, 0.30, and 0.33 with QLP replacement levels of 0 %, 10 %, 20 %, 30 %, and 40 wt.%.
- **Testing program (EFNARC standards)**:
  - Paste rheology: Hagerman mini-cone slump flow and mini V-funnel.
  - Fresh SCC: Slump flow, $T_{500}$ time, V-funnel efflux time, and L-box 3-bar passing test ($H_2/H_1$).
  - Compressive strength: $150\ \text{mm}$ cubes (BS 1881-116 at 1, 7, and 28 days).

### 2. Self-compactability and economic-environmental analysis
- **Passing ability and workability**: QLP 20~30 % mixes satisfied EFNARC SF2 ($660\sim 750\ \text{mm}$) and PA2 ($H_2/H_1 \ge 0.80$) criteria, completely filling congested reinforcement without vibration.
- **Hydration heat mitigation**: Reducing unit cement from $500\ \text{kg/m}^3$ to $350\ \text{kg/m}^3$ reduced early hydration thermal peaks and cut cement-related carbon emissions by 30 %.

- **Overall conclusion**: Limestone quarry waste QLP serves as an effective mineral filler for high-performance self-compacting concrete, optimizing rheological stability while cutting cement demand by 30 %.
