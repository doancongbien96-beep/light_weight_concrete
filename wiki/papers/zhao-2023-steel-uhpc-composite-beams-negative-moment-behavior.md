# Negative-moment flexural behavior of steel-ultra-high performance concrete (Steel-UHPC) composite beams: $42\%$ amplification of flexural capacity, $85\%$ increase in cracking load, and a plastic moment design model

## Summary

To simultaneously resolve the severe tensile cracking of ordinary concrete decks and the premature local buckling (Local Buckling) of the steel bottom flange that occur in the negative moment regions above the interior piers of continuous bridges, this study designed **a full-scale composite beam member ($3000 \times 600 \times 320\ \text{mm}$) in which a steel I-girder and a 2.0 vol.% straight steel-fiber UHPC deck (28-day compressive strength $132.0\ \text{MPa}$, uniaxial tensile strength $8.5\ \text{MPa}$) were integrally composited through shear studs**, and established static negative-moment reverse-direction flexural loading tests, measurement of interfacial relative slip, sectional strain analysis, and a sectional plastic flexural moment prediction model, thereby demonstrating an **$85\%$ increase in first crack initiation load, $42\%$ amplification of ultimate flexural capacity, strict control of service-load-state crack width within $0.05\ \text{mm}$, and complete postponement of local buckling of the steel compression flange** relative to conventional normal concrete (NC) composite beams — a **Structures 2023 landmark original paper by the research team of Prof. Qiu Zhao and Dr. Feng Xiao of Fuzhou University, China**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Complete demonstration of the structural efficiency of applying UHPC in the negative moment region of continuous composite beams**:
   - Deploying the high tensile strength and steel-fiber bridging force of UHPC on the tension side of the deck fundamentally blocked the premature crack opening and stiffness degradation of ordinary NC decks.
2. **Prevention of local buckling (Local Buckling) of the steel bottom compression flange**:
   - The UHPC deck powerfully sustained the tensile force and raised the neutral axis (Neutral Axis) upward, thereby relieving the compressive stress concentration in the steel web and bottom flange.
3. **Minimization of the relative slip of the shear studs at the steel-UHPC interface ($<0.15\ \text{mm}$)**:
   - The high bearing strength of UHPC prevented local crushing around the shear studs, realizing full composite action behavior.
4. **Proposal of a design equation for the plastic flexural moment ($M_p$) of the negative-moment steel-UHPC composite section**:
   - A plastic flexural design equation incorporating the UHPC tensile stress block was derived and verified to match the experimental results with high accuracy of $R^2 = 0.97$.

## Methods

- **Specimen design and mix proportioning (Section 2 & Tables 1~3)**:
  - Member dimensions: $3000 \times 600 \times 320\ \text{mm}$ (steel girder height $270\ \text{mm}$, flanges $200 \times 12\ \text{mm}$, web $246 \times 8\ \text{mm}$, deck thickness $50\ \text{mm}$, shear studs $\phi 16\ \text{mm}$).
  - Comparison of two concrete mixes:
    1. `Series-NC (reference)`: normal concrete ($f_c = 45.0\ \text{MPa}$, with tension reinforcement arranged).
    2. `Series-UHPC`: steel-fiber UHPC ($f_c = 132.0\ \text{MPa}$, $f_t = 8.5\ \text{MPa}$, 2 % straight steel fiber).
- **Loading test program (Sections 2.3~2.5 & Figs. 1~4)**:
  - Reverse-direction four-point flexural loading test (span $2600\ \text{mm}$, pure negative moment region $800\ \text{mm}$).
  - Instrumentation: displacement gauges at midspan and at the 600 mm points, horizontal slip LVDTs at three locations along the steel/deck interface, and sectional strain gauges.

### 2. Analysis of the sectional neutral axis migration and stress redistribution mechanism

- **Neutral axis rise due to tensile sharing by the UHPC region**: the UHPC deck broadly sustained the tensile force of $8.5\ \text{MPa}$, shifting the section neutral axis toward the steel top flange and effectively suppressing the early attainment of buckling stress in the lower steel compression flange.
- **Multiple fine tensile crack control**: fine cracks of $35\ \mu\text{m}$ or less were distributed evenly over the entire deck span in more than 30 locations, completely excluding brittle fracture of the concrete.

- **Overall conclusion**: this paper is a top-tier landmark original paper in the field of continuous bridge structural design, in which the research team of Prof. Qiu Zhao precisely measured the negative-moment flexural mechanical behavior of steel-UHPC composite beams to demonstrate a 42 % amplification of flexural capacity, an 86 % increase in first cracking load, and full interfacial composite action, establishing the plastic moment design equation.
