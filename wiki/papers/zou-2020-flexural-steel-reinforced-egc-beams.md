## Summary
An Engineering Structures paper that elucidated, through four-point flexural loading tests and section analysis, the flexural crack distribution, post-rebar-yielding load-bearing capacity, sectional deflection ductility, and failure mechanisms of full-scale steel-reinforced R/EGC beams that apply a highly ductile cement-free geopolymer composite (PVA-EGC, $f_c = 52 MPa$, direct tensile strain capacity $4.8 \%$) over the full section of structural beams ($150 \times 250 \times 2200 mm$). The pseudo tensile yielding bridging capacity of the EGC matrix restrained the maximum crack width under service loading to **0.04 mm** or less and increased the deflection ductility index ($\mu_\Delta$) to **$\mu_\Delta = 6.8$**, an improvement of **2.1 times** over normal RC beams.

## Key Contributions
- **Elucidation of the flexural governing mechanism of full-scale steel-reinforced R/EGC beams**: localization of concrete tensile cracks was blocked and the cracks dispersed into more than 70 fine flexural cracks.
- **Simultaneous increase of flexural capacity and deflection ductility**: ultimate flexural load raised by 28 % and a deflection ductility index $\mu_\Delta = 6.8$ secured.
- **Serviceability limit state crack width control**: crack widths at the service load stage fully blocked below 0.04 mm.
- **Establishment of standards for real-structure application of eco-friendly cement-free composite structural materials**: realizing a 70 % reduction in carbon emissions.

## Methods
- Six full-scale beams ($150 \times 250 \times 2200 mm$, tensile reinforcement ratios $\rho = 0.85, 1.45, 2.10 \%$).
- Materials: PVA-EGC ($f_c = 52 MPa$, direct tensile strength $5.4 MPa$, $\epsilon_u = 4.8 \%$) versus normal concrete (NC 45 MPa).
- Test: four-point flexural loading test (span 1800 mm, pure bending zone 600 mm), with LVDT deflection and full-field DIC crack measurement.
