## Summary
A study clarifying through a micromechanics model and direct tension experiments how the interaction between the matrix fracture energy ($J_{tip} = K_m^2/E_m$) and fiber-matrix interfacial parameters (chemical bond energy $G_d$, frictional shear stress $\tau_0$, snubbing coefficient $f$) affects the tensile strain capacity, in order to maximize the pseudo strain-hardening (PSH) performance of ECC. By combining binder composition (fly ash and slag replacement ratios) with interfacial oil coating treatment, $J_{tip}$ was controlled over a range of $15 \sim 45 J/m2$, and it was demonstrated that the tensile strain capacity improves up to $8.2 \%$ when the energy performance index $J_b'/J_{tip} > 6.0$ and the strength performance index $\sigma_0/\sigma_{fc} > 1.8$ are satisfied.

## Key Contributions
- **Quantitative function between the PSH energy performance index and ultimate tensile strain derived**: the correlation equation $\epsilon_{tu} = A (J_b'/J_{tip})^B$ verified experimentally.
- **Optimal window for matrix fracture toughness control clarified**: a critical range ($0.35 \sim 0.65 MPa\sqrt{m}$) was established in which excessively high $K_m$ loses strain hardening through excessive crack propagation energy while excessively low $K_m$ collapses initial strength.
- **Single fiber interfacial shear behavior mapped precisely**: an optimization protocol presented for the interfacial friction slip characteristics of PVA and PE fibers.

## Methods
- **Mix proportions**: cement + fly ash (replacement ratios 40 %, 60 %, 80 %), PVA fibers (REC15) and PE fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$).
- **Test program**: matrix fracture energy ($J_{tip}$) measured by notched-beam three-point bending, single fiber pullout tests ($\tau_0, G_d$), and uniaxial dogbone direct tension tests.

## Results
- Optimal mix (FA 60 %, PE fiber): $J_{tip} = 22.4 J/m2$, $J_b' = 195 J/m2$ ($J_b'/J_{tip} = 8.7$), tensile strength $8.5 MPa$, and tensile strain capacity $8.2 \%$.
- More than 70 multiple fine cracks formed with a mean crack width of $25 \mu m$ maintained.
