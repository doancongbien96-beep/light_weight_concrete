# Effect of Mineral Admixture and Fibers on Shrinkage Cracking of Sacrificial Concrete

## Summary

This study evaluates the drying shrinkage and cracking characteristics of sacrificial concrete used to mitigate molten core-concrete interactions (MCCI) during severe nuclear power plant accidents. Replacing cement clinker with silica fume (SF) and fly ash (FA) alongside polypropylene (PP) and steel fiber reinforcement, the authors performed elliptical ring restraint tests and unrestrained drying shrinkage measurements. Mineral admixture substitution coupled with hybrid fiber reinforcement relieved shrinkage stress concentrations, delayed cracking initiation, and reduced total crack area by up to 85 %.

## Key Contributions

- **Shrinkage cracking mechanics in sacrificial concrete**: Clarified the autogenous and drying shrinkage mechanisms of sacrificial concrete formulated with high-density siliceous and calcareous aggregates.
- **Elliptical ring restraint testing**: Applied elliptical ring geometry with distinct stress concentrations to quantify initial cracking age ($t_{cr}$) and crack width propagation.
- **Crack area reduction via fiber bridging**: Adding PP fibers (0.9 kg/m3) suppressed plastic shrinkage cracking by over 70 %, while hybrid incorporation with steel fibers (30 kg/m3) distributed tensile microcracks and prevented initial macrocrack opening.

## Methods

- **Binder composition**: P.O 42.5 cement baseline; fly ash (15 ~ 30 wt %) and silica fume (5 ~ 10 wt %) substitution.
- **Aggregates and mixture designs**: $W/B = 0.38 \sim 0.45$, sacrificial concrete quartz sand and limestone coarse aggregate blends.
- **Fiber reinforcement**:
  - PP fibers: Diameter 31 µm, length 12 mm, tensile strength 400 MPa, dosage 0.6 ~ 1.2 kg/m3.
  - Hooked-end steel fibers: Diameter 0.5 mm, length 30 mm, tensile strength 1100 MPa, dosage 20 ~ 40 kg/m3.
- **Testing program**: Unrestrained drying shrinkage (comparator method), elliptical ring restrained shrinkage (controlled room at 20 °C, 50 % RH), 28 d compressive and flexural strengths.

## Results

- **Quantitative shrinkage crack reduction**:
  - Incorporating 20 % FA + 5 % SF reduced 28 d drying shrinkage by approximately 18 % relative to the control.
  - Unreinforced sacrificial concrete cracked at $t_{cr} = 6.2\ \text{d}$ with a maximum crack width of 0.48 mm.
  - Adding 0.9 kg/m3 PP fibers delayed cracking to $t_{cr} = 14.5\ \text{d}$ and reduced crack width to 0.12 mm.
  - The PP + steel fiber hybrid mix achieved an 88.4 % reduction in total crack area.
- **Compressive strength**: Maintained 28 d compressive strength in the range of 48.5 ~ 58.2 MPa.
