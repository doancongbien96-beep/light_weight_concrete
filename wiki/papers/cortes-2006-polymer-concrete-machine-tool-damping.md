# Comparison of the Dynamic Properties and Structural Damping Capacity of Polymer Concrete and Grey Cast Iron for Machine Tool Applications

## Summary

To improve structural damping — the key factor determining machining precision and dynamic stability of machine tools — this study precisely measured and compared the complex elastic modulus (Complex Modulus, storage modulus and loss factor $\eta$) of grey cast iron (Grey Cast Iron), the conventional machine-frame material, against polymer concrete (Polymer Concrete) based on mineral aggregates and viscoelastic resin, over the 160 Hz frequency band using transient response measurements and Nelder-Mead optimization curve fitting.

## Key Contributions

1. **Demonstration of polymer concrete's overwhelming vibration damping performance**: across the principal machine-tool excitation frequency band (0–160 Hz), the loss factor ($\eta$) of polymer concrete is **3–5 times** higher than that of grey cast iron, proving that chatter vibrations arising during machining are radically suppressed.
2. **Potential for structural geometry and natural frequency optimization identified**: unlike the thin open-box (Open Box) sections required in cast-iron casting, polymer concrete can be formed into thick-walled closed-box (Closed Box) sections, establishing that a structure's natural frequencies can be raised while static and dynamic stiffness are secured simultaneously.
3. **Development of an inverse-analysis technique for complex moduli based on hundreds of measurement points**: overcoming the error of the classical logarithmic decrement method, frequency-dependent dynamic elastic moduli and damping parameters were derived with high precision from transient vibration signals of beam specimens via nonlinear optimization.

## Methods

- **Materials**: polymer concrete (epoxy resin + quartz mineral aggregate blend) vs grey cast iron (GG25).
- **Test method**: impact hammer tests on cantilever beams of various geometries, accelerometer transient-response measurement, curve fitting based on the Nelder-Mead algorithm.

## Results

- Polymer concrete substantially exceeds grey cast iron in loss modulus and damping capacity, confirming its excellent suitability for vibration reduction as beds and structural members of high-speed precision machine tools.
