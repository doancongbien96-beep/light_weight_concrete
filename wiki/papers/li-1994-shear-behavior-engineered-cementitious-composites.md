# Pure Shear Behavior and Pseudo Shear Strain-Hardening Mechanism of Engineered Cementitious Composites (ECC): Demonstration of Shear Strength $9.6\ \text{MPa}$ and Shear Strain $4.0\%$

## Summary

To suppress diagonal shear failure, the most dangerous brittle failure mode of concrete structures, and to secure highly ductile shear deformation capacity even without shear reinforcement, the team designed an **Ohno pure shear beam specimen** and comparatively evaluated the pure shear stress-strain ($\tau-\gamma$) behavior of plain concrete (PC), steel fiber reinforced concrete (FRC), reinforced concrete with shear reinforcement (RC, $\rho_v = 0.75\%$), Spectra PE fiber based ECC (SPECC), and Dramix steel fiber based ECC (DRECC) using a **non-contact 3D optical displacement measurement system (OPTOTRAK)**. The joint research team of **University of Michigan professor Victor C. Li, Antoine E. Naaman, James K. Wight, and Japan's Shimizu Corp.** demonstrated for the first time in the world **shear strain-hardening behavior with multiple fine diagonal cracks ($w < 0.1\ \text{mm}$), reaching ultimate shear strength $9.6\ \text{MPa}$ (1.5x that of RC) and shear strain $4.0\%$ despite the complete absence of shear reinforcement** — the top landmark origin paper of Advanced Cement Based Materials, 1994.

## Key Contributions

1. **World-first demonstration of pseudo shear strain-hardening behavior in ECC**:
   - Proved for the first time that the multiple-cracking strain-hardening mechanism observed in direct tension operates identically under a pure shear stress field, producing $\tau-\gamma$ hardening behavior in which load keeps rising after shear yielding instead of failure.
2. **Achieved shear strength 1.5x ($9.6\ \text{MPa}$) and shear ductility 4x ($4.0\%$) relative to RC members with shear reinforcement**:
   - Without any dedicated stirrups, the fiber bridging force of the ECC material alone exceeded the shear capacity and energy dissipation capability of a reinforced concrete beam with standard seismic reinforcement detailing.
3. **Visualization of fine shear crack propagation through OPTOTRAK optical 3D measurement**:
   - Measured that while PC and RC failed early and brittlely through localization into a single diagonal crack ($w > 1.0\ \text{mm}$), more than ten fine diagonal cracks with widths of $0.1\ \text{mm}$ or less were uniformly dispersed across the entire panel in ECC.
4. **Presented the potential to resolve reinforcement congestion in shear-governed structural members (bridge decks, corbels, seismic shear walls)**:
   - Provided the structural engineering basis for solving constructability degradation and concrete compaction defects caused by complex reinforcement details through substitution with ECC.

## Methods

- **Specimen configuration and Ohno shear loading system (Section 2 & Figs. 1~3)**:
  - Specimen geometry: dumbbell-type pure shear beam (central shear panel $154 \times 114 \times 114\ \text{mm}$; two #3 flexural bars fully restrained to prevent flexural failure and induce pure shear with zero moment).
  - Five material systems:
    1. `PC`: ordinary normal concrete (reference).
    2. `FRC`: hooked steel fiber reinforced concrete ($V_f = 1.0\ \text{vol.}\%$, $d_f = 0.5\ \text{mm}$, $L_f = 30\ \text{mm}$).
    3. `RC`: PC matrix + shear reinforcement (#3 stirrups, $\rho_v = 0.75\%$).
    4. `SPECC`: Spectra PE fiber ECC ($V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$, $d_f = 38\ \mu\text{m}$, $L_f = 12.7\ \text{mm}$).
    5. `DRECC`: Dramix steel fiber ECC ($V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$, $d_f = 0.15\ \text{mm}$, $L_f = 6\ \text{mm}$).
- **Measurement system (Section 2.4 & Fig. 4)**:
  - A North American standard OPTOTRAK non-contact 3D infrared LED target sensor network was attached to the shear panel to track the average shear strain ($\gamma$) and crack width in real time with $0.01\ \text{mm}$ resolution.

### 2. Analysis of shear resistance mechanisms and fine crack control mechanisms
- **Aggregate interlock vs. fiber bridging shear**: ordinary concrete loses its aggregate interlock force as soon as cracks open and collapses abruptly, whereas in ECC thousands of fibers bridging the crack faces in vertical and inclined directions transmit shear friction directly and physically restrain the crack width below $0.1\ \text{mm}$.
- **Shear multiple cracking mechanism**: as the first diagonal crack forming along the principal stress direction ($45^\circ$) redistributes stress into the surrounding matrix through fiber bridging, numerous parallel secondary and tertiary diagonal shear cracks arise consecutively, dissipating a huge plastic shear deformation energy ($\int \tau d\gamma$).

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is a historic top landmark origin paper in which professor Victor C. Li proved for the first time that fiber bridging micromechanics design can deliver a shear strength of $9.6\ \text{MPa}$ and shear strain-hardening of $4.0\%$ exceeding RC members even without shear reinforcement, changing the shear design paradigm of structural members.
