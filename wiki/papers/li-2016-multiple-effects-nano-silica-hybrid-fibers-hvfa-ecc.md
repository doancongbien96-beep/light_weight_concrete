## Summary
Research from Tongji University that investigated hydration acceleration, single-fiber interfacial frictional stress ($\tau_0$), flexural strain-hardening envelopes, and direct tensile performance of hybrid HVFA-ECC combining colloidal nano-silica (Nano-SiO2, 1.0 ~ 3.0 wt %) with macro steel fiber/micro PVA fiber hybrid reinforcement (total $V_f = 2.0 \%$), in order to solve the early-age compressive strength loss and insufficient initial crack strength of high-ductility composites caused by high-volume fly ash replacement ($FA/B = 0.70$). The nucleation effect of nano-silica accelerated early-age (3 d, 7 d) strength by more than 45 %, and through the multi-scale bridging synergy of steel and PVA fibers, a 28 d compressive strength of 52 MPa, a direct tensile strength of 6.8 MPa, and a tensile strain capacity of **4.8 %** were achieved.

## Key Contributions
- **Elucidation of the dual multi-scale synergy of nano-silica and hybrid fibers**: nano-silica densifies ITZ hydration products and raises fiber interfacial friction, while the steel–PVA fiber pair maintains bridging capacity over the entire crack-width range.
- **Complete resolution of the early-age strength retardation problem of HVFA-ECC**: 7 d compressive strength raised from 22 MPa to 36 MPa, a 63 % increase.
- **50 % enhancement of initial crack strength**: confinement of fine crack opening by macro steel fibers secured an initial cracking strength of $\sigma_{fc} = 4.8 MPa$.
- **Sustainability and eco-friendliness**: cutting cement content by 70 % to minimize embodied carbon emissions.

## Methods
- Binder: cement 30 % + Class-F fly ash 70 % + colloidal nano-silica (1.0, 2.0, 3.0 wt %), $W/B = 0.25$.
- Fiber combinations: PVA only (2.0 %), Hybrid-1 (PVA 1.5 % + Steel 0.5 %), Hybrid-2 (PVA 1.0 % + Steel 1.0 %).
- Testing: dogbone direct tensile test, four-point bending test, compressive strength at 3 d/7 d/28 d, SEM-EDS microstructural analysis.
