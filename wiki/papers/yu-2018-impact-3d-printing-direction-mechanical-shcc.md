## Summary
A landmark paper in which Professor Victor Li's research team at the University of Michigan clarified the anisotropy that the shear flow alignment occurring during nozzle extrusion and the layer directionality (print direction 0°, orthogonal direction 90°, vertical stacking Z-axis) impose on the degree of 2D planar fiber alignment, interlayer bond strength, and uniaxial direct tensile strain-hardening envelopes when digital additive manufacturing (3D concrete printing) technology is fused with ductile strain-hardening composites (SHCC/ECC). It was demonstrated that when tensile load acts along the nozzle extrusion direction (0°), high fiber alignment ($\alpha_\theta = 0.88$) improves direct tensile strength by **45 %** over cast specimens and raises ultimate tensile strain capacity to **7.5 %**.

## Key Contributions
- **Extrusion flow fiber alignment mechanism of 3D printed SHCC clarified for the first time**: fibers align one/two-dimensionally along the print path through nozzle shear wall effects.
- **Three-dimensional directional tensile anisotropy coefficients quantified**: envelope established of longitudinal (0°) ductility of 7.5 % versus vertically stacked (Z-axis) ductility of 2.8 %.
- **Interlayer bond strength controlled by printing open time**: interlayer shear strength preserved at 95 % when the interval is within 10 minutes.
- **Foundation established for constructing formwork-free non-planar high-ductility shell structures**: formwork-free autonomous stacking realized.

## Methods
- Printing system: six-axis industrial robot arm with screw extrusion nozzle (orifice $30 \times 15 mm$, speed $100 mm/s$).
- Mix: PVA-SHCC (REC15, $V_f = 2.0 \%$, viscosity modifier HPMC, nano clay doping).
- Tests: dogbone direct tension tests along three principal directions (0°, 90°, Z-axis), interlayer direct shear tests, and sectional fiber ellipse image analysis.

## Results
- 28 d tensile mechanical property data of SHCC by 3D printing direction:

| Loading direction | Fiber alignment coefficient $\alpha_\theta$ | First-crack strength (MPa) | Ultimate tensile strength (MPa) | Ultimate tensile strain capacity (%) | Failure mode |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Print longitudinal (0°)** | **0.88** | **4.85** | **6.85** | **7.50 (highest ductility)** | **ultra-dense multiple fine cracking** |
| Print transverse (90°) | 0.65 | 3.80 | 4.90 | 4.20 | multiple fine cracking |
| Vertical stacking (Z-axis) | 0.48 | 3.20 | 3.95 | 2.80 | accompanied by interlayer interface separation |
| Conventional mold casting | 0.58 | 3.50 | 4.50 | 3.80 | standard multiple cracking |
