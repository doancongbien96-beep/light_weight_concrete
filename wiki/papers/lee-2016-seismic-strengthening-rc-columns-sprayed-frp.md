# Seismic Behavior of Non-Seismic RC Columns Retrofitted with a Sprayed-FRP Three-Dimensional Shear-Confining Tube: Complete Prevention of Shear Failure, 2.8-Fold Amplification of Lateral Displacement Ductility ($\mu = 5.8$), and the Seismic Energy Dissipation Mechanism

## Summary

To fundamentally prevent the collapse of aging reinforced concrete (RC) columns constructed before the introduction of seismic design provisions in the 1980s–90s — whose oversized transverse hoop spacing and defective hook anchorage trigger brittle shear rupture and axial collapse under strong earthquakes — the research team of **Prof. Kang Seok Lee and Prof. Bang Yeon Lee (corresponding author) of Chonnam National University and Prof. Soo Yeon Seo of Korea National University of Transportation** developed a **sprayed-FRP (Sprayed-FRP) seismic strengthening system that forms a seamless three-dimensional shear-confining jacket 3–5 mm thick by high-pressure continuous on-site spraying of chopped carbon fiber (Chopped Carbon Fiber: $L_f = 36\ \text{mm}$) and glass fibers together with low-viscosity vinyl-ester resin onto the column surface**, and carried out cyclic lateral loading tests (Cyclic Lateral Loading Test) on full-scale RC column specimens ($300 \times 300 \times 1500\ \text{mm}$, shear-span ratio $a/d = 2.5$) under a constant axial load ($0.2 f_c A_g$), thereby demonstrating **complete conversion of the premature brittle shear failure of unretrofitted columns into fully flexural plastic hinge behavior, a dramatic 2.8-fold amplification of lateral displacement ductility ($\mu = 2.10 \rightarrow 5.85$), a 3.6-fold improvement in seismic energy dissipation capacity, complete blocking of cover-concrete spalling, and fully monolithic confinement with zero interfacial loss** — the highest-authority landmark original paper of the team published in MDPI Polymers in 2016.

## Key Contributions

1. **World's first full-scale seismic-excitation demonstration of the Sprayed-FRP column seismic strengthening system**:
   - Unlike conventional FRP sheet methods that are vulnerable at lap splices, the system wraps the column corners and the entire section in a seamless, continuous monolithic jacket to maximize confinement efficiency.
2. **Complete suppression of the brittle shear failure of non-seismic RC columns and conversion to flexurally ductile behavior**:
   - Whereas the unretrofitted column collapsed abruptly with X-shaped shear cracks at a drift angle of $1.5\%$, the Sprayed-CFRP-retrofitted column supported the axial load perfectly up to a drift of $5.0\%$.
3. **Lateral displacement ductility $\mu = 5.85$ (2.8-fold amplification) and a 3.6-fold improvement in hysteretic energy dissipation**:
   - The three-dimensional non-directional fiber network strongly confines the lateral expansion of the core concrete, preventing buckling of the main reinforcement and creating a large plastic-hinge energy-absorption zone.
4. **Establishment of a standard for ultra-fast, tailor-made seismic strengthening of steeply inclined corners and areas with complex piping interference**:
   - Perfectly adherent installation was achieved by spraying alone while minimizing rounding (Chamfering) of the column corners, shortening the construction period by $75\%$.

## Methods

- **Materials and spray process (Section 2 & Tables 1~3 & Figs. 1~4)**:
  - Fibers: high-strength carbon roving and E-glass roving (cut length $L_f = 36\ \text{mm}$).
  - Resin: weather-resistant vinyl-ester resin (room-temperature catalytic cure).
  - Spray thickness: carbon-sprayed column (SC-3.5mm), glass-sprayed column (SG-5.0mm).
- **Full-scale column reversed cyclic lateral loading test (Sections 2.2~2.4 & Figs. 5~9)**:
  - Column specifications: $300 \times 300 \times 1500\ \text{mm}$ (main bars 8-D19, hoops D10@250mm — a severely non-seismic, shear-vulnerable arrangement, $f_c = 24.0\ \text{MPa}$).
  - Loading: incrementally increasing repeated lateral loading at $\pm 0.5\%, \pm 1.0\%, \pm 1.5\%, \ldots, \pm 5.0\%$ drift steps under a constant axial load $P = 432\ \text{kN}$ ($0.2 f_c A_g$) maintained throughout.

### 2. Interpretation of the three-dimensional shear-confining tube and main-rebar buckling prevention mechanism
- **Formation of a triaxial compressive stress field in the core concrete**: The Sprayed-CFRP tube applies a uniform confining stress ($f_l$) of $4.5\ \text{MPa}$ around the entire circumference of the column, expanding the compressive strain limit of the concrete 4.5-fold ($0.0033 \rightarrow 0.0150$).
- **Prevention of main-rebar buckling and lap-splice slip**: Even in regions with wide hoop spacing, the FRP tube firmly suppresses buckling of the internal reinforcement, maintaining full, fat hysteretic loops without pinching up to the ultimate lateral displacement ($\text{Drift } 5.0\%$).

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is the highest-level landmark original study in the fields of seismic strengthening of existing buildings and disaster-prevention composite materials engineering, in which the research team of Prof. Kang Seok Lee, Prof. Bang Yeon Lee, and Prof. Soo Yeon Seo demonstrated through the Sprayed-FRP system the complete suppression of the brittle shear failure of non-seismic RC columns and the 2.8-fold amplification of the lateral displacement ductility to 5.85.
