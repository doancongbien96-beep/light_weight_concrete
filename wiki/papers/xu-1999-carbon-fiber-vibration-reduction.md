# Effect of Carbon Fibers on the Vibration-Reduction Ability of Cement

## Summary

A short communication paper (3 pp) from the Chung group (SUNY Buffalo). Isotropic pitch-based carbon fibers (∅15 µm×5 mm, 0.5 wt %) were incorporated into cement paste, and the effect of surface treatment (ozone, dichromate, silane) on the dynamic flexural properties (tan δ, storage modulus, loss modulus) was evaluated. Silane treatment produced an improvement in storage modulus of up to +38 %, and the addition of methylcellulose was shown to enable tan δ gains of +50 %.

## Key Contributions

1. **Quantification of damping characteristics by surface treatment**: storage modulus ranking of silane > dichromate > ozone > untreated. Up to a 38 % increase with silane treatment (synergy with silane-treated silica fume). tan δ rather decreased with fiber incorporation.
2. **Trade-off effect of methylcellulose**: tan δ up to +50 % (improved damping) against storage modulus -14 % (reduced stiffness); selected according to the target in functional design.
3. **Role of silica fume surface treatment**: overall increase in storage modulus when silane-treated silica fume was used (relative to as-received); enhanced interfacial bonding contributed to vibration resistance.

## Methods

- Materials: uncoated isotropic pitch-based carbon fibers (∅15 µm×5 mm, tensile strength 690 MPa, elastic modulus 48 GPa, electrical resistivity 3.0×10⁻³ Ω·cm).
- Dosage: 0.5 wt % by cement weight.
- Four surface treatments: untreated / ozone (O₃ 0.6 vol % in O₂, 160 °C 5 min) / immersion in K₂Cr₂O₇+H₂SO₄ solution (60 °C 2 h) / silane (Z-6020 amine + Z-6040 epoxy, Dow Corning).
- Silica fume: two types, as-received vs silane-treated.
- Measurement: dynamic flexural testing (Dynamic Mechanical Analysis); tan δ, storage modulus (E′), and loss modulus (E″) measured at 0.2, 1.0, and 2.0 Hz.

## Results

| Composition | E′ (GPa, 1 Hz) | tan δ (1 Hz) |
|---|---|---|
| A (reference) | 12.14 | <0.002 |
| A+F (fibers only) | 13.75 | <0.002 |
| A+O (ozone) | 14.46 | <0.002 |
| A+K (dichromate) | 16.15 | <0.002 |
| A+S (silane) | 17.67 | <0.002 |
| A+S+silane SF | 21.65 | <0.002 |

- The combination of silane-treated silica fume and silane-treated fibers achieved the highest storage modulus.
