## Summary
To prevent brittle concrete crushing in over-reinforced concrete flexural beams reinforced with glass fiber-reinforced polymer (GFRP) bars (diameters 12 and 16 mm, nominal tensile strength 1000 MPa, modulus of elasticity 60 GPa), this study by Dr. S.V.M. Esfahani and Professor M.K. Sharbatdar at Semnan University investigated the flexural behavior of full-scale beams (clear span 2060 mm, width 210 mm, height 230 mm, effective depth $d = 190$ mm) wherein normal concrete (NC: 28 d cylinder compressive strength 55 MPa) was substituted across the full section or selectively in compression/tension zones with high-performance fiber-reinforced cementitious composites (HPFRCC: micro-sinusoidal hooked steel fibers $V_f = 1\%$, $81\ \text{kg/m}^3$, length 50 mm, diameter 1 mm, tensile strength 1050 MPa, 28 d compressive strength 59 MPa). The beam with full-depth 1% HPFRCC substitution (BCHG1) increased peak flexural load by **44 % to 188.0 kN** relative to the control normal concrete beam (BCMG1, $P_{max} = 132.6$ kN) and expanded ultimate deflection ($\Delta_u$) by **77 % from 47.09 mm to 84.14 mm**, demonstrating ductile behavior.

## Key Contributions
- **Transformation of brittle crushing into ductile failure in over-reinforced GFRP-RC beams**: Full-depth HPFRCC substitution (BCHG1) increased maximum flexural capacity by 44 % and expanded ultimate displacement by 1.77 times.
- **Evaluation of selective zone substitution (compression vs tension)**: Compression-zone HPFRCC substitution (BCMHG1) achieved a maximum load of 157.33 kN (+19 %), while tension-zone substitution (BCHMG1, BCHMG3) enhanced ultimate displacement to 65.08 ~ 78.19 mm (+38 ~ 66 %).
- **Elucidation of failure mechanisms across reinforcement-to-balanced ratios (1.42 ~ 2.0)**: HPFRCC delayed compressive crushing and restrained concrete tensile crack opening.
- **Formulation of flexural ductility design guidelines for GFRP-reinforced members**: Established design criteria for corrosion-free composite-reinforced bridge decks.

## Methods
- Beam specimen dimensions: clear span 2060 mm, width 210 mm, total height 230 mm, effective depth 190 mm.
- GFRP reinforcement: FIREP bars (diameters 12, 16 mm, tensile strength 1000 MPa, elastic modulus 60 GPa).
- Mixture proportions ($\text{kg/m}^3$):
  - **Normal concrete (NC)**: water 242, Type II cement 805, sand 499, gravel 741, microsilica 80, superplasticizer 4 (28 d compressive strength 55 MPa).
  - **HPFRCC**: water 283, Type II cement 808, sand 1086, microsilica 81, silica powder 24, superplasticizer 8, steel fibers 81 (1.0 vol %, 28 d compressive strength 59 MPa).
- Steel fiber properties: sinusoidal hooked straight steel fibers (length 50 mm, diameter 1 mm, aspect ratio 50, tensile strength 1050 MPa).
