# Performance-Based Design (PBD) of All-Grade Strain-Hardening Cementitious Composites (PE-SHCC) with Compressive Strengths of $40\sim 120\ \text{MPa}$ and the Governing Equation of the Fiber Reinforcing Index ($V_f L_f/d_f$)

## Summary

To realize highly ductile multiple microcracking strain-hardening (SHCC) at any strength grade from normal strength (40 MPa) up to ultra-high strength (120 MPa), matched to the diverse performance requirements of structures, this is the landmark 2019 Cement and Concrete Composites source paper by the joint research team of Linzhi Li and Kequan Yu (corresponding author) of Tongji University together with UNSW and Chongqing University, which carried out a comprehensive analysis of single-fiber pullout mechanics, uniaxial direct tensile behavior (first cracking strength, tensile strength, tensile strain), energy dissipation capacity ($g_{se}$), and crack patterns on a total of 36 PE-SHCC mixes parameterized by the **water-to-binder ratio (W/B = 0.14, 0.16, 0.18, 0.22, 0.32) and the fiber reinforcing index ($V_f L_f / d_f = 5\sim 18$)**, and became the first in the world to formally establish **an all-grade performance-based design (PBD: Performance-Based Design) chart and design equations capable of freely predicting and controlling a compressive strength of $40\sim 120\ \text{MPa}$, a tensile strength of $4.0\sim 15.8\ \text{MPa}$, and a tensile strain of $5.0\sim 10.2\%$**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Establishment of an all-grade (compressive strength $40\sim 120\ \text{MPa}$) SHCC performance-based design (PBD) framework**:
   - Moving beyond existing single-strength optimization studies, completed a universal design chart that determines the desired compressive strength, tensile strength, and tensile strain ($\le 10.2\%$) all at once from W/B and the fiber reinforcing index ($V_f L_f / d_f$) alone.
2. **Derivation of quantitative prediction equations for first cracking strength and tensile strength based on the fiber reinforcing index ($V_f L_f / d_f$)**:
   - Derived empirically–micromechanically integrated governing relations for the first cracking strength $\sigma_{tc} = \sigma_{tm} + 0.11 \left(\frac{L_f}{d_f} V_f\right)^{0.86}$ and the peak tensile strength $\sigma_{tu} = 0.24 \tau_0 \frac{L_f}{d_f} V_f$ (error within $\pm 10\%$).
3. **Attainment of a tensile strain of $8.5\%$ in the ultra-high strength (120 MPa) range and $10.2\%$ in the high strength (70~90 MPa) range**:
   - Demonstrated that slip-type pullout of PE fibers can be induced even in the ultra-dense W/B = 0.14 matrix, realizing world-leading mechanical properties of compressive strength $115\ \text{MPa}$, tensile strength $15.8\ \text{MPa}$, and tensile strain $8.5\%$.
4. **Analysis of the stress fluctuation and crack saturation mechanisms by strength grade**:
   - Proved that the stress oscillation ratio during the strain-hardening regime (mean oscillation / peak stress) stays within $6\sim 10\%$, maintaining an extremely stable plastic flow.

## Methods

- **Materials and design of 36 mixes across all strength grades (Section 2 & Tables 1~4)**:
  - Binders: OPC + silica fume (SF) + fly ash (FA) + slag (GGBS).
  - Five W/B levels: **0.14 (UHPC grade), 0.16, 0.18 (high strength), 0.22 (medium strength), 0.32 (normal strength)**.
  - Three PE fibers: diameter $d_f = 20, 24\ \mu\text{m}$, length $L_f = 12, 18\ \text{mm}$, aspect ratio $L_f/d_f = 500, 750, 900$, volume fraction $V_f = 1.0, 1.5, 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$.
  - Fiber reinforcing index: $V_f L_f / d_f \in [5.0, 18.0]$.
- **Test methods (Section 2.2 & Figs. 2~4)**:
  - Single-fiber pullout test: micro-load cell (0.6 mm/min), embedment length 6 mm.
  - Compressive strength: 70.7 mm cubes (water curing for 28 days).
  - Uniaxial direct tension: dogbone-shaped plate specimens ($350 \times 50 \times 15\ \text{mm}$), displacement control at $0.5\ \text{mm/min}$, measured over an LVDT gauge length of 80 mm.

### 2. Performance-based design (PBD) charts and micromechanics interpretation
- **Dominant role of $V_f L_f / d_f$**: At every strength grade the tensile strength and the energy dissipation capacity exhibit an almost perfectly linear proportional relationship with the fiber reinforcing index $V_f L_f / d_f$, and when $V_f L_f / d_f \ge 15.0$ an ultimate tensile strain of 8 % or more is reliably guaranteed.
- **Interfacial friction optimization of the ultra-high strength matrix**: Even in the ultra-high strength W/B = 0.14 matrix, silica fume and fly ash homogenize the micro morphology of the C-S-H gel around the fibers and control the interfacial frictional stress $\tau_0$ within the appropriate range ($2.5\sim 3.5\ \text{MPa}$), thereby preventing fiber rupture and manifesting the ultra-high tensile strength of $15.8\ \text{MPa}$.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is the foremost landmark source paper in the field of cement composite materials engineering, unifying all-grade strain-hardening composites from normal strength (40 MPa) through ultra-high strength (120 MPa) under a single design framework and completing a practical performance-based design (PBD) system that precisely tunes the target strength and ductility based on the fiber reinforcing index.
