# Non-Corrosive Polymer Reinforcement and Flexural Evaluation of Concrete Beams Using Recycled PET Bars and CFRP Strips

## Summary

To eliminate rebar corrosion—the primary cause of deterioration in reinforced concrete structures exposed to marine and deicing salt environments—and promote the recycling of post-consumer plastic waste (recycled PET beverage bottles), Professor Dora Foti at the Technical University of Bari investigated non-metallic polymer reinforcements by replacing longitudinal steel rebar with **recycled PET round bars (diameter $\phi 12\ \text{mm}$, tensile strength 135 MPa, elastic modulus 3.1 GPa)** and **carbon fiber strips (CFRP Strips: width 50 mm, thickness 1.2 mm, tensile strength 2800 MPa, elastic modulus 165 GPa)** across six concrete beam specimens ($150\ \text{mm} \times 200\ \text{mm} \times 1000\ \text{mm}$, clear span 840 mm). Through mid-point three-point bending tests and LVDT displacement monitoring, the study evaluated flexural failure modes, load-deflection responses, concrete-polymer interfacial bonding, and load-carrying capacities. The CFRP strip-reinforced beams achieved ultimate flexural capacities of **$34.2\sim 38.5\ \text{kN}$**, developing distributed $45^\circ$ diagonal flexural-shear microcracks with substantial deflection ductility. The recycled PET bar-reinforced beams sustained ultimate loads of **$21.5\sim 24.8\ \text{kN}$**, matching conventional concrete flexural capacities while offering complete immunity to corrosion to reduce long-term maintenance costs in marine and infrastructure applications.

## Key Contributions

1. **Demonstration of structural performance in recycled PET bar tensile reinforcement**:
   - Deployed extruded round bars ($3\phi 12$) from recycled PET bottles as primary longitudinal tensile reinforcement in concrete beams, with ultimate loads exceeding theoretical capacities ($P = 21.5\ \text{kN}$) by up to 15 % and validating structural applicability.
2. **Amplification of flexural capacity and ductility via embedded CFRP strips**:
   - Embedded ultra-high-strength CFRP strips ($f_{tk} = 2800\ \text{MPa}$) using multi-layer concrete casting to prevent premature debonding typical of externally bonded systems, increasing ultimate load by 75 % to **38.5 kN**.
3. **Complete elimination of chloride and chemical corrosion risks (100 %)**:
   - Eliminated concrete cover spalling and section loss induced by corrosion expansion of steel rebar through non-corrosive polymer alternatives.

## Methods

- **Mechanical properties of reinforcement (Tables 1 & 3)**:
  - **Recycled PET Bars**:
    - Diameter $\phi 12\ \text{mm}$ (area $113\ \text{mm}^2 \times 3 = 339\ \text{mm}^2$).
    - Tensile strength 135 MPa, elastic modulus 3100 MPa, ultimate elongation 50 %.
  - **CFRP Strips**:
    - 2 plies of width $50\ \text{mm} \times$ thickness $1.2\ \text{mm}$ (area $60\ \text{mm}^2 \times 2 = 120\ \text{mm}^2$).
    - Tensile strength 2800 MPa, elastic modulus 165 GPa, ultimate elongation 1.7 %.
  - Top assembly bars: 2 $\phi 8\ \text{mm}$ B450C steel bars, shear stirrups $\phi 8\ \text{mm}$ @ 70 mm spacing.
- **Concrete mix and beam specimen fabrication (Figures 2–8)**:
  - Concrete mix: CEM II/A-LL 42.5 R cement, maximum aggregate size 10 mm, W/C = 0.50, 28 d compressive strength 30.0 MPa.
  - Beam dimensions: Width $b = 150\ \text{mm}$, height $h = 200\ \text{mm}$, length $L = 1000\ \text{mm}$ (cover 20 mm).
- **Testing program**:
  - Three-point bending: Simply supported span $L_s = 840\ \text{mm}$, central concentrated point load at $0.05\ \text{mm/s}$ displacement control.
  - Mid-span deflection: Dual high-precision LVDTs capturing real-time load-deflection histories.

### 2. Interfacial bond and non-corrosive reinforcement analysis
- **Bond performance of smooth PET bars**: Despite the absence of surface ribs, frictional bond with concrete transferred load without premature slip, sustaining over 24 kN.
- **Interlayer integrity of CFRP**: CFRP strips embedded between 20 mm vibrated concrete layers exhibited 100 % composite action without debonding.

- **Overall conclusion**: This work demonstrated that recycled PET polymer bars and embedded CFRP strips serve as effective non-corrosive internal tensile reinforcements for concrete beams, resolving corrosion durability challenges while maintaining high flexural capacity and ductility.
