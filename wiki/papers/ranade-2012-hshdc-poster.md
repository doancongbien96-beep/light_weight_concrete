## Summary
A landmark poster paper demonstrating the micromechanical mix-design mechanism of High-Strength High-Ductility Concrete (HSHDC / Super-ECC), developed by Dr. Ravi Ranade and Prof. Victor Li at the University of Michigan with support from the U.S. Department of Defense (DoD) and NSF to maximize the blast/impact resistance of military protective structures and critical national infrastructure, together with its dynamic tensile/compressive behavior under high strain rates ($\dot{\epsilon} = 10^{-4} \sim 10^2 s^{-1}$) and its projectile penetration resistance. By achieving a compressive strength of **165 MPa**, a uniaxial tensile strain capacity of **3.8 %**, and a dynamic increase factor $DIF = 1.65$, it demonstrated that back-face scabbing caused by blast shock waves is suppressed 100 %.

## Key Contributions
- **Meeting PSH strain-hardening criteria in an ultra-high-strength matrix ($f_c = 165 MPa$)**: slip-hardening frictional design of ultra-high-molecular-weight PE fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, tensile strength 3000 MPa).
- **Realization of a tensile fracture energy density of $540 kJ/m^3$**: energy absorption capacity increased by more than 300 times compared with normal concrete.
- **Demonstration of high-velocity projectile and blast resistance**: 60 % reduction in 7.62 mm armor-piercing projectile penetration depth and zero back-face cratering.
- **Presentation of a design standard for thin protective panels for defense bunkers and nuclear power plant containment buildings**: realization of a 50 % reduction in structural member thickness.

## Methods
- Binder: Type I cement + silica fume (20 %) + fine ground quartz (30 %), $W/B = 0.16$, UHMWPE fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$).
- Tests: dogbone direct tensile test, Split Hopkinson Pressure Bar (SHPB) high-rate tensile test, high-velocity projectile impact test.

## Results
- Comparison table of mechanical performance between HSHDC and existing protective materials:

| Material | Compressive strength (MPa) | Direct tensile strength (MPa) | Ultimate tensile strain capacity (%) | Fracture energy density ($kJ/m^3$) | Back-face scabbing under blast |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Normal concrete (NC) | 35.0 | 3.2 | 0.015 (brittle) | 1.5 | Severe fragment scabbing and perforation |
| Steel-fiber UHPC | 160.0 | 8.5 | 0.25 (softening) | 45.0 | Back-face spalling fragments produced |
| **HSHDC (Super-ECC)** | **165.0** | **14.8** | **3.80 (ultra strain-hardening)** | **540.0** | **Back-face scabbing blocked 100 %** |
