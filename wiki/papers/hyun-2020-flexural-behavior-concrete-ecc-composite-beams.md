# Flexural Capacity, Deflection Ductility, and Energy Dissipation of Concrete-ECC Composite Beams with Variable Plastic Hinge Replacement Lengths ($0, 5d, \text{Full}$)

## Summary

To alleviate the economic material cost of applying full-depth engineered cementitious composites (ECC) throughout entire structures while maximizing flexural ductility in high-moment plastic hinge zones, Professor Bang Yeon Lee at Chonnam National University and Professor Yun Yong Kim at Chungnam National University investigated reinforced concrete (RC) beams with PVA-ECC (W/B = 0.45, PVA fibers $V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.\%}$, compressive strength 35.5 MPa, tensile strain capacity 3.0 %, tensile strength 4.2 MPa) strategically replacing concrete across varying longitudinal lengths: plain RC control, central partial replacement across 5 times effective depth ($5d = 800\ \text{mm}$, ECC5d), and full span replacement ($2200\ \text{mm}$, ECCFull). Six full-scale composite beams ($110\ \text{mm} \times 180\ \text{mm} \times 2200\ \text{mm}$) instrumented with 52 rebar strain gauges were tested under 4-point bending (400 mm pure bending span). The **ECC5d specimen (800 mm replacement) achieved an ultimate flexural capacity of 74.0 kN (a 10.0 % increase over plain RC), reaching 97.5 % of the capacity of the full-span ECCFull beam (75.9 kN)**. Furthermore, replacing concrete with ECC distributed tensile crack localization into fine multiple microcracks ($w < 100\ \mu\text{m}$), **increasing ultimate deflection by 17.1 % ~ 50.0 % ($80.2\ \text{mm} \rightarrow 93.9\ \text{mm} \rightarrow 120.3\ \text{mm}$)** and enhancing energy dissipation by over 62 %.

## Key Contributions

1. **97.5 % flexural capacity parity achieved with partial $5d$ replacement**:
   - Concentrating ECC material across only 36 % of the total span ($800\ \text{mm}$) yielded flexural strength ($67.2 \rightarrow 74.0\ \text{kN}$) nearly identical to full-depth placement.
2. **Attainment of a displacement ductility index of 5.2 via microcrack dispersion ($w < 100\ \mu\text{m}$)**:
   - Suppressed premature localized crack opening and concrete compressive crushing, sustaining ductile deformation up to an ultimate deflection of $120.3\ \text{mm}$.
3. **Curvature and plastic strain distribution via 52-channel rebar strain mapping**:
   - Verified stress transfer continuity across material transition boundaries ($x = \pm 400\ \text{mm}$), proving that no shear slip or delamination occurred at composite interfaces.
4. **62 % increase in energy absorption capacity**:
   - Substantially expanded the area under load-deflection curves, confirming excellent seismic and dynamic load resistance.

## Methods

- **Materials and beam fabrication (Section 2–3 & Tables 1–5)**:
  - Substrate concrete: OPC, slump 150 mm, 28 d compressive strength 21.0 MPa.
  - ECC mixture (mass ratios): OPC 1.0, silica sand ($d_{50} = 110\ \mu\text{m}$) 0.36, water 0.45 (W/B = 0.45), Kuraray PVA fibers ($L_f = 12\ \text{mm}, d_f = 40\ \mu\text{m}$, tensile strength 1600 MPa, modulus 40 GPa, $V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.\%}$).
  - ECC properties: 28 d compressive strength 35.5 MPa, tensile strength 4.2 MPa, strain capacity 3.0 %.
  - Steel reinforcement: Tensile 3-D13 ($f_y = 400\ \text{MPa}$), compressive 2-D13, shear stirrups D6@100 ($f_y = 400\ \text{MPa}$).
- **Specimen series (3 types, 2 duplicates each, 6 beams total)**:
  - RC series (RC-1, RC-2): Monolithic normal concrete (2200 mm).
  - ECC5d series (ECC5d-1, ECC5d-2): Central $5d = 800\ \text{mm}$ zone replaced with ECC.
  - ECCFull series (ECCFull-1, ECCFull-2): Full-span 2200 mm monolithic ECC casting.
- **Testing protocols (Figures 3–5)**:
  - 4-point bending: Span 1900 mm, pure bending span 400 mm, 500 kN UTM under displacement control.
  - Instrumentation: Laser displacement sensor at mid-span, 52 synchronized rebar strain gauges.

### 2. Flexural mechanics and composite interface behavior
- **Plastic hinge confinement**: Concentrating ECC in the central $5d$ zone redistributed rebar tensile stresses across the plastic hinge, matching 97.5 % of full-span ECC capacity (74.0 kN vs 75.9 kN).
- **Interface bond integrity**: Continuous wet casting established monolithic composite bonding without interface slip or cracking (100 % composite integrity).

- **Overall conclusion**: This work demonstrated that selective partial replacement with ECC in the plastic hinge zone ($5d$) achieves flexural strength and ductility comparable to full-depth placement while cutting material costs.
