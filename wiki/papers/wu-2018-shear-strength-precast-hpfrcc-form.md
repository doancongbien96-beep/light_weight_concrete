# Shear strength of RC beams with precast HPFRCC permanent form

## Summary

The shear strength of composite beams composed of thin (20 mm thick) U-shaped precast HPFRCC permanent formwork (wire mesh reinforced) filled with cast-in-place concrete was determined through full-scale three-point loading tests and analysis (joint study of Harbin Institute of Technology, Seoul National University, and Hunan University; bibliography confirmed from the paper body). The study showed that the thin HPFRCC U-section substantially augments the shear contributions of the infilled concrete and the shear reinforcement, and proposed a modified shear strength design equation reflecting composite action, the diagonal crack angle, and the shear span-to-depth ratio.

## Key Contributions

- Quantitative proof that existing design equations (ACI 318, EC 2, MC 2010, GB 50010) predict RC beams accurately (test/prediction ratio 0.98–1.20) but underestimate HPFRCC-RC composite beams by 1.38–2.83 times, and achievement of test/prediction ratios of 0.96–1.02 with a proposed equation introducing three correction coefficients.
- Physical explanation of the augmented stirrup contribution through the reduced inclination of critical diagonal cracks (38.0° → 33.4°/36.7°), with the equivalent coefficient Ccv = cotθC/cotθRC = 1.12 proposed accordingly. The difference in stirrup contribution between the two specimens predicted with this value, 101.4 kN, matched the measured difference of 104.5 kN.
- Proposal of a closed-form shear strength equation with a regression-calibrated composite action coefficient CtCuc = 2.5 and a shear span-to-depth ratio coefficient γ (0.7 when λ ≤ 1.5, otherwise 1.0) built on the CECS fiber-reinforced concrete shear model.
- External validation with the experimental data of Wirojjanapirom et al. (2013) for a similar detail (U-shaped UFC formwork + bolted connections): mean test/prediction ratio 1.08, range 0.89–1.31. The applicability range was stated as HPFRCC sectional area ratio 21.6–31.6 %, beam depth 300–400 mm, shear span-to-depth ratio 1.00–3.27, ft = 2.10–2.70 MPa, and fUtm = 5.40–9.34 MPa.

## Methods

- Four specimens (cross-section 200 × 400 mm): NCS-2-1 (RC control), UCS-2-1 (HPFRCC + RC, shear reinforcement A8@100, shear span-to-depth ratio 1.83), UCS-2-2 (A8@200), and UCS-1.5-1 (shear span-to-depth ratio 1.38). Three-point loading; tension reinforcement 3C25 + 3C18 (reinforcement ratio 3.11 %).
- HPFRCC mix (weight ratios): cement 1, silica fume 0.25, heavy calcium carbonate filler (0.04 mm) 0.3, river sand 1.1, water 0.2, steel fiber (diameter 0.2 mm, length 13 mm, tensile strength 2500 MPa) 0.015 (1.5 % by volume), and high-range water reducer 0.036. Cube compressive strength 99.5 MPa (cylinder equivalent 84.5 MPa). Tensile tests on notched dog-bone specimens of wire-mesh-incorporated HPFRCC gave a cracking strength of 5.40 MPa and an ultimate tensile strength of 6.23 MPa.
- Infilled concrete C30: cube 28.2 MPa (cylinder equivalent 22.6 MPa).
- Fabrication process: M16 through-bolts (spacing 200 mm) were embedded in the mold, 10 mm of HPFRCC was cast, the wire mesh (Q235, diameter 0.6 mm, grid 6 mm) was placed, and the remaining HPFRCC was cast to a total thickness of 20 mm. The side panels were cured naturally for 24 h, folded at 90° to lap-splice the bottom panel mesh, and the bottom panel was steam cured at 90 ± 2 °C and RH 95 % for 48 h.

## Results

- Peak loads: NCS-2-1 510 kN, UCS-2-1 843 kN, UCS-2-2 634 kN, and UCS-1.5-1 1090 kN. Under identical conditions the 20 mm thick U-shaped HPFRCC produced increases of +74.3 % in crack resistance and +67.4 % in shear strength, despite a sectional area ratio of only 0.24.
- Crack resistance: the load at initial fine cracking rose from 175 kN (RC) to 305 kN (composite). The composite beams developed about 144 cracks versus about 75 in RC, showing multiple fine cracking with narrower widths.
- When the shear span-to-depth ratio decreased from 1.83 to 1.38, the diagonal crack angle grew steeper and the stirrup contribution decreased, yet shear strength increased by +29 % (direct compressive arch force transfer).
- In the comparison table of test/prediction ratios between the proposed equation and five existing design equations, the proposed equation averaged 0.99 (0.96–1.02) while the existing equations averaged 1.41–1.98 (underestimation of the composite beams).
- The ACI 544.4R equation was conservative for the cracking moment in all specimens (measured/calculated 1.88–3.67).
