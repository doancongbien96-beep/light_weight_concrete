# Wire-Woven Cellular Metals: 3D Truss Lattice (Kagome/Octet) Topologies, Continuous Brazing Processes, Stretching-Dominated Mechanics ($E \propto \rho_{rel}^1$), and Impact Energy Absorption Review

## Summary

To achieve continuous mass production and extreme mechanical and thermal performance in ultra-lightweight multifunctional cellular metals, this comprehensive 95-page review by Professor Ki-Ju Kang at Chonnam National University synthesizes the state of the art in **wire-woven cellular metals (single- and multi-layer Kagome, Octet, Pyramidal, Diamond, and Wire-woven Bulk Kagome: WBK)**. The paper details topology classifications, helical forming and 6-directional continuous assembly processes, analytical models for effective moduli, compressive/shear strength, and plastic buckling across relative densities of $\rho_{rel} = 0.01\sim 0.20$. The author demonstrates the stretching-dominated mechanical superiority of periodic wire trusses over disordered metal foams ($E_{eff} \propto \rho_{rel}^1$ vs $\rho_{rel}^2$), high-velocity impact and blast wave attenuation in sandwich panels, and forced-convection heat dissipation media. By replacing bending-dominated cell struts with axial-load-bearing wire trusses, this framework **enhances strength and stiffness by 3~10-fold** at identical relative densities, maximizing volumetric energy absorption ($U_v$) through plastic hinge collapse.

## Key Contributions

1. **First systematic formulation of WBK continuous manufacturing and 3D truss topologies**:
   - Established the WBK process assembling helically formed wires along 6 orientations followed by one-step liquid-phase brazing, enabling continuous production of multi-layer truss cores.
2. **Formulation of stretching-dominated linear scaling laws**:
   - Rigorously proved linear scaling ($E_{eff}/E_s = C_1 \rho_{rel}^1$, $\sigma_{eff}/\sigma_s = C_2 \rho_{rel}^1$), in contrast to the non-linear degradation of random metal foams ($E \propto \rho_{rel}^2, \sigma \propto \rho_{rel}^{1.5}$).
3. **Three-stage energy absorption mechanism under blast/impact loads**:
   - Delineated the sequence of linear elasticity $\rightarrow$ plastic yield plateau $\rightarrow$ densification crushing, absorbing up to $80\ \text{kJ/kg}$ of shock energy through uniform strut buckling.
4. **Design standards for multifunctional sandwich panels and heat exchangers**:
   - Optimized out-of-plane compression/shear strength and open-cell fluid flow passages to maximize heat transfer efficiency relative to pressure drop.

## Methods

- **Topology and manufacturing processes (Sections 2–3 & Figs. 2–18)**:
  - Raw materials: Stainless steel (SUS304), copper (Cu), aluminum (Al), and high-strength steel wires (diameters $0.5\sim 2.0\ \text{mm}$).
  - 5-stage fabrication:
    1. Wire pre-forming (triangular/helical bending).
    2. Multi-axial weaving and assembly (interlocking Kagome lattice in 6 orientations).
    3. Post-forming (press calibration of thickness and cell geometry).
    4. High-temperature vacuum/reducing brazing (Cu/Ni paste coating, bonding at $1050\ ^\circ\text{C}$).
    5. Face sheet bonding and finishing.
- **Theoretical mechanics models (Section 4 & Tables 2–4)**:
  - Kagome truss out-of-plane compressive strength: $\sigma_{33} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{3} \rho_{rel} \sigma_y \sin^2\omega$.
  - Out-of-plane shear strength: $\tau_{13} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{6} \rho_{rel} \sigma_y \sin\omega \cos\omega$.
  - Euler elastic buckling limit: $\sigma_{buckling} = \frac{n^2 \pi^2 E_s I}{A l^2} \rho_{rel}$.

### 2. Stretching-dominated mechanics and impact dissipation
- **Fundamental advantage of stretching-dominated lattices**: Truss struts undergo pure axial tension or compression under external load, avoiding localized bending stresses and delivering 10-fold higher specific stiffness than random foams.
- **Blast mitigation in sandwich panels**: Plastic buckling of truss cores under blast overpressures attenuates peak impact pressures by over 75 %, converting sudden shocks into steady plateau stresses.

- **Overall conclusion**: This landmark review established the mechanics, fabrication, and multifunctional design principles of wire-woven cellular metals, providing the authoritative global reference for metallic metamaterials.
