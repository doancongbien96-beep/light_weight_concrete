## Summary
To resolve the deck tensile cracking and rebar corrosion problems that arise in the negative moment regions near interior piers of continuous-span steel-concrete composite bridges, this study elucidated the negative-moment flexural behavior of a full-scale steel-UHPC continuous composite beam ($300 \times 500 \times 6000 mm$) employing ultra-high performance concrete (UHPC, $f_c = 145 MPa$, steel fiber $V_f = 2.0 \%$) in the tension slab, together with load transfer through shear stud connectors, distribution of tensile cracks in the slab, and ultimate flexural capacity, through precise experiments and nonlinear section analysis. Under negative moment loading, the UHPC steel-fiber bridging action restrained the service-load-state crack width to **0.05 mm** or less and demonstrated a **52 %** increase in negative moment flexural capacity over normal concrete.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishment of the flexural capacity and crack control mechanisms of steel-UHPC negative moment composite beams**: the UHPC tensile bridging membrane relieved rebar stress concentration and maintained stiffness after yielding.
- **Clarification of the effective group effect of stud shear connectors**: the high-strength bearing resistance of UHPC raised stud shear capacity by 45 %.
- **Derivation of a crack width control formula**: verification of the safety of the crack width estimation equation against highway bridge design codes.
- **Innovation in superstructure design of continuous composite bridges**: a 70 % reduction in maintenance costs for expansion joints and deck waterproofing layers.

## Methods
- Four large composite beams ($300 \times 500 \times 6000 mm$, I-section steel beam H-$350 \times 175$, top slab thickness 80 mm).
- Materials: steel-fiber UHPC ($f_c = 145 MPa$, $f_t = 9.5 MPa$, steel fiber $V_f = 2.0 \%$) versus normal concrete (NC 45 MPa).
- Testing: reverse-direction two-point loading flexural tests simulating negative moment (maximizing mid-span negative moment), with LVDT deflection and stud slip measurement.
