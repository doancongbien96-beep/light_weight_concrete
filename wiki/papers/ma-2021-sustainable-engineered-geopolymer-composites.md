## Summary

A study evaluating the mechanical performance and micromechanical interfacial characteristics of an eco-friendly strain-hardening engineered geopolymer composite (EGC) that induces alkali-activation reactions based on fly ash (FA) and ground granulated blast-furnace slag (GGBS) without using any cement. Under ambient-curing conditions, PVA and high-strength PE fibers were incorporated, and the matrix fracture toughness ($K_m$), single-fiber pullout behavior, and uniaxial direct tension tests were analyzed. By controlling the GGBS replacement ratio (20 ~ 50 wt %) and the molar ratio of the sodium silicate ($Na_2SiO_3$) activator solution, a tensile strength of $5.5 \sim 8.2 MPa$ and a tensile strain capacity of $4.5 \sim 7.2 \%$ were achieved after 28 d of ambient curing.

## Key Contributions

- **Meeting the PSH micromechanics design criteria for ambient-cured EGC**: leveraging the low fracture toughness of the geopolymer matrix ($K_m \approx 0.45 \sim 0.65 MPa\sqrt{m}$) to secure an energy performance ratio of $J_b'/J_{tip} > 5.0$.
- **Matrix-fiber interface tailoring via precursor compound reactions**: elucidation of the mechanism by which increasing the GGBS fraction densifies the microstructure and precisely controls the fiber frictional shear stress ($\tau_0$).
- **Eco-friendliness and carbon reduction**: realization of equal-or-better ultra-high ductility in tension while reducing manufacturing-stage $CO_2$ emissions by 68 % compared with conventional OPC-based ECC.

## Methods

- **Materials and mix proportioning**: low-calcium Class F fly ash, GGBS, and a sodium silicate solution + $NaOH$ mixed activator ($Na_2O$ equivalent of 6 ~ 10 wt %).
- **Fiber reinforcement**: PE fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 12 µm, length 12 mm) and PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, REC15).
- **Test items**: dog-bone uniaxial direct tension test, three-point bending test for matrix fracture toughness, and 28 d compressive strength.

## Results

- PE-EGC (FA 60 % + GGBS 40 %): 28 d compressive strength $58.4 MPa$, tensile strength $8.15 MPa$, tensile strain capacity $7.15 \%$, average crack width $30 \mu m$.
- PVA-EGC: 28 d compressive strength $48.2 MPa$, tensile strength $5.20 MPa$, tensile strain capacity $4.20 \%$.
