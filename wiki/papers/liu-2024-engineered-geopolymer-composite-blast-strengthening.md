# Blast Shockwave and Contact Detonation (0.4 kg TNT) Resistance of Structures through Strain-Hardening Engineered Geopolymer Composite (EGC) Overlay Strengthening: Demonstration of 90 % Back-Face Spalling Suppression and Blast-Protection Retrofitting

## Summary

To prevent back-face fragment ejection (Spalling), which triggers brittle failure of masonry walls and concrete members plus human casualties under terrorist explosion and accidental gas explosion loads, this landmark 2024 Journal of Building Engineering origin paper from **Prof. Chunyuan Liu of Tianjin University, Prof. Yifei Hao of Hebei University of Technology, and the protective engineering research team of the Army Engineering University of PLA** developed an eco-friendly cement-free slag-fly ash binder-based **strain-hardening engineered geopolymer composite (EGC, fibers 3 vol.%: MPP, PVA, PE)**; while evaluating uniaxial direct tension, flexural ductility index (DI), and drying shrinkage, it characterized the dynamic blast resistance and back-face spalling suppression mechanisms of EGC overlay-strengthened walls and slabs through **full-scale vented chamber gas explosion tests (Vented Gas Explosion) and 0.4 kg TNT contact explosion tests (Contact Explosion)**. The results demonstrated that **PE-EGC strengthening recorded a uniaxial tensile strain of $6.20\%$ and a flexural ductility index of 14.5, and that under 0.4 kg TNT contact explosion, in contrast to the ordinary concrete slab that collapsed by perforation through large-scale back-face spalling, back-face fragment ejection was blocked by more than $90\%$, delivering complete protection performance**.

## Key Contributions

1. **World-first demonstration of the effectiveness of eco-friendly cement-free EGC for blast structural strengthening (Blast Strengthening)**:
   - Overcomes the early interfacial debonding and fire vulnerability of conventional organic FRP strengtheners, preventing explosive collapse of masonry walls at the source through an inorganic geopolymer-based EGC overlay (thickness 20 mm).
2. **Comparison of EGC tensile strain-hardening and flexural ductility by fiber type (MPP, PVA, PE)**:
   - Established that the PE fiber ($V_f = 3.0\%$) EGC exerts overwhelming mechanical energy absorption performance with tensile strength $6.85\ \text{MPa}$, tensile strain $6.20\%$, and flexural strength $16.5\ \text{MPa}$.
3. **Suppression of back-face spalling failure by 90 % under 0.4 kg TNT contact detonation loading**:
   - When the powerful blast shockwave reflects off the rear face as a tension wave, the three-dimensional fiber bridging network of the EGC layer absorbs the tensile stress, completely blocking fragmentation of the back-face concrete.
4. **Securing interfacial bond reliability through low-shrinkage eco-friendly mix design**:
   - Reduces the chronic drying shrinkage of alkali-activated slag via fly ash replacement, keeping integrity with the concrete substrate stably maintained for over 56 days.

## Methods

- **EGC mix proportioning and material composition (Section 2.1 & Tables 1~2)**:
  - Binder: GGBS (slag) + fly ash (FA) (FA/GGBS = 0.5~1.0).
  - Alkaline activator: liquid sodium silicate ($\text{Na}_2\text{O}\cdot n\text{SiO}_2$) + sodium hydroxide (NaOH), alkaline equivalent $\text{Na}_2\text{O} = 6.0\%$, modulus $M_s = 1.2$.
  - Three fiber types ($V_f = 3.0\ \text{vol.}\%$):
    1. `EGC-MPP`: modified polypropylene fiber ($d_f = 25\ \mu\text{m}$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}$, $\sigma_f = 900\ \text{MPa}$).
    2. `EGC-PVA`: Kuraray PVA fiber ($d_f = 39\ \mu\text{m}$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}$, $\sigma_f = 1600\ \text{MPa}$).
    3. `EGC-PE`: high-strength PE fiber ($d_f = 20\ \mu\text{m}$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}$, $\sigma_f = 3000\ \text{MPa}$).
- **Mechanical and explosion testing (Section 2.2~2.3 & Figs. 2~7)**:
  - Static mechanics: uniaxial direct tension (0.5 mm/min, LVDT gauge), four-point flexural test (2 mm/min, span 150 mm).
  - Full-scale gas explosion: a $1.0 \times 1.0\ \text{m}$ masonry wall mounted over the opening of a $1.2 \times 1.2 \times 1.2\ \text{m}$ blast-resistant chamber, followed by methane-air mixed gas explosion.
  - Contact explosion test: $0.4\ \text{kg}$ of cylindrical TNT charge placed directly on the top surface of a $500 \times 500 \times 100\ \text{mm}$ concrete slab and detonated.

### 2. Interpretation of blast shockwave dissipation and crack-bridging mechanisms
- **Ultra-high-strain-rate energy absorption**: as the high-speed blast wave penetrates the slab, the hundreds of millions of high-tensile-strength PE fibers inside the PE-EGC layer undergo micro-slippage pull-out and plastic deformation, converting the kinetic energy of the shockwave into fracture energy and dissipating it.
- **Reflected tension-wave interception**: the EGC layer strengthened on the concrete rear face distributes the tensile stress wave uniformly over the full cross-section, preventing localized tensile fracture and inducing a global flexural resistance mechanism of the structure.

- **Overall conclusion**: this landmark origin paper in the fields of protective civil engineering and geopolymer composite applications demonstrated that eco-friendly strain-hardening geopolymer (EGC) overlay strengthening dramatically improves the blast resistance of structures and blocks back-face fragment ejection by more than 90 % even under 0.4 kg TNT contact explosion.
