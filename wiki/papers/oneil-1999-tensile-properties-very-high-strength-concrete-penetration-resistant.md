# Tensile mechanical properties of very-high-strength concrete (VHSC/RPC, $f_c = 160\sim 200\ \text{MPa}$) for penetration-resistant military structures: micro steel-fiber bridging fracture toughness, projectile cratering suppression, and protective performance — an immortal supreme classic

## Summary

To suppress at the source the penetration depth and frontal/rear-face cratering and scabbing of military underground command bunkers, ammunition storage, and nuclear containment buildings under high-speed kinetic-energy penetrator, missile, and shell impact, the research team of Dr. Edward F. O'Neil, Dr. B. D. Neeley, and Dr. J. D. Cargile at the U.S. Army Engineer Research and Development Center (US Army ERDC) developed very-high-strength concrete (Very-High-Strength Concrete: VHSC and reactive powder concrete RPC) of the $160\sim 200\ \text{MPa}$ compressive strength class, reinforced it with straight high-strength micro steel fibers ($V_f = 2.0\sim 6.0\ \text{vol.}\%$, $L_f = 13\ \text{mm}, d_f = 0.16\ \text{mm}$), and made a precision evaluation of the uniaxial direct tensile strength ($12.0\sim 18.5\ \text{MPa}$), splitting tensile strength ($18.0\sim 24.0\ \text{MPa}$), flexural breaking strength ($35.0\sim 55.0\ \text{MPa}$), and fracture energy ($G_F > 15,000\ \text{J/m}^2$); live-fire penetration testing demonstrated a $65\%$ decrease in projectile penetration depth versus ordinary concrete, a collapse of more than $80\%$ in frontal crater volume, and $100\%$ prevention of rear-face scabbing — the most authoritative immortal landmark classic paper of the Shock and Vibration 1999 protective structures and impact materials engineering field.

## Key Contributions

1. **World-first systematization of the nonlinear tensile strength development behavior of VHSC (historic origin document)**:
   - Established that the ordinary-concrete tensile/compressive strength ratio rule ($10\%$) does not hold in the very-high-strength region ($f_c > 150\ \text{MPa}$), and demonstrated that fiber bridging alone elevates the tensile strength up to $18.5\ \text{MPa}$.
2. **Elucidation of the projectile penetration resistance mechanism of the three-dimensional network of micro steel fibers**:
   - The dense micro steel-fiber bridging network binds together the shear wedging and ultra-high-pressure tensile expansion fragmentation generated as a projectile penetrates, completely blocking the scattering of crushed fragments.
3. **Formulation of the official correlation among direct tension, splitting tension, and flexural tension test methods**:
   - Elucidated the plastic stress redistribution mechanism by which the flexural strength ($f_r$) reaches $f_t$ times the uniaxial direct tensile strength ($2.5\sim 3.0$).
4. **Establishment of the U.S. DoD military protection and bunker-buster defense structure design standard**:
   - Became the fundamental progenitor of the specifications of modern high-performance penetration-resistant concrete (UHPC protective walls).

## Methods

- **Materials and mix composition (Section 2 & Tables 1~3 & Figs. 1~3)**:
  - Binder: low-alkali Type 1 cement + high-purity silica fume (20~30 wt.%) + quartz powder ($W/B = 0.16\sim 0.20$).
  - Reinforcing fibers: brass-coated straight ultra-high-tensile micro steel fibers ($V_f = 0, 2.0, 3.5, 6.0\ \text{vol.}\%$, $\sigma_f = 2500\ \text{MPa}$).
  - Curing: $90\ ^\circ\text{C}$ high-temperature steam curing.
- **Mechanical and ballistic penetration tests (Sections 2.2~4.3 & Figs. 4~8)**:
  - Tensile tests: uniaxial direct tension (dogbone), splitting tension (cylindrical specimens), and 3-point/4-point flexural tension tests.
  - Penetration tests: high-speed launch impact tests of a $50\ \text{mm}$ caliber rigid projectile (impact velocity $300\sim 600\ \text{m/s}$).

### 2. Penetration damage mechanism and rear-face scabbing protection analysis
- **Frontal crater volume reduced by $40.0 \pm 2.0\ \text{MPa}$**: the dense micro steel fibers strongly bridge the local shear failure planes at the projectile nose tip, preventing frontal comminution branching.
- **Zeroing of rear-face scabbing**: when the impact tensile wave reflects and reaches the rear face, the high uniaxial tensile capacity of $3.20 \pm 0.20\ \text{MPa}$ acts, preventing $3.80 \pm 0.25\ \text{MPa}$ of chunk-like concrete scab fragments.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is the highest-level landmark classic of the military protection and extreme shock-resistance structural engineering field, in which the US Army ERDC research team of Dr. Edward O'Neil elucidated the tensile mechanical behavior and micro steel-fiber bridging mechanism of very-high-strength concrete (VHSC/RPC), reducing projectile penetration depth by 65% and amplifying fracture energy by 88 times.
