# Pine Resin-Based Bio-Adhesives and Concrete Surface Properties

## Summary

A study that quantitatively characterizes concrete surface properties by formwork type and evaluates the bonding strength of a bio-adhesive (pine resin + wax), positioned to replace fossil-fuel-based epoxy, on concrete (corresponding author Bang Yeon Lee of Chonnam National University, jointly with the Biology and Mechanical Engineering departments). As empirical research filling a literature gap on bio-adhesives for concrete, it confirmed that bond strength increases with wax fraction and surface roughness, demonstrating feasibility as an eco-friendly construction material. The motivation is the attachment of structural monitoring sensors to concrete surfaces.

## Key Contributions

1. Formwork type (FN plain plywood / FC surface-coated plywood / FS sandpaper-applied) governs surface roughness (Ra 7.83~22.46 μm), while showing no significant influence on chemical composition (EDS) or zeta potential (-9.31~-11.26 mV) — cross-checked with four techniques: visual inspection, SEM/EDS, white-light interferometry, and electrophoretic light scattering.
2. Two bond-strength design guidelines: (a) higher wax fraction is advantageous (lower melting point → longer working time and greater fluidity → larger contact area); (b) greater surface roughness is advantageous.
3. A strong positive correlation between bond strength and surface roughness (r = 0.82~0.98) — quantitative support that the bonding mechanism is mechanical anchorage.
4. An initial quantitative data set in the literature on bio-adhesives dedicated to concrete.

## Methods

Concrete: Type I OPC, w/c 0.41 by mass, Jumunjin standard sand + coarse aggregate up to 13 mm, fc′ = 46.3 MPa (ASTM C39). Adhesive: pine resin (melting point 70~80 °C) + blended wax (unrefined beeswax 60 % + paraffin 30 % + ceresin 10 %, melting point ~62 °C), mixed at P1W9/P3W7/P5W5 mass ratios. Surface analysis: SEM/EDS (15 kV), WSI (Ra/Rq/Rt extraction), zeta potential (Helmholtz–Smoluchowski). Bond test: two 40 mm cubes bonded over a Ø15 mm area (area fixed with punched film) using molten adhesive above 100 °C (within 10 s), then pulled in tension on a UTM at 20 kN under displacement control at 0.4 mm/min after 1 h elapsed.

## Results

| Item | FN | FC | FS |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ra (μm) | 14.14±1.18 | 7.83±0.47 | 22.46±5.64 |
| Rq (μm) | 17.87±1.12 | 10.18±0.53 | 27.16±6.19 |
| Rt (μm) | 144.57±14.96 | 92.43±6.47 | 155.45±7.14 |
| ζ (mV) | −9.31±1.27 | −11.26±1.16 | −10.33±0.78 |

Bond strength (MPa): FC-P1W9 1.41 / FC-P3W7 1.18 / FC-P5W5 0.99 / FN-P3W7 1.50 / FS-P3W7 2.34.

- Wax effect: wax 90 % (P1W9) was highest. Increasing resin fraction gave FC-P3W7 -16.7 % and FC-P5W5 -30.2 %.
- Roughness effect: with the same adhesive (P3W7), +27 % for FN and +98 % for FS relative to FC.
- Bond strengths corresponded to 21~30 % of the estimated concrete tensile strength (10 % of compressive strength, i.e., 4.6 MPa).
- EDS principal components Ca·Si (C-S-H); the mean zeta potential of -10.30 mV lies inside the existing literature range (+17~-22 mV) — formwork type does not affect electrochemical surface properties.
