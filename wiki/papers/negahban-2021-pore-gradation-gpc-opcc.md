# Effect of Pore Gradation on Portland Cement and Geopolymer Concretes

## Summary

A study by the Sanjayan group at Swinburne University that quantitatively compared ten microstructural characteristics of ambient-cured geopolymer concrete (GPC) and OPC concrete (OPCC) using UPV, permeable pore volume (AVPV), color-image-based pore detection (PDIA), and liquid nitrogen porosimetry (LNP), and established their correlation with strength and durability. GPC has a larger pore volume than OPCC (8~32 % vs 7~14 %) and a 50~66 % larger BET specific surface area, and an anisotropy was confirmed in which the pore distribution varies stratigraphically between the upper and lower layers of a cross-section.

## Key Contributions

- Integrated analysis of nm–mm-scale pores via a quadruple measurement scheme (UPV·AVPV·PDIA·LNP): PDIA automatically quantifies pores of 10.58 µm and larger with a MATLAB RGB histogram script — eliminating manual error.
- Sealant incorporation effect: adding a 20 % silica fume+GGBFS blend raised the GPC compressive strength 31→58.7 MPa(+90 %) and cut impregnated volume 21→9 %(OPC gained +11 %) — geopolymers are far more sensitive to SCMs.
- L/B sensitivity of GPC: from L/B 0.45→0.50, GPC strength fell -19 % vs OPCC -14 %. Moisture content is even more critical to the geopolymer microstructure.
- Discovery of layer-wise anisotropy: GPC shows decreasing porosity toward lower layers + increasing nano-gel pores (confirmed by LNP), while OPCC is interlayer-homogeneous — the larger the casting thickness, the more GPC quality-deviation control is needed.

## Methods

- Mix: binder:fine aggregate:coarse aggregate fixed at 1:1.5:3.0. GPC uses Class F fly ash+GGBFS, activators Na₂SiO₃(Ms=2)+NaOH 8M(SS/SH=2), L/B 0.45·0.50. OPCC control of 3 mixes plus a type incorporating 20 % silica fume/GGBFS.
- Curing: OPCC water curing (21~23 °C), GPC unsealed ambient curing (RH 50±4 %).
- Tests: compression ASTM C39(∅100×200 mm), UPV ASTM C597(55 kHz direct method, cross-section divided into 3 layers), PDIA(blue epoxy impregnation followed by 2400 dpi scanning), LNP(BET/BJH, 1~500 nm).

## Results

- Compressive strength: OPCC 39.8~52.0 MPa, GPC 13.5~58.7 MPa. GPC-0(F100 %) at 13.5 MPa reconfirms the need for heat curing.
- UPV: OPCC mean 3.99~4.10 km/s(good) vs GPC 2.53~3.37 km/s(questionable~poor).
- PDIA impregnated pore volume: OPCC 7~12 %, GPC 9~31 %. The PDIA–strength correlation is stronger for GPC(R² 0.985 vs 0.980).
- BET specific surface area: GPC 9.3~32.4 m²/g > OPCC 7.6~16.2 m²/g. BJH pore volume is also larger for GPC.
- Water absorption: GPC 4.82~6.64 %, OPCC 5.19~6.65 % — similar levels.
