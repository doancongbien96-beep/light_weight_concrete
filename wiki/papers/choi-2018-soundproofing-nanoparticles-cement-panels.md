## Summary

To radically improve the soundproofing performance of polymer-resin composites, this 2008 JMST paper by the research team of Prof. Yeon Jun Kang and Prof. Seong Hun Ahn at Seoul National University investigated ABS/CNT nanocomposites produced by injection molding an ABS resin (Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene) mixed with multi-walled carbon nanotubes (MWCNT: ILJIN CM-95, diameter 10–15 nm, length 10–20 µm, purity 95 vol.%) at loadings of 0, 5, 10, and 15 vol.%, quantifying the normal-incidence sound transmission loss (STL) measured in an acoustic impedance tube together with the reduction of the operating noise of mechanical relays. At 3400 Hz, the sound transmission loss of the ABS/CNT 15 vol.% nanocomposite improved by **21.7 % (4.1 dB)** over neat ABS, and when applied to the case of a mechanical relay the average noise level was demonstrated to drop by **1.0–3.3 dB**.

## Key Contributions

- **Measurement of the sound transmission loss (STL) of ABS/CNT nanocomposites and clarification of the soundproofing mechanism**: confirmed that the increase in flexural stiffness (stiffness) of the composite driven by CNT addition dominates the soundproofing performance in the mass-law region.
- **Establishment of a two-microphone transfer-function acoustic measurement system in an impedance tube**: STL quantified across CNT loadings (0, 5, 10, 15 vol.%) in the 1000–6400 Hz band.
- **Demonstration of actual blocking of the operating noise of electromechanical relays (mechanical relays)**: relay switching impact noise reduced significantly, by 1.0–3.3 dB on average.
- **Provision of a design basis for polymer-based ultra-light thin-panel soundproof housings**: applicability verified for noise-shielding structures of home appliances and precision machinery.

## Methods

- Raw materials: ABS polymer resin (LG Chem HF-380, density 1.05 g/cm³), MWCNT (ILJIN CM-95, bulk density 0.1 g/cc, average diameter 10–15 nm, length 10–20 µm, purity 95 vol.%).
- Mixing and molding: melt-mixed at CNT fractions of 0, 5, 10, and 15 vol.% and injection molded at 225 °C.
- Specimen dimensions: disk specimens with a diameter of 29 mm and a thickness of 3.0 mm.
- Tests: B&K 4206 acoustic impedance tube (frequency range 1000–6400 Hz, transfer-function method); relay operating noise measured as sound pressure level (SPL) with a half-inch microphone.
