## Summary

A 2021 CBM paper by Dr. Jong-Il Choi and Prof. Bang Yeon Lee (Chonnam National University)
with Prof. Mo Li (University of Michigan) that reinforced an eco-friendly calcium-based cement-
free AAS matrix (GGBFS 90 % + powdered slaked lime Ca(OH)₂ 10 wt %, W/B = 0.30) with
ultra-high-molecular-weight PE fibers of three aspect ratios (AR-L 1500, AR-M 750, AR-S 375;
V_f = 1.75 vol %), air- (A) and water-cured (W), characterizing single-fiber pullout interfacial
friction ($\tau_0$), fiber-bridging complementary energy (G_b), direct tension, and crack-width
control. The water-cured long-fiber specimen (AR-L-W; L_f = 18 mm, d_f = 12 µm) achieved
interfacial friction **1.437 MPa**, complementary energy **119 J/m²**, direct tensile strength
**9.00 MPa**, fine crack width **58.2 µm**, and ~**48 MPa** compression — a **3.86x** tensile-
strength leap over short fibers (AR-S, 2.33 MPa).

## Key Contributions

- **Interfacial bridging mechanisms quantified across PE fiber aspect ratios**: AR-L frictional
  bond (1.437 MPa) proved 76 % higher than AR-M (0.818 MPa).
- **Bridging complementary energy linked to PSH hardening performance**: G_b of the AR-L
  composite was 1.3x AR-M and 4.8x AR-S — inducing stable multiple steady-state cracking.
- **Numerically predicted σ–δ bridge stress–opening models verified**: fiber-bridging analysis
  reflecting pullout behavior predicted strengths (AR-L 10.13 MPa, AR-M 6.47 MPa, AR-S 3.23 MPa)
  consistent with experiments.
- **Fine-crack suppression demonstrated**: mean crack width of 58.2 µm in AR-L — 61 % finer
  than AR-S (150.7 µm).

## Methods

- Binder: GGBFS (specific surface 4320 cm²/g, SiO₂ 34.95 %, CaO 42.88 %, Al₂O₃ 13.58 %) 90 % +
  powdered Ca(OH)₂ 10 %, W/B = 0.30, SP 0.35 %, VMA 1 %, defoamer 0.1 %.
- PE fiber specs (volume fraction V_f = 1.75 % fixed):
  - **AR-L**: length L_f = 18 mm, diameter d_f = 12 µm, AR 1500, tensile strength 2700 MPa,
    modulus 88 GPa, density 0.97 g/cm³.
  - **AR-M**: length L_f = 12 mm, diameter d_f = 16 µm, AR 750, tensile strength 3030 MPa,
    modulus 112 GPa, density 0.97 g/cm³.
  - **AR-S**: length L_f = 12 mm, diameter d_f = 32 µm, AR 375, tensile strength 3030 MPa,
    modulus 112 GPa, density 0.97 g/cm³.
- Tests: single-fiber pullout (0.1 mm/min), dog-bone direct tension (gauge length 80 mm,
  0.2 mm/min), 28 d compressive strength (air A and water W curing).
