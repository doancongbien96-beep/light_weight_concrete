## Summary

A landmark RILEM HPFRCC5 paper in which Dr. W.P. Boshoff and Prof. G.P.A.G. van Zijl of Stellenbosch University precisely investigated the tensile creep deformation that a highly ductile strain-hardening cement composite (PVA-SHCC, uniaxial tensile strength $f_t = 2.65 MPa$, ultimate tensile strain capacity $3.8 \%$) exhibits under long-term sustained tensile loading after initial crack formation, the time-dependent widening of individual fine cracks, the single PVA fiber pull-out creep mechanism, and the risk of creep rupture, at sustained load levels of 30, 50, 70, and 80 % of the ultimate tensile strength. Below a sustained stress level of $70 \%$ the crack width converges stably at $50 \mu m$ or less, whereas above $80 \%$ (2.11 MPa) progressive fiber interfacial debonding slip was demonstrated to cause creep rupture.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishment of a tensile creep test protocol for cracked SHCC**: long-term displacement measurement by sustained stress ratio ($30, 50, 70, 80 \%$) after introduction of pre-cracks.
- **Elucidation of individual crack-width growth kinetics under sustained load**: under creep loading the existing fine crack widths widen progressively without formation of new cracks.
- **Identification of the single-fiber pull-out creep mechanism**: viscoelastic stretching of PVA fibers and time-dependent decay of the frictional stress at the fiber-matrix interface.
- **Proposal of long-term limit-state design criteria for SHCC structures**: establishment of a design guideline limiting sustained tensile stress to $60 \%$ or less of the ultimate strength.

## Methods
- Mix: PVA-SHCC (OPC + fly ash, $W/B = 0.40$, Kuraray REC15 PVA fiber $V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 39 µm, length 12 mm).
- Specimen: $160 \times 60 \times 30 mm$ dog-bone specimens.
- Test conditions: sustained loading applied after introduction of pre-cracks ($\epsilon = 0.5 \%$):
  - 30 % ($0.81 MPa$)
  - 50 % ($1.32 MPa$)
  - 70 % ($1.87 MPa$)
  - 80 % ($2.11 MPa$)
- Instrumentation: digital optical microscope crack-width measurement, continuous LVDT strain measurement, temperature $23 \pm 1 °C$, RH 65 %.
