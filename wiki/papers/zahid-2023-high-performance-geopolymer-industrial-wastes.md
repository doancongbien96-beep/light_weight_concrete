## Summary
A research team led by Dr. M. Zahid at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS (UTP), Malaysia investigated the hydration-polymerization reaction rate, densification of the composite C-A-S-H / N-A-S-H gel, elastic modulus, and the strength development characteristics at 3 d/7 d/28 d in compression/splitting tension/flexure of high-performance eco-friendly geopolymer concrete (HPGC) that completely excludes cement (100 %) through optimal proportioning of a ternary inorganic binder of industrial by-products: Class F fly ash (FA), ground granulated blast-furnace slag (GGBS), and silica fume (SF). By combining a ternary mix of GGBS 50 % + FA 40 % + SF 10 % with a liquid sodium silicate activator, a 28 d compressive strength of **84.5 MPa**, splitting tensile strength of **6.8 MPa**, and flexural strength of **11.2 MPa** were achieved under room-temperature ($23 °C$) curing, and embodied carbon was reduced by **78 %**.

## Key Contributions
- **Completion of a ternary industrial by-product (FA-GGBS-SF) ambient-curing high-performance geopolymer mix**: 84.5 MPa high strength developed at room temperature without heat curing.
- **Elucidation of the ultrafine nano-filling and dissolution-reaction accelerating effects of silica fume**: optimizing the $SiO_2/Al_2O_3$ molar ratio to 4.2 densified the pores of the three-dimensional aluminosilicate network.
- **Substantial improvement in early-age strength (3 d, 7 d)**: securing a 3 d compressive strength of 48.0 MPa enabled early formwork removal and shortened construction schedules.
- **Demonstration of ultra-high durability and low-carbon sustainability**: total porosity below 8.5 % achieved and carbon emissions reduced by 80 % compared with cement.

## Methods
- Binder: GGBS (50 wt %) + Class F fly ash (40 wt %) + silica fume (10 wt %), $W/B = 0.30$.
- Activator: $NaOH 12 M$ aqueous solution + liquid sodium silicate ($Ms = 2.0$, $Na_2O = 6.0 wt \%$).
- Aggregates: granite coarse aggregate (maximum size 12.5 mm) and natural river sand.
- Testing: ASTM C39 compressive strength ($100 \times 200 mm$ cylinders), ASTM C496 splitting tensile, ASTM C78 flexural test, XRD and SEM-EDS.
