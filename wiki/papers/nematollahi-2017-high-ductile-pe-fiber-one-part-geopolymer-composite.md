# Micromechanics-Based Ultra-High-Ductility Tensile Strain-Hardening ($\varepsilon_u = 4.80\%$) and $80\%$ Carbon-Emission Reduction Mechanisms of a Just-Add-Water Ambient-Cured One-Part PE Fiber-Reinforced Geopolymer Composite (One-Part PE-EGC)

## Summary

To overcome the field-constructability limits of conventional geopolymers — the handling of hazardous corrosive liquid alkaline solutions — and their mandatory high-temperature heat-curing requirement, and to realize ambient-temperature dry premix casting in which only water is added on site like ordinary cement, the team of **Prof. Jay Sanjayan and Dr. Behzad Nematollahi at Swinburne University of Technology, Australia, and Prof. En-Hua Yang at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore** developed a **one-part ultra-high-ductile composite (One-Part PE-EGC: 28-day ambient compressive strength $38.5\ \text{MPa}$)** that reinforces a one-part geopolymer matrix — made by dry-blending solid sodium silicate powder (solid activator) with fly ash and slag powders and adding only water, cured at ambient temperature ($23\ ^\circ\text{C}$) — with hydrophobic ultra-high-molecular-weight PE microfibers ($V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$); through single-fiber pullout tests, matrix fracture toughness measurement, and uniaxial direct tension they demonstrated **elimination of chemical adhesion at the fiber–one-part matrix interface and induction of pure sliding friction ($\tau_0 = 1.95\ \text{MPa}$), securing a crack-bridging complementary energy ratio of $J_b'/J_{tip} = 5.2$, a uniaxial ultimate tensile strain capacity of $4.80\%$, an ultimate tensile strength of $5.20\ \text{MPa}$, more than 50 extremely fine multiple cracks ($w \le 28\ \mu\text{m}$), and an $80\%$ reduction in $\text{CO}_2$ emissions versus OPC**, in this most authoritative landmark original paper of Ceramics International 2017.

## Key Contributions

1. **World-first development of an ambient-cured just-add-water one-part geopolymer EGC**:
   - Completely eliminated the hazards of liquid alkali handling and the heat-curing constraint, opening a commercialization path for field casting in ready-mix and dry-mix bag forms.
2. **Elucidation of the ideal sliding mechanics ($\tau_0 = 1.95\ \text{MPa}$) of the PE fiber–one-part geopolymer interface**:
   - The rupture risk arising from the excessive chemical bonding of PVA fibers was blocked at its source by hydrophobic PE fibers, achieving a fiber rupture rate of $0\%$ and a uniaxial strain capacity of $4.80\%$.
3. **Perfect demonstration of satisfying the crack-bridging complementary energy margin ($J_b'/J_{tip} = 5.2$)**:
   - The moderate matrix fracture toughness of the one-part geopolymer ($J_{tip} = 12.0\ \text{J/m}^2$) combined with the bridging energy of high-strength PE fibers to complete saturated multiple cracking.
4. **An $80\%$ carbon-footprint cut and establishment of an eco-friendly construction paradigm**:
   - A zero-cement ($0\%$) binder together with an ambient-curing process offers a complete solution for carbon-neutral construction of high-ductility structural members.

## Methods

- **One-part mix design and materials (Section 2 & Tables 1~3 & Figs. 1~3)**:
  - Precursor powders: Class F fly ash ($60\ \text{wt.}\%$) + GGBS slag ($40\ \text{wt.}\%$).
  - Solid activator: anhydrous sodium silicate solid powder ($M_s = 2.0$, 8.0 wt.% relative to the binder).
  - Water/binder ratio: $W/B = 0.32$ (mixed with ordinary tap water).
  - Reinforcing fiber: Toyobo ultra-high-molecular-weight PE fiber ($V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}, d_f = 24\ \mu\text{m}, \sigma_f = 3000\ \text{MPa}$).
- **Micromechanics and tensile mechanics evaluation (Sections 2.2~3.3 & Figs. 4~7)**:
  - Uniaxial tension: dogbone specimens (28-day ambient curing, displacement control at 0.5 mm/min).
  - Single-fiber pullout: precise derivation of the interfacial frictional shear stress ($\tau_0$) and slip coefficient ($\beta$).

### 2. Crack-bridging composition curve and eco-efficiency interpretation
- **Complete fiber pullout mechanism**: Owing to the low surface energy of PE fibers, fibers slip out $100\%$ without rupture, dissipating large slip-bridging deformation energy.
- **Completeness of ambient one-part $\text{CO}_2$ reduction**: With heat-curing energy unnecessary and the transport risk of liquid activators removed, whole-process $\text{CO}_2$ emissions are held to $185\ \text{kg CO}_2\text{e/m}^3$.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is the highest-level landmark original paper in the field of carbon-neutral high-performance construction materials engineering, in which the research teams of Prof. Jay Sanjayan and Prof. En-Hua Yang combined a one-part geopolymer cured at ambient temperature by simply adding water with high-strength PE fibers to demonstrate an ultra-eco-friendly high-ductility composite exhibiting a uniaxial tensile strain capacity of 4.80%.
