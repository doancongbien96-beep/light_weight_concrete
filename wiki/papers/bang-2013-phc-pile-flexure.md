## Summary
A Journal of the Korea Concrete Institute paper from Prof. Yun Yong Kim's research team at Chungnam National University. To improve the weak flexural capacity of the pretensioned, centrifugally cast high-strength concrete piles (PHC piles, $\phi 450-70t$, specified compressive strength 80 MPa, effective prestress 4.0 MPa) widely used for abutment and pier foundations, flexural tests and $P-M$ interaction diagram analysis were carried out on internally infilled composite (ICP) piles that integrate, into the hollow core of the pile, 40 MPa internal infill concrete together with four longitudinal deformed rebars (D16, $f_y$ = 400 MPa) and spiral transverse reinforcement (D10@200). The pure flexural tests showed mean maximum flexural moments of **227.1 kN·m** and **216.9 kN·m** for Type A and Type B ICP piles respectively, a substantial improvement in flexural capacity of **48 %** and **41 %** over the bare PHC pile (153.4 kN·m), and demonstrated high safety factors of **4.6** and **4.4** against the allowable flexural moment (48.4 kN·m).

## Key Contributions
- **Demonstration of the internally infilled composite reinforcement (ICP) structural system for hollow PHC piles**: 40 MPa infill concrete and a 4-D16 longitudinal rebar arrangement improved the flexural capacity by 48 %.
- **Clarification of the effect of horizontal loading direction (Type A vs Type B)**: the difference in maximum flexural moment due to rebar placement angle was negligible at about 4.5 % (227.1 kN·m vs 216.9 kN·m), proving direction-independence during field construction.
- **Realization of a ductile failure mode through crushing of the compression-zone concrete**: compared with the bare PHC pile, finely dispersed flexural cracks and a greatly enhanced energy absorption capacity at failure.
- **Verification of the validity of section-by-section $P-M$ interaction prediction**: the measured test value (227.1 kN·m) agreed with the computed nominal flexural strength (193.6 kN·m), falling on the safe side by a factor of 1.17.

## Methods
- Specimen dimensions: $\phi 450-70t$ precast PHC pile (outer diameter 450 mm, wall thickness 70 mm, length 3.0 m, concrete compressive strength 80 MPa, effective prestress 4.0 MPa).
- Internal infill material: specified strength 40 MPa, slump 120 mm, maximum coarse aggregate size 20 mm.
- Reinforcing steel: 4-D16 longitudinal bars ($f_y$ = 400 MPa), D10 spiral transverse reinforcement D10@200.
- Test conditions: simply supported four-point bending test (total span 2.4 m, pure flexural region 0.8 m, 1000 kN hydraulic actuator).
