## Summary
This study micromechanically formulated and experimentally validated the direct tensile behavior of high-strength, ultra-high-ductility ECC (PE-ECC) using high-strength ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene (UHMWPE) fibers. A single-fiber bridging model reflecting the extremely high aspect ratio of PE fibers ($L_f/d_f \approx 1000$) and the frictional slip characteristics of the hydrophobic interface was constructed, and the tensile stress-strain response was predicted as a function of matrix fracture toughness ($K_m$) and fiber volume fraction ($V_f = 1.0 \sim 2.0 \%$). The optimally designed PE-ECC realized extreme ductility and energy absorption performance, reaching a compressive strength of 75 MPa, an ultimate tensile strength of 10.5 MPa, and a tensile strain capacity of 8.5 %.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishment of a slip-hardening interface model specific to PE fibers**: formulation of the shear stress transfer law $\tau(s) = \tau_0 (1 + \beta s/d_f)$ reflecting changes in matrix abrasion and fiber dilation pressure with increasing pullout displacement.
- **Verification of the strain-hardening saturation criteria for ultra-high ductility**: achievement of an energy performance index $J_b'/J_{tip} > 8.0$ and a strength performance index $\sigma_0/\sigma_{fc} > 2.0$, demonstrating a tensile strain $> 8.0 \%$.
- **Precise prediction of multiple microcrack spacing and crack width**: visualization of steady-state crack propagation in which the crack width remains constant at $25 \sim 35 \mu m$.

## Methods
- **Material mix**: cement + silica fume (15 %) + blast-furnace slag (40 %), $W/B = 0.20$, PE fiber (diameter 12 µm, length 12 mm, tensile strength 3000 MPa, $V_f = 2.0 \%$).
- **Test items**: uniaxial dogbone direct tension test, single-fiber pullout test, crack counting, and digital optical microscopy measurement.

## Results
- PE-ECC ($V_f = 2.0 \%$): initial cracking strength $4.5 MPa$, ultimate tensile strength $10.5 MPa$, tensile strain capacity $8.5 \%$, average crack width $28 \mu m$, 75 or more cracks.
- Realized a design space satisfying high strength and ultra-high ductility simultaneously, demonstrating applicability to seismic and impact-energy-absorbing members.
