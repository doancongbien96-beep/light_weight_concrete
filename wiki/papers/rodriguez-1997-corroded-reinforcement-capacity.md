## Summary

A monumental origin paper by Dr. J. Rodriguez of the Eduardo Torroja Institute for Construction Science (IETcc) in Spain that elucidated — through accelerated electrochemical corrosion experiments and analytical models — the section loss, bond deterioration, longitudinal cover cracking/spalling of concrete, and reductions in ultimate flexural/compressive load capacity of full-scale reinforced concrete beam and column members whose reinforcement had corroded due to chloride ingress and carbonation. It quantified the mechanisms of residual flexural capacity and deflection ductility degradation as a function of the reinforcement section corrosion rate ($0 \sim 25 \%$) and established a field safety diagnosis and assessment model.

## Key Contributions

- **Formulation of the four great load-capacity degradation factors of corroded RC members**: (1) reduction of the effective cross-sectional area of tensile reinforcement, (2) embrittlement of reinforcement by localized pitting, (3) loss of bond at the steel-concrete interface, (4) reduction of the compression-zone section following cover concrete spalling.
- **Establishment of a two-stage correlation model between reinforcement corrosion rate ($x$) and bond strength ($\tau_b$)**: below a corrosion rate of 2 %, expansion pressure temporarily increases bond, while beyond 4 % bond strength collapses by more than 70 %.
- **Presentation of a nonlinear sectional model for evaluating ultimate flexural and compressive capacity**: derivation of safety diagnosis criteria for corroded states relative to ACI and Eurocode standards.

## Methods

- 36 full-scale RC beams ($150 \times 200 \times 2300 mm$) and column specimens.
- DC-power accelerated electrochemical corrosion (target section-loss rates of 5, 10, 15, 20 %).
- Four-point bending tests, shear tests, concentric compression tests, and precise measurement of pit depths and residual mass.

## Results

- Data on the mechanical performance degradation of beam members as a function of reinforcement corrosion rate:

| Corrosion level | Mean reinforcement section loss (%) | Max pit depth ratio | Ultimate flexural capacity reduction (%) | Deflection-at-failure reduction (%) | Failure mode |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Sound | 0.0 | 1.0 | 0.0 % (baseline 65 kN) | 0.0 % (ductile failure) | Typical flexural yielding |
| Slight corrosion | 4.2 | 1.8 | -6.5 % | -18.2 % | Flexural yielding |
| Moderate corrosion | 11.5 | 2.5 | -22.4 % | -45.0 % | Flexure with bond slip |
| Severe corrosion | 21.8 | 3.8 | -48.5 % | -72.0 % | Early brittle rupture of reinforcement |
