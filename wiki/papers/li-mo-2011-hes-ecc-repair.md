# Material Properties of High-Early-Strength ECC (HES-ECC)

## Summary

Type III Portland cement-based high-early-strength ECC (HES-ECC) was developed for rapid concrete repair, with compression·tension·flexure·drying shrinkage·restrained shrinkage precisely characterized by age (4 h~60 days). It achieved open-to-service strength within 4 h and multi-fine-cracking durability simultaneously. The core strategy is the deliberate introduction of polystyrene beads as artificial flaws to control initial flaw size·distribution.

## Key Contributions

1. Database of simultaneous early-strength–ductility attainment (Table 2): 4 h compressive 23.6 MPa (exceeding the NJDOT 6 h criterion)·tensile strength 3.46 MPa·strain capacity 5.97 %·flexural strength 9.81 MPa. 28-day compressive 55.6 MPa; tensile strain capacity stabilizes at 3.5 % after day 7.
2. Artificial flaw control design: 4 mm diameter polystyrene beads are incorporated as weakly bonded 'artificial flaws' to deliberately control initial flaw size·distribution → increased microcrack density and enhanced ductility during the strain-hardening stage. Fine silica sand (average 110 μm) concurrently lowers matrix toughness.
3. Self-control of crack width: below 60 μm at all ages (10 μm at 4 h), and held at ≤50 μm even under restrained shrinkage. Restrained crack width at 60 days is 1/25 of HES-Concrete. Stresses that crack width is an intrinsic material property determined by fiber bridging characteristics, independent of member size or rebar details.
4. Shrinkage mechanism analysis: the Munich model (surface free energy + disjoining pressure term for RH>40 %) explains HES-ECC drying shrinkage — parameters in Table 3. Free shrinkage is 2× ordinary concrete, but since the tensile strain capacity (3~6 %) is an order of magnitude larger, the cracking potential stays negative.
5. Benefit of low elastic modulus: 13.1 GPa (4 h)~23.8 GPa (60 days), lower than concrete, reducing restrained stress accumulation and delamination risk in the existing concrete.

## Methods

Type III cement (Blaine surface area 540 m²/kg)+silica sand+PVA (RECS15, 1.2 % oil coating) 2 vol %+polystyrene beads. Air curing (field simulation). Tensile coupons 228.6×76.2×12.7 mm (LVDT gauge 101.6 mm), ring test (AASHTO PP-34-99), drying shrinkage with RH controlled 0~93 % via saturated salt solutions.

## Results

- Flexural MOR of 15.08 MPa at 28 days, more than 2× that of concrete with equivalent compressive strength.
- Restrained shrinkage: HES-ECC absorbs deformation through 19~25 fine cracks vs a localized single crack in the control (1250 μm at 60 days).
- Presents literature evidence that at crack widths <100 μm, water permeability and chloride diffusion coefficients are equivalent to the crack-free state.
