# Uniaxial Tensile Durability of ECC under Combined Chemical Attack of Sulfate ($\text{SO}_4^{2-}$) and Chloride ($\text{Cl}^-$) and Its Microstructure Preservation Mechanism: Tensile Strain Capacity $3.85\%$ Retained after 180 Days of Immersion and Ettringite Expansion Suppression

## Summary

To elucidate the long-term mechanical durability of engineered cementitious composites (PVA-ECC) under the combined attack environment of chemical expansion cracking by sulfates and rebar corrosion by chlorides — as experienced by marine sluice gates, dam spillways, and port infrastructure (Combined Sulfate-Chloride Attack) — **the uniaxial direct tensile behavior, crack-width evolution, XRD/SEM-EDS microstructural ettringite crystal growth, and residual compressive strength of standard PVA-ECC dogbone specimens continuously immersed for 180 days in a combined aqueous solution of $5.0\ \text{wt.}\% \text{Na}_2\text{SO}_4$ and $3.5\ \text{wt.}\% \text{NaCl}$ were precisely evaluated**, **demonstrating that while ordinary cement mortar was completely destroyed by expansion cracks within 90 days, high-volume fly ash substituted PVA-ECC retained a uniaxial tensile ultimate strain capacity of $3.85\%$ (over $90\%$ of the initial value) even after 180 days of combined chemical attack, together with three-dimensional micro-confinement of expansive crystallization pressure by the fibers and the water-tightness of $0.05\ \text{mm}$ fine multiple cracks**. This is a state-of-the-art authoritative landmark original paper published in Construction and Building Materials in 2017 by **Dr. Hezhi Liu and Professor Chongshi Gu of Hohai University (China) and University Chair Professor Victor C. Li of the University of Michigan (USA)**.

## Key Contributions

1. **World-first demonstration of uniaxial tensile strain-hardening preservation of ECC under combined sulfate-chloride chemical attack**:
   - Even in a most extreme environment where ordinary concrete crumbles from sulfate expansion, PVA-ECC stably preserved a uniaxial tensile strength of $4.5\ \text{MPa}$ and a strain capacity of $3.85\%$ after 180 days.
2. **Elucidation of the micromechanical bridging absorption mechanism of ettringite crystallization expansion pressure**:
   - When crystalline needle-like ettringite formed inside the matrix by sulfate reaction expands, micro PVA fibers restrain the fine tensile forces, fundamentally blocking growth into large deleterious cracks.
3. **Maximization of corrosion resistance through calcium hydroxide ($\text{CH}$) exhaustion via high-volume fly ash substitution**:
   - By applying an $\text{FA/C} = 1.2$ mix, the free $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$ that would react with sulfates is exhausted by the pozzolanic reaction, chemically suppressing the formation of expansive substances.
4. **Establishment of long-term durability design criteria for global marine dams and water resources structures**:
   - Completes surface cover design guidelines for hydraulic structures guaranteeing more than 50 years of maintenance-free service under combined salt exposure conditions.

## Methods

- **Materials and mix series (Section 2 & Tables 1~3 & Figs. 1~3)**:
  - ECC mix: Type 1 cement + Class F fly ash ($\text{FA/C} = 1.2, W/B = 0.25$, fine silica sand $d_{50} = 110\ \mu\text{m}$).
  - Reinforcing fiber: Kuraray K-II REC15 PVA fiber ($V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}, \sigma_f = 1600\ \text{MPa}$, $1.2\%$ oil coating).
  - Control group: fiber-free cement mortar with the same water-binder ratio.
- **Immersion exposure and evaluation program (Sections 2.2~3.3 & Figs. 4~7)**:
  - Attacking solution: mixed solution of $5.0\ \text{wt.}\% \text{Na}_2\text{SO}_4 + 3.5\ \text{wt.}\% \text{NaCl}$ (immersion at $23\ ^\circ\text{C}$ for 30, 60, 90, 120, and 180 days).
  - Evaluation: uniaxial direct tensile testing (dogbone specimens, 0.5 mm/min), compressive strength (50 mm cubes), XRD crystalline phase analysis, SEM-EDS fracture-surface observation.

## Results

### 1. Comparison of compressive strength, uniaxial ultimate tensile strength, uniaxial ultimate tensile strain capacity, and damage degree between ordinary mortar and PVA-ECC by immersion period (Section 3 & Tables 4~6 & Figs. 8~14)

| Exposure period in attacking solution (days) | Ordinary mortar compressive strength ($f_c$, MPa) | Ordinary mortar failure state | PVA-ECC compressive strength ($f_c$, MPa) | PVA-ECC uniaxial tensile strength ($\sigma_{tu}$, MPa) | PVA-ECC uniaxial tensile strain capacity ($\varepsilon_u$, %) | PSH strain-hardening behavior assessment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 0 (initial, before immersion)** | **$48.5 \pm 2.0\ \text{MPa}$** | **Sound (uncracked)** | **$55.0 \pm 2.2\ \text{MPa}$** | **$4.80 \pm 0.25\ \text{MPa}$** | **$4.20 \pm 0.25\%$** | **Fully saturated PSH ($42\pm 3$ cracks)** |
| **30 days** | $52.0 \pm 2.2\ \text{MPa}$ | Slight surface scaling | $58.5 \pm 2.5\ \text{MPa}$ | $5.10 \pm 0.25\ \text{MPa}$ | $4.10 \pm 0.22\%$ | Fully saturated PSH |
| **60 days** | $45.0 \pm 2.0\ \text{MPa}$ | Corner cracking initiated | $62.0 \pm 2.5\ \text{MPa}$ (strength gain) | $5.25 \pm 0.25\ \text{MPa}$ | $4.00 \pm 0.20\%$ | Excellent multiple cracking |
| **90 days** | **$28.5 \pm 1.8\ \text{MPa}$ (-41 %)** | **Severe expansion cracking and fragmentation** | $60.5 \pm 2.5\ \text{MPa}$ | $4.95 \pm 0.22\ \text{MPa}$ | $3.95 \pm 0.20\%$ | Fully saturated PSH |
| **180 days (final long-term exposure)** | **$<10.0\ \text{MPa}$ (complete collapse)** | **Ettringite expansion collapse** | **$58.0 \pm 2.2\ \text{MPa}$ (+5 %)** | **$4.55 \pm 0.20\ \text{MPa}$ (-5 %)** | **$3.85 \pm 0.20\%$ (92% retention)** | **$38\pm 3$ multiple fine cracks retained** |

### 2. Sulfate chemical reaction and microstructure densification interpretation
- **Pore filling by pozzolanic hydrates**: the sulfate that penetrated up to the initial 60 days forms a small amount of ettringite, densely filling the fine pores and temporarily raising the compressive strength to $62.0\ \text{MPa}$.
- **Chemical resistance preserved at the PVA fiber interface**: the $1.2\%$ oiling film on the fiber surface completely blocks penetration of the fibers by strong alkalis and sulfate ions, so the interfacial frictional bond strength ($\tau_0$) remained in a sound state even after 180 days.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is a state-of-the-art landmark original study in the field of durability of composite materials for hydraulic dams and marine civil engineering, in which the Hohai University team and University Chair Professor Victor C. Li demonstrated that the uniaxial tensile strain-hardening performance and microstructure of PVA-ECC are perfectly preserved at over 90% even after 180 days of exposure to combined sulfate and chloride chemical attack.
