# Taguchi Optimization and Micromechanical Validation of High-Ductility (5.2~6.8 %) PVA-Fiber-Reinforced Metakaolin-Fly Ash Engineered Geopolymer Composites (MFA-EGC)

## Summary

To completely eliminate cement clinker (100 % cementless) and develop sustainable, ductile **Engineered Geopolymer Composites (MFA-EGC)** using industrial byproducts (high-calcium HFA, low-calcium LFA, and metakaolin MK), Professor Li-li Kan at USST and Professor Min Wu at Aarhus University applied a **Taguchi $L_9$ orthogonal experimental design (PVA fiber volume fraction 1.2~2.0 vol.%, MK replacement 0~20 %, curing temperature $20\sim 60\ ^\circ\text{C}$)**. The authors evaluated direct tensile stress-strain responses at 3 d, 7 d, and 28 d, saturated multiple microcracking (average crack width $25\ \mu\text{m}$), three-point notched beam matrix fracture toughness ($J_{tip}$), and single-crack fiber bridging complementary energy ($J_b'$). The Taguchi optimal mixture (PVA 1.7 vol.%, MK 10 %, curing at $40\ ^\circ\text{C}$) achieved exceptional tensile ductility: **$6.80\pm 0.45\%$ at 3 d, $6.40\pm 0.40\%$ at 7 d, and $5.20\pm 0.35\%$ at 28 d**, with a 28 d tensile strength of **$5.85\ \text{MPa}$** and compressive strength of **$43.5\ \text{MPa}$**. Micromechanical analysis yielded an energy performance index **$J_b'/J_{tip} = 3.82 > 1.0$** and a strength performance index **$\sigma_0/\sigma_{cr} = 1.45 > 1.0$**, rigorously satisfying the conditions for steady-state flat cracking and saturated multiple cracking in geopolymer matrices.

## Key Contributions

1. **Taguchi optimization achieving ultra-high tensile ductility (6.8 %)**:
   - Statistically optimized the synergies among fiber content, MK replacement, and curing temperature, securing 6.8 % strain capacity at 3 d and 5.2 % at 28 d.
2. **Rigorous validation of the 2 micromechanics criteria ($J_b'/J_{tip} = 3.82$, $\sigma_0/\sigma_{cr} = 1.45$)**:
   - Formulated pseudo-strain-hardening criteria for EGC matrices via notched beam fracture toughness and single-crack fiber bridging tests.
3. **Control of saturated multiple microcracking with average crack widths of $25\ \mu\text{m}$**:
   - Induced dense microcracking with $2\sim 5\ \text{mm}$ spacing, restricting crack widths to block aggressive ion ingress and facilitate self-healing.
4. **Regulation of geopolymer setting and reactivity via HFA/LFA blending**:
   - Balanced the delayed setting of low-calcium fly ash (LFA) with the rapid reactivity of high-calcium fly ash (HFA) to ensure workability and early strength.

## Methods

- **Materials and mixture proportions (Tables 1–3, mass ratios)**:
  - Precursors: Class F low-calcium fly ash (LFA) + Class C high-calcium fly ash (HFA) (LFA:HFA = 6:4) + metakaolin (MK, calcined at $800\ ^\circ\text{C}$).
  - Activator: $\text{NaOH}$ (99 % purity) + liquid $\text{Na}_2\text{SiO}_3$ ($\text{SiO}_2/\text{Na}_2\text{O} = 3.2$ modulus).
  - Fine aggregates: Silica sand ($d_{50} = 150\ \mu\text{m}$, Sand/Binder = 0.30).
  - Reinforcing fibers: Kuraray Kuralon-II REC-15 PVA fibers (diameter $40\ \mu\text{m}$, length 12 mm, tensile strength 1600 MPa, modulus 41 GPa, 1.2 wt.% oil coating, $V_f = 1.7\ \text{vol.\%}$).
  - Taguchi $L_9(3^3)$ design: Factor A (PVA 1.2, 1.7, 2.0 vol.%), Factor B (MK 0, 10, 20 %), Factor C (curing temperature 20, 40, 60 °C).
- **Testing protocols (Section 2.3 & Figs. 2–4)**:
  - Direct tension: Dogbone specimens (80 mm gauge length, 0.5 mm/min, MTS servo-hydraulic).
  - Matrix fracture toughness: ASTM E399 three-point notched beams ($354\ \text{mm} \times 75\ \text{mm} \times 40\ \text{mm}$, notch depth 30 mm).
  - Single-crack tension: 40 mm gauge length single-crack specimens (yielding peak bridging stress $\sigma_0$ and complementary energy $J_b'$).

### 2. Micromechanics and strain-hardening mechanisms
- **Steady-state flat cracking criterion ($J_b'/J_{tip} > 1$)**: At 28 d, fiber bridging complementary energy was $J_b' = 32.5\ \text{J/m}^2$ and matrix toughness was $J_{tip} = 8.5\ \text{J/m}^2$, providing an energy margin of $J_b'/J_{tip} = 3.82$ that ensured steady flat crack propagation at constant opening width.
- **$\text{Si/Al}$ polycondensation control with metakaolin**: Incorporating 10 % MK densified N-A-S-H 3D network polycondensation, raising compressive strength to 43.5 MPa without rupturing PVA fiber oil coatings and maintaining 5.2 % ductility.

- **Overall conclusion**: This work established the Taguchi optimization framework for fly ash-metakaolin EGC, validating 6.8 % tensile strain-hardening and the two core micromechanical design criteria.
