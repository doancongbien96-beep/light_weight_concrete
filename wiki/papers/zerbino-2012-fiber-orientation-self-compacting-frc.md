## Summary
A landmark paper in which Professor R. Zerbino's research team at the National University of La Plata, Argentina clarified through electromagnetic induction coil nondestructive testing (inductive method) and cut-surface image analysis the flow-induced alignment mechanism by which shear flow during casting of self-compacting concrete affects the three-dimensional spatial orientation angles ($\theta$) and effective orientation coefficient ($\alpha_\theta$) of macro steel fibers (hooked-end, $L_f = 50 mm$, $V_f = 0.5 \sim 1.0 \%$), along with the anisotropy of member flexural post-cracking toughness resulting from it. In the principal direction parallel to the casting flow, a fiber orientation coefficient $\alpha_\theta = 0.78$ was achieved, demonstrating that the flexural residual tensile strength manifests **2.2 times** higher than in the orthogonal direction.

## Key Contributions
- **Fluid dynamics correlation between SCC shear flow fields and steel fiber orientation formulated**: fiber rotation behavior formalized as a function of rheological viscosity and velocity gradients.
- **Precise measurement of fiber orientation coefficients ($\alpha_\theta$) by nondestructive electromagnetic induction coils**: principal fiber direction vectors derived automatically without cutting specimens.
- **Anisotropy envelopes of flexural residual strength ($f_{R1}, f_{R3}$) established by flow direction**: measured $f_{R3} = 8.5 MPa$ parallel versus $f_{R3} = 3.8 MPa$ orthogonal.
- **Guidelines presented for casting positions of real precast slabs and tunnel segments**: design aligning structural principal stress directions with casting flow paths.

## Methods
- Mix: SCFRC (OPC + limestone powder, $W/B = 0.38$, slump flow 680 mm), hooked steel fibers ($L_f = 50 mm, d_f = 0.75 mm, V_f = 0.5, 0.75, 1.0 \%$).
- Casting: single-point injection flow casting at one end of $150 \times 150 \times 1500 mm$ rectangular molds.
- Tests: EN 14651 standard three-point notched flexure (span 500 mm), electromagnetic induction measurement, and cut-section optical microscopy elliptical image analysis.
