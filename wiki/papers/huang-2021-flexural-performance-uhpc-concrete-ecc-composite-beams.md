# Flexural Behavior of Functionally Graded UHPC-NC-ECC Layered Composite Beams with Perforated Steel Plates (PBL): 3.2-Fold Capacity Amplification, 4.5-Fold Energy Dissipation, and $0.03\ \text{mm}$ Crack Width Control

## Summary

To match specific sectional mechanics requirements across beam depth—high compressive stresses at the top flange, low shear stresses in the central web, and high tensile ductility in the bottom flange—Professor Jian-Guo Dai and Bo-Tao Huang at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, together with Professor Shi-Lang Xu at Zhejiang University, developed a functionally graded sandwich composite beam ($1600 \times 150 \times 200\ \text{mm}$). The system couples an upper ultra-high-strength steel fiber UHPC layer ($f_c = 150.0\ \text{MPa}$), a middle conventional concrete (NC: $f_c = 40.0\ \text{MPa}$) web, and a lower PVA-ECC layer ($f_c = 50.0\ \text{MPa}$, uniaxial tensile strain capacity $3.50\%$), integrated via perforated steel plates (PBL shear connectors) with penetrating rebar dowels. Testing under 4-point bending alongside 2D-DIC full-field strain mapping, the authors demonstrated that **compared to conventional RC beams of identical dimensions, the composite beam amplified ultimate flexural capacity by 3.2-fold ($85.0\ \text{kN} \rightarrow 272.0\ \text{kN}$), enhanced plastic energy absorption by 4.5-fold, restricted service-load crack widths below $0.03\ \text{mm}$, and completely prevented interlayer delamination across all three concrete phases**.

## Key Contributions

1. **First implementation of UHPC(compression)-NC(web)-ECC(tension) 3-layer functionally graded composite beams**:
   - Strategically concentrated expensive UHPC and ECC only in extreme compression and tension fibers while utilizing conventional concrete in the neutral core, optimizing structural performance at minimal cost.
2. **Monolithic dowel action across layered interfaces via PBL perforated plates**:
   - Concrete dowel pins cast through the perforated plate holes locked the two inter-layer interfaces, achieving full composite action without slip up to ultimate deflection.
3. **3.2-fold flexural capacity amplification and high displacement ductility ($\mu = 6.50$)**:
   - Formed a robust plastic hinge by combining the compressive bearing of UHPC with tensile strain-hardening in ECC and tensile yielding in the steel plate.
4. **Crack width restriction below $0.03\ \text{mm}$ in the tensile zone**:
   - Developed over 80 saturated microcracks across the bottom ECC layer without cover spalling, shielding rebar from chloride-induced corrosion in harsh environments.

## Methods

- **Specimen design and mix configurations (Section 2 & Tables 1–3 & Figs. 1–5)**:
  - Beam dimensions: $1600 \times 150 \times 200\ \text{mm}$ (span 1350 mm, pure bending zone 450 mm).
  - Layering scheme: Top 50 mm UHPC + Middle 100 mm NC + Bottom 50 mm PVA-ECC.
  - Shear connectors: 4 mm thick Q355B perforated steel plate (PBL hole diameter 25 mm, D10 penetrating rebar).
  - Material properties: UHPC ($f_c = 152.0\ \text{MPa}$, 2% straight steel fibers), ECC ($f_c = 52.0\ \text{MPa}, \varepsilon_u = 3.5\%$, 2% Kuraray REC15 PVA fibers), NC ($f_c = 41.5\ \text{MPa}$).
- **Testing program (Sections 2.3–2.5 & Figs. 6–9)**:
  - 4-point bending under displacement control (1.0 mm/min).
  - Instrumentation: 5 LVDTs, interlayer relative slip gauges, and 2D-DIC strain/crack tracking.

### 2. PBL dowel stress and interfacial composite integrity
- **Zero interlayer shear slip**: Concrete dowels inside PBL holes established 3D mechanical interlock, restricting interlayer relative slip below $0.05\ \text{mm}$.
- **Balanced compression-tension mechanics**: Top UHPC sustained 150 MPa without crushing while bottom ECC and perforated plates absorbed tensile energy up to 3.5 % strain.

- **Overall conclusion**: This work established the structural design and superior flexural mechanics of functionally graded UHPC-NC-ECC composite beams with PBL perforated plates, delivering 3.2-fold load capacity gains, a ductility ratio of 6.50, and 0.03 mm crack control.
