## Summary
A landmark paper published in Cement and Concrete Research by Dr. H. Zhu and Professor Victor Li of the University of Michigan on the complete compressive stress-strain curves, uniaxial direct tensile strain-hardening envelopes, and fracture energy absorption mechanisms of an ultra-high strength ductile composite (HS-ECC) reinforced with ultra-high molecular weight PE fibers (diameter 12 µm, tensile strength 3000 MPa, $V_f = 2.0 \%$) in a densely packed matrix of fine silica fume and ground slag, in order to prevent premature fiber rupture and brittle crushing failure in ultra-high-strength matrices ($f_c > 120 MPa$) and achieve ultra-high strength-ultra-high ductility simultaneously. A 28 d compressive strength of **128.5 MPa**, a compressive failure strain of **0.0115**, a uniaxial direct tensile strength of **13.5 MPa**, a direct tensile strain capacity of **8.5 %**, and a tensile fracture energy of **$920 kJ/m^3$** were achieved.

## Key Contributions
- **Integrated three-dimensional constitutive equations for compression and tension of ultra-high-strength HS-ECC established**: compression toughness coefficient increased by a factor of 4.2 over ordinary high-strength concrete.
- **Slip-hardening bridging at the PE fiber interface ($\beta = 0.85)$ precisely demonstrated**: full slip pullout without fiber rupture induced.
- **Tensile strain capacity of 8.5 % and an ultra-fine multiple crack network demonstrated**: more than 140 fine cracks dispersed within the gauge (mean crack width $18 \mu m$).
- **Slimming design criteria established for lower-story columns of super-tall buildings and seismic braces**: member sections reduced by 45 %.

## Methods
- Matrix: OPC (50 %) + silica fume (20 %) + fine slag (30 %), $W/B = 0.18$, micro silica sand ($d_{50} = 110 \mu m$).
- Fiber: Spectra PE fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 12 µm, length 12 mm).
- Tests: dogbone direct tension test ($0.5 mm/min$, full-field DIC strain), uniaxial compression tests of $100 \times 200 mm$ cylinders (displacement control), single fiber pullout test.
