## Summary
To accelerate the hydration-polymerization reaction rate during ambient-temperature curing of fly ash-slag based alkali-activated geopolymer (FA-GGBS Geopolymer) and to densify the N-A-S-H / C-A-S-H gel nanostructure, this latest 2024 study elucidated the compressive/flexural strength development, nano-pore size distribution, and degree of polymerization ($Si/Al$ ratio) of hybrid nano-geopolymer composites doped with nano-silica (Nano-SiO2, 10–20 nm) and nano-alumina (Nano-Al2O3, 20–30 nm), singly and in hybrid combinations (0.5–2.0 wt %), through XRD, FTIR, 29Si/27Al MAS-NMR, and MIP analysis. It demonstrated that introducing the hybrid nanoparticles (1.0 wt % NS + 0.5 wt % NA) surged the 28 d compressive strength to **78.5 MPa**, a 42 % increase, and cut the harmful capillary porosity above 100 nm by **65 %**.

## Key Contributions
- **Elucidation of the 1D-2D nano-silica/nano-alumina hybrid nucleation synergy**: silica promotes silicate chain cross-linking while alumina reinforces $AlO_4$ tetrahedral coordination, densifying the 3D aluminosilicate network.
- **Rapid early-age (3 d, 7 d) compressive strength gain of 55 % or more**: a heat-curing level of early polymerization degree achieved under ambient curing conditions.
- **Demonstration of nano-pore refinement (Pore Refinement) through mercury intrusion porosimetry (MIP)**: total porosity decreased from 18.5 % to 9.2 %, less than half.
- **Establishment of ultra-durable low-carbon binder technology**: chloride diffusion coefficient blocked by 70 %.

## Methods
- Binder: F-class fly ash 50 % + GGBS 50 %, combined activation of sodium silicate solution and $NaOH 10 M$ ($W/B = 0.35$).
- Nanoparticles: Nano-SiO2 (NS) and Nano-Al2O3 (NA), dispersed by ultrasonic probe and then mixed into the activator solution (total 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 wt %).
- Testing: ASTM C109 compressive strength (50 mm cubes), three-point flexural test, 29Si/27Al MAS-NMR, MIP porosity.
