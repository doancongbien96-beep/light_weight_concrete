## Summary
This 2015 CBM paper by a Chonnam National University research team of Dr. Choi Jeong-Il, Prof. Song Ki-Il, and Prof. Bang Yeon Lee investigated an eco-friendly low-viscosity, high-ductility binder-free composite that reinforces a cement-free alkali-activated slag (AAS: GGBS 89.5 % + powdered Ca(OH)₂ 7.5 % + Na₂SO₄ 3.0 %) matrix with 0.6 %-oil-coated short-cut PVA fibers (length 8 mm, diameter 40 µm, tensile strength 1600 MPa, elastic modulus 41 GPa, $V_f$ = 1.0 and 1.3 vol %) while controlling the water-to-binder ratio ($W/B$ = 0.40 and 0.45), covering the rheological plastic viscosity, mini V-funnel flow time, slump flow, uniaxial direct tensile behavior, and the PSH micromechanics energy index ($J'_b/J_{tip}$). The M40-1.3 mix ($W/B$ = 0.40, $V_f$ = 1.3 vol %) achieved a 28-day compressive strength of **18.3 MPa**, a direct tensile strength of **2.26 MPa**, a uniaxial tensile strain capacity of **2.38 %**, and a fiber-bridging complementary energy index $J'_b/J_{tip}$ = **1.67**, demonstrating stable multiple fine-cracking strain-hardening behavior.

## Key Contributions
- **Rheological evaluation of the low-viscosity alkali-activated slag (AAS) composite and establishment of slump-flow correlations**: demonstrated the correlation between V-funnel flow time and plastic viscosity, securing a rheology window for uniform fiber dispersion without fiber bundling.
- **Quantification of interfacial bond characteristics as functions of W/B and PVA fiber fraction**: the M40 mix exhibited a frictional bond stress $\tau_0$ = 1.27 MPa and chemical bond strength $G_d$ = 1.13 J/m² versus M45 ($\tau_0$ = 1.14 MPa, $G_d$ = 0.71 J/m²), indicating formation of a denser matrix interface.
- **Demonstration of the PSH micromechanics strain-hardening index $J'_b/J_{tip}$ > 1.0**: through $J'_b/J_{tip}$ = 1.67 of the M40-1.3 mix, theoretically clarified stable strain-hardening and multiple cracking mechanisms.
- **Verification of the field castability of eco-friendly binder-free high-ductility composites**: maximized constructability through powder-type activators and optimized rheological design.

## Methods
- Binder: GGBS (Blaine fineness 4204 cm²/g, SiO₂ 34.7 %, CaO 44.6 %) 89.5 % + powdered Ca(OH)₂ 7.5 % + powdered Na₂SO₄ 3.0 %.
- Mix variables:
  - **M40-1.0**: $W/B$ = 0.40, HRWRA 0.35 %, PVA $V_f$ = 1.0 %
  - **M40-1.3**: $W/B$ = 0.40, HRWRA 0.36 %, PVA $V_f$ = 1.3 %
  - **M45-1.0**: $W/B$ = 0.45, HRWRA 0.27 %, PVA $V_f$ = 1.0 %
  - **M45-1.3**: $W/B$ = 0.45, HRWRA 0.30 %, PVA $V_f$ = 1.3 %
- Tests: mini slump-flow and mini V-funnel tests, 28-day compressive strength (50 mm cubes), dogbone direct tension test (0.2 mm/min), single-fiber pullout test.
