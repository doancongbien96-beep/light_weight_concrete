## Summary
To realize a high-efficiency photo-thermal conversion and thermal energy storage building envelope that directly converts and stores solar energy as heat, this paper investigated the thermal conductivity, photo-thermal conversion efficiency ($\eta_{opt}$), latent heat capacity, and thermal cycling durability of a novel form-stable photo-thermal conversion phase change material (LA/g-C3N4/Graphene ss-PCM) prepared by vacuum impregnating lauric acid (Lauric Acid, LA, phase change temperature $43.5 °C$, latent heat $\Delta H = 182 J/g$) into a hybrid nano aerogel support composed of two-dimensional graphene (thermally conductive skeleton) and graphitic carbon nitride (g-C3N4, photocatalytic light absorber). Through the 2D hybrid nano network, the thermal conductivity was increased 12-fold to **2.65 W/(m·K)**, and a photo-thermal conversion efficiency of **92.5 %** and an effective latent heat of **145.2 J/g** were achieved.

## Key Contributions
- **Synthesis of a g-C3N4/graphene three-dimensional hierarchical nanoporous photo-thermal aerogel**: a light absorptance of 98 % secured across the full visible-to-near-infrared band (300 ~ 1100 nm).
- **Twelve-fold maximization of thermal conductivity ($0.22 \rightarrow 2.65 W/(m\cdot K)$)**: phase change thermal storage charging time reduced by 80 %.
- **Photo-thermal energy conversion-storage efficiency of 92.5 % achieved**: rapid temperature rise to $60 °C$ within 5 min of solar irradiation and completion of latent heat charging.
- **Zero leakage over 1000 solid-liquid phase transition cycles**: demonstration of complete chemical and thermodynamic reversibility.

## Methods
- Synthesis: preparation of a g-C3N4/Graphene composite hydrogel via hydrothermal synthesis $\rightarrow$ freeze-drying into an aerogel $\rightarrow$ vacuum impregnation with molten lauric acid (LA loading of 80 wt %).
- Testing: Xe lamp solar-simulated photo-thermal test ($100 mW/cm^2$), Hot Disk TPS thermal conductivity analyzer, DSC latent heat, TGA thermal stability.

## Results
- Thermodynamic and photo-thermal conversion property data of the nanocomposite PCMs:
