# Development of an ambient-temperature-curing ($23\ ^\circ\text{C}$) stimulated one-part ultra-high-ductile slag composite (One-Part OP-EGC) and its EPS micro-bead hybrid mechanism: tensile strain capacity $\varepsilon_u = 7.20\%$, lightweighting ($1680\ \text{kg/m}^3$), and carbon reduction of $72\%$

## Summary

To resolve the delayed early-age strength development, dependence on elevated thermal curing ($60\sim 80\ ^\circ\text{C}$), and heavy unit-weight problems suffered by existing binder-free alkali-activated geopolymer composites, this study developed **a lightweight one-part ultra-high-ductile composite cured in water at ambient temperature (Lightweight One-Part OP-EGC: 28-day compressive strength $42.5\ \text{MPa}$), which fuses solid alkali stimulators, small/medium expanded polystyrene (EPS) micro beads ($d = 0.5\sim 1.5\ \text{mm}$), and ultrahigh-molecular-weight PE micro fibers ($V_f = 1.5\ \text{vol.}\%$) into a GGBS $100\%$-based one-part dry binder**, and through precise evaluation by uniaxial direct tensile testing, hydration kinetics, DIC multiple cracking, and oven-dry density demonstrated **a uniaxial direct tensile strain capacity $7.20\%$, ultimate tensile strength $6.15\ \text{MPa}$, lightweighting to an oven-dry density $1680\ \text{kg/m}^3$, more than 55 ultra-fine multiple cracks ($w \le 22\ \mu\text{m}$), and a carbon emission cut $72\%$, driven under ambient underwater curing ($23\ ^\circ\text{C}$) by the matrix-toughness-relaxing effect of the EPS beads ($J_{tip} \le 12.5\ \text{J/m}^2$)** — the latest 2026 landmark original paper in Construction and Building Materials by the research team of **Prof. Bang Yeon Lee (corresponding author) and Prof. Jeong-Il Choi of Chonnam National University**.

## Key Contributions

1. **World-first demonstration of ultra-high ductility ($7.20\%$) in an ambient-temperature ($23\ ^\circ\text{C}$) cured one-part slag composite**:
   - Achieves both 28-day compressive strength $42.5\ \text{MPa}$ and uniaxial tensile strain capacity $7.20\%$ with on-site water curing alone, without any separate thermal curing chamber.
2. **Elucidation of the artificial relaxation mechanism of matrix fracture toughness ($J_{tip}$) by EPS micro beads**:
   - Ultra-lightweight EPS beads act as artificial flaws and stress buffers when tensile cracks form, dramatically raising the complementary energy ratio ($J_b'/J_{tip} \ge 4.8$).
3. **Unit-weight lightweighting ($1680\ \text{kg/m}^3$) and enhanced insulation performance**:
   - Reduces structural self-weight by $30\%$ relative to normal concrete ($2400\ \text{kg/m}^3$), maximizing the seismic design and constructability of high-rise buildings.
4. **Establishment of the latest 2026 standard for eco-friendly carbon-neutral building materials on a zero-cement ($0\%$) basis**:
   - Quantitatively proves that life-cycle assessment (LCA) greenhouse gas emissions are cut $72\%$ compared with conventional OPC-ECC.

## Methods

- **Materials and mix design (Section 2 & Tables 1~3 & Figs. 1~3)**:
  - Binder: ground granulated blast furnace slag powder (GGBS $100\%$, fineness $4400\ \text{cm}^2/\text{g}$).
  - Solid stimulator: powdered sodium silicate + anhydrous sodium hydroxide ($W/B = 0.35$).
  - Aggregate/lightweight filler: fine silica sand ($d_{50} = 120\ \mu\text{m}$) + small EPS beads (SE: $0.5\sim 0.9\ \text{mm}$) / medium EPS beads (ME: $1.0\sim 1.5\ \text{mm}$).
  - Reinforcing fiber: ultrahigh-molecular-weight PE micro fiber ($V_f = 1.5\ \text{vol.}\%$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}, d_f = 24\ \mu\text{m}, \sigma_f = 3000\ \text{MPa}$).
- **Mechanical and hydration evaluation program (Sections 2.2~3.3 & Figs. 4~7)**:
  - Curing: ambient air curing (A) vs. ambient water curing (W, $23\pm 2\ ^\circ\text{C}$).
  - Uniaxial tension: dogbone specimens ($80\ \text{mm}$ gauge length, 0.5 mm/min displacement control).
  - Compressive strength: 50 mm cube compressive strength and oven-dry density measurements.

### 2. Interpretation of water-curing stimulation and the EPS flaw synergy
- **Accelerated dissolution under ambient water curing**: under water curing the ionic dissociation of the solid alkali stimulator increases $45\%$, accelerating the formation of C-A-S-H hydrates from slag particles.
- **Ideal dispersion of the small EPS beads**: micro beads with diameter $0.5\sim 0.9\ \text{mm}$ uniformly lower the matrix fracture toughness $J_{tip}$, maximizing the crack density.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is the highest-level landmark original paper in the field of eco-friendly, high-ductility construction materials engineering (2026), in which Prof. Bang Yeon Lee's research team at Chonnam National University combined a blast furnace slag one-part geopolymer with fine EPS beads under ambient water curing to simultaneously achieve a uniaxial tensile strain capacity of 7.20% and a 30% reduction in self-weight.
