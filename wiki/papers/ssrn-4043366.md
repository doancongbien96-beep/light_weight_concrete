# Experimental investigation on low density foamed concrete blended with nano-RHA and nano-GGBS as cement replacement

## Abstract from PDF

The integration of supplementary cementitious materials in the LDFC matrix improves the concrete features. The CO 2 emission has increased in the environment due to the production of cement and improper disposal of residual wastes like Ground granulated blast furnace slag (GGBS) , and Rice husk ash (RHA) causes air pollution and landfill problems. To reduce such problems, the GGBS and RHA have been incorporated as c ementitious additive in LDFC matrix. The objective of this experimental research is to study the outcomes of various included percentages of Nano -RHA and Nano -GGBS on compressive strength, split tensile strength and flexural strength up to 28 days on LDFC samples. The experimental outcomes represents , the 28 days mechanical strength increased with increasing Nano-GGBS and Nano-RHA percentages and 10% replacements of both the materials shows good agreement. The present research mainly highlights on the potential of Nano-GGBS and Nano-RHA as an efficient supplementary cementitious material which achieves the aspects of sustainable and economical concrete. Keyword : Low density foamed concrete (LDFC), Nano-GGBS, Nano-RHA, mechanical characterization, morpholo gy.
