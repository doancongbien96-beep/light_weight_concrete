# Uniaxial Compressive Constitutive Behaviour of High-Performance Ferrocement (HPF): Mechanisms Amplifying Compressive Strength ($75.0\ \text{MPa}$) and Ultimate Strain ($0.0085$) 3.5-Fold through Lateral Confinement of Multi-Layer Wire Mesh (2–8 Layers)

## Summary

To minimize self-weight and maximize seismic/impact load resistance in thin-plate structures with three-dimensional curved surfaces such as domes, arches, and shells, **Prof. P. Rathish Kumar and Prof. C. B. K. Rao's research team at the National Institute of Technology Warangal (NIT Warangal), India fabricated 144 high-performance ferrocement (HPF: High-Performance Ferrocement) hollow cylindrical specimens laterally confined with 2–8 layers of fine welded wire mesh (Welded Wire Mesh, $V_r = 0.5\sim 3.2\ \text{vol.}\%$) embedded in a high-strength slag cement-silica fume matrix (28-day compressive strength $52.0\ \text{MPa}$)**, precisely measured the full ascending and descending branches of the stress-strain ($\sigma-\varepsilon$) curve through displacement-controlled uniaxial compression testing, and established **a comprehensive nonlinear compression constitutive model ($R^2 \ge 0.96$) in which reinforcement with 8 mesh layers amplifies compressive strength from $52.0 \rightarrow 75.0\ \text{MPa}$ ($+45\%$ rise) and ultimate compressive strain reaches $0.0024 \rightarrow 0.0085$ (3.5× ductility amplification)** — the landmark origin paper of the Materials and Structures 2006 archive.

## Key Contributions

1. **Completion of a compression constitutive model for high-performance ferrocement based on large-scale data from 144 specimens**:
   - Established an integrated stress-strain numerical model of the form $\frac{\sigma}{f_{cu}} = \frac{A(\varepsilon/\varepsilon_{cu})}{1 + B(\varepsilon/\varepsilon_{cu}) + C(\varepsilon/\varepsilon_{cu})^2}$, providing a standard constitutive model for nonlinear finite element analysis.
2. **Quantification of the three-dimensional lateral confinement mechanism of multi-layer wire mesh**:
   - Demonstrated that raising the number of mesh layers from $2 \rightarrow 8$ increases the volumetric confinement ratio, suppressing lateral expansion of the internal matrix and amplifying fracture energy 4.8-fold.
3. **Experimental demonstration of suppressed premature buckling and delamination in hollow-section thin-plate structures**:
   - Showed that dense mesh arrangement prevents local concentration of compressive cracks and blocks surface spalling, inducing uniform multiple fine compressive-shear failure.
4. **Optimization of a low-water-binder, blast-furnace-slag high-performance mortar mix**:
   - Developed an ultra-low-permeability matrix combining slag cement with 10 % silica fume, securing self-healing capacity and water tightness simultaneously.

## Methods

- **Materials and HPF mix design (Sections 2~3 & Tables 1~3)**:
  - Binder: Portland slag cement (PSC) + silica fume (SF/B = 0.10).
  - Aggregate: silica sand ($d_{50} = 300\ \mu\text{m}$, S/B = 1.0, W/B = 0.28, naphthalene-based high-range water reducer).
  - Wire mesh: welded wire mesh (Welded Wire Mesh, wire diameter $d_w = 0.90\ \text{mm}$, mesh spacing $12.5 \times 12.5\ \text{mm}$, tensile strength $f_y = 480\ \text{MPa}$).
  - Five mesh-layer levels: 0 layers (Plain), 2 layers, 4 layers, 6 layers, 8 layers ($V_r = 0.0, 0.8, 1.6, 2.4, 3.2\ \text{vol.}\%$).
- **Compression test program (Sections 3.2~3.4 & Figs. 1~4)**:
  - Specimen geometry: hollow cylindrical specimens with outer diameter $\phi 150\ \text{mm}$, inner diameter $\phi 100\ \text{mm}$, and height $300\ \text{mm}$ (144 total).
  - Loading: 1000 kN universal testing machine (0.01 mm/s displacement control).
  - Instrumentation: three-directional LVDT displacement transducers over the full specimen length plus strain gauges.

### 2. Interpretation of continuous lateral confinement stress and energy absorption mechanisms
- **Circumferential tensile resistance of the wire mesh**: when the mortar core dilates by the Poisson effect under compressive load, the multi-layer wire mesh applies an immediate circumferential confinement stress ($f_r = 2 f_y A_s / s d$), suppressing propagation of shear slip planes.
- **Formation of a ductile descending curve**: even after the peak stress, the mesh prevents crushing collapse of the core and induces a gradual stress descent, thereby absorbing 4.8 times more nonlinear plastic energy.

- **Overall conclusion**: through large-scale testing of 144 specimens, this paper completed the uniaxial compression constitutive model of high-performance ferrocement and demonstrated a 45 % strength increase and 3.5-fold strain capacity amplification via mesh lateral confinement — a landmark origin paper in the fields of thin-plate shell structures and concrete mechanics.
