# Comparative 1:1 Study of Cementitious ECC versus Cementless EGC Incorporating Metakaolin (MK 20~80 %) and 1.6 Tesla Magnetized Water: Mechanical Strength (60.3 MPa) and Capillary Sorptivity

## Summary

To replace high-carbon traditional concrete and maximize sustainable infrastructure performance, Professor Mostafa M. Keshta and colleagues at Mansoura University and Zagazig University conducted a 1:1 comparative study between cementitious ductile composites (**ECC: Engineered Cementitious Composites**, cement-slag based) and cementless geopolymer composites (**EGC: Engineered Geopolymer Composites**, fly ash-slag based). The authors formulated 14 mixture series (7 ECC, 7 EGC, reinforced with PVA fibers at $V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.\%}$) incorporating **metakaolin (MK 20 %, 40 %, 60 %, 80 % replacement) and 1.6 Tesla magnetized water (MW, 150 circulation cycles)** as mixing water. Slump workability, compressive strength at 7 d, 28 d, and 90 d, 28 d flexural strength (ASTM C348 three-point bending), water absorption (ASTM C642), and capillary sorptivity were measured. Using magnetized water refined water molecular clusters, accelerating binder wetting and dissolution to **elevate 28 d compressive strength by 14.8 % in ECC ($52.5 \rightarrow 60.3\ \text{MPa}$) and 18.2 % in EGC ($44.5 \rightarrow 52.6\ \text{MPa}$)**. In 20 % metakaolin mixes with magnetized water (MK20-MW), pozzolanic and geopolymeric polycondensation peaked, delivering maximum flexural strengths of **$12.8\ \text{MPa}$ for ECC** (compressive strength $64.5\ \text{MPa}$) and **$10.5\ \text{MPa}$ for EGC** (compressive strength $56.2\ \text{MPa}$), while reducing capillary sorptivity by up to 32 %.

## Key Contributions

1. **Complete 1:1 comparative mapping of cementitious ECC vs cementless EGC with MK and magnetized water**:
   - Established systematic correlations between mechanical strength, flexural performance, and water sorptivity across 14 mixtures under identical PVA fiber content (2.0 vol.%).
2. **Demonstration of 15~18 % compressive strength enhancement via 1.6 Tesla magnetized water (150 cycles)**:
   - Proved that magnetic treatment disrupts hydrogen-bonded water clusters, expanding effective reaction surfaces and accelerating early and long-term hydration in both ECC and EGC.
3. **Attainment of peak flexural strength of 12.8 MPa (ECC) and 10.5 MPa (EGC) with 20 % metakaolin (MK20)**:
   - Amorphous aluminosilicate supply densified C-A-S-H and N-A-S-H 3D gel networks, maximizing matrix toughness and flexural resistance.
4. **Verification of over 30 % reduction in capillary sorptivity and pore refinement**:
   - Confirmed pore structure refinement via ASTM C642 absorption and ASTM C1585 sorptivity tests, blocking aggressive ion penetration pathways.

## Methods

- **Materials and mixture design (Tables 1–3, 14 mixtures)**:
  - Binders: OPC CEM I 42.5N, ground granulated blast-furnace slag (GGBFS, Blaine $4200\ \text{cm}^2/\text{g}$), Class F fly ash, metakaolin (MK, $\text{SiO}_2+\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 > 90\%$).
  - Alkaline activators (for EGC): $12\ \text{M}$ $\text{NaOH}$ solution + liquid $\text{Na}_2\text{SiO}_3$.
  - Reinforcing fibers: Kuraray PVA fibers (diameter $39\ \mu\text{m}$, length 12 mm, tensile strength 1600 MPa, modulus 41 GPa, $V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.\%}$).
  - Mixing water: Tap water (TW) vs 1.6 Tesla magnetized water (MW, 150 circulation cycles).
  - 14 mixtures across two groups:
    - ECC Group (7 series): Control-TW, Control-MW, MK20-TW, MK20-MW, MK40-TW, MK60-TW, MK80-TW.
    - EGC Group (7 series): Control-TW, Control-MW, MK20-TW, MK20-MW, MK40-TW, MK60-TW, MK80-TW.
- **Testing protocols (Section 2 & Figs. 2–4)**:
  - Magnetized water generation: 1.6 Tesla permanent magnet pipeline closed-loop circulation pump system (150 cycles).
  - Compressive strength: ASTM C109 ($50\ \text{mm}$ cubes, water cured at 7, 28, 90 d).
  - Flexural strength: ASTM C348 three-point bending ($40\ \text{mm} \times 40\ \text{mm} \times 160\ \text{mm}$ prisms).
  - Durability: ASTM C642 water absorption and ASTM C1585 capillary sorptivity.

### 2. Magnetized water activation and metakaolin reaction mechanisms
- **Surface tension reduction via magnetic treatment**: Water exposed to 1.6 Tesla fields broke into smaller clusters, reducing surface tension by 12 % and accelerating dissolution and early hydration kinetics in both cement and geopolymer gels.
- **Microstructural threshold of metakaolin dosage**: While 20 % MK supplied optimal reactive $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$ to densify C-A-S-H/N-A-S-H matrices, dosages above 40 % increased paste viscosity excessively and left unreacted particles that degraded mechanical strengths.

- **Overall conclusion**: This 2025 comparative study demonstrated that combining 20 % metakaolin with 1.6 Tesla magnetized water elevates compressive and flexural strengths while decreasing sorptivity in both cementitious ECC (64.5 MPa) and geopolymer EGC (56.2 MPa).
