## Summary
A paper by Professor Victor Li's research team at the University of Michigan clarifying the high-temperature interfacial shear bond strength, thermal expansion compatibility, and fire-resistant insulation behavior of a steel-ECC composite member in which a fire-resistive ductile composite (FR-ECC, density $1250 kg/m^3$, thermal conductivity $0.22 W/(m\cdot K)$) combining ultra-lightweight porous cenospheres with heat-resistant fibers was bonded onto structural steel surfaces using epoxy/inorganic hybrid adhesives, in order to overcome the fire vulnerability of structural steel (yield strength plunging by 50 % upon reaching the critical temperature of $550 °C$) and prevent detachment failure of fireproof coverings. With an FR-ECC covering thickness of 25 mm applied, the steel temperature was kept **below $420 °C$** under the two-hour standard fire curve and interfacial delamination was blocked 100 % completely.

## Key Contributions
- **Integral composite system of steel FR-ECC fireproof covering developed**: spray-applied traditional fireproof coatings' impact spalling and crack detachment problems solved at the source.
- **Interfacial shear bond retention mechanism under high-temperature exposure clarified**: the high tensile ductility of FR-ECC ($\epsilon_u = 3.5 \%$) absorbs thermal stress differentials, preventing debonding.
- **Two-hour standard fire test (ISO 834) certification passed**: internal steel temperature fully blocked below the $550 °C$ limit.
- **Integrated design criteria for steel structure strengthening plus fireproofing presented**: construction period and costs cut by 35 %.

## Methods
- FR-ECC mix: low-heat cement + cenospheres (40 vol %) + PVA/heat-resistant fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$), density $1250 kg/m^3$.
- Bonding agent: high-temperature heat-resistant silane-modified epoxy adhesive.
- Tests: double shear bonding tests of steel-ECC joints (room temperature and at $200$ and $400 °C$), ISO 834 standard time-temperature fire resistance tests.

## Results
- Interfacial bond strength and insulation data of steel-FR-ECC joints by heating condition:
