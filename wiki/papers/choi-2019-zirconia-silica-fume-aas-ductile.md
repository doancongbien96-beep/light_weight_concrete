# Tensile Strain-Hardening, $13.5\ \text{MPa}$ High Tensile Strength, and $7.5\%$ Ultra-High-Ductile Behavior of Alkali-Activated Slag Composites with Added Zirconia Silica Fume

## Summary

A leading landmark origin paper of ultra-high-performance composite materials from Prof. Bang Yeon Lee's research team at Chonnam National University, which systematically characterized, as functions of the water-to-binder ratio (W/B = 0.21, 0.25, 0.30), the density, 28-day compressive strength, JSCE dogbone uniaxial tensile stress-strain response, ultimate tensile strength ($7.8\sim 13.5\ \text{MPa}$), tensile strain capacity ($4.8\sim 7.5\%$), multiple fine-crack widths ($w < 30\ \mu\text{m}$), and SEM/EDS microstructure of eco-friendly alkali-activated slag (AAS) composites that exclude Portland cement clinker entirely, manufactured by replacing 5 wt.% of the binder with ultrafine zirconia silica fume (ZSF: $\text{SiO}_2$ 94.51 %, $\text{ZrO}_2$ 1.51 %, specific surface area $22.0\ \text{m}^2/\text{g}$, mean particle size $0.15\ \mu\text{m}$), which excels in nano-pozzolanic and particle-filling effects, and incorporating powdered combined activators ($\text{Ca(OH)}_2 + \text{Na}_2\text{SO}_4$), a defoamer, and ultra-high-strength polyethylene (PE) fibers ($L_f = 18\ \text{mm}, d_f = 12\ \mu\text{m}$, tensile strength 3000 MPa, elastic modulus 88 GPa, $V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.\%}$) into the matrix. The study showed that the W21-S5 mix containing 5 % ZSF achieved, thanks to dense particle packing and pozzolanic C-A-S-H gel formation, ultra-high strengths of **$73.5\ \text{MPa}$** in compression, an increase of 18.8 % over the unreinforced control, together with a uniaxial tensile strength of **$13.5\ \text{MPa}$**. In addition, the W/B = 0.30 mix (W30-S5) exhibited, through optimization of interfacial bridging friction, an ultimate tensile strain capacity of **$7.5\ \%$**, a mean crack width of **$30\ \mu\text{m}$**, and fully saturated multiple microcracking (Saturated Multiple Microcracking) with more than 50 cracks, demonstrating the world's highest level of ductility for structural composite materials.

## Key Contributions

1. **Achievement of 73.5 MPa compressive strength and 13.5 MPa tensile strength through 5 % zirconia silica fume incorporation**:
   - Through the ultrafine filling effect and secondary pozzolanic reaction of high-purity ZSF containing trace amounts of $\text{ZrO}_2$, defects in the AAS matrix were minimized, realizing a top-level direct tensile strength of 13.5 MPa.
2. **World-leading uniaxial tensile strain capacity of $7.5\%$ and ultrafine crack-width control ($30\ \mu\text{m}$)**:
   - Realized a phenomenal tensile strain capacity reaching 7.5 % in the W/B = 0.30 mix while strictly controlling the crack width at or below $30\ \mu\text{m}$ throughout the entire deformation process, securing self-healing capability and complete blocking of harmful ion ingress.
3. **Source-level removal of harmful macro air voids (entrapped air) through defoamer application**:
   - Suppressed the error between measured and theoretical densities to within 0.5 %, completing microstructural homogeneity and maximizing the fiber-matrix interfacial bond strength.
4. **Establishment of a strength-ductility trade-off map versus W/B (0.21–0.30)**:
   - Established the governing mechanical mechanism whereby lower W/B (0.21) densifies the matrix and exhibits ultra-high strength (13.5 MPa), while higher W/B (0.30) optimizes the interfacial frictional stress and exhibits ultra-high ductility (7.5 %).

## Methods

- **Raw materials and AAS composite mix design (Tables 1–2, binder mass ratios)**:
  - Primary binder: ground granulated blast-furnace slag (GGBS, $\text{CaO}$ 40.4 %, $\text{SiO}_2$ 30.6 %, $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$ 13.8 %, specific surface area $4300\ \text{cm}^2/\text{g}$).
  - Functional additive: zirconia silica fume (ZSF, $\text{SiO}_2$ 94.51 %, $\text{ZrO}_2$ 1.51 %, specific surface area $22.0\ \text{m}^2/\text{g}$, mean particle size $0.15\ \mu\text{m}$).
  - Powdered alkali activators: $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$ ($7.1\sim 7.5\ \text{wt.\%}$ of binder) + $\text{Na}_2\text{SO}_4$ ($2.9\sim 3.0\ \text{wt.\%}$ of binder).
  - Admixtures: polycarboxylate superplasticizer (HRWRA $0.7\sim 3.0\ \text{wt.\%}$), non-silicone-based defoamer (Defoamer 0.1 wt.%).
  - Reinforcing fiber: ultra-high-molecular-weight PE fiber ($L_f = 18\ \text{mm}, d_f = 12\ \mu\text{m}$, tensile strength 3000 MPa, elastic modulus 88 GPa, $V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.\%}$).
  - Four mix groups: W21-S0 (ZSF 0 %, W/B 0.21), W21-S5 (ZSF 5 %, W/B 0.21), W25-S5 (ZSF 5 %, W/B 0.25), W30-S5 (ZSF 5 %, W/B 0.30).
- **Test equipment and methods (Figures 1–3)**:
  - Particle size analysis: Beckman Coulter LS230 laser diffraction particle size analyzer.
  - Compressive strength and density: ASTM C109 ($50\ \text{mm}$ cube specimens, 28-day air curing).
  - Uniaxial tensile test: JSCE dogbone specimens ($330\ \text{mm} \times 30\ \text{mm} \times 13\ \text{mm}$, gauge length 80 mm, servo testing machine at $0.2\ \text{mm/min}$, dual LVDT).
  - Microstructure: FE-SEM (accelerating voltage 20 kV, probe current 71.5 nA) and EDS elemental mapping.

### 2. Interpretation of the microstructure and strain-hardening fracture mechanism

- **Nano-pozzolanic synergy of ZSF**: the active silica ($\text{SiO}_2$) and zirconia ($\text{ZrO}_2$) nanoparticles of the ZSF combine with $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ ions from the slag to form an ultra-dense C-A-S-H gel, perfectly filling the voids of the fiber-matrix interfacial transition zone (ITZ).
- **Saturated multiple microcracking**: in the W30-S5 mix, smooth frictional pull-out without fiber surface damage was maintained, with more than 60 fine cracks densely distributed and exhibiting a strain capacity of $7.5\%$.

- **Overall conclusion**: this paper by Prof. Bang Yeon Lee's research team at Chonnam National University is a landmark origin paper marking the pinnacle of next-generation eco-friendly ultra-high-toughness composite materials, simultaneously realizing 73.5 MPa compressive strength, 13.5 MPa high tensile strength, and 7.5 % ultra-high ductility by fusing zirconia silica fume into a cement-free slag binder.
