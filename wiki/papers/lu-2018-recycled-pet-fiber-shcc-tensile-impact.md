# Static tensile ductility and modified Charpy impact energy absorption mechanisms of recycled PET fiber / PVA hybrid Strain Hardening Cementitious Composites (SHCC)

## Summary

To replace expensive commercial PVA fibers and achieve resource circularity for waste plastics, **recycled polyethylene terephthalate (PET) fibers and PVA/PET hybrid ($V_f = 1\sim 2\ \text{vol.}\%$) Strain Hardening Cementitious Composites (SHCC)** were developed, and static strain-hardening ductility and dynamic impact energy absorption capacity were comparatively evaluated through uniaxial static direct tensile testing and the **Modified Charpy Impact Test**, while a single-fiber dynamic pullout-based numerical model of impact energy absorption was established — the landmark 2018 original paper in Construction and Building Materials by the team of Prof. Jing Yu and Prof. Christopher K.Y. Leung of the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) together with Cong Lu of Southeast University. The results showed that under static tension the elongation ($1.2\%$) of PET-SHCC with its low interfacial bond was lower than that of PVA-SHCC ($3.5\%$), yet it was established that **under dynamic impact loading, owing to long slip-pullout distances, the impact absorbed energy of 1 vol.% PET-SHCC (0.702 J) was on par with PVA-SHCC (0.697 J), while applying the 1:1 hybrid (1% PVA + 1% PET) delivered the highest impact resistance of 1.422 J**.

## Key Contributions

1. **First quantification of the dynamic impact energy absorption mechanism of recycled PET fibers**:
   - Unlike PVA fibers, whose strong interfacial bond causes premature fiber rupture under impact, PET fibers possessing moderate frictional bond dissipate impact-wave energy with high efficiency through sliding pullout across the entire embedded length.
2. **Simultaneous maximization of static ductility and dynamic impact resistance via PVA-PET hybrid reinforcement**:
   - Through a 1 vol.% PVA (crack dispersion) + 1 vol.% PET (impact-pullout dissipation) hybrid, a high impact energy absorption of 1.422 J was achieved, on par with 2 vol.% pure PVA-SHCC.
3. **Construction and validation of a micromechanics theoretical model of modified Charpy impact energy absorption**:
   - Development of an analytical model that predicts the impact absorbed energy of the specimen cross-section with $98\%$ accuracy by combining the single-fiber pullout friction coefficient $\tau$ with a fiber orientation integral.
4. **Presentation of practical guidelines for low-carbon eco-SHCC**:
   - Establishment of a sustainable mix design method that cuts commercial PVA fiber usage by $50\%$ while fully preserving impact protection performance.

## Methods

- **Materials and fiber properties (Section 2.1 & Tables 1~2)**:
  - Matrix: OPC + fly ash (ultra-high FA/C = 3.0 content) + microsilica sand + SP.
  - PVA fibers: Kuraray K-II REC15 ($d_f = 40\ \mu\text{m}$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}$, $\sigma_f = 1600\ \text{MPa}$, $E_f = 40\ \text{GPa}$).
  - Recycled PET fibers: extruded filament recycled from waste PET bottles ($d_f = 37.5\ \mu\text{m}$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}$, $\sigma_f = 850\ \text{MPa}$, $E_f = 14\ \text{GPa}$).
- **Mix series (Section 2.2 & Table 3)**:
  - `PVA1%`, `PVA2%` (PVA alone).
  - `PET1%`, `PET2%` (recycled PET alone).
  - `Hybrid (1% PVA + 1% PET)`.
- **Test methods (Section 2.3 & Fig. 2)**:
  - Static uniaxial direct tensile test: dogbone specimens ($80 \times 30 \times 13\ \text{mm}$ gauge section, 0.5 mm/min).
  - Modified Charpy impact test: small prismatic specimens of $10 \times 10 \times 55\ \text{mm}$, drop pendulum impact machine (impact velocity 2.9 m/s), with an angular encoder for precise measurement of fracture energy (J).

### 2. Interpretation of the mismatch between static ductility and dynamic impact resistance
- **Trade-off between interfacial shear bond strength and impact failure**: Under static conditions, PVA fibers with strong chemical bonds efficiently induce multiple cracking and deliver high strain capacity, but under high-speed impact ($\dot{\varepsilon} > 10\ \text{s}^{-1}$) the interfacial bond becomes even more strengthened, so PVA fibers fail to pull out and rupture prematurely.
- **High-speed frictional energy dissipation of PET fibers**: Hydrophobic PET fibers slip out over long distances without fiber rupture even under impact, maximizing the frictional heat energy $\int \tau \pi d_f s ds$ and fully attenuating the impact wave.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper establishes that, despite its limits in static ductility, recycled PET fiber from waste PET bottles exhibits excellent impact energy absorption performance under dynamic impact loading that can fully substitute for expensive PVA fiber, and that the PVA-PET hybrid simultaneously satisfies eco-friendliness and impact resistance — a landmark original paper.
