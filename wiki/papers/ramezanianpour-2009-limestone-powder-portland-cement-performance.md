# Mechanisms of Compressive Strength ($52.5\ \text{MPa}$), Surface Electrical Resistivity, and Chloride Penetration Resistance of Portland Limestone Cement (PLC) by Limestone Powder Replacement Ratio ($5\sim 20\%$)

## Summary

A landmark 2009 Cement and Concrete Composites source paper by the research team of **Prof. A. A. Ramezanianpour of Amirkabir University of Technology, Iran**, which took Portland limestone cement (PLC) produced by intergrinding finely ground limestone powder (LP: Limestone Powder, $0, 5, 10, 15, 20\ \text{wt.}\%$) with cement — to cut clinker calcination energy and reduce carbon dioxide emissions in the cement industry — and systematically evaluated **compressive strength up to 7, 28, 90, and 180 days at three water-cement ratios (W/C = 0.40, 0.45, 0.50), 4-point Wenner Probe surface electrical resistivity, ASTM C1202 rapid chloride ion permeability (RCPT), and capillary absorption rate**, demonstrating **excellent durability: at $10\ \text{wt.}\%$ limestone powder replacement, the micro-filler effect and hydration acceleration secured a 28-day compressive strength of $52.5\ \text{MPa}$ together with a 180-day electrical resistivity of $35\ \text{k}\Omega\cdot\text{cm}$ and an RCPT passed charge of $1850\ \text{Coulombs}$ (Low class)**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Establishment of mechanical and durability threshold criteria for the optimal limestone powder replacement ratio ($10\ \text{wt.}\%$)**:
   - Proved that up to $10\%$ replacement, strength equal to or better than plain OPC plus higher electrical resistivity are exhibited, while beyond $15\%$ the dilution effect degrades both strength and penetration resistance.
2. **Quantitative correlation between 4-point Wenner Probe surface electrical resistivity and chloride penetration**:
   - Derived a close inverse correlation of $R^2 = 0.95$ between nondestructive surface electrical resistivity and ASTM C1202 RCPT passed charge, presenting a nondestructive evaluation standard for diagnosing chloride attack on field structures.
3. **Elucidation of the micro-filler and monocarboaluminate ($\text{Mc}$) stabilization chemical mechanisms of limestone powder**:
   - Interpreted how fine $\text{CaCO}_3$ particles physically fill nanoscale gaps between clinker grains and react with the aluminate ($\text{C}_3\text{A}$) phase to convert monosulfoaluminate into stable monocarboaluminate ($3\text{CaO}\cdot\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3\cdot\text{CaCO}_3\cdot 11\text{H}_2\text{O}$), refining the pore structure.
4. **Demonstrated eco-friendliness through a $10\sim 15\%$ reduction in cement clinker usage**:
   - Established a sustainable, commercially viable green PLC cement specification that cuts greenhouse gas emissions by more than $10\%$ without any strength loss.

## Methods

- **Materials and PLC concrete mix proportioning (Section 2 & Tables 1~4)**:
  - Binder: ASTM Type II cement + high-purity natural limestone fines ($\text{CaCO}_3 = 96.5\%$, Blaine specific surface area $380\ \text{m}^2/\text{kg}$).
  - Five replacement levels: $0\%$ (OPC reference), $5\%, 10\%, 15\%, 20\ \text{wt.}\%$.
  - Three W/C ratios: 0.40, 0.45, 0.50 (unit binder content $380\sim 420\ \text{kg/m}^3$).
  - Aggregates: river sand ($0\sim 4.75\ \text{mm}$) + crushed coarse aggregate ($d_{max} = 19\ \text{mm}$).
- **Test program (Sections 2.2~2.4 & Figs. 1~2)**:
  - Compressive strength: $150\ \text{mm}$ cube specimens (ages of 7, 28, 90, 180 days).
  - Surface electrical resistivity: Resipod 4-electrode Wenner probe ($a = 40\ \text{mm}$ electrode spacing, four-direction measurement on saturated specimens).
  - Chloride permeability: ASTM C1202 RCPT ($60\ \text{V}$, passed charge over 6 hours).
  - Capillary absorption rate: BS 1881-122 standard test.

### 2. Interpretation of the hydration acceleration and monocarboaluminate phase stabilization mechanisms
- **Accelerated nucleation of clinker hydration (heterogeneous nucleation)**: Limestone fines surfaces provide early precipitation sites for C-S-H hydrates, accelerating strength development over the initial 7~28 days.
- **Reduced electrical conductivity through pore blocking**: Fine carbonate prevents ettringite decomposition and stabilizes it as monocarboaluminate ($\text{Mc}$); the expanding solid volume seals fine continuous pores, raising surface electrical resistivity by $24\%$.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is a landmark source study in which Prof. A. A. Ramezanianpour quantitatively established the strength and nondestructive electrical-resistivity-to-chloride-penetration resistance of PLC concrete with 10% limestone powder replacement, laying a reliable foundation for commercializing green limestone cements and for durability assessment.
