# Mechanical Performance and Nano-Bridging Mechanism of Strain-Hardening Engineered Geopolymer Composite (EGC) through Graphene Nanoplatelet (GNP) Nano-Reinforcement: Achieving Tensile Strength of $5.8\ \text{MPa}$ and Tensile Strain Capacity of $4.85\%$

## Summary

To maximize the mechanical properties and nano-to-micro multi-scale crack resistance of engineered geopolymer composite (**EGC: Engineered Geopolymer Composite**) combining PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$) with an eco-friendly fly ash-slag based geopolymer binder that excludes Portland cement at $100\%$, this Ceramics International 2022 landmark original paper by the joint research team of **Zeya Li, M. Neaz Sheikh, and Muhammad N. S. Hadi at the University of Wollongong, Australia, and Zhengzhou University** systematically evaluated workability, hardening density, 28-day compressive strength, three-point flexural behavior, uniaxial direct tensile strain-hardening behavior, and SEM fracture-surface microstructure by incorporating **ultrasonically dispersed graphene nanoplatelets (GNP: Graphene Nanoplatelet, 0 %, 0.1 %, 0.5 %, 1.0 % by weight)**. The results showed that the EGC-GNP0.5 mix incorporating GNP at 0.5 wt.% achieved a 28-day compressive strength of $50.5\ \text{MPa}$, ultimate tensile strength of $5.8\ \text{MPa}$ (+31.8 %), and an ultra-ductile multiple saturated microcracking tensile strain capacity of $4.85\%$, together with a flexural strength of $14.2\ \text{MPa}$, and elucidated the nano-critical behavior mechanism whereby fiber premature Rupture and workability degradation occur beyond 1.0 wt.%.

## Key Contributions

1. **Simultaneous enhancement of EGC tensile strength by 32 % and tensile ductility to $4.85\%$ via GNP 0.5 wt.% nano-reinforcement**:
   - Dramatically raises tensile strength ($4.4 \rightarrow 5.8\ \text{MPa}$) and flexural strength ($11.2 \rightarrow 14.2\ \text{MPa}$) through graphene nanoplatelets in a cement-free alkali-activated geopolymer system while retaining ultra-high ductility strain-hardening above 4.8%.
2. **Elucidation of multi-scale hybrid synergy between nanoscale GNP bridging and micro PVA fibers**:
   - Demonstrates a two-stage failure-delay mechanism in which two-dimensional sheet-structured GNPs deflect and bridge nanocracks inside N-A-S-H/C-A-S-H hydration gels as the first stage, and micro PVA fibers bridge macrocracks as the second stage.
3. **Establishment of the threshold for excessive interface bonding and premature fiber Rupture as a function of GNP dosage**:
   - Identifies the optimum limit concentration ($0.5\ \text{wt.}\%$): overdosing GNP beyond 1.0 wt.% makes the fiber-matrix interfacial friction excessively dense, so PVA fibers fail to pull out and rupture prematurely, collapsing the tensile strain capacity to $2.1\%$.
4. **Presentation of low-carbon sustainability and eco-friendly structural material indicators**:
   - Completes a high-performance structural material mix that reduces $\text{CO}_2$ emissions by $76\%$ and cuts energy consumption by $36\%$ relative to OPC-based standard ECC.

## Methods

- **Materials and mix design (Section 2.1 & Table 1)**:
  - Precursors: low-calcium Class F fly ash (FA) + ground granulated blast-furnace slag (GGBS) (1:1 by weight).
  - Alkaline activator: liquid sodium silicate ($\text{SiO}_2/\text{Na}_2\text{O} = 2.0$) + 10M NaOH solution (activator/binder = 0.40).
  - Fine aggregate: fine silica sand ($d_{50} = 120\ \mu\text{m}$, S/B = 0.30).
  - Fibers: Kuraray K-II REC15 PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$, $d_f = 40\ \mu\text{m}$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}$, strength 1600 MPa).
  - Four GNP dosage levels: **REF (0.0 %), EGC-GNP0.1 (0.1 %), EGC-GNP0.5 (0.5 %), EGC-GNP1.0 (1.0 %)**.
  - Ultrasonic dispersion: 20 kHz ultrasonication for 30 minutes together with a polycarboxylate-based dispersant.
- **Test methods (Section 2.3 & Figs. 2~4)**:
  - Compressive strength: ASTM C109 50 mm cubes.
  - Uniaxial direct tension: JSCE dumbbell-shaped specimens ($30 \times 13\ \text{mm}$ cross-section, gauge length 80 mm), displacement control at $0.2\ \text{mm/min}$, LVDTs on both faces.
  - Three-point flexure: $40 \times 40 \times 160\ \text{mm}$ prisms with a span of 100 mm.
  - Microstructure: JEOL JSM-7500FA field-emission scanning electron microscope (FE-SEM).

### 2. Nanocrack deflection and interfacial bridging mechanism analysis
- **Nano-bridging effect of GNP**: two-dimensional graphene sheets with a high elastic modulus ($\sim 1\ \text{TPa}$) disperse among the geopolymer nano-gels, deflecting (Deflection) the crack tip during crack propagation and providing bridging traction, thereby raising first-crack strength and tensile strength by 32%.
- **Excessive interfacial bonding and ductility reduction**: when GNP reaches 1.0 wt.%, the chemical/frictional bonding between the PVA fiber surface and the matrix increases excessively, hindering fiber pullout slip and making fiber fracture dominant; the $J_b'/J_{tip}$ energy margin shrinks and the tensile strain capacity falls below half.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is a landmark original paper in the ceramics and composite materials fields, fusing graphene nanomaterial (GNP) into cement-free geopolymer composite (EGC) at the optimum concentration (0.5 wt.%) to realize outstanding mechanical performance—tensile strength $5.8\ \text{MPa}$, flexural strength $14.2\ \text{MPa}$, and tensile strain capacity $4.85\%$—and establishing the nano-micro multi-scale bridging theory.
