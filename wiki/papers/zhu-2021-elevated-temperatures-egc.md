## Summary
A study by Dr. H. Zhu's research team at the University of Western Australia, Australia clarifying through high-temperature electric furnace heating experiments and XRD/SEM-EDS/MIP analysis the anti-spalling mechanism, residual compressive strength, elastic modulus, uniaxial tensile behavior, and zeolite phase crystal evolution of eco-friendly geopolymer composites (PVA-EGC, $f_c = 52 MPa$, room-temperature tensile strain capacity $4.5 \%$) under fire and high-temperature heating environments ($200 \sim 800 °C$). Owing to the excellent thermal stability of the inorganic aluminosilicate geopolymer matrix and the steam devolatilization action of PVA fibers at $230 °C$, spalling was blocked 100 % across the entire heating range, and a compressive strength retention of **75 %** was maintained even after exposure at $400 °C$.

## Key Contributions
- **Ultra-high-temperature fire resistance mechanism of the geopolymer matrix clarified**: unlike Portland cement there is no calcium hydroxide thermal decomposition failure, maintaining shape stability even at $800 °C$.
- **Formation of anti-spalling steam venting channels by PVA fibers demonstrated**: internal steam pressure released completely.
- **Residual compressive strength-elastic modulus decay model by heating temperature ($200 \sim 800 °C$) established**: strength retained at 75 % at $400 °C$ and 45 % at $600 °C$.
- **Design criteria presented for eco-friendly fire-resistant linings of tunnels and nuclear underground structures**: fire safety maximized.

## Methods
- Mix: PVA-EGC (fly ash 60 % + GGBS 40 %, Kuraray REC15 PVA fibers $V_f = 2.0 \%$, $W/B = 0.32$).
- Heating tests: electric furnace ramping ($10 °C/min$) to target temperatures of 200, 400, 600, and 800 °C, held 2 hours then slow cooled.
- Tests: dogbone direct tension test, 28 d compressive strength (100 mm cubes), dynamic elastic modulus, MIP porosity, XRD.

## Results
- Measured residual mechanical property data of PVA-EGC by heating temperature:

| Heating temperature ($°C$) | Spalling | 28 d compressive strength (MPa) | Compressive strength retention (%) | Elastic modulus retention (%) | Residual tensile strain capacity (%) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Room temp ($20 °C$) | none (normal) | 52.5 | 100 % (reference) | 100 % (24.5 GPa) | **4.50 % (ultra-ductile)** |
| $200 °C$ | **no spalling** | 50.8 | **96.8 %** | 88.0 % | **3.20 % (hardening retained)** |
| $400 °C$ | **no spalling** | 39.5 | **75.2 %** | 62.5 % | 0.45 % (softening transition) |
| $600 °C$ | **no spalling** | 23.8 | **45.3 %** | 35.0 % | 0.08 % (brittle) |
| $800 °C$ | **no spalling** | 12.5 | **23.8 %** | 15.2 % | 0.02 % |
