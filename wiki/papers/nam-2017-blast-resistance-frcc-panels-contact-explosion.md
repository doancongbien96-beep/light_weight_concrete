# Blast Resistance and 80% Rear-Face Spalling Suppression Mechanism of 2 vol.% Fiber-Reinforced Cementitious Composite (FRCC) Panels under Contact Explosion (Emulsion Explosive $25\sim 200\ \text{g}$)

## Summary

To prevent perforation fragmentation and rear-face spall ejection (Spalling/Breach) of concrete protective structures under terrorist and military high-explosive contact-explosion (Contact Explosion) loading, full-scale contact detonation tests using emulsion explosive (25, 50, 100, 200 g) together with static fracture energy ($G_F$) testing were performed on $400 \times 400 \times 70\ \text{mm}$ panels fabricated from **five types of 2 vol.% monolithic and hybrid fiber-reinforced cementitious composites (FRCC: PVA, PE, steel fiber SF, SF+PVA, SF+PE)**, thereby identifying an anti-blast mechanism that **reduces the rear-face fragment-scatter area by more than $80\%$ relative to unreinforced concrete and completely blocks perforation breach (Breach) even under 100 g high-explosive exposure** — the 2017 landmark original paper of the Chungnam National University team of Prof. Gyuyong Kim and Dr. Jeong-Soo Nam in the International Journal of Concrete Structures and Materials (IJCSM).

## Key Contributions

1. **Quantification of the local damage modes of five fiber-composite panels under full-scale contact-explosion loading**:
   - Precisely analyzed the damage-grade transition with increasing charge mass (crater Crater $\rightarrow$ rear-face spalling Spall $\rightarrow$ perforation breach Breach), demonstrating that FRCC mitigates damage severity by 1–2 grades relative to unreinforced concrete.
2. **Identification of the outstanding anti-blast synergy of the steel-fiber–PE-fiber hybrid (Hyb-PE)**:
   - Steel fibers reduce and confine the front-face crater fragmentation zone by $45\%$, while the ultra-high-ductility PE fiber perfectly suppresses fragment detachment driven by the rear-face tensile reflected wave, achieving the best protective performance.
3. **Demonstration of a 120-fold increase in static fracture energy ($G_F$) ($0.15 \rightarrow 18.5\ \text{kJ/m}^2$)**:
   - RILEM notched-beam flexural test results show that 2% fiber addition spikes the static fracture energy by a factor of 120, providing the physical basis for dissipating blast shock-wave energy throughout the entire matrix.
4. **Proposal of a limiting protection criterion line versus the charge-to-panel-thickness ratio ($C_d/T$)**:
   - At $C_d/T > 0.26$ ordinary concrete collapses with immediate perforation, whereas the Hyb-PE panel safely maintains a non-perforated state even at $C_d/T = 0.53$ (100 g charge).

## Methods

- **Materials and FRCC mix composition (Section 2.1 & Tables 1~3)**:
  - Matrix concrete: OPC + fly ash (15 wt.%) + fine aggregate + coarse aggregate (28-day compressive strength $42.5\ \text{MPa}$).
  - Five reinforcing fibers (total $V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$):
    1. `PVACC`: Kuraray PVA fiber ($d_f = 39\ \mu\text{m}$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}$, $\sigma_f = 1600\ \text{MPa}$).
    2. `PECC`: ultra-high-molecular-weight PE fiber ($d_f = 31\ \mu\text{m}$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}$, $\sigma_f = 2700\ \text{MPa}$).
    3. `SFCC`: hooked steel fiber ($d_f = 0.7\ \text{mm}$, $L_f = 30\ \text{mm}$, $\sigma_f = 1100\ \text{MPa}$).
    4. `Hyb-PVA`: steel fiber 1.0% + PVA fiber 1.0%.
    5. `Hyb-PE`: steel fiber 1.0% + PE fiber 1.0%.
  - Controls: normal concrete (`NCC`, 35 MPa) and unreinforced high-strength concrete (`NHC`, 60 MPa).
- **Contact detonation test procedure (Section 2.2 & Figs. 2~4)**:
  - Panel dimensions: $400 \times 400 \times 70\ \text{mm}$ (mounted in a steel support frame).
  - Explosive: cylindrical emulsion charge ($\phi 32\ \text{mm}$, detonation velocity $5500\ \text{m/s}$); charges of 25 g, 50 g, 100 g, and 200 g detonated in direct contact with the center of the panel front surface.
  - Instrumentation: measurement of front-face crater diameter/depth, rear-face spall damage area ($A_d$), and perforation hole diameter.

### 2. Interpretation of the contact-explosion shock-wave dispersion and fragment-capture mechanisms
- **Suppression of front-face compressive-wave comminution**: the high elastic modulus and shear resistance of the steel fiber strongly constrain the ultra-high-pressure crushing (Crushing) zone at the instant of shock-wave incidence, halving the crater diameter.
- **Micro-fiber capture of the rear-face tensile reflected wave**: when the compressive shock wave reflects off the rear face and forms a powerful tensile Hopkinson relief wave, ultra-high-density PE fibers of $24\sim 31\ \mu\text{m}$ form a tensile bridging network that suppresses fragment detachment and spalling by more than 80%.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper demonstrates that a 2 vol.% steel-fiber–PE-fiber hybrid composite completely defends a 70 mm thin panel against perforation even under 100 g contact-explosion loading and blocks rear-face fragment scattering by 80%, thereby establishing experimental criteria essential to the design of military protective and counter-terrorism anti-blast structures — a landmark original paper.
