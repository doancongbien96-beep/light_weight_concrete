## Summary
A paper published in International Journal of Fatigue by Dr. H. Zhu's research team on the repeated flexural fatigue behavior (stress ratios $S = 0.60 \sim 0.85$, $R = 0.1$, frequency 5 Hz), loading-unloading hysteresis stiffness decay, deformation energy dissipation evolution, and Weibull statistical $S-N-P_f$ fatigue life prediction model of eco-friendly strain-hardening geopolymer composites (PVA-EGC, $f_c = 50 MPa$, static flexural modulus of rupture $f_r = 15.8 MPa$, PVA fibers $V_f = 2.0 \%$) applied to bridge decks and heavy-traffic road pavements. Under the high fatigue load of $S = 0.65$, PVA-EGC recorded a **run-out limit of $2 \times 10^6$ cycles without failure**, demonstrating dynamic fatigue durability comparable to ordinary cement-based PVA-ECC.

## Key Contributions
- **World's first establishment of the flexural fatigue ($S-N$) governing equation of eco-friendly EGC**: fatigue limit stress ratio $S_e = 0.64$ derived at a failure probability of 50 %.
- **Stability of multiple fine crack dispersion under repeated fatigue demonstrated**: networks of more than 25 fine cracks within the gauge disperse fatigue loads, suppressing premature fatigue rupture.
- **Interfacial fatigue wear resistance of PVA fibers in the alkali geopolymer matrix proven**: fiber surface friction damage reduced by 30 % versus cement matrices.
- **Fatigue design criteria presented for eco-friendly road pavements and bridge slabs**: 100-year fatigue design life guaranteed.

## Methods
- Mix: PVA-EGC (fly ash 60 % + GGBS 40 %, Kuraray REC15 PVA fibers $V_f = 2.0 \%$, $f_r = 15.8 MPa$).
- Fatigue testing: four-point flexural fatigue machine with $100 \times 100 \times 400 mm$ prismatic beams (span 300 mm, sine wave at 5 Hz, $S = 0.60, 0.70, 0.75, 0.80, 0.85$).
- Instrumentation: continuous deflection measurement by laser displacement meters and electron-microscope observation of fractured fiber surfaces.
