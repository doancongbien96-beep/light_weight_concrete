## Summary
A landmark paper by the research team of Dr. C. Zou published in the ASCE Journal of Structural Engineering, which investigated, in order to overcome the diagonal shear brittle failure (Diagonal Shear Failure) and the severe pinching effect (Pinching Effect) arising in squat shear walls of low aspect ratio ($H_w/L_w = 1.0$) applied to nuclear power plant containment buildings, protective bunkers, and low-rise earthquake-resistant buildings, the cyclic lateral load hysteresis curves, shear strength, energy dissipation area, and damage resistance of full-scale R/EGC squat shear walls ($1000 \times 1000 \times 100 mm$) cast with an eco-friendly high-ductility composite (PVA-EGC, $f_c = 52 MPa$, direct tensile strain capacity $4.8 \%$) over the entire shear wall. The R/EGC shear walls increased shear capacity by **42 %** and plastic energy dissipation capacity **3.8-fold** relative to ordinary RC shear walls, and demonstrated complete conversion of the shear failure mode into a ductile flexural-shear failure.

## Key Contributions
- **Complete exclusion of diagonal tension brittle failure in low-aspect-ratio ($H_w/L_w = 1.0$) squat walls**: the EGC bridging membrane restrained the rapid propagation of diagonal tension cracks.
- **Substantial mitigation of the pinching phenomenon in seismic hysteresis curves**: formation of full spindle-shaped hysteresis loops increased energy dissipation 3.8-fold.
- **Securing an ultimate story drift angle of 2.8 %**: deformation ductility capacity extended 2.5-fold compared with ordinary RC walls (1.1 %).
- **Presentation of seismic shear wall design guidelines for eco-friendly nuclear power and defense facilities**: realization of a 50 % reduction in shear reinforcement bars.

## Methods
- Four full-scale shear wall specimens ($1000 \times 1000 \times 100 mm$, cast monolithically with top and bottom loading beams).
- Variables: ordinary concrete (NC 45 MPa) versus PVA-EGC ($f_c = 52 MPa, \epsilon_u = 4.8 \%$), horizontal/vertical shear reinforcement ratios (0.35 % ~ 0.70 %).
- Testing: quasi-static cyclic lateral loading under a constant axial load ratio ($n = 0.15 f_c A_g$), applied in stages of story drift angle ($0.1 \sim 3.0 \%$).
