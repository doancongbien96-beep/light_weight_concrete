# Composite Mechanical Behavior and Micromechanical Optimization of Ultra-High-Strength, Ultra-High-Ductility Concrete (HSHDC) Simultaneously Achieving 165 MPa Compressive Strength and $4.5\%$ Uniaxial Tensile Strain

## Summary

To fundamentally overcome the severe brittle-failure limit of ultra-high-performance concrete (UHPC) and to endow a cementitious composite with tensile plastic ductility rivaling structural steel, the team developed **High-Strength High-Ductility Concrete (HSHDC)**, in which an ultra-high-strength matrix (compressive strength $165\ \text{MPa}$) is reinforced with 2.0 vol.% high-stiffness ultra-high-molecular-weight Spectra PE fiber (tensile strength $3000\ \text{MPa}$) while the matrix fracture toughness ($J_{tip}$) and the fiber bridging complementary energy ($J_b'$) are optimally balanced through micromechanics, and precisely evaluated its 28-day compressive strength, elastic modulus, uniaxial direct tensile behavior, four-point flexural behavior, and multiaxial deformation characteristics, achieving **a 28-day compressive strength of $165.0\ \text{MPa}$, elastic modulus $E_c = 42.5\ \text{GPa}$, ultimate uniaxial tensile strength $14.5\ \text{MPa}$, ultimate uniaxial tensile strain $4.5\%$, and four-point flexural modulus of rupture (MOR) of $32.5\ \text{MPa}$**, the 2013 landmark original paper in ACI Materials Journal by the research team of **Prof. Victor C. Li (University of Michigan), Dr. Ravi Ranade, Prof. Michael Stojadinovic (UC Berkeley), and Dr. William Heard (US Army Engineer Research and Development Center, ERDC)**.

## Key Contributions

1. **First simultaneous realization of ultra-high strength ($165\ \text{MPa}$) and ultra-high ductility ($\varepsilon_u = 4.5\%$)**:
   - Breaks the inverse strength-ductility trade-off and completes more than 4 % of uniaxial tensile strain-hardening in the ultra-high-strength regime beyond 150 MPa.
2. **Extension of the micromechanics-based PSH design criteria (PSH Performance Index) into the extreme regime**:
   - To overcome the high fracture toughness ($J_{tip} \approx 35\ \text{J/m}^2$) of the ultra-high-strength matrix, the pressurized pullout slip friction of the PE fiber was maximized to secure $J_b'/J_{tip} \ge 2.5$.
3. **50-fold amplification of the uniaxial tensile energy dissipation density ($g \approx 550\ \text{kJ/m}^3$)**:
   - Whereas ordinary UHPC exhibits an abrupt load drop immediately after tensile cracking, HSHDC maintains continuous strain-hardening up to $14.5\ \text{MPa}$.
4. **Provision of source data for the design of US DoD / Army Corps of Engineers protective and impact-resistant structures**:
   - Establishes a next-generation composite-material standard capable of completely preventing back-face spalling of structural members under penetrating explosion and high-velocity impact.

## Methods

- **Materials and HSHDC mix design (Sections 2~3 & Tables 1~3)**:
  - Binder: Type I ordinary Portland cement + silica fume (SF/C = 0.25) + low-calcium fly ash (FA/C = 0.50).
  - Aggregate: finely graded silica sand ($d_{50} = 100\ \mu\text{m}$, S/B = 0.30, W/B = 0.20, polycarboxylate superplasticizer).
  - Reinforcing fiber: Spectra 900 PE microfiber ($V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}$, $d_f = 38\ \mu\text{m}$, $\sigma_f = 3000\ \text{MPa}$, $E_f = 117\ \text{GPa}$).
- **Mechanical testing program (Sections 3.2~3.5 & Figs. 1~4)**:
  - Uniaxial direct tension: dogbone-shaped specimens ($80 \times 30 \times 13\ \text{mm}$, displacement control at 0.15 mm/min).
  - Compressive strength: $\phi 75 \times 150\ \text{mm}$ cylinders and $50\ \text{mm}$ cubes (28-day age, with parallel steam curing at 90 °C).
  - Four-point bending test: $100 \times 100 \times 400\ \text{mm}$ prismatic specimens (span 300 mm).

### 2. PE fiber bridging inside the ultra-high-strength matrix and micromechanics interpretation
- **Pressure-driven slip-hardening of the Spectra PE fiber**: the shrinkage confinement pressure of the ultra-dense matrix applies an optimal frictional stress ($\tau_0 \approx 4.5\ \text{MPa}$) onto the PE fiber surface, continuously mobilizing maximum pullout resistance without fiber rupture.
- **Complete saturation of multiple microcracking**: after steam curing, the ultimate bridging strength ($14.5\ \text{MPa}$) far exceeds the first-cracking strength ($9.2\ \text{MPa}$), so more than 52 ultra-fine cracks form uniformly.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is the landmark original publication in which Prof. Victor C. Li and Dr. Ravi Ranade fully established the composite mechanical performance of HSHDC, which simultaneously attains 165 MPa compressive strength and 4.5 % tensile strain, presenting the most authoritative standard for the design of next-generation protective and extreme-seismic structures.
