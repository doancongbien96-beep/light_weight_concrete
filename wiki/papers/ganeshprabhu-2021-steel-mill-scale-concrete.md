# Engineering Behavior of Sustainable Concrete with Steel Mill Scale as Fine Aggregate Replacement

## Summary

This study experimentally investigated the fresh workability, hardened compressive, splitting tensile, and flexural strengths, elastic modulus, abrasion resistance, and chloride penetration resistance of sustainable heavy-weight concrete incorporating steel mill scale (SMS: $\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3 > 70\ \%$, density $4.85\ \text{g/cm}^3$)—an industrial byproduct from steel hot-rolling—as a replacement for natural river sand (at 0, 20, 40, 60, 80, and 100 wt %). Owing to the angular particle shape and high hardness of mill scale, concrete incorporating 40 wt % SMS achieved an 18.5 % increase in 28 d compressive strength and a 35 % enhancement in surface abrasion resistance.

## Key Contributions

- **Identification of optimal replacement ratio**: Replacing 40 wt % fine aggregate with SMS yielded maximum mechanical performance, reaching a compressive strength of 41.5 MPa and flexural strength of 5.8 MPa.
- **35 % increase in abrasion and impact resistance**: Attributed to the high Mohs hardness ($H = 6.5$) of mill scale particles and enhanced mechanical interlocking at the paste-aggregate interface.
- **Improved rapid chloride permeability (RCPT)**: Dense interfacial transition zones (ITZ) and micro-pore filling reduced total charge passed from 2200 C to 1450 C (a 34 % reduction).
- **River sand preservation and waste upcycling**: Promoted circular economy by converting landfilled metallurgical byproducts into heavy-weight construction resources.

## Methods

- Binders: Grade 53 OPC ($W/C = 0.45$).
- Aggregates: Natural river sand (specific gravity 2.62) replaced with steel mill scale (specific gravity 4.85, fineness modulus 2.85, $\text{Fe}_3\text{O}_4/\text{FeO}$ 92 %) at 0 ~ 100 % by weight, crushed coarse aggregate (maximum aggregate size 20 mm).
- Testing program: Slump test, 28 d compressive strength (150 mm cubes), splitting tensile strength (150 × 300 mm cylinders), flexural strength (100 × 100 × 500 mm prisms), abrasion resistance (IS 1237), and RCPT (ASTM C1202).

- The SMS 40 % mixture exhibited the highest mechanical strength, highest abrasion resistance, and lowest chloride diffusivity.
