## Summary
A latest 2025 study that characterized, through instrumented impact testing and high-speed 3D DIC image analysis, the contact load-deflection histories, impact energy absorption ratios, rear-face crack distribution, and damage tolerance of thin plate panels ($400 \times 400 \times 20 mm$) of an eco-friendly cementless geopolymer composite mixed as one part (One-Part EGC, $f_c = 52 MPa$, direct tensile strain capacity $6.5 \%$, PE fiber $V_f = 2.0 \%$) using solid powder activator and water only, under low-velocity drop-weight impact loading (Drop-Weight Impact, impact energy 30 ~ 120 J). One-Part EGC was demonstrated to achieve **100 % complete absorption** without perforation or brittle failure even under an impact energy of 120 J, confining the maximum rear-face crack width to **0.05 mm** or less.

## Key Contributions
- **First worldwide quantification of the low-velocity impact damage resistance of one-part geopolymer (One-Part EGC)**: brittle punching shear failure completely excluded.
- **Impact energy absorption capacity enhanced by a factor of 4.5**: superior impact deformation energy dissipation compared with ordinary concrete and conventional cement ECC.
- **Demonstrated durability against fatigue damage under repeated impacts**: shape preserved without perforation even after 10 consecutive drop impacts were applied at the same location.
- **Established the commercialization basis for eco-friendly protective thin panels and modular exterior cladding**: constructability and impact resistance satisfied simultaneously.

## Methods
- Mix: One-Part EGC (fly ash 60 % + GGBS 40 % + solid sodium silicate powder, $W/B = 0.33$, Spectra PE fiber $V_f = 2.0 \%$).
- Impact testing: CEAST 9350 instrumented drop-weight impact tester (hemispherical steel indenter diameter 20 mm, dropping weight 5.0 kg, impact energies 30, 60, 90, 120 J).
- Instrumentation: piezo load cell, laser displacement gauge, and strain tensor mapping by DIC with a 10000 fps ultra-high-speed camera.
