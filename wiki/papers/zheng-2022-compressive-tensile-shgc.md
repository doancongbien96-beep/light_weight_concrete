## Summary

Paper by Dr. Y. Zheng's research team at Tongji University that systematically established the alkali activation reaction mechanism of a fly ash-slag binder, matrix fracture toughness ($K_m$), single PVA fiber interfacial frictional stress ($\tau_0$), the uniaxial compressive stress-strain envelope and uniaxial direct-tension strain-hardening performance, in order to accelerate practical use of ambient curing ($23 °C$) for environmentally friendly cement-free geopolymer composites. Under ambient curing conditions the composite achieved a 28 d compressive strength of **55.4 MPa**, a compressive failure strain of **0.0125**, a direct tensile strength of **5.8 MPa** and an ultimate tensile strain capacity of **5.2 %**, proving that high strength and ultra-high ductility can be realized simultaneously even without heat curing.

## Key Contributions

- **Establishment of an integrated three-dimensional compressive-tensile constitutive model for ambient-cured SHGC**: compressive toughness increased 3.5-fold and completion of the tensile strain-hardening envelope.
- **Stabilization of the chemical bond at the PVA fiber/geopolymer matrix interface**: an adequate interfacial frictional stress of $\tau_0 = 1.62 MPa$ was maintained without alkali attack.
- **Securing a PSH energy index of $J_b'/J_{tip} = 5.8$**: more than 45 distributed fine cracks formed within the gauge length (average crack width $30 \mu m$).
- **Establishment of a mix for environmentally friendly infrastructure repair materials suitable for cast-in-place work**: a 70 % reduction in $CO_2$ emissions.

## Methods

- Binder: Class F fly ash 65 % + GGBS 35 %, activated by liquid sodium silicate and $NaOH 10 M$ activator ($W/B = 0.33$).
- Fiber: Kuraray REC15 PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 39 µm, length 12 mm).
- Curing: ambient air curing ($23 °C, RH 60 \%$).
- Testing: dog-bone direct tension test ($0.5 mm/min$, DIC strains), uniaxial compression test on $100 \times 200 mm$ cylinders (displacement control), 28 d elastic modulus.
