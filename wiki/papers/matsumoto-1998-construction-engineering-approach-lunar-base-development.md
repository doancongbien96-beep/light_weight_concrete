# Shimizu Corporation's systems engineering framework for permanent crewed lunar base construction: the immortal classic of the six-phase evolutionary scenario, local resource utilization (ISRU lunar concrete and cast basalt), and robotic automated construction

## Summary

To secure the phased establishment and economic viability of a 21st-century permanent crewed lunar base (Permanent Lunar Base: a resident habitat module for 15 crew, power demand of 280 kWe) after the Apollo program, this immortal supreme landmark classic, the greatest paper in the history of space civil engineering construction, published in the ASCE Journal of Aerospace Engineering in 1998 by the research team of Shinji Matsumoto, Chief Researcher at Shimizu Corporation of Japan, and Dr. Hiroshi Kanamori, comprehensively consolidates the **systems engineering (Systems Engineering) approach to lunar base construction pursued for 10 years by the Shimizu Corporation Space Engineering Research Institute, the six-phase evolutionary scenario (Phase 1~6: from unmanned preliminary exploration in 2005 to completion of the permanent base in 2050), $N^2$ matrix diagram-based functional interfacing, the design of shield dome structures in locally produced lunar-soil concrete (Lunar Concrete: bonded with ultra-rapid-hardening cement binder) and cast basalt (Cast Basalt), and the robotic remote autonomous construction process**.

## Key Contributions

1. **World-first establishment of the six-phase evolutionary scenario (Phase 1~6) and the systems engineering model for lunar base construction**:
   - Completed the phase-by-phase mass transportation and power grid linkage framework expanding from unmanned landing exploration (Phase 1, 0 people) through initial camp (Phase 2, 4 crew) and continuous operation (Phase 4, 8 crew) to a permanently settled city (Phase 6, 15 or more crew).
2. **Establishment of space civil engineering functional interfaces through the $N^2$ matrix diagram**:
   - Optimized the input/output interfaces linking resource excavation-transport-refining-temporary structure erection-life support modules, raising the local resource (ISRU) contribution rate above $80\%$.
3. **Demonstration of lunar concrete (Lunar Concrete) and basalt interlocking block construction methods**:
   - Designed high-strength concrete using lunar soil as aggregate together with high-temperature melt-cast basalt panels, erecting radiation/micrometeorite shielding walls $0.5\sim 1.5\ \text{m}$ thick.
4. **Establishment of the unmanned remote robot teleoperation (Teleoperation) construction automation protocol**:
   - Presented work scheduling for autonomous excavation, ground compaction, and crane bolt assembly robots that overcome the 3-second Earth-Moon communication time delay (Time Delay).

## Methods

- **Systems engineering and the evolutionary scenario (Sections 2~4 & Tables 1~3 & Figs. 1~4)**:
  - Phase 1 (unmanned site stabilization), Phase 2 (4 crew, 45-day stay, 20 kW), Phase 3 (5 crew, 90 days, 40 kW), Phase 4 (8 crew, 180 days, 120 kW), Phase 5 (10 crew, 1 year, 200 kW), Phase 6 (15 crew, permanent, 280 kW).
  - $N^2$ functional analysis: power supply, resource refining (ISRU), transport logistics, habitat modules, environmental control (ECLSS).
- **Structural design and construction engineering (Sections 4~6 & Figs. 5~8)**:
  - Structural scheme: an inflatable spherical dome (Inflatable Dome) + an external cast basalt interlocking block shielding layer (inner diameter 15 m, shielding thickness 1.5 m).
  - Materials: compressed concrete based on local lunar soil ($f_c = 45.0\ \text{MPa}$) and molten basalt tiles.

### 2. Radiation shielding and thermal control structural analysis

- **Integrity of the 1.5 m basalt block shielding**: completely blocks annual space radiation exposure down to below the permissible dose for the general public on Earth ($<5\ \text{mSv/year}$).
- **Buffering of the extreme day-night temperature difference ($-150\ ^\circ\text{C} \leftrightarrow +100\ ^\circ\text{C}$)**: stabilizes the interior temperature of the shielded dome to within a fluctuation of $\pm 2\ ^\circ\text{C}$.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper, in which the research team of Shimizu Corporation of Japan systematized for the first time in the world the six-phase systems engineering evolutionary scenario for lunar base construction and the local-resource-based shielded structure construction methods, establishing the historical standard for future space civil construction and planetary habitat engineering, is an immortal supreme landmark classic paper.
