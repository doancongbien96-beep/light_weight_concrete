# Effect of Silica Fume on Dynamic Properties and Vibration Damping of Concrete

## Summary

To improve vibration damping and dynamic response control in railway slab tracks, bridge decks, and precision machinery foundations, Professor V. T. Giner and co-workers at Universitat Politècnica de València investigated the resonant frequency, dynamic modulus of elasticity ($E_d$), damping ratio ($\xi$), and loss factor ($\eta$) of structural concrete ($W/B = 0.45$) incorporating undensified silica fume ($\text{SiO}_2\ 94.11\ \%$, unburnt carbon 2.85 %, specific surface area $200000 cm^2/g$) as a cement replacement at 5, 10, and 15 % by mass using the impact-echo resonance method. Incorporating 10 % silica fume densified the hydration microstructure, increasing the dynamic elastic modulus by **11.3 % from 38.2 GPa to 42.5 GPa**, while simultaneously increasing the dynamic damping ratio $\xi$ by **32.4 % from 1.85 % to 2.45 %** via viscoelastic friction mechanisms at refined ITZ interfaces.

## Key Contributions

- **Quantification of dynamic parameters across silica fume contents (0, 5, 10, 15 %)**: Identified an optimal performance window that simultaneously enhances stiffness and damping capacity.
- **Resonant frequency testing via ASTM C215 impact-echo**: Measured longitudinal, flexural, and torsional resonant frequencies using accelerometers and high-speed FFT spectrum analysis.
- **Viscoelastic damping mechanisms of micro-fillers**: Explained damping enhancements via viscoelastic energy dissipation in C-S-H gel nanopores and frictional micro-defect interfaces.
- **Mixture criteria for vibration mitigation in high-speed infrastructure**: Enabled structural designs reducing dynamic vibration amplitudes by 25 %.

## Methods

- Raw materials: Ordinary Portland cement (CEM I 52.5 R), undensified silica fume ($\text{SiO}_2\ 94.11\ \%$, $\text{LOI} = 3.08\ \%$, moisture 0.60 %), melamine-based superplasticizer (Sikament FF 0.5 ~ 2.0 %).
- Mixture proportions (0, 5, 10, 15 wt % replacement):
  - **SF-0 (Control)**: 100 % OPC ($W/C = 0.45$)
  - **SF-5**: 5 wt % silica fume replacement
  - **SF-10**: 10 wt % silica fume replacement
  - **SF-15**: 15 wt % silica fume replacement
- Specimens: 100 × 100 × 400 mm prismatic concrete beams.
- Testing: ASTM C215 impact resonance testing (longitudinal, transverse, and torsional modes) with damping ratio $\xi$ evaluated via the half-power bandwidth method.
