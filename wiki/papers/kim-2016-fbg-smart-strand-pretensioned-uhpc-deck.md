# Continuous Monitoring of Initial Tensioning, 50,000-Cycle Wheel Load Fatigue, and Long-term Losses in Full-Scale Pretensioned UHPC Bridge Decks Using Smart Strands with Core-Embedded FBG Sensors

## Summary

To continuously and directly measure prestressing force evolution and fatigue behavior within ultra-high-performance concrete (UHPC) pretensioned bridge decks, Prof. Jae-Min Kim's research team at Chonnam National University, KISTEC, and Dr. Sung-Yong Park at KICT developed a **Smart Prestressing Strand**. The core wire (king wire, diameter 5.24 mm) of a 7-wire strand was replaced by a steel tube (outer diameter 2.0 mm, inner diameter 1.0 mm) housing a low-viscosity-epoxy-encapsulated **fiber Bragg grating (FBG, Bragg wavelength $\lambda_B$) sensor**. The authors embedded these smart strands inside a **$3.7\ \text{m} \times 1.8\ \text{m} \times 0.26\ \text{m}$ full-scale pretensioned UHPC deck, tracking initial jacking (211 kN) and seating slip losses, a 124.8 kN truck wheel load static test, 50,000 dynamic fatigue cycles (0.33 Hz), and 10 months of long-term creep/shrinkage losses alongside temperature variations** in real time. The sensor accurately captured an instantaneous **anchorage seating slip loss of $12.3\%$ ($22.4\ \text{kN}$, from $182.8\ \text{kN}$ down to $160.4\ \text{kN}$)**. During **50,000 cycles of 124.8 kN wheel load fatigue testing, the sensor showed zero signal drift or damage, measuring $2.63\ \text{kN}$ dynamic compression fluctuations at $0.1\ \text{kN}$ resolution**. Long-term monitoring over 10 months verified that the dense UHPC matrix restricted additional creep prestress losses to under $1.5\%$.

## Key Contributions

1. **Field implementation of smart strands embedded in full-scale ($3.7\ \text{m} \times 1.8\ \text{m}$) pretensioned UHPC decks**:
   - Demonstrated complete sensor survival during casting of 150 MPa UHPC with high steel fiber content and subsequent $90\ ^\circ\text{C}$ steam curing.
2. **Real-time capture of 12.3 % instantaneous anchorage slip losses**:
   - Resolved the $22.4\ \text{kN}$ prestress drop from hydraulic jacking ($182.8\ \text{kN}$) to wedge lock-in ($160.4\ \text{kN}$) with microsecond resolution.
3. **Verification of dynamic stability across 50,000 cycles of 124.8 kN wheel load fatigue**:
   - Reliably tracked localized compressive strains in the strand ($2.63\sim 3.48\ \text{kN}$ transient tension decrease) caused by downward flexural deformation under cyclic wheel loads.
4. **10-month tracking of long-term prestress losses ($\le 1.5\%$) in UHPC decks**:
   - Verified that ultra-low creep and shrinkage in UHPC preserved residual tendon forces above $158.0\ \text{kN}$.

## Methods

- **Smart strand specifications and fabrication (Section 2 & Fig. 2)**:
  - Base strand: 7-wire strand (diameter 15.24 mm, nominal area $138.7\ \text{mm}^2$, tensile strength 1860 MPa).
  - Smart king wire: High-strength steel tube (outer diameter 2.0 mm, inner diameter 1.0 mm) housing the FBG sensor with vacuum-injected epoxy.
  - Optical parameters: Photoelastic coefficient $p_e = 0.22$, thermal expansion coefficient $\alpha = 0.55\times 10^{-6}/\text{K}$, thermo-optic coefficient $\xi = 8.60\times 10^{-6}/\text{K}$.
- **UHPC deck fabrication and loading regimes (Sections 3–4 & Figs. 3–6)**:
  - Deck dimensions: Length 3.7 m, width 1.8 m, thickness 0.26 m (design compressive strength 150 MPa).
  - Tensioning: Pretension jacking target of 211 kN (stepwise tensioning following 20 kN pre-alignment).
  - Static wheel test: 124.8 kN center load sustained for 10 min.
  - Dynamic fatigue: 124.8 kN sinusoidal loading at 0.33 Hz for 50,000 cycles.
  - Interrogation system: Micron Optics SM130 (130) with 1 pm wavelength resolution.

## Results

### 1. Prestress force and wheel load fatigue responses across testing stages (Tables 1–3 & Figs. 4–7)

| Loading & Testing Stage | Tensioning Condition | Measured Tension ($P_{FBG}$, kN) | Force Variation ($\Delta P$, kN) | Mechanical Assessment & Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Initial Fixing** | Wedge seating | 20.0 ± 0.5 kN | 0.0 kN | Initial alignment complete |
| **Peak Jacking** | Hydraulic jack maximum | **182.8 ± 1.2 kN (Peak)** | +162.8 kN | Elastic strand extension |
| **Anchorage Lock-in** | Wedge grip locked | **160.4 ± 1.0 kN** | **-22.4 kN (-12.3 %)** | **Instantaneous anchorage slip loss** |
| **Static Wheel Load** | 124.8 kN center point | 156.9 ± 0.8 kN | -3.48 kN | Deck flexural compressive strain |
| **Dynamic Wheel Load** | 124.8 kN, 50,000 cycles | 157.8 ± 0.8 kN | -2.63 kN | Stable dynamic elastic fatigue |
| **10-Month Long-term** | Ambient exposure | **158.2 ± 1.5 kN** | **-2.2 kN (-1.4 %)** | **Ultra-low creep stability in UHPC** |

### 2. Sensor survivability and dynamic response mechanics
- **Shielding against steam curing and steel fiber interference**: The internal steel tube and 7-wire helical geometry prevented direct contact with steel fibers, buffering shrinkage pressures during $90\ ^\circ\text{C}$ steam curing and 150 MPa hydration.
- **Wheel-induced flexural compression**: Accurately captured localized tendon decompression (2.63~3.48 kN reduction) below the neutral axis during upper wheel loading.

- **Overall conclusion**: This landmark paper demonstrated that smart strands with embedded FBG sensors provide reliable, long-term prestress force monitoring in full-scale pretensioned UHPC bridge decks across initial jacking (12.3 % slip loss), 50,000 cycles of wheel load fatigue, and 10 months of field service.
