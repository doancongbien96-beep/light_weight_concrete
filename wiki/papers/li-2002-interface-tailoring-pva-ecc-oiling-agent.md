# Interface tailoring of PVA-ECC via oiling surface treatment (Oiling Agent) to prevent premature fiber rupture and achieve strain-hardening: tensile strain $4.7\%$ and the birth of commercial Kuraray REC15 fiber

## Summary

The hydrophilic hydroxyl groups ($-\text{OH}$) in the molecular structure of Polyvinyl Alcohol (PVA) fibers form excessively strong chemical adhesion ($G_d = 4.71\ \text{J/m}^2$) and severe slip hardening ($\beta = 2.9$) with cement hydration products, causing fibers to fail to pull out and rupture prematurely upon crack formation. To solve this problem, **an interface tailoring technology that precisely coats the PVA fiber surface with a hydrophobic Oiling Agent (0 %, 0.3 %, 0.5 %, 0.8 %, 1.2 % by weight) to precisely control the chemical bond energy ($G_d \le 2.2\ \text{J/m}^2$), frictional strength ($\tau_0 = 1.0\sim 2.1\ \text{MPa}$), and slip-hardening coefficient ($\beta \le 1.5$) into the ideal micromechanics PSH design domain** was developed for the first time in the world; through single-fiber pullout tests and composite direct-tension tests, **a PVA-ECC with ultra-high ductility of tensile strain $4.7\%$ and saturated multiple fine cracking ($w \approx 70\ \mu\text{m}$) was demonstrated, establishing the manufacturing standard for the commercial Kuraray REC15 fiber** — the immortal supreme landmark original paper of Victor C. Li, endowed professor at the University of Michigan, together with Hwai-Chung Wu and Shuxin Wang, published in ACI Materials Journal in 2002.

## Key Contributions

1. **Establishment of the oiling interface tailoring technology that led to the birth of commercial PVA-ECC (Kuraray K-II REC15 standard)**:
   - Devised a 1.2 % oiling surface treatment that physicochemically suppresses the excessive chemical adhesion of hydrophilic PVA fibers, laying the foundation for economical PVA-based commercial ECC as opposed to expensive PE fibers.
2. **Proposal of the optimal interface design domain conforming to the two principal micromechanics PSH criteria**:
   - Derived the quantitative targets $G_d < 2.2\ \text{J/m}^2$, $\tau_0 = 1.0\sim 2.1\ \text{MPa}$, $\beta < 1.5$, and formulated the degradation behavior of interface properties with oiling content in a one-to-one correspondence.
3. **Elucidation of the transition mechanism to saturated PSH strain-hardening by oiling content**:
   - Ductility surges more than fivefold from 0 % oil (tensile strain $0.9\%$) $\rightarrow$ 0.5 % oil ($2.8\%$) $\rightarrow$ **1.2 % oil ($4.7\%$)**, demonstrating that multiple cracking becomes fully saturated when $J_b'/J_{tip} \ge 3.0$ is attained.
4. **Stable control of fine multiple crack widths ($70\ \mu\text{m}$) and crack spacing (2.4 mm)**:
   - Crack widths remain below $100\ \mu\text{m}$ even under large strains of $4\%$ or more, guaranteeing excellent self-healing and watertight durability.

## Methods

- **Micromechanics optimal design criteria (Section 2 & Figs. 1~3)**:
  - Fiber-bridging complementary energy: $J_b' = \sigma_0 \delta_0 - \int_0^{\delta_0} \sigma(\delta) d\delta$.
  - Conditions for saturated PSH: $\frac{J_b'}{J_{tip}} \ge 3.0$, $\frac{\sigma_0}{\sigma_{fc}} \ge 1.2$.
  - Target interface domain: $G_d \le 2.2\ \text{J/m}^2$, $\tau_0 \in [1.0, 2.1]\ \text{MPa}$, $\beta \le 1.5$.
- **Single-fiber pullout test (Section 3.2 & Figs. 4~6)**:
  - Equipment: high-precision micro-pullout tester ($0.05\ \mu\text{m}$ displacement resolution).
  - Fiber: Kuraray PVA fiber ($d_f = 39\ \mu\text{m}$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}$, strength 1600 MPa, elastic modulus 42.8 GPa).
  - Five levels of oiling treatment: **0.0 %, 0.3 %, 0.5 %, 0.8 %, 1.2 %**.
- **Composite uniaxial direct-tension test (Section 3.3 & Fig. 8)**:
  - Mix: W/C = 0.45, S/C = 0.40 (silica sand $d_{50} = 100\ \mu\text{m}$), $V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$.
  - Dumbbell-shaped plate specimens ($304.8 \times 76.2 \times 12.7\ \text{mm}$), displacement control at $0.005\ \text{mm/s}$.

### 2. Interpretation of interface debonding and the saturated multiple-cracking mechanism of PSH
- **Suppression of chemical bonding and preservation of fiber integrity**: untreated PVA fibers have their surfaces torn during pullout due to strong hydrogen bonding with the hydrophilic hydrates, undergoing shear delamination, whereas the 1.2 % oiling coating layer relaxes the chemical bond to $1.61\ \text{J/m}^2$, inducing full-length sliding pullout without fiber rupture.
- **Physical significance of the energy performance index $J_b'/J_{tip} \ge 3.0$**: when the oiling content is 0.8 % or higher, the bridging complementary energy of the composite exceeds three times the matrix fracture toughness, overcoming local scatter in the flaw size distribution within the material and forming fully saturated multiple cracking with a crack spacing of 2.4 mm across the entire gauge length.

- **Overall conclusion**: this is the paper in which Victor C. Li solved a molecular-level hydrophilic-interface problem through oiling surface tailoring, completed for the first time in the world a $4.7\%$ ultra-high-ductility ECC using PVA fibers, and gave birth to the commercial Kuraray REC15 fiber — the most shining, immortal landmark original paper in the history of cementitious composite materials.
