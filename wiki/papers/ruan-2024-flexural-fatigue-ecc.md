## Summary
This study established the flexural fatigue behavior and fatigue life (S-N curves) of high-ductility cement composites (PE-ECC and PVA-ECC) applied to road pavements, bridge decks, and railway slabs through four-point flexural cyclic loading tests. Under stress levels ($S = \sigma_{max}/f_r = 0.60 \sim 0.90$) and a frequency of 5 Hz, the deflection increase over fatigue cycles, the decay of the dynamic modulus of elasticity, and multiple microcrack propagation behavior were measured in detail. A two-parameter Weibull distribution model was applied to derive fatigue life prediction equations by failure probability, demonstrating that ECC possesses a fatigue life more than 100 times superior to that of ordinary concrete.

## Key Contributions
- **Identification of the three-stage flexural fatigue damage progression mechanism of ECC**: (1) an initial stage of multiple microcrack formation and rapid stiffness loss ($< 10 \% N_f$), (2) a stable microcrack propagation and linear fatigue deformation stage ($10 \sim 90 \% N_f$), and (3) a stage of major-crack localization and rapid failure ($> 90 \% N_f$).
- **Establishment of a Weibull statistics-based $S-N-P$ fatigue life prediction model**: derivation of the flexural fatigue limit stress ratio ($S_e = 0.68$) at the 95 % reliability level.
- **Demonstration of crack width retention performance under fatigue loading**: crack widths remained at or below $40 \mu m$ even during $10^6$ cycles of fatigue loading, securing durability under fatigue environments.

## Methods
- **Materials**: high-strength PE-ECC ($V_f = 1.5 \%$, $f_c = 65 MPa$, flexural strength $f_r = 16.5 MPa$).
- **Fatigue test**: $100 \times 100 \times 400 mm$ beams, four-point flexural fatigue test (load ratio $R = 0.1$, frequency 5 Hz, up to $2 \times 10^6$ cycles).
- **Instrumentation**: real-time recording of midspan dynamic deflection through laser displacement gauges, and fiber damage analysis of fracture surfaces.

## Results
- At a fatigue stress ratio of $S = 0.70$, no fatigue failure occurred during $2 \times 10^6$ cycles, achieving infinite life (a 36 % improvement over the ordinary concrete fatigue limit of $S \approx 0.50$).
- Demonstrated that the high-ductility fiber bridging action continuously dissipates stress concentration at crack tips under repeated loading.
