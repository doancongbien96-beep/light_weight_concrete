# Flexural Performance of Precast Steel-UHTCC Composite Bridge Decks with Wet Joints: Integral-Equivalent $98.5\%$ Capacity, Crack Width Control to $0.04\ \text{mm}$, and a High-Precision Prediction Model ($R^2 = 0.96$)

## Summary

To fundamentally block the early cracking and leakage problems at field joints (wet joints) of assembled precast steel-concrete composite bridge decks and to secure rapid assembly constructability, this study designed **large-scale steel-UHTCC composite deck members ($2800 \times 600 \times 200\ \text{mm}$) applying 2.0 vol.% PVA ultra-high toughness cementitious composite (UHTCC: 28-day compressive strength $52.0\ \text{MPa}$, uniaxial tensile ductility $4.5\%$) integrally for both the precast panels and the wet joint casting material**, and developed static four-point flexural loading tests, 2D-DIC strain field mapping, interfacial bond shear tests, and a theoretical crack width prediction model. The composite deck with wet joints perfectly expressed **98.5 % of the flexural ultimate capacity of the integral cast deck, controlled maximum service-load crack width below $0.04\ \text{mm}$, and established a high-precision UHTCC crack width prediction formula with coefficient of determination $R^2 = 0.96$**. This is the landmark origin paper of Prof. Shi-Lang Xu and Prof. Qing-Hua Li of Zhejiang University, China, with Prof. Wei Gao of UNSW Australia, Engineering Structures, 2025.

## Key Contributions

1. **Full realization of structural continuity of wet joints in precast composite bridge decks**:
   - Unlike conventional concrete wet joints suffering premature crack opening and capacity loss at the joint, UHTCC joints achieved 98.5 % flexural capacity and continuous stiffness identical to integral cast decks.
2. **Strict control of joint crack width to $0.04\ \text{mm}$ or less with waterproofing secured**:
   - The high local tensile stress around loop bar splices was dissipated by UHTCC multiple fine cracking, limiting joint crack width to $40\ \mu\text{m}$ or less.
3. **Proposal of a dedicated high-precision crack width prediction model for UHCC composite decks ($R^2 = 0.96$)**:
   - A modified crack width formula integrating fiber bridging stress-crack opening ($\sigma-w$) relations with rebar-matrix bond slip overcame the over-prediction limits of ACI 318 and Eurocode 2.
4. **Assembly constructability innovation through 40 % shortening of rebar lap splice length**:
   - The excellent bond strength of UHTCC substantially reduced wet joint width and rebar lap length, enabling rapid construction of precast bridges.

## Methods

- **Specimen design and mixes (Section 2 & Tables 1~3)**:
  - Member dimensions: $2800 \times 600 \times 200\ \text{mm}$ (steel T-girder + $50\ \text{mm}$ deck slab, shear stud connection).
  - Specimen series (6 full-scale members total):
    1. `C-F / C-J`: ordinary concrete integral cast / wet joint.
    2. `U-F / U-J`: UHTCC integral cast / UHTCC wet joint (loop bar splice).
    3. `U-JE`: strengthened UHTCC wet joint.
  - UHTCC mix: P·O 42.5 cement + fly ash (FA/C = 2.5, W/B = 0.25, Kuraray PVA fiber $V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$).
- **Flexural testing and instrumentation (Sections 2.3~2.5 & Figs. 1~5)**:
  - Four-point flexural loading test (span $2400\ \text{mm}$, pure bending zone $600\ \text{mm}$).
  - 2D-DIC full-field imaging measurement and high-resolution LVDT deflection tracking.

### 2. Interpretation of joint interface stress redistribution and crack modeling
- **Loop bar-UHTCC interface shear bridging**: the 3D PVA fiber network of UHTCC absorbed the stress discontinuity at wet joint boundaries, converting interface debonding cracks into 25 finely dispersed cracks.
- **High consistency of the theoretical crack width prediction**: the derived UHTCC crack width prediction model matched measured values with $R^2 = 0.96$ and mean error of $5.2\%$.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is the landmark origin in which Prof. Shi-Lang Xu's team applied UHTCC wet joints to precast steel-UHTCC composite bridge decks, demonstrating load equivalence of 98.5 % versus integral cast decks and ultra-tight crack width control at 0.04 mm or less together with a high-precision prediction model.
