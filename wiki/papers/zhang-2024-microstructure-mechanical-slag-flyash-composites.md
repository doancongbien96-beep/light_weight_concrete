## Summary
This recent study evaluated the microstructure evolution, polycondensation reaction kinetics, mechanical strength, and drying shrinkage characteristics of ambient-cured alkali-activated composites in which colloidal nano-silica (Nano-Silica, 1.0 ~ 3.0 wt %) was incorporated as a combined modifier into a binary binder of ground granulated blast-furnace slag (GGBS) and fly ash (FA). It was established that nano-silica provides reaction nucleation sites during the early-stage aluminosilicate gelation process, optimizes the Si/Al ratio, and increases the degree of polymerization of C-(A)-S-H and N-A-S-H gels, thereby improving the 28 d compressive strength to 82.5 MPa and reducing the 90 d drying shrinkage by 30 %.

## Key Contributions
- **Elucidation of the mechanism by which nano-silica accelerates alkali-activated gel polymerization**: FTIR and 29Si NMR analysis confirmed the increased connectivity of the $Q^3$ and $Q^4$ silicate networks.
- **Dramatic densification of the micropore structure**: MIP analysis showed that the fraction of nanopores below $20 nm$ increased to 78 %, cutting off harmful capillary pores.
- **Improved drying shrinkage crack resistance**: formation of a dense gel matrix delayed the moisture evaporation rate and controlled long-term shrinkage strain below $480 \mu\epsilon$.

## Methods
- **Mix proportioning**: GGBS 50 % + FA 50 %, nano-silica (particle size 20 nm, 0 ~ 3 wt %), alkaline activator ($Na_2SiO_3 / NaOH = 1.5$).
- **Curing**: ambient air curing ($23 °C, 60 \% RH$).
- **Test items**: isothermal calorimetry heat of reaction, compressive strength, continuous drying shrinkage measurement, 29Si MAS-NMR.

## Results
- Mix incorporating 2.0 wt % nano-silica: 28 d compressive strength of $82.5 MPa$ (a 29 % increase over the plain mix at $64.0 MPa$), flexural strength of $11.2 MPa$.
- The drying shrinkage cracking index improved by 40 %, securing durability.
