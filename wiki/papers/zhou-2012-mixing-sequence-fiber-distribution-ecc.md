## Summary

Study by Dr. J. Zhou of TU Delft and Prof. Victor Li of the University of Michigan that precisely established how mixer charging order (Mixing Sequence: dry powder mixing followed by fiber addition vs dispersed addition after formation of the liquid slurry) affects the spatial dispersion coefficient ($\alpha_{disp}$) of fine PVA fibers (diameter 39 µm), suppression of fiber balling, single-fiber interfacial bond strength and direct-tension strain-hardening performance in manufacturing an environmentally friendly high-ductility composite with combined replacement by limestone powder (LP) and GGBS (LP/GGBS-ECC). It was demonstrated that dispersing fibers into the formed liquid slurry improved the dispersion coefficient from 0.52 to **0.88**, raising direct-tension strain capacity **1.85-fold (2.5 % → 4.65 %)**.

## Key Contributions

- **Clarification of the fluid-mechanics mechanism by which mixing sequence governs fiber dispersion**: shear stress in the liquid slurry rapidly debundles and disperses fiber bundles.
- **Quantification of the dispersion coefficient ($\alpha_{disp}$) through fluorescence cross-sectional image analysis**: cross-sectional fiber density deviation reduced by 60 %.
- **Demonstration of a tensile strain capacity of 4.65 % with a saturated multiple cracking network**: stable PSH behavior without early fracture.
- **Standardization of mixing protocols for ready-mixed concrete plants and large on-site mixers**: process optimization toward ECC commercialization.

## Methods

- Mix: cement + LP (30 %) + GGBS (30 %), $W/B = 0.30$, Kuraray REC15 PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$).
- Mixing sequence variables:
  - Sequence A (dry addition): dry mixing of cement/aggregates/fibers $\rightarrow$ water + superplasticizer added.
  - Sequence B (slurry addition): cement/aggregates/water mixed for 3 min $\rightarrow$ fibers dispersed into the uniform slurry for a further 3 min.
- Testing: dog-bone direct tension test ($0.5 mm/min$), dispersion coefficient by UV fluorescence image analysis, 28 d compressive strength.

## Results

- Comparison of fiber dispersion coefficient and tensile properties by mixing sequence:

| Mixing sequence | Mixing method | Fiber dispersion coefficient $\alpha_{disp}$ | 28 d compressive strength (MPa) | Uniaxial tensile strength (MPa) | Ultimate tensile strain capacity (%) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Sequence A | dry mixing followed by water addition | 0.52 | 48.5 | 4.10 | 2.50 % (localized failure) |
| **Sequence B** | **slurry mixing followed by fiber addition** | **0.88 (+69 %)** | **52.0** | **5.45 (+33 %)** | **4.65 % (+86 %)** |
