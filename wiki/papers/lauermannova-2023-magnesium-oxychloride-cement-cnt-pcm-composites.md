# Magnesium Oxychloride Cement (MOC) Reinforced with MWCNTs and Microencapsulated PCM: Preserving 42.5 MPa Compressive Strength and Latent Heat Storage ($17.6\ \text{kJ/kg}$) Mechanics

## Summary

To mitigate indoor temperature fluctuations while maintaining structural strength in low-carbon calcined magnesia ($\text{MgO}$) based Magnesium Oxychloride Cement (MOC), researchers at UCT Prague and CTU in Prague developed smart thermal storage composites. The system incorporates **multi-walled carbon nanotubes (MWCNT: 0.2 wt.%) and microencapsulated paraffin phase change materials (PCM: 10 wt.%, 20 wt.%) into needle-like Phase 5 ($5\text{Mg(OH)}_2\cdot\text{MgCl}_2\cdot 8\text{H}_2\text{O}$) crystalline MOC matrices**. The authors characterized 28 d compressive strength, flexural strength, DSC phase transition enthalpies, thermal conductivity ($\lambda$), volumetric heat capacity ($c_p$), MIP pore size distributions, and microstructures. Results demonstrated that even at 20 wt.% PCM loading, the MWCNT nano-bridging network preserved a **compressive strength of $42.5\ \text{MPa}$ and flexural strength of $11.2\ \text{MPa}$ while providing a latent heat storage capacity of $\Delta H = 17.59\ \text{kJ/kg}$ at a comfortable $19.0\ ^\circ\text{C}$ phase transition temperature**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Development of low-carbon MOC composites with MWCNT-PCM functionalization**:
   - Addressed compressive strength degradation commonly caused by PCM incorporation by utilizing inherently high-strength Phase 5 needle-like crystalline MOC binders.
2. **Nanostructural reinforcement and strength offset via 0.2 wt.% MWCNTs**:
   - Carbon nanotubes formed a 3D bridging network between Phase 5 needle crystal bundles and microcapsule PCM shells, stably preserving $42.5\ \text{MPa}$ compressive strength at 20 wt.% PCM content.
3. **High-efficiency latent heat storage ($17.59\ \text{kJ/kg}$) in the indoor comfort range ($19.0\ ^\circ\text{C}$)**:
   - DSC measurements confirmed a melting enthalpy of $17.59\ \text{kJ/kg}$ and solidification enthalpy of $22.48\ \text{kJ/kg}$, buffering daytime overheating and releasing heat at night.
4. **Chemical compatibility between Phase 5 crystals and carbon nanotubes**:
   - Confirmed via XRD and SEM that MWCNTs remain stably dispersed in magnesium chloride solutions, fostering dense interlocking Phase 5 networks.

## Methods

- **Materials and MOC-PCM formulations (Section 2.1, Table 1)**:
  - MOC precursor: Light-burned magnesia powder ($\text{MgO}$ purity 81 %, $d_{50} = 15\ \mu\text{m}$) + $\text{MgCl}_2\cdot 6\text{H}_2\text{O}$ solution ($\text{MgO}/\text{MgCl}_2$ molar ratio = 5:1, stoichiometric Phase 5 ratio).
  - Nano-reinforcement: MWCNTs (outer diameter $10\sim 20\ \text{nm}$, length $10\sim 30\ \mu\text{m}$, purity $> 95\%$, 0.2 wt.% of binder).
  - Latent heat material: Microencapsulated paraffin PCM (Micronal DS 5040, core melting point $23\ ^\circ\text{C}$, capsule diameter $5\sim 20\ \mu\text{m}$).
  - 4 mixtures: `MOC-REF` (neat MOC), `MOC-CNT-REF` (MOC+CNT), `MOC-CNT-M10` (PCM 10%), `MOC-CNT-M20` (PCM 20%).
- **Testing protocols (Sections 2.2–2.4, Fig. 1)**:
  - Mechanical properties: $40 \times 40 \times 160\ \text{mm}$ prisms tested for 28 d flexural and compressive strengths (EN 196-1, 3-point bending).
  - Thermal properties: Netzsch DSC 204 F1 ($-20\sim 60\ ^\circ\text{C}$, 5 $^\circ\text{C}$/min), ISOMET 2114 thermal conductivity analyzer.
  - Microstructure: FE-SEM (Hitachi S-4700), XRD (Bruker D8 Discover).

### 2. Needle crystal interlocking and nano-reinforcement mechanics
- **Dense Phase 5 needle network**: The primary hydration product, $5\text{Mg(OH)}_2\cdot\text{MgCl}_2\cdot 8\text{H}_2\text{O}$ Phase 5, forms fine needles ($1\sim 5\ \mu\text{m}$ length) that interlock around spherical PCM capsules.
- **MWCNT microcrack bridging**: MWCNTs bridge Phase 5 crystal boundaries, mitigating stress concentrations around PCM capsules and sustaining strengths above 40 MPa.

- **Overall conclusion**: This study developed high-strength smart thermal storage panels by pairing low-carbon MOC with MWCNT nano-bridging, preserving 42.5 MPa compressive strength with $17.59\ \text{kJ/kg}$ latent heat capacity.
