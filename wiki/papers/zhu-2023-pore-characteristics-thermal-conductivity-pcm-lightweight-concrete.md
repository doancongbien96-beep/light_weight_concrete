# Unified Prediction Model for the Effective Thermal Conductivity ($\lambda = 0.42\ \text{W/m}\cdot\text{K}$) of Microencapsulated PCM Lightweight Aggregate Concrete Based on X-ray CT Three-Dimensional Pore Characteristics (Porosity, Flatness, Sphericity)

## Summary

To maximize the heating and cooling energy efficiency of buildings and to design the integrated insulation and heat storage performance of lightweight structures, **shale ceramic lightweight aggregate concrete incorporating microencapsulated phase change material (MPCM: paraffin core, melamine-formaldehyde shell, $T_m = 24.5\ ^\circ\text{C}$, $\Delta H_m = 145.0\ \text{J/g}$) at weight fractions of $0, 10, 20, 30\ \text{wt.}\%$ (MPCM-LWAC) was produced**. **High-resolution X-ray computed tomography (X-ray CT) combined with stereology was applied to precisely extract the three-dimensional pore characteristics inside the composite (porosity, pore flatness, degree of sphericity)**, and a **new multiphase effective thermal conductivity (ETC: Effective Thermal Conductivity) theoretical model reflecting these characteristics was established ($R^2 = 0.98$)**, identifying the optimal performance of **$20\ \text{wt.}\%$ MPCM incorporation: 28-day compressive strength of $26.5\ \text{MPa}$, latent heat of $28.5\ \text{J/g}$, and thermal conductivity of $\lambda = 0.42\ \text{W/m}\cdot\text{K}$ ($75\%$ insulation compared with ordinary concrete)**. This is the landmark Case Studies in Construction Materials 2023 original paper by the research team of Professors Weihua Ding and Faning Dang of Xi'an University of Technology, China.

## Key Contributions

1. **Quantification of the three-dimensional fine pore characteristics of MPCM-incorporated concrete based on X-ray CT**:
   - The three-dimensional spatial pore morphology, in which the sphericity of fine pores changes from $0.78 \rightarrow 0.62$ and the flatness varies with MPCM addition, was extracted precisely and non-destructively.
2. **Establishment of an effective thermal conductivity (ETC) theoretical model reflecting pore structure morphology**:
   - Overcoming the limitations of simple series/parallel and Maxwell models, a multiphase prediction equation integrating the pore shape factor and the MPCM phase-change core-shell heat transfer was derived ($R^2 = 0.98$).
3. **Identification of the insulation-heat storage synergy between lightweight aggregate (LWA) and MPCM**:
   - The low thermal conductivity of the porous shale aggregate combined with the high latent heat storage capacity of MPCM lowered the thermal conductivity to $0.42\ \text{W/m}\cdot\text{K}$ while attenuating the indoor temperature fluctuation amplitude by $60\%$.
4. **Securing the compressive strength ($26.5\ \text{MPa}$) required of structural lightweight concrete**:
   - Even at $20\ \text{wt.}\%$ MPCM incorporation, the strength requirement for structural lightweight concrete was satisfied, proving applicability as a structural material for walls and slabs.

## Methods

- **Materials and MPCM-LWAC mix design (Section 2 & Tables 1~3)**:
  - Binder: P·O 42.5 ordinary Portland cement.
  - Aggregates: expanded shale ceramic lightweight aggregate ($d = 5\sim 15\ \text{mm}$, apparent density $1250\ \text{kg/m}^3$) + river sand.
  - MPCM: paraffin/melamine resin capsule (mean particle diameter $d_{50} = 15\ \mu\text{m}$, $T_m = 24.5\ ^\circ\text{C}$, $\Delta H_m = 145.0\ \text{J/g}$).
  - Four MPCM incorporation levels: $0, 10, 20, 30\ \text{wt.}\%$ (by cement weight).
- **CT scanning and thermal property measurement (Sections 2.2~2.4 & Figs. 1~5)**:
  - X-ray CT: GE v|tome|x m industrial 3D CT ($180\ \text{kV}$, voxel size $15\ \mu\text{m}$).
  - Thermal conductivity measurement: transient plane source thermal constants analyzer (Hot Disk TPS 2500S, $20\ ^\circ\text{C}$).
  - Latent heat: DSC (PerkinElmer DSC 8000).

### 2. Interpretation of the multiphase heat transfer and pore morphology resistance mechanisms

- **Heat flux detour induced by reduced pore sphericity**: the irregular flattened pores generated by MPCM addition increase the tortuosity of the heat transfer paths, blocking phonon conduction.
- **Thermal interface resistance of the core-shell capsules**: the organic polymer shell of the microcapsule wall blocks the thermal bridge between the cement matrix and the paraffin core, reducing the effective thermal conductivity substantially to $0.42\ \text{W/m}\cdot\text{K}$.

- **Overall conclusion**: By combining X-ray CT three-dimensional pore morphology with multiphase heat transfer theory, this paper established a high-precision analytical model that predicts the effective thermal conductivity of MPCM lightweight aggregate concrete with $R^2 = 0.98$ and demonstrated smart heat storage insulating concrete design with a strength of 26.5 MPa and $\lambda = 0.42\ \text{W/m}\cdot\text{K}$; a landmark original paper.
