# Mechanical, Thermal, and Solar Thermoregulation Performance of Functional Foam Concrete Containing Shape-Stabilized Attapulgite-Based Phase Change Material (ATP/C-M SSPCM)

## Summary

To simultaneously achieve ultra-light weight, high thermal insulation, and passive indoor thermoregulation, Professor Osman Gencel and co-workers at Bartin University synthesized a shape-stabilized phase change composite (ATP/C-M SSPCM, 45 wt.% loading, latent heat $64.1\ \text{J/g}$) by vacuum impregnating a eutectic mixture of capric-myristic acid (C-M: melting point $21.5\ ^\circ\text{C}$, latent heat of fusion $142.5\ \text{J/g}$) into the acicular nanoporous structure of natural attapulgite clay (ATP). Replacing silica sand in foam concrete by 15 %, 30 %, and 45 wt.% with ATP/C-M SSPCM, the authors characterized fresh spread, dry density, 7 d/28 d compressive and flexural strengths, water absorption, sorptivity, thermal conductivity, drying shrinkage, and solar thermal regulation under simulated radiation. Capillary forces within ATP nanopores completely prevented C-M leakage (0.0 % leakage at 55 °C). Foam concrete with 30 wt.% ATP/C-M (FCPCM-30) achieved a dry density of $890\ \text{kg/m}^3$, 28 d compressive strength of **$13.27\ \text{MPa}$**, thermal conductivity of **$0.22\ \text{W/m}\cdot\text{K}$**, and latent heat capacity of **$19.2\ \text{J/g}$**, damping peak indoor temperatures by **$4.8\ ^\circ\text{C}$** and suppressing thermal fluctuations by over 40 %.

## Key Contributions

1. **Synthesis of leakage-free high-latent-heat SSPCM based on natural nano-clay attapulgite (ATP)**:
   - Encapsulated up to 45 wt.% C-M organic eutectic acids within the 1D nanofibrous pore network of attapulgite, maintaining chemical and thermal stability without mass loss after 100 thermal cycles.
2. **Development of lightweight structural-grade phase change foam concrete (FCPCM)**:
   - Retained a 28 d compressive strength of **13.27 MPa** at a dry density of $890\ \text{kg/m}^3$, satisfying structural insulating wall criteria (>10 MPa).
3. **Ultra-low thermal conductivity ($0.22\ \text{W/m}\cdot\text{K}$) and 4.8 °C peak temperature damping**:
   - Combined porous foam concrete matrix insulation with latent heat thermal storage, reducing external heat ingress by 50 % and mitigating daytime indoor overheating.

## Methods

- **Raw materials and ATP/C-M SSPCM synthesis (Table 1 & Figures S1–S3)**:
  - Binders: CEM I 42.5 R ordinary Portland cement (28 d strength 53.5 MPa).
  - Clay carrier: Attapulgite clay ($\text{SiO}_2$ 58.2 %, $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$ 10.5 %, $\text{MgO}$ 9.8 %, $d_{50} = 45\ \mu\text{m}$).
  - Eutectic PCM: Capric acid (CA) 73.5 wt.% + myristic acid (MA) 26.5 wt.% (phase change temperature $21.5\ ^\circ\text{C}$, latent heat $142.5\ \text{J/g}$).
  - Vacuum impregnation: Liquid C-M absorbed into ATP at 50 °C $\rightarrow$ ATP/C-M SSPCM (latent heat $64.1\ \text{J/g}$, 0.0 % leakage).
- **Foam concrete mix design (Table 1)**:
  - Foaming agent: Protein-based foaming agent (density $45\ \text{kg/m}^3$).
  - Cement slurry: W/C = 0.40, sand replaced by ATP/C-M at 0 % (FCPCM-0), 15 % (FCPCM-15), 30 % (FCPCM-30), and 45 % (FCPCM-45 wt.%).
- **Testing program**:
  - Compressive and flexural strengths: $40\ \text{mm} \times 40\ \text{mm} \times 160\ \text{mm}$ prisms (ASTM C348/C349 at 7 and 28 days).
  - Thermal conductivity: ISOMET 2114 probe.
  - Drying shrinkage: ASTM C596 (28 d length change).
  - Solar thermoregulation: $200\ \text{mm} \times 200\ \text{mm} \times 200\ \text{mm}$ test chamber under 250 W infrared lamp heating for 6 h followed by natural cooling.

### 2. Microstructural mechanisms and thermal energy savings
- **Optimal 30 % replacement**: FCPCM-30 suffered only an 11.8 % compressive strength reduction while lowering thermal conductivity by 31 % and providing $19.2\ \text{J/g}$ latent heat capacity.
- **Indoor thermal comfort**: The phase change temperature of $21.5\ ^\circ\text{C}$ matched human indoor thermal comfort ranges, cutting HVAC operational runtimes by over 30 %.

- **Overall conclusion**: This study established a smart building envelope composite by integrating a shape-stabilized attapulgite-PCM composite into foam concrete, achieving structural load-bearing capacity (13.3 MPa), ultra-low thermal conductivity (0.22 W/mK), and passive indoor thermal regulation.
