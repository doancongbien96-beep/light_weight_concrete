## Summary
A study by Professor J. Zhang's research team at Tsinghua University clarifying the early-age (1 to 7 days) uniaxial tensile creep behavior, nonlinear stress relaxation characteristics, and restrained drying-shrinkage cracking resistance of ductile composites (PVA-ECC), using the restrained ring test (ASTM C1581) and a tensile creep loading frame, in order to assess the cracking risk from drying shrinkage and hydration heat stresses arising at early ages in massive concrete and overlay repair structures. It was demonstrated that the high early tensile creep strain of PVA-ECC ($\epsilon_{cr} = 380 \times 10^{-6}$) relaxes restrained tensile stress by more than **65 %**, preventing early crack formation at its source.

## Key Contributions
- **Early-age (1 d, 3 d, 7 d) PVA-ECC tensile creep constitutive equation established**: an early-age stress relaxation function formulated by modifying the B3 creep model.
- **65 % relaxation of restrained shrinkage stress quantified**: combined action of viscoelastic C-S-H gel slip and micro creep at fiber-matrix interfaces.
- **Crack-free stability demonstrated through the ASTM C1581 ring test**: whereas ordinary concrete failed through-cracked within 8 days, ECC remained crack-free beyond 90 days.
- **Foundation presented for jointless design of large floor slabs and bridge expansion joints**: standardization of shrinkage crack control design.

## Methods
- Mix: PVA-ECC (Kuraray REC15, $V_f = 2.0 \%$, $f_c = 48.5 MPa$, $FA/C = 1.2$).
- Tensile creep tests: temperature $20 ± 1 °C$, RH $60 \%$; sustained tensile stress ratios ($\sigma/f_t = 0.30$ and $0.50$, loaded at ages 1, 3, and 7 days, with displacement measured over 90 days).
- Restrained ring tests: continuous measurement with strain gauges on the inner perimeter of an ASTM C1581 steel ring.

## Results
- 28-day cumulative tensile creep strains and stress relaxation rates by loading age:

| Loading age (days) | Sustained stress level | Initial elastic strain ($\times 10^{-6}$) | 28-day creep strain ($\times 10^{-6}$) | Creep coefficient $\phi(t, t_0)$ | Stress relaxation (%) | Ring crack day (days) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
