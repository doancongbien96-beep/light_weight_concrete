## Summary
A research paper from China University of Mining and Technology that precisely characterized, through nanoindentation, single-fiber pull-out tests, and uniaxial tension tests, the multiscale interaction mechanisms by which nano titanium dioxide (Nano-TiO2, 1.0 ~ 5.0 wt %) particles possessing self-cleaning and photocatalytic air-purification functions affect the nanoscale C-S-H gel elastic modulus of high-ductility composites (PVA-ECC), the microscale single-fiber pull-out interfacial bond parameters ($\tau_0, G_d$), and the macroscopic uniaxial direct tensile strain-hardening envelope. When doped with 3.0 wt % nano TiO2, the high-density C-S-H gel fraction increased by 28 % and the fiber interfacial frictional stress $\tau_0$ was optimized from $1.45 \rightarrow 1.85 MPa$, achieving a 28 d compressive strength of **52.5 MPa**, a direct tensile strength of **5.8 MPa**, and a tensile strain capacity of **4.6 %**.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishment of a three-stage nano-micro-macro multiscale bridging model**: completed back-calculation from nanoindentation hardness/elastic modulus to single-fiber interfacial parameters and the composite tensile envelope.
- **Demonstrated hydration acceleration and ITZ microstructure densification by nano TiO2**: the nucleation effect of the nanoparticles increased the effective contact area on fiber surfaces by 35 %.
- **Maximization of the PSH performance index**: complementary energy index $J_b'/J_{tip} = 5.8$ secured, enabling formation of a stable multiple fine crack network.
- **Simultaneous realization of smart self-cleaning and high-ductility structural cladding**: rhodamine B dye degradation of at least 90 % was achieved.

## Methods
- Mix: OPC + fly ash ($FA/C = 1.2$) + anatase-phase Nano-TiO2 (15 nm, 1.0, 3.0, 5.0 wt %), Kuraray PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$).
- Multiscale measurements:
  - Nanoscale: Hysitron TriboIndenter nanoindentation (indentation load $1000 \mu N$, grid mapping).
  - Microscale: single-fiber pull-out test ($0.1 mm/min$, load-slip curves).
  - Macroscale: dogbone direct tension test ($0.5 mm/min$), 28 d compressive strength.
