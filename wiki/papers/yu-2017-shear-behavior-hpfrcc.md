## Summary
A study systematically evaluating the two-dimensional shear stress-shear strain ($\tau-\gamma$) behavior, shear strain-hardening capability, and shear multiple fine crack propagation mechanisms of ductile fiber composites (PE-ECC and PVA-ECC) under pure shear loading through Iosipescu V-notch direct shear tests. It was clarified that PE-ECC exhibits remarkable shear strain-hardening performance reaching a maximum shear strength of 9.2 MPa and an ultimate shear strain of 6.8 % in the pure shear state. A quantitative correlation transformation model between uniaxial tensile strength ($f_t$) and tensile strain capacity ($\epsilon_u$) and pure shear mechanical indicators was derived.

## Key Contributions
- **Pure shear strain-hardening curves derived through Iosipescu direct shear tests**: shear strain of $6.8 \%$ exhibited from post-yield until failure.
- **Shear bridging fiber orientation mechanism clarified**: fibers bridge more than 50 fine shear cracks formed along the $45°$ principal tensile diagonal direction, blocking shear slip failure.
- **Shear-to-tensile strength ratio ($\tau_u/f_t \approx 1.25$) formulated**: providing the shear constitutive equation needed for shear capacity analysis of structural shear walls and beam members.

## Methods
- **Materials**: PE-ECC ($V_f = 1.5 \%, 2.0 \%$) and PVA-ECC ($V_f = 2.0 \%$).
- **Shear testing**: ASTM D5379 Iosipescu V-notch shear specimens, displacement-controlled pure shear loading.
- **Instrumentation**: DIC two-dimensional shear strain field measurement, optical microscope crack width measurement.

## Results
- PE-ECC ($V_f = 2.0 \%$): pure shear strength $\tau_u = 9.25 MPa$, shear strain capacity $\gamma_u = 6.80 \%$, mean crack width $25 \mu m$.
- PVA-ECC: pure shear strength $\tau_u = 5.60 MPa$, shear strain capacity $\gamma_u = 3.85 \%$.
