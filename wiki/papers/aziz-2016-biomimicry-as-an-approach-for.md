## Summary

Representative paper by Prof. M.S. Aziz's research team at Alexandria University that establishes an integrated design framework combining the three biomimicry approach levels (organism Form, material structure Material, ecosystem Process) with computational parametric optimization (Computational Parametric Optimization: Grasshopper, FEA) for applying the mechanisms of 3.85 billion years of evolution in living organisms (plant cell walls, mollusk-shell nacre, trabecular bone, honeycomb structures) to architectural and civil structural engineering. Through bio-inspired porous lattices and topology optimization, it systematizes design techniques that cut the self-weight of building structures by **45 %** while improving lateral stiffness and energy absorption capacity **2.2-fold**.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishes a three-tier hierarchical classification system for biomimetic structural design**: defines the Organism Level, Behavior Level, and Ecosystem Level.
- **Develops a Computational Bio-mimicry Workflow**: natural-form scanning $\rightarrow$ mathematical geometry parameterization $\rightarrow$ FEA topology optimization in structural mechanics.
- **Mechanical analysis of five representative structural systems in nature**: variable-density bone architecture (Trabecular Bone), hollow nodal reinforcement of bamboo (Bamboo nodes), hierarchical nacre brick-and-mortar structure (Nacre brick-and-mortar).
- **Realizes eco-friendly high-efficiency building structures**: carbon footprint cut 35 % and structural safety maximized.

## Methods
- Classification system: analysis of the three biomimicry levels based on the Zari framework.
- Computational tools: Rhinoceros 3D, Grasshopper parametric generator, ANSYS structural analysis solver.
- Application cases: long-span roof shells (leaf-venation structure), high-rise building envelopes (termite-mound natural ventilation), ultralight columns (bird skeleton).

## Results
- Mapping table for engineering imitation of natural biological structural mechanisms:

| Biomimicry source | Natural feature and principle | Engineering mechanism | Structural performance gain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Trabecular bone** | porous density distributed along principal stress trajectories | topology-optimized variable-density porous structure | self-weight **cut 45 %**, stiffness retained |
| **Nacre** | $95 \%$ aragonite platelets + $5 \%$ organic protein | hierarchical brick-and-mortar bridging slip | fracture toughness amplified **3000-fold** (eliminating brittleness) |
| **Bamboo** | outer high-density fibers + hollow node (Node) placement | graded functionally graded material (FGM) and buckling prevention | flexural–torsional resistance improved **2.4-fold** |
| **Termite mound** | internal convection circulation channels driven by a temperature gradient | passive thermal-circulation ventilation envelope | heating/cooling energy **cut 35 %** |
