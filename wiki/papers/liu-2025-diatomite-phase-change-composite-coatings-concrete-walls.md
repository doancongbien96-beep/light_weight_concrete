# Solar Thermal Storage and Insulation Performance of Diatomite–Polydopamine-Functionalized Form-Stable Phase Change Composite Coatings on Concrete Walls: Latent Heat Capacity $95.8\ \text{J/g}$ and Indoor Peak Temperature Reduction of $4.8\ ^\circ\text{C}$ Mechanisms

## Summary

To drastically cut the heating and cooling energy consumption of buildings and to absorb and preserve solar thermal energy efficiently, this paper — the latest landmark origin paper of Solar Energy Materials and Solar Cells 2025 by the research team of **Prof. Pan Guo (Soochow University, China) and Prof. Peng Gong (China University of Mining and Technology, CUMT)** — developed a **form-stable phase change composite (DT/PDA-PCM) in which a biomimetic polydopamine (Polydopamine: PDA) nanolayer modified onto the surfaces of natural porous diatomite (Diatomite: DT) maximizes the adsorption loading of organic paraffin-wax phase change material (PCM) at $52.5\ \text{wt.}\%$**, and demonstrated through reduced-scale building chamber experiments with the composite formulated into and applied as a coating paint on concrete wall surfaces, DSC thermodynamic analysis, 100 consecutive heating–cooling thermal cycles, and infrared thermographic tracking a **latent heat storage capacity of $95.8\ \text{J/g}$ (phase change temperature $27.5\ ^\circ\text{C}$), a summer daytime indoor peak temperature reduction of $4.8\ ^\circ\text{C}$, a 3.5 h delay in the winter nighttime indoor temperature drop, zero liquid-paraffin leakage rate ($0.0\%$) after 100 thermal cycles, and a $26.5\%$ saving in building heating/cooling energy**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Development of a diatomite–polydopamine (PDA) nano-hybrid PCM encapsulation technology**:
   - Forming an oleophilic PDA coating on the inner walls of diatomite's natural cylindrical nanopores ($10\sim 50\ \text{nm}$) boosts the capillary fixation force on the paraffin PCM by $35\%$ and blocks liquid-phase leakage at its source.
2. **Achievement of high latent heat storage density ($\Delta H = 95.8\ \text{J/g}$) and an indoor comfort phase change temperature**:
   - Designing the phase transition to occur within the residential indoor comfort temperature range ($26\sim 28\ ^\circ\text{C}$) so that heating and cooling loads are autonomously leveled (Peak-Shaving).
3. **Demonstration of an indoor peak temperature reduction of $4.8\ ^\circ\text{C}$ and thermal delay when applied to concrete walls**:
   - Even under intense solar irradiance, the composite coating layer absorbs solar heat as latent heat, delaying heat penetration into the concrete interior by more than 4 h.
4. **Loss-free long-term durability over 100 thermal cycles and establishment of an eco-friendly building-envelope standard**:
   - Establishing a non-toxic zero-energy building coating that preserves $98.2\%$ of its latent heat capacity even after 100 heating–cooling repetitions.

## Methods

- **DT/PDA-PCM synthesis and characterization (Section 2 & Tables 1–3 & Figs. 1–4)**:
  - Porous support: acid-washed purified natural diatomite (specific surface area $45.8\ \text{m}^2/\text{g}$).
  - PDA functionalization: stirring of dopamine hydrochloride in aqueous solution ($8.5\ \text{pH}$, 12 h self-polymerization coating).
  - PCM impregnation: vacuum heating impregnation method ($65\ ^\circ\text{C}$, absorption of pure paraffin at $52.5\ \text{wt.}\%$).
- **Thermodynamics and full-scale concrete wall testing (Sections 2.3–3.5 & Figs. 5–10)**:
  - DSC analysis: TA Instruments Q2000 ($-10\sim 60\ ^\circ\text{C}$, $5\ ^\circ\text{C/min}$).
  - Building chamber: $300 \times 300 \times 300\ \text{mm}$ concrete wall cube (coating thickness 2.0 mm, solar simulator $800\ \text{W/m}^2$).
  - Infrared thermographic camera: FLIR T540 continuous temperature mapping.

### 2. Interpretation of capillary nano-adsorption and thermal buffering mechanisms

- **Enhanced interfacial compatibility from polydopamine**: Catechol molecules in the PDA layer strongly cross-link the inorganic diatomite surface with the organic paraffin chains, absorbing expansion/contraction stresses during phase change and preventing interfacial detachment.
- **Maximized indoor thermal comfort**: Under a full daily cycle of solar irradiance fluctuation, the indoor temperature swing ($\Delta T$) is drastically eased by $42\%$, from $22.3\ ^\circ\text{C} \rightarrow 12.9\ ^\circ\text{C}$.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper — in which Prof. Pan Guo's research team at Soochow University used polydopamine-functionalized diatomite as the support to develop a highly stable phase change composite coating with a latent heat capacity of 95.8 J/g and demonstrated an indoor peak temperature reduction of 4.8 °C and an energy saving of 26.5 % when applied to concrete walls — is a top-tier landmark origin paper in eco-friendly zero-energy building envelopes and phase change energy storage.
