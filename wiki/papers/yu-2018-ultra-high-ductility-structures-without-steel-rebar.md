## Summary
An ASCE JMCE landmark paper in which Professor K.Q. Yu of Tongji University and Professor Jian-Guo Dai's research team at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University clarified the flexural capacity, load-deflection envelopes, modulus of rupture (MOR), and deflection ductility of rebar-free structural beams ($100 \times 100 \times 400 mm$) whose sections consist solely of an ultra-high ductility cementitious composite (UHDCC, uniaxial tensile strain capacity **8.0 ~ 10.5 %**) with steel reinforcement omitted entirely, through four-point bending experiments and nonlinear section analysis, in order to eliminate the rebar corrosion problem at its root in chloride and severe corrosive environments. Without steel bars, a flexural modulus of rupture of **22.5 MPa** and an ultimate deflection of **18.5 mm** were exhibited, demonstrating flexural capacity rivaling ordinary reinforced concrete (RC) beams and deflection ductility more than three times higher.

## Key Contributions
- **Concept of a rebar-free ultra-high ductility structural element demonstrated**: corrosion-free semi-permanent structures realized by blocking rebar corrosion risk at the source 100 %.
- **Flexural moment support mechanism by the ultra-ductile tensile membrane clarified**: a tensile strain capacity of 10 % shifts the neutral axis of the flexural section upward, securing a large moment arm.
- **Deflection ductility index $\mu_\Delta > 8.5$ achieved**: perfectly ductile deflection behavior without rebar fracture or brittle crushing.
- **Design formulas established for marine breakwaters and precast thin-plate structures**: foundation laid for maintenance-free zero-cost structural systems.

## Methods
- Materials: UHDCC (low-heat cement + silica fume, Spectra PE fiber $V_f = 2.0 \%$, $f_c = 68.5 MPa$, direct tensile strength $8.5 MPa$, $\epsilon_u = 10.2 \%$).
- Flexural testing: four-point bending tests of $100 \times 100 \times 400 mm$ prismatic beams (span 300 mm, LVDT measurement), compared one-to-one against RC beams with reinforcement ($\rho = 1.0 \%$).

## Results
- Comparison table of flexural mechanical performance between rebar-free UHDCC beams and standard RC beams:
