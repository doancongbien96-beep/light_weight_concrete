## Summary

Professors R. Arunothayan and J. Sanjayan's team (Swinburne University of Technology)
elucidated the open time, extrudability, buildability, shape-retention ability (SRA), and the
mechanism of unidirectional short-fiber alignment under nozzle-extrusion shear flow for a
3D-printable ultra-high performance fiber-reinforced concrete built to erect complex free-form
structures without formwork. Incorporating straight brass-coated micro steel fibers
($V_f = 2.0 \%, L_f = 13 mm, d_f = 0.2 mm$) into a modified Andreasen & Anderson optimally
packed matrix, the printable UHPFRC achieved a nozzle-extrusion-direction flexural modulus of
rupture (MOR) of **32.5 MPa**, uniaxial compressive strength of **118.0 MPa**, and interlayer
direct bond strength of **5.8 MPa**.

## Key Contributions

- Completed mix design for 3D-printable UHPFRC with optimal particle packing (A&A model,
  $q=0.23$): excellent flowability and shape retention at an ultra-low $W/B = 0.16$.
- Demonstrated unidirectional steel fiber alignment by nozzle extrusion flow: nozzle-size
  confinement ($30 \times 15 mm$) lifted perpendicular-to-printing flexural strength to
  $32.5 MPa$ — a **62.5 %** surge over mold-cast ($20.0 MPa$).
- Quantified build anisotropy: a three-dimensional directional strength envelope of 118 MPa
  longitudinal, 108 MPa vertical, and 92 MPa lateral compressive strength.
- Successfully 3D-printed a full-scale 25-layer, 2 m curved structural member — verified
  constructability with zero collapse or sagging.

## Methods

- Binder mix: ordinary Portland cement 0.7 + silica fume 0.3 (mass ratio); blended silica sands
  in three sizes (Fine $176 \mu m$ 0.4, Medium $498 \mu m$ 0.3, Coarse $840 \mu m$ 0.3);
  $W/B = 0.16$; PCE superplasticizer 0.015; VMA 0.003.
- Fibers: brass-coated straight steel fiber ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 200 µm, length 13 mm,
  tensile strength 2500 MPa, elastic modulus 200 GPa).
- Printing system: piston-extrusion 3D printer with a 45° inclined $30 \times 15 mm$ nozzle
  on a gantry system ($1.8 \times 1.6 \times 1.8 m$).
- Tests: three-point flexure ($280 \times 25 \times 25 mm$, span 160 mm), 25 mm cube
  compression ($0.33 MPa/s$), direct-tension interlayer bond ($50 \times 25 \times 25 mm$).

## Results

- Comparative mechanics/physics data, 3D-printed versus mold-cast UHPFRC:

| Property | Test condition and direction | Plain matrix | Steel-fiber composite (UHPFRC, $V_f=2\%$) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Uniaxial compressive strength (MPa) | Mold-cast | 105.0 | **128.0** |
| | Print longitudinal | 95.0 | **118.0** |
| | Print perpendicular | 88.0 | **108.0** |
| | Print lateral | 78.0 | **92.0** |
| Flexural MOR (MPa) | Mold-cast | 12.0 | **20.0** |
| | Print perpendicular | 14.0 | **32.5 (+62.5 %)** |
| | Print lateral | 13.0 | **29.5 (+47.5 %)** |
| Interlayer bond (MPa) | 3D-printed layer interface | 3.8 | **5.8 (+52.6 %)** |
