# Thermally adaptive ECC with microencapsulated paraffin PCM: passive heat storage, artificial-flaw mechanics, and 4 % tensile elongation

## Summary

A leading smart-composite origin paper by Prof. Victor C. Li's team (University of Michigan): to
maximize passive heat storage in building envelopes (facades and thin curtain-wall panels),
cutting HVAC energy while actively controlling indoor comfort, a thermally adaptive
strain-hardening composite (PCM-ECC; PVA fiber 2.0 vol.%) was designed with microencapsulated
paraffin PCM — solid-liquid transition at indoor comfort temperature ($23\ ^\circ\text{C}$,
particle size $10\sim 30\ \mu\text{m}$, latent heat $140\ \text{J/g}$) dosed at 3 wt.% of binder.
Thermal and mechanical behavior was systematically characterized via DSC specific heat capacity,
Hot Wire thermal conductivity, 28-day compression, and JSCE dog-bone direct tension. The 3 wt.%
PCM raised ECC specific heat at the $23\ ^\circ\text{C}$ transition peak from **1.8 to 2.5 J/g·K
(+40 %)** while lowering conductivity from 1.2 to $0.9\ \text{W/m}\cdot\text{K}$ — improving
insulation simultaneously. Moreover, uniformly dispersed PCM microcapsules acted as ideal fine
artificial initial flaws, optimizing matrix fracture toughness ($K_m$): tensile strain doubled to
**4.0 %** versus control ECC and residual crack width shrank sixfold, **from 60 µm to 10 µm**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Passive latent storage at comfort temperature with +40 % heat capacity**: the encapsulated
   paraffin stores and releases latent heat across the $20\sim 26\ ^\circ\text{C}$ living band,
   raising wall specific heat capacity **over 40 %**.
2. **Tensile strain doubled through PCM capsules as artificial flaws**: $10\sim 30\ \mu\text{m}$
   spherical capsules act as uniform stress concentrators, equalizing fine-crack propagation
   stress — uniaxial strain jumped from 2.0 % to **4.0 %**, forming a perfect strain-hardening
   plateau.
3. **Extreme residual crack-width refinement ($60\ \mu\text{m} \rightarrow 10\ \mu\text{m}$)**:
   dense multiple cracking confines individual widths to ~**10 µm** even at 4 % ultimate strain,
   fully blocking moisture and gas ingress.
4. **Meets structural thin-panel specifications (28 MPa)**: despite PCM-induced softening,
   28-day compressive strength of **28.0 MPa** (above ACI's 17 MPa structural minimum) and
   tensile strength **4.3 MPa** prove self-supporting lightweight ZEB envelope panels feasible.

## Methods

- **Materials and PCM-ECC mix (unit contents $\text{kg/m}^3$, Table 1)**:
  - OPC Type I $570\ \text{kg/m}^3$ + Class F fly ash $684\ \text{kg/m}^3$ (FA/C = 1.20).
  - Fine silica sand ($d_{50} = 110\ \mu\text{m}$, Sand/Binder = 0.36); mixing water
    $390\ \text{kg/m}^3$ (W/B = 0.31).
  - PCM: microencapsulated paraffin powder (Microtek Laboratories; melt point $23.0\
    ^\circ\text{C}$, latent heat $140\ \text{J/g}$, particles $10\sim 30\ \mu\text{m}$; 0 and
    3 wt.% of binder).
  - Fiber: Kuraray REC15 PVA (diameter $39\ \mu\text{m}$, length 12 mm, strength 1600 MPa,
    modulus 41 GPa, $V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.\%}$).
  - Polycarboxylate SP 0.8 wt.%.
- **Testing**:
  - Compression: $50\ \text{mm}$ cubes (ASTM C109, 28 days).
  - Direct tension: JSCE dog-bones ($13\ \text{mm} \times 30\ \text{mm} \times 80\ \text{mm}$
    gauge), displacement control $0.5\ \text{mm/min}$, two LVDTs.
  - Optical crack-width measurement (Microscope Crack Width Analyzer).
  - DSC per ASTM E1269 over $10\ ^\circ\text{C} \sim 40\ ^\circ\text{C}$ at $2\ ^\circ\text{C/min}$.

## Results

### 1. Mechanical and thermal comparison: 0 % vs 3 % PCM-ECC

| Property | 0 % PCM (Control ECC) | 3 % PCM-ECC | Change / analysis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **28 d compression ($f'_c$)** | 47.0 ± 2.5 MPa | **28.0 ± 1.8 MPa** | −40.4 % (above 17 MPa structural floor) |
| **Direct tension ($\sigma_u$)** | 5.2 ± 0.3 MPa | **4.3 ± 0.2 MPa** | −17.3 % (high tension retained) |
| **First-crack stress ($\sigma_{fc}$)**| 3.0 ± 0.2 MPa | **3.0 ± 0.2 MPa** | unchanged |
| **Tensile strain ($\varepsilon_u$)** | 2.0 ± 0.4 % | **4.0 ± 0.5 %** | **+100 % (doubled)** |
| **Mean crack width ($w_{cr}$)** | 60 ± 15 µm | **10 ± 3 µm** | **−83.3 % (sixfold finer)** |
| **Specific heat at 23 °C (SHC)** | 1.80 ± 0.05 J/g·K | **2.52 ± 0.08 J/g·K** | **+40.0 % (storage maximized)** |
| **Thermal conductivity ($k$)** | 1.20 ± 0.04 W/m·K | **0.92 ± 0.03 W/m·K** | **−23.3 % (insulation improved)** |

- **Artificial-flaw mechanism**: PCM capsules serve as uniform fine pores, lowering matrix
  fracture toughness ($K_m$) to the optimal level and homogenizing flaw-size distribution —
  realizing ideal PSH conditions where multiple fine cracks propagate stably.
- **Durability of 10 µm cracking**: capillary surface tension makes external liquid-water
  ingress impossible at 10 µm width — a watertight state guaranteeing permanent envelope durability.

- **Overall conclusion**: introducing PCM microcapsules proves a breakthrough for multifunctional
  smart envelope composites — improving thermal storage (specific heat +40 %) while dramatically
  enhancing tensile strain capacity (4.0 %) and crack-width control (10 µm).
