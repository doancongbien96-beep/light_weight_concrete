# Eco-Friendly Korean-Style Traditional Roof Tiles Based on Alkali-Activated Slag

## Summary

In this user-group paper (co-authored by Professor Bang Yeon Lee, Chonnam National Univ), cementless AAS binders were developed to satisfy the KS F 4029 pressed cement roof tile standard (flexural breaking load 1.47 kN or more, water absorption 10 % or less) for eco-friendly traditional Korean roof tiles, incorporating carbon black to achieve a clay-tile-like color without paint coating. Depending on activator combinations, the flexural breaking load ranged between 1.30~1.89 kN, successfully meeting KS standards with Na₂SiO₃ 6 % or more, or Ca(OH)₂+Na₂SO₄ 10.5 %.

## Key Contributions

1. **Practical application of clinker-free architectural exterior materials**: Simultaneously solved the problem of 0.8 t CO₂ emissions per 1 t cement and the paint deterioration problem of pressed cement roof tiles by establishing an AAS binder with self-coloring fabrication.
2. **Activator optimization**: Superior performance was achieved with Ca(OH)₂+Na₂SO₄ 10.5 % (mix G1, flexural breaking load 1.84 kN, lowest absorption of 8.5 %) or Na₂SiO₃ alone at 6 % (mix G3, 1.86 kN). Above 6 % Na₂SiO₃, strength gains leveled off due to side reactions with fly ash, establishing 6 % as the economic optimum. A small dosage of Na₂SO₄ alone (mix G2, Na₂SiO₃ 3 %) fell short of KS requirements at 1.30 kN due to insufficient hydration stimulation.
3. **Self-coloring with carbon black**: Incorporating 1 % carbon black (diameter 1 µm or less) relative to binder mass replicated colors most similar to traditional clay tiles. A 0.5 % dosage yielded light gray, while 2 % showed no perceptible color difference from 1 %, confirming saturation concentration. Mixing methods (Method A: liquid premixing vs Method B: solid premixing) yielded identical results when adequately dispersed with PC-based superplasticizer.
4. **Passage of all KS standard criteria and freeze-thaw durability**: All developed mixes satisfied water absorption ≤9.5 % and flexural breaking load ≥1.47 kN (KS criteria), with zero cracking or spalling after 30 freeze-thaw cycles (KS requires 10 cycles or more).

## Methods

- **Binder selection**: Three types of GGBS (fineness 4204 cm²/g) + refined fly ash (3500~4000 cm²/g), screened across 10 combinations of Ca(OH)₂, Na₂SO₄, and Na₂SiO₃ activators (W/B 0.485, S/B 2.45, steam curing at 65 °C for 10 h). Selected 4 formulations with 3-day compressive strength ≥15 MPa.
- **Tile fabrication**: Binder No. 9 (SP 90 % + SSu 10 % series) investigated with 20~50 % fly ash replacement, fixing at 40 % to balance surface quality and strength. Fine aggregate ratios of 1.5, 1.75, and 2.0 were compared, fixing at 1.7. Approximately 3.6 kg of mortar was placed in a pressure molding press for dry compaction $\rightarrow$ steam curing at 65 °C for 10 h $\rightarrow$ dry curing at 20±2 °C and RH 70 %.
- **Testing**: Flexural breaking load (span 200 mm, loading rate 49.0 N/s, 14 days of curing), water absorption (oven-dry and surface-dry mass method), freeze-thaw durability (KS F 3510, 30 cycles of -20±3 °C for 8 h + water immersion for 6 h).
- **Coloration**: Carbon black at 0.5/1/2 % (binder mass ratio) dispersed with PC-based superplasticizer, comparing mixing Method A (liquid premixing for 15 min) and Method B (solid premixing for 15 min).

## Results

| Mix ID | Activator (binder ratio) | Flexural breaking load (kN) | Flexural strength (MPa) | Water absorption (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | CH+SSu 4.2+1.8 % | 1.84 | 8.35 | 8.5 |
| G2 | SSu 3.6 % (Na₂SiO₃ 3 % equivalent) | 1.30 (substandard) | 5.90 | 8.9 |
| G3 | Na₂SiO₃ 6 % | 1.86 | 8.45 | 9.1 |
| G4 | Na₂SiO₃ 8 % | 1.58 | 7.14 | 9.5 |
| G5 | Na₂SiO₃ 10 % | 1.89 | 8.58 | 9.3 |
| OPC Control | — | 1.51 | 8.98 | 7.8 |

- Efflorescence in G2: Leaching of SO₃ from Na₂SO₄, resulting from insufficiently activated binder behaving merely as a filler.
- All mixes showed zero cracking or spalling after 30 freeze-thaw cycles.
