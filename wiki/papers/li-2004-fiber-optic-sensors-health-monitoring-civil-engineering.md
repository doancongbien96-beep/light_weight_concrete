# Fiber Optic Sensor Technologies (FBG, Fabry-Perot, Distributed Brillouin) for Civil Structural Health Monitoring (SHM) Distilled: An Immortal Classic Review of Real Bridge and Dam Applications

## Summary

To fundamentally overcome the electromagnetic interference (EMI), long-term drift, signal attenuation, and lead-wire breakage failures that electrical resistance sensors (strain gauges) and vibrating wire sensors suffer under harsh outdoor environments, and to monitor in real time the 100-year life-cycle safety of large infrastructure structures, this paper provides a full-scale consolidation of the optical operating principles of **fiber Bragg gratings (FBG: point sensors), extrinsic Fabry-Perot interferometers (EFPI), long-gage fiber optic sensors (SOFO), and continuous distributed Brillouin/Raman scattering sensors spanning tens of km (BOTDA/ROTDR)**; their concrete-embedding encapsulation packaging technology; and application cases on global large-scale real infrastructure (Hong Kong's Tsing Ma Bridge and Ting Kau Bridge, Canada's Confederation Bridge, China's Three Gorges Dam) — an Engineering Structures 2004 immortal, supreme landmark classic review by the research team of **Prof. Hong-Nan Li of Dalian University of Technology, China and Prof. Gang-Bing Song of the University of Houston, USA**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Systematic classification and principle formalization of the four major fiber optic sensor systems dedicated to civil infrastructure**:
   - It precisely compares the strengths and weaknesses and the measurement ranges ($\pm 5000\ \mu\varepsilon$) of FBG wavelength-modulation sensors, EFPI phase-interference sensors, optical intensity-modulation sensors, and distributed Brillouin scattering sensors.
2. **Comprehensive coverage of embedment packaging technologies that protect the optical fiber from concrete casting pressure and hydration heat**:
   - It presents smart structural-material integration techniques that seal the fragile silica-core optical fiber inside metal tubes (Stainless Steel Tube) and carbon-fiber composite sections (FRP Smart Rebar).
3. **Integrated analysis of the monitoring performance of large-scale global long-span bridge and dam SHM projects**:
   - By analyzing Hong Kong's Tsing Ma Bridge (an FBG sensor network of more than 500 sensors) and the hydration heat and stress measurement data of the Three Gorges Dam concrete, it demonstrates electromagnetic-wave immunity and permanent stability beyond 20 years.
4. **Establishment of the vision of future smart structures and optically self-diagnosing infrastructure**:
   - It establishes the monitoring paradigm of simultaneously tracking strain, temperature, vibration, and crack opening at hundreds of locations over a single fiber optic cable through multiplexing technology.

## Methods

- **Analysis of sensor optical mechanisms (Sections 2~3 & Figs. 1~6)**:
  - FBG sensor: Bragg reflected wavelength $\lambda_B = 2 n_{eff} \Lambda$, with strain/temperature sensitivity $\Delta\lambda_B / \lambda_B = (\alpha + \xi)\Delta T + (1 - P_e)\Delta\varepsilon$.
  - EFPI sensor: phase change of the interference resonant cavity length $s$: $\phi = 4\pi s / \lambda$.
  - BOTDA distributed sensor: acoustic phonon interaction Brillouin frequency shift $\nu_B = 2 n v_a / \lambda_0$.
- **Field demonstration database (Sections 4~6 & Tables 1~3 & Figs. 7~14)**:
  - Bridge structures: cable-stayed, suspension, and PSC girder bridges (strain, deflection, cable tension).
  - Dams and tunnels: concrete gravity dams and hydraulic tunnels (hydration heat temperature distribution, seepage ground displacement).

### 2. Analysis of Sensor Packaging and Long-Term Measurement Reliability
- **Temperature-strain separation compensation (Temperature Compensation)**: pairing reference stress-free dummy FBG sensors offsets thermally induced expansion drift by $100\%$.
- **Blocking harsh alkaline/moisture environments**: special Teflon/stainless-tube encapsulation fundamentally prevents optical fiber degradation for more than 20 years even inside hydrating ($80\ ^\circ\text{C}$) and strongly alkaline ($\text{pH} > 13$) concrete.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is an immortal, textbook-standard landmark classic review of smart structures and SHM engineering, in which Prof. Hong-Nan Li's research team consolidated the operating principles of fiber optic sensors (FBG/EFPI/BOTDA) for civil infrastructure, packaging technologies, and global large-scale bridge and dam monitoring cases.
