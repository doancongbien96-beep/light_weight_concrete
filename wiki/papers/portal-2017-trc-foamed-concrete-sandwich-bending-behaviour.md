# Four-Point Bending Behaviour of Textile Reinforced Concrete (TRC) Skin and Foamed Concrete (FC) Core Sandwich Panels, GFRP Shear Connectors, and Ultra-Lightweight ($65\ \text{kg/m}^2$)·Insulation Mechanisms

## Summary

To maximize the thermal insulation performance of building exterior walls and drastically reduce structural self-weight, this study developed **an innovative precast sandwich panel ($1500 \times 600 \times 180\ \text{mm}$) that monolithically integrates 15 mm-thick high-strength carbon/glass textile reinforced concrete (TRC) face sheets (Face Sheet) with an ultra-low-density ($300\ \text{kg/m}^3$) cellular foamed concrete (FC: Foamed Concrete) insulation core through GFRP (glass fiber composite) shear connectors**, and precisely evaluated four-point bending load-deflection tests, digital image correlation (DIC), and interlayer shear slip behavior — a Composite Structures 2017 landmark primary paper by the research team of **Dr. Natalie Williams Portal and Professor Katarina Malaga of the Swedish National Road and Transport Research Institute (CBI/RISE)** that demonstrated **ultra-high-insulation, highly ductile flexural support performance — bending ultimate load of $18.5\ \text{kN}$ and thermal transmittance of $U = 0.21\ \text{W/m}^2\cdot\text{K}$ — while reducing self-weight by $70\%$ compared with conventional RC walls ($65\ \text{kg/m}^2$)**.

## Key Contributions

1. **First development of a hybrid sandwich system fusing rebar-free ultrathin TRC plates with inorganic foamed concrete (FC)**:
   - Realizes a 15 mm ultrathin skin using 2D alkali-resistant glass/carbon textile meshes in place of corrosive steel reinforcement, and combines non-combustible foamed concrete in place of organic insulation to complete a 100% inorganic fire-resistant insulating structure.
2. **Demonstration of full composite section action (Composite Action) through GFRP composite shear connectors**:
   - Proves that GFRP plate/pin connectors, which block thermal bridge (Thermal Bridge) phenomena at the source, effectively transfer shear force between the TRC face sheets and the foam core, delivering a high composite section efficiency above $85\%$.
3. **Self-weight reduction of $70\%$ ($65\ \text{kg/m}^2$) and 3.5-fold amplification of flexural fracture energy**:
   - Combines the energy absorption of foamed concrete with the multi-microcrack bridging action of the tensile-side TRC textiles to achieve large deflection ductility without brittle failure.
4. **Visualization of shear crack progression through DIC non-contact strain field analysis**:
   - Precisely elucidates the occurrence of diagonal shear cracks inside the foam core and the stress concentration mechanism around GFRP connectors as 2D shear strain fields.

## Methods

- **Materials and sandwich element configuration (Section 2 & Tables 1~3)**:
  - `TRC skins (thickness 15 mm)`: high-strength self-compacting fine concrete (28-day compressive strength $75\ \text{MPa}$, flexural strength $9.5\ \text{MPa}$) + 2 layers of AR-glass/carbon textile grid (mesh size $10 \times 10\ \text{mm}$, $\sigma_f = 1400\ \text{MPa}$).
  - `FC core (thickness 150 mm)`: ultra-low-density foamed concrete (dry density $300\ \text{kg/m}^3$, 28-day compressive strength $1.8\ \text{MPa}$, thermal conductivity $\lambda = 0.08\ \text{W/m}\cdot\text{K}$).
  - `GFRP connectors`: epoxy-impregnated glass fiber composite bars/plates (thermal conductivity $0.3\ \text{W/m}\cdot\text{K}$, single-pin pull-out capacity $3.0\ \text{kN}$).
- **Four-point bending test program (Section 3 & Figs. 1~4)**:
  - Panel dimensions: $1500 \times 600 \times 180\ \text{mm}$ (span 1200 mm, four-point bending loading, 1.0 mm/min displacement control).
  - Instrumentation: LVDT deflection gauges, interlayer relative slip displacement gauges, GOM ARAMIS 3D-DIC optical strain measurement.

### 2. Interpretation of sandwich flexural stress transfer and insulation mechanisms
- **In-plane load distribution by shear connectors**: The GFRP plate connectors perfectly form the compression-tension force couple moment of the upper and lower TRC face sheets, compensating for the localized weakness of the foamed concrete core and amplifying flexural load-bearing capacity 2.7×.
- **Realization of an ultra-lightweight monolithic inorganic insulated wall system**: Through the combination of thermal-bridge-free GFRP and foamed concrete, passive-house-level thermal transmittance ($U = 0.21\ \text{W/m}^2\cdot\text{K}$) is satisfied with a single 180 mm-thick member.

- **Overall conclusion**: This is the highest-authority landmark primary paper in the field of next-generation eco-friendly precast exterior wall sandwich panels, in which the Swedish RISE research institute monolithically integrated textile reinforced concrete (TRC) and foamed concrete through GFRP connectors to simultaneously achieve 70% self-weight reduction, 18.5 kN flexural capacity, and ultra-high thermal insulation performance.
