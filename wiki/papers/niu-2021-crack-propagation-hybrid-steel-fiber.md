# Crack Propagation Behavior of UHPC Reinforced with Hybrid Steel Fibers under Flexural Loading

## Summary

A study that hybridized straight steel fibers (∅ 0.2 mm) by length (6, 13, 20 mm) at a fixed total fiber volume fraction of 2.0 % (ASTM C1609 three-point flexure + DIC) and quantified the crack initiation and propagation process (Guangzhou University · South China University of Technology group). By interpreting the DIC strain fields after dividing them into four zones — compression, crack process, localization, and macro-crack — the study found that the fiber-matrix full debonding strain of about 0.32 % and the macro-crack onset threshold strain of about 3.25 % are constant regardless of fiber length. The optimum combination was 0.5 % of 13 mm fiber + 1.5 % of 20 mm fiber (M0.5L1.5), which exhibited the lowest crack initiation strain rate and crack propagation speed among all specimens. Two copies of the same paper (niu-2021-crack-propagation-behavior-of-ultra-high-performance.pdf) exist in sources/, so they were consolidated into a single note.

## Key Contributions

- Identification of the optimum point of fiber-length hybridization: the long-fiber-dominant + small intermediate-fiber combination L1.5M0.5 achieved f_MOR 20.47 MPa and d_MOR 1.34 mm, i.e., +9.15 % and +8.06 % respectively over single long-fiber L2.0 (18.78 MPa, 1.24 mm). This is interpreted as a mechanism whereby adding only a small amount of medium-length fiber improves the long-fiber distribution, preserving bridging/crack-resistance benefits while suppressing the drawbacks of poor dispersion and interfacial weak zones. In contrast, short fibers (6 mm) alone yield flexural softening (S2.0 and M0.5S1.5 show f_LOP>f_MOR).
- DIC-based 4-zone frame and two candidate material constants: the crack-tip region is separated into (I) compression, (II) crack process zone (FPZ, the fiber debonding section, width boundary 0.022 mm), (III) localization (up to width 0.05 mm), and (IV) macro-crack zones. The full-debonding strain of about 0.32 % (corresponding to the debonding displacement D0 = 0.022 mm of Kang's equation) and the macro-crack onset threshold strain of about 3.25 % are invariant for any fiber configuration — usable as quasi-material constants of UHPC flexural cracking. At the 30 %F_MOR load stage only the localization zone remains (length about 15 mm, tip strain 3.25 %).
- Hybrid dependence of the COD evolution profile: substituting long fibers with short fibers by 0.5~1.0 % keeps the beam-direction COD profile linear, while substitution of 1.5~2.0 % produces abrupt, irregular changes. The maximum COD at the unload stage is 1.88 mm for S2.0, 4.26 mm for M2.0, and 7.32 mm for L2.0, with L1.5M0.5 the largest at 7.69 mm (+5.05 % vs L2.0).
- A two-stage quantitative law of crack initiation and propagation: the crack initiation strain is linear in time (y=ax+b), and the crack length is fitted by the exponential function y=A1−A2·e^(−x/B) (A1 = beam height 100 mm, with A2·B provided in an experimental coefficient table), and the propagation speed dy/dx=(A2/B)e^(−x/B) shows rapid early propagation followed by decay and stabilization. The initiation strain rate of M0.5L1.5 was 0.00492 %/s (the source's unit notation 'L/s' is judged to be a typo), which is 16.89 % lower than L2.0.

## Methods

- Mix: cement CEM II 52.5R 770 + silica fume 230 + silica sand (0.16~0.315 mm 300, 0.63~1.25 mm 700) in kg/m³, W/B 0.16, superplasticizer 3.5 % (solids content 30 %). One-sided casting against the mold face to homogenize fiber orientation, 90 s vibration, 28-day water curing.
- Specimen series: single-fiber (S2.0/M2.0/L2.0) + 9 hybrid types (e.g., M1.5S0.5, at substitution ratios 0.5/1.0/1.5 %).
- Compression · splitting tension: 100 mm cubes, 0.5 and 1.2 MPa/s respectively (UTM 300 t). Flexure: MTS 250 kN under displacement control at 0.2 mm/min, 100×100×400 mm beams, span 300 mm, up to a deflection of 3 mm (2 LVDTs mounted on a steel frame).
- DIC: two 1024×1024 px cameras, speckle pattern, ROI 100×200 mm, two blue light sources, image capture at 1 s intervals. Strain-field comparison by load stage (F_LOP, F_MOR, 30 %F_MOR).

## Results

- Compressive strength: relative to the fiber-free reference (NF) of 120.4 MPa, fiber addition produced 147.1~170.5 MPa (maximum M1.0L1.0, minimum S1.5M1.5); the effect of the hybrid configuration itself is small. Splitting tensile strength increased from the NF value of 8.1 MPa by +51.9 %, +134.6 %, and +159.2 % with single addition of 6/13/20 mm fibers respectively, with M0.5L1.5 the highest at 21.8 MPa (+3.81 % vs L2.0).
- f_LOP averaged 11.97 MPa (deflection 0.075 mm) — a matrix-intrinsic value independent of fiber length and configuration — whereas f_MOR and d_MOR depend strongly on fiber configuration (d_MOR of L2.0 at 1.24 mm is +552.63 % over S2.0).
- Crack patterns: in the 6+13 mm combinations, higher substitution ratios make the crack path more irregular, while combinations containing 20 mm fibers show zigzag paths. L1.5M0.5 had the shortest macro-crack length at F_MOR, 13.62 mm.
- On the load-deflection curves, flexural deflection-hardening (f_LOP<f_MOR) appeared in the specimens with an intermediate-fiber ratio of 1.0 % or more (M1.0S1.0, M1.5S0.5, M2.0) and in the specimens incorporating 20 mm fibers.
- The introduction cites the user group's (Bang Yeon Lee et al.) study on the tensile behavior of UHPC with hybrid fibers (steel + synthetic fibers) as literature on hybrid classification.
