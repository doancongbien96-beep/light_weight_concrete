# Mechanical properties of eco-friendly high-ductility cement composites upcycling industrial-waste selvage short fibers from high-performance ballistic fabric

## Summary

To upcycle into short fibers for construction composites the high-value selvage — a hybrid yarn
of 80 wt.% PE fiber + 20 wt.% SUS 316L metal wire generated in large quantities as edge waste
during ballistic/stab-resistant high-performance fabric manufacture and currently landfilled or
incinerated in its entirety — this study produced four fiber types (N3, O3, N6, O6) with cutting
length (3 mm vs 6 mm) and mechanical opening treatment as variables, incorporated them into
cement paste (W/C 0.35, 1.75 vol.%), and evaluated 28-day compressive strength, uniaxial direct
tension, strain capacity, toughness, and multiple cracking (corresponding author Prof.
Bang-Yeon Lee of Chonnam National University; with Se-Eon Park and Yun-Yong Kim of Chungnam
National University and Young-Min Kim). All selvage-reinforced composites held compressive
strengths of 63–66 MPa while showing distinct strain-hardening and saturated fine multiple
cracking — tensile elongation **2.6 %–3.5 %** and strength **7.5–9.0 MPa** — demonstrating for
the first time worldwide that an eco-friendly high-ductility composite can replace expensive
virgin fibers.

## Key Contributions

1. **Successful upcycling of industrial-waste hybrid fabric (PE+SUS selvage)**: incorporating
   waste selvage (high-strength PE at 3000 MPa + stainless wire at 800 MPa) at 1.75 vol.%
   achieved **2.6–3.5 %** ultra-high ductility, greatly exceeding the 2.0 % standard ECC requirement.
2. **Mechanical effects of opening treatment and fiber length elucidated**:
   - **Opening effect**: the O series (O3, O6), untangled by the opening machine, gained cement-
     paste contact area, improving first-crack strength (4.8–5.1 MPa) and compression
     (65.5–66.0 MPa).
   - **Length effect**: 6 mm fibers (N6) extended bridging length, pushing the PSH index
     ($f_t/f_{1st}$) to **2.05**, achieving the highest strength **9.0 MPa** and strain capacity
     **3.5 %**.
3. **Sustainable resource-circulating materialization**: drastically cuts emissions and costs of
   virgin PVA/PE fiber manufacture, re-sourcing an industrial by-product into high-value seismic/
   repair-grade composites.

## Methods

- **Materials and binder (Table 1)**:
  - OPC (density $3.15\ \text{g/cm}^3$) 1.0; water W/C 0.35; SP 0.005; VMA 0.0003;
    antifoamer 0.001.
- **Recycled selvage fiber composition (Table 2)**:
  - Core: SUS 316L wire (700–800 MPa, elongation 28 %, 20 wt.%).
  - Sheath: high-strength PE (diameter 18 µm, 3000 MPa, modulus 120 GPa, SG 0.97, 80 % of total
    weight).
  - Fiber volume fraction fixed at 1.75 vol.%.
- **Processing variables (4 types)**:
  - **M-N3**: length 3.77 mm, non-opened. **M-O3**: 3.65 mm, opened.
  - **M-N6**: length 7.03 mm, non-opened. **M-O6**: 6.32 mm, opened.
- **Testing**:
  - Compression: KS L 5105 (three $50\ \text{mm}$ cubes, 28-day water curing).
  - Direct tension: four JSCE dogbones ($30\ \text{mm} \times 13\ \text{mm}$ section, 80 mm gauge),
    20 kN machine at 0.1 mm/min, two LVDTs.
  - Crack analysis: gauge-zone crack counts and failure modes post-test.

## Results

### 1. 28-day compression and uniaxial direct-tension performance

| Mix | Fiber form | Mean length (mm) | Compression (MPa) | First crack (MPa) | Ultimate tension (MPa) | Strain capacity (%) | PSH ratio ($f_t/f_{1st}$) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **M-N3** | Non-opened, 3 mm | 3.77 ± 0.95 | 63.3 ± 4.2 | 4.3 ± 0.4 | 7.5 ± 0.5 | 2.6 ± 0.5 % | 1.74 |
| **M-O3** | **Opened, 3 mm** | 3.65 ± 0.80 | **66.0 ± 2.9** | 4.8 ± 0.3 | 8.5 ± 0.6 (+13 %) | 2.8 ± 0.6 % | 1.77 |
| **M-N6** | **Non-opened, 6 mm** | 7.03 ± 0.71 | 63.8 ± 5.3 | 4.4 ± 0.8 | **9.0 ± 1.1 (+20 %)** | **3.5 ± 1.1 %** | **2.05 (highest)** |
| **M-O6** | Opened, 6 mm | 6.32 ± 1.33 | 65.5 ± 2.8 | **5.1 ± 0.2** | 8.9 ± 0.8 | 3.0 ± 0.6 % | 1.74 |

### 2. Toughness and multiple-cracking failure behavior
- All mixes developed strain capacities above 2.6 %, fully satisfying the hardening criteria
  for high-ductility cement composites (ECC/HPFRCC).
- Mix M-N6, with its long bridging length, recorded the best deformation performance — ultimate
  strength 9.0 MPa and elongation 3.5 %.
- Opening treatment (O series) strengthened the fiber-matrix bond, raising crack-initiation
  strength by 11–16 %.

- **Overall conclusion**: processing high-performance waste selvage into short fibers achieves
  waste recycling and low-cost production of a 65 MPa-class, ~3.5 %-class ultra-high-ductility
  composite simultaneously.
