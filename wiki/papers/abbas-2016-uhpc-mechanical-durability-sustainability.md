# Review of mix optimization, mechanical performance, durability, sustainability, and field implementation challenges of ultra-high performance concrete (UHPC)

## Summary

An authoritative global review by Prof. M. L. Nehdi's team (University of Western
Ontario, Canada) synthesizing worldwide progress in UHPC achieving 150–250 MPa compressive
strength with maximized impermeability. Covering over 200 leading studies, it systematically
consolidates binder composition (cement, silica fume, nano-silica, slag, fly ash), particle
packing theory (Modified A&A and CPM models), curing regimes (standard ambient, $90\
^\circ\text{C}$ steam curing, $200\ ^\circ\text{C}$ heat treatment), fresh-state flow and
setting, hardened mechanical properties (compression, direct tension, flexure, fatigue),
extreme-environment durability (chloride ingress, freeze-thaw, carbonation, acid attack),
structural application limits, and cost reduction paths. The paper details gradation
optimization cutting cement content to $650\sim 800\ \text{kg/m}^3$, C-S-H gel modification
through nanoparticles ($\text{nano-SiO}_2$, $\text{nano-CaCO}_3$), and the mechanism of
strength surges from zeolite/tobermorite crystallization under $90\ ^\circ\text{C}$ steam curing.

## Key Contributions

1. **Systematization of global UHPC constituent materials and particle packing design**:
   - Statistically established design ranges for unit binder composition (cement
     $700\sim 1100\ \text{kg/m}^3$, silica fume $150\sim 250\ \text{kg/m}^3$, microsilica
     sand $200\sim 400\ \text{kg/m}^3$, W/B $0.14\sim 0.22$, steel fiber $1.5\sim 3.0\
     \text{vol.\%}$).
   - Eco-efficient UHPC design paths cutting cement use by over 50 % via Modified A&A
     ($q = 0.22\sim 0.25$) and CPM packing models.
2. **Quantification of microstructure and strength development by curing regime**:
   - **Standard ambient curing (20 °C)**: 28-day compressive strength 150–175 MPa,
     tensile strength 8–10 MPa.
   - **Steam curing (90 °C, 48 h)**: pozzolanic reaction maximized, forming tobermorite
     and xonotlite; 28-day compressive strength surges to **200–230 MPa** with elastic
     modulus **50–55 GPa**.
3. **Demonstrated durability and shielding performance in extreme environments**:
   - Chloride diffusion coefficient ($< 10^{-13}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s}$), resistivity
     ($> 1000\ \text{k}\Omega\cdot\text{cm}$), freeze-thaw resistance (relative dynamic
     modulus >98 % after 1000 cycles) — extending service life 3–5x versus ordinary concrete.
4. **Early hydration and ITZ nano-modification through nanomaterials**:
   - Nano-silica (1–3 wt.%) and nano-CaCO_3 nearly eliminate capillary porosity and fully
     dissolve Ca(OH)_2 crystals at the steel fiber-matrix interface.

## Methods

- **Analysis database and property ranges (Tables 2 & 3)**:
  - W/B: 0.14–0.22; PCE superplasticizer solids 1.5–3.5 wt.%.
  - Reinforcing fibers: smooth/twisted/hooked-end micro steel fibers (diameter
    $0.15\sim 0.22\ \text{mm}$, length $13\sim 20\ \text{mm}$, tensile strength
    $>2500\ \text{MPa}$, volume fraction $1.5\sim 3.0\ \text{vol.\%}$).
  - Curing comparison: ambient moist curing (NC), accelerated steam curing at
    $90\ ^\circ\text{C}$ (SC), autoclave/heat treatment at $200\ ^\circ\text{C}$ (TT).
- **Performance assessment categories**:
  - Fresh state: slump flow ($180\sim 260\ \text{mm}$), air content ($<2.0\ \%$),
    setting time ($6\sim 15\ \text{h}$).
  - Mechanics: uniaxial compression (ASTM C39/C109), uniaxial direct tension, four-point
    flexure (ASTM C1609), static elastic modulus (ASTM C469).
  - Durability: ASTM C1202 RCPT rapid chloride penetration; ASTM C666 freeze-thaw resistance.

## Results

### 1. Representative UHPC mechanics by curing regime and fiber content

| Curing | Steel fiber (vol.%) | Compressive strength (MPa) | Direct tension (MPa) | Flexural strength (MPa) | Static modulus (GPa) | Fracture energy ($G_F$, $\text{N/m}$) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ambient (20 °C)** | 0 % (plain matrix) | 140 ~ 160 | 4.0 ~ 6.0 | 8.0 ~ 12.0 | 42 ~ 46 | 150 ~ 250 |
| **Ambient (20 °C)** | 2.0 % (straight micro fiber) | **160 ~ 185** | **8.0 ~ 11.0** | **22.0 ~ 30.0** | **45 ~ 50** | **15,000 ~ 25,000** |
| **Steam (90 °C, 48h)** | 2.0 % (straight micro fiber) | **200 ~ 230** | **11.0 ~ 15.0** | **32.0 ~ 45.0** | **50 ~ 55** | **25,000 ~ 35,000** |
| **Heat treatment (200 °C TT)** | 2.5 % (twisted fiber) | **230 ~ 280** | **14.0 ~ 18.0** | **45.0 ~ 60.0** | **55 ~ 62** | **35,000 ~ 50,000** |

### 2. Benchmark durability indicators of UHPC
- **RCPT passed charge**: below **100 Coulombs (Negligible)** against ordinary concrete's
  2000–4000 C (Moderate) — chloride ingress is effectively zero.
- **Freeze-thaw durability factor**: mass loss < 0.1 % and relative dynamic modulus ≥ 98 %
  after 1000 cycles.

- **Overall conclusion**: UHPC delivers not only 200 MPa-class strength but permanent
  impermeability and extreme durability — the key next-generation construction material for
  long-life, maintenance-free super-long-span bridges, offshore plants, and military
  protective structures.
