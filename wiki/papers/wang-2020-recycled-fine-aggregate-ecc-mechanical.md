## Summary
This study investigated the effects of the recycled fine aggregate (RFA) replacement ratio (0 %, 25 %, 50 %, 75 %, 100 %) and pre-wetting treatment on the uniaxial direct tensile behavior of PVA-ECC, the single fiber-matrix interfacial shear bond strength, and the microstructural porosity. The fluidity loss caused by the high water absorption of RFA was controlled through additional mixing water compensation, and dogbone direct tensile tests were performed. It was demonstrated that a secondary pozzolanic reaction around RFA particles (reaction with fly ash) strengthened the old-new interfacial transition zones (ITZ), and that the mix with 50 % RFA replacement achieved an ultimate tensile strength of 5.1 MPa and a tensile strain capacity of 4.2 %.

## Key Contributions
- **Quantification of single fiber pull-out characteristics ($\tau_0, G_d$) with RFA incorporation**: the surface roughness of RFA was found to slightly increase the mechanical frictional shear stress from $1.8 MPa$ to $2.2 MPa$.
- **Demonstration of the internal curing effect of RFA pre-wetting**: internally stored water in RFA promoted later-age hydration and reduced drying shrinkage cracking by 20 %.
- **Assessment of tensile strain-hardening saturation**: formation of multiple fine cracking with more than 30 cracks was confirmed at every replacement ratio.

## Methods
- **Mix proportion**: cement + fly ash (55 wt %), natural silica sand and RFA ($d_{max} = 0.6 mm$), PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, REC15).
- **Tests conducted**: uniaxial direct tensile test (displacement-controlled at $0.5 mm/min$), single fiber pull-out test, SEM-EDS interfacial analysis, and mercury intrusion porosimetry.

## Results
- Mix with 50 % RFA replacement: tensile strength $5.12 MPa$, tensile strain capacity $4.25 \%$, 28 d compressive strength $46.8 MPa$.
- Mix with 100 % RFA: tensile strength $4.35 MPa$, tensile strain capacity $3.50 \%$.
