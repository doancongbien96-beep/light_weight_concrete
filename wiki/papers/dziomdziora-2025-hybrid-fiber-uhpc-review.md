# Hybrid Fiber Reinforcement Mechanisms, Mechanical Toughness, and Durability of Ultra-High-Performance Concrete (UHPC): A Comprehensive Review

## Summary

To mitigate brittle failure in ultra-high-performance concrete (UHPC with compressive strengths of 150~300 MPa) and maximize strain energy absorption, crack control, and durability, Professor Piotr Smarzewski and Dr. P. Dziomdziora at the Military University of Technology in Warsaw synthesized global research on hybrid fiber-reinforced UHPC across 51 pages, combining diverse fiber materials (steel, polypropylene PP, polyethylene PE, polyvinyl alcohol PVA, basalt, carbon) and geometries (straight micro, hooked-end, twisted macro). The review systematically analyzed multiscale crack bridging, modulus-based hybridization (high-modulus steel + low-modulus synthetic), high-temperature explosive spalling mitigation, residual load-bearing capacity, and long-term durability against chloride penetration and freeze-thaw cycles, establishing guidelines for hybrid fiber optimization.

## Key Contributions

1. **Classification of multiscale and multifunctional fiber hybridization systems**:
   - **Scale hybridization (Micro + Macro)**: Micro-fibers (Micro-SF, PVA, $6\sim 13\ \text{mm}$) delay matrix microcrack nucleation, while anchored macro-fibers (Hooked/Twisted Macro-SF, $30\sim 60\ \text{mm}$) bridge macrocracks, **enhancing flexural toughness by 50 ~ 150 %**.
   - **Modulus hybridization (High Modulus + Low Modulus)**: Combines the load-bearing capacity of high-modulus steel fibers ($E_f = 210\ \text{GPa}$) with the post-cracking deformation capacity of synthetic fibers (PP/PE, elongation $>6\%$).
2. **Identification of explosive spalling prevention and fire-resistance synergy**:
   - Micro-PP fibers ($V_f = 0.1\%\sim 0.3\%$) melt at $165\ ^\circ\text{C}$, creating capillary channels for vapor pressure release in dense UHPC matrices to prevent spalling (100 % prevention), while steel fibers maintain structural integrity beyond $800\ ^\circ\text{C}$.
3. **Durability enhancement under harsh environments (chloride barrier, freeze-thaw resistance)**:
   - Restricting crack widths below 30 µm maintained chloride diffusion coefficients below $1.0 \times 10^{-13}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s}$, preserving relative dynamic modulus above 95 % after over 600 freeze-thaw cycles.

## Methods

- **Analyzed fiber systems and property ranges (Table 1 & Comprehensive Synthesis)**:
  - **Steel fibers**: Straight micro ($L_f = 6\sim 13\ \text{mm}, d_f = 0.12\sim 0.20\ \text{mm}$), hooked-end ($L_f = 30\sim 60\ \text{mm}$), twisted macro (Torex, $L_f = 30\ \text{mm}$).
  - **Synthetic & mineral fibers**: PVA ($39\ \mu\text{m}$), ultra-high-strength PE ($24\ \mu\text{m}$), PP ($18\sim 35\ \mu\text{m}$), basalt ($13\sim 17\ \mu\text{m}$), carbon ($7\ \mu\text{m}$).
  - **Hybrid combinations**:
    1. Steel–Steel (Micro straight + Macro hooked/twisted)
    2. Steel–Synthetic (Micro steel + PVA / PE / PP)
    3. Basalt–Synthetic (Non-metallic eco-friendly hybrid)
    4. Carbon–Steel / Carbon–PE (High-strength multifunctional hybrid)
  - Total fiber volume fraction: $V_f = 1.0\%\sim 3.0\ \text{vol.\%}$.

## Results

### 1. Mechanical and durability synergy across fiber hybridization types

| Hybrid combination type | Representative composition and volume fraction | Primary mechanical synergy | Flexural strength and toughness enhancement | Special performance and durability contribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Steel – Steel (Macro + Micro)** | Micro-SF 1.0 % + Hooked-SF 1.0 % | Microcrack arrest + large-deflection anchor pullout | MOR **18 ~ 32 MPa (+60 %)**, flexural toughness **+120 %** | 3-fold fatigue life, balling prevention |
| **Steel – Synthetic (SF + PVA/PE)** | Straight-SF 1.5 % + PE 0.5 % | High-strength capacity + enhanced tensile ductility | Tensile strength **12 ~ 18 MPa**, elongation **3.5 ~ 6.0 %** | Multiple microcracking ($w < 30\ \mu\text{m}$) autogenous healing |
| **Steel – Synthetic (SF + PP)** | Straight-SF 1.8 % + PP 0.2 % | Flexural strength + high-temperature vapor channels | MOR **22 ~ 28 MPa**, toughness retention | **100 % prevention of explosive spalling** |
| **Basalt – Synthetic (BF + PP)** | Basalt 1.0 % + PP 0.5 % | Low-cost eco-friendly non-metallic toughening | MOR **12 ~ 16 MPa (+40 %)** | Non-magnetic, chemical and chloride resistance |

### 2. Hybrid synergy index ($S_f$) and mechanisms
- **Synergy index**: Positive synergy occurs when $S_f = \frac{T_{hybrid}}{V_1 T_1 + V_2 T_2} > 1.0$, peaking at **1.45 ~ 1.68** for a 50:50 ratio of Micro-SF and Hooked-SF.
- **Cost and workability**: Replacing portions of micro-steel fibers with synthetic fibers or macro-steel fibers reduced material costs by 30 % and improved mixture workability.

- **Overall conclusion**: Hybrid fiber reinforcement in UHPC coordinates multiscale crack control, high-toughness flexural ductility, fire-spalling resistance, and durability for advanced cementitious composites.
