## Summary
A recent study evaluating the thermal-mechanical performance of thermal energy storage concrete that integrates paraffin-based phase change material (PCM), adsorbed and encapsulated in a porous ceramic carrier, into structural concrete in order to dramatically reduce the heating and cooling energy consumption of buildings and actively control indoor thermal comfort. Micro/macro-encapsulated PCM with a latent heat of phase change ($\Delta H = 120 \sim 160 J/g$, phase-change temperature $22 \sim 26 °C$) was substituted for 10 ~ 30 vol % of the fine aggregate, and differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) thermal characterization and insulated full-scale test room experiments were performed. It was demonstrated that PCM concrete walls lower the indoor peak temperature by $3.8 °C$ and mitigate the indoor daily temperature swing by 45 %.

## Key Contributions
- **Development of dual-shell encapsulation technology to prevent PCM leakage**: porous expanded shale carrier adsorption followed by epoxy/silica nanocoating, achieving a PCM leakage rate of 0.5 % or less even after 1000 thermal cycles.
- **Quantification of building energy saving performance**: demonstration of a 28 % reduction in summer cooling load and an 18 % reduction in winter heating load.
- **Mix design compensating for the strength reduction caused by PCM incorporation**: silica fume (10 wt %) and fine steel fiber (0.5 vol %) reinforcement securing a 28 d compressive strength of $42.5 MPa$.

## Methods
- **Materials**: OPC, silica fume, paraffin PCM capsules (particle size 2 ~ 4 mm), $W/B = 0.35$.
- **Test items**: DSC latent heat measurement, hot-wire thermal conductivity measurement, real-time indoor temperature monitoring of a scaled full-size model ($1.0 m^3$), and compressive strength.

## Results
- 20 vol % PCM concrete: volumetric heat capacity $2.45 MJ/(m3\cdot K)$, thermal conductivity $1.15 W/(m\cdot K)$, 28 d compressive strength $42.5 MPa$.
- The indoor peak temperature time lag (Time lag) extended by 3.5 h, achieving a peak power load leveling effect.
