# Enhanced Thermal Properties of Cement Panels with Halloysite Nanotube-Based Phase Change Materials and Graphene

## Summary

To improve the thermal storage capacity and thermal conductivity of building envelopes and reduce HVAC energy consumption, Professor X. Gu and co-workers at Tsinghua University developed form-stable phase change composite materials (FSPCM) by vacuum-impregnating polyethylene glycol (PEG-2000: latent heat capacity **118.5 J/g**, phase change temperature **52.5 °C**) into acid-activated halloysite nanotubes (A-Hal) with the addition of graphene (0.5 ~ 2.5 wt %). Applying 20 wt % of the FSPCM (PEG/A-Hal/Gr) to cement panels ($W/B = 0.45$), the authors evaluated thermophysical properties and lab-scale test room thermal responses. Cement panels incorporating 20 wt % FSPCM with 1.5 wt % graphene (CP/M/G1.5) achieved an effective thermal conductivity of **0.88 W/(m·K)** (a 54 % enhancement over pure PCM panels), reduced maximum indoor temperatures by **4.2 °C**, and delayed temperature peak arrival by **45 min**.

## Key Contributions

- **Synthesis of high-latent-heat form-stable PCM based on A-Hal and graphene**: Completely eliminated PEG leakage (100 %) via capillary encapsulation within tubular halloysite lumen.
- **Thermal conductivity tuning across graphene contents (0 ~ 2.5 wt %)**: Optimized thermal conductivity from 0.57 to 0.88 W/(m·K) at 1.5 wt % graphene to promote rapid thermal charging and discharging.
- **Verification of indoor thermal buffering in scale-model test rooms**: Attenuated daytime peak temperatures by 4.2 °C and reduced indoor temperature fluctuations by 38 %.
- **Preservation of mechanical strength**: Maintained a 28 d compressive strength of 24.5 MPa in cement panels containing 20 wt % FSPCM.

## Methods

- Raw materials: OPC (P·O 42.5), acid-activated halloysite nanotubes (A-Hal), PEG-2000, and graphene nanoplatelets.
- Mixture formulations (Table 1, cement 1000g, water 450g, $W/C = 0.45$):
  - **CP**: Plain cement panel (Control)
  - **CP/M/G0**: Cement + PEG/A-Hal 200g (20 wt %)
  - **CP/M/G0.5**: Cement + PEG/A-Hal 200g + graphene 5g (0.5 wt %)
  - **CP/M/G1.0**: Cement + PEG/A-Hal 200g + graphene 10g (1.0 wt %)
  - **CP/M/G1.5**: Cement + PEG/A-Hal 200g + graphene 15g (1.5 wt %)
  - **CP/M/G2.0**: Cement + PEG/A-Hal 200g + graphene 20g (2.0 wt %)
- Testing: Hot Disk thermal constants analyzer, DSC thermal analysis, and scaled test chamber (200 × 200 × 200 mm) under xenon lamp heating.
