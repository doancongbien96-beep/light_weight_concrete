## Summary
This Master's thesis by Adham El-Newihy at the University of Windsor systematically investigates the dynamic elastic modulus ($E_d$), dynamic shear modulus ($G_d$), dynamic Poisson's ratio ($\mu_d$), and material damping ratio ($\zeta$) of polypropylene fiber-reinforced concrete (PP-FRC) reinforced with synthetic macro- and micro-polypropylene (PP) fibers ($V_f = 0.1 \sim 1.0 \%$) using the non-destructive ASTM C215 impact resonance method (longitudinal, transverse flexural, and torsional vibration modes), establishing comprehensive correlations with static elastic modulus and four-point flexural toughness ($I_{20}, I_{30}$). The results demonstrated that macro-PP fibers formed micro-damping interfaces, increasing the dynamic damping ratio of concrete by up to **65 %**.

## Key Contributions
- **Development of an automated computational analysis program for the three ASTM C215 vibration modes (longitudinal, transverse flexural, torsional)**: Accurate determination of damping ratios based on Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) spectral peaks and half-power bandwidth methods.
- **Formulation of empirical conversion relationships between dynamic modulus ($E_d$) and static elastic modulus ($E_c$)**: Established the model $E_c = 0.82 E_d + 1.25$ (GPa) ($R^2 = 0.96$).
- **Elucidation of the damping enhancement mechanism with PP fiber dosage**: Viscoelastic micro-friction along fiber-matrix micro-interfaces rapidly dissipated high-frequency vibration energy.
- **Formulation of non-destructive monitoring guidelines for freeze-thaw and crack damage**: Developed dual-damage indicators combining relative dynamic modulus ($RDM$) and damping ratio change rates.

## Methods
- Mixture: Ordinary Portland cement ($W/C = 0.40, 0.50$), maximum aggregate size 19 mm.
- Fibers: Micro-PP fibers ($L_f = 12 mm$, $V_f = 0.1, 0.2 \%$) and macro-synthetic twisted PP fibers ($L_f = 40 mm$, $V_f = 0.3, 0.5, 0.8, 1.0 \%$).
- Specimens and testing: $100 \times 100 \times 400 mm$ prismatic beams, PCB accelerometers, impact hammer resonance testing, uniaxial compressive strength, and four-point flexural toughness tests (ASTM C1609).
