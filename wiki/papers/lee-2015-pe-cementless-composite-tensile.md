# Tensile Behavior of PE Fiber-Reinforced Cementless Composite

## Summary

Paper with a first author from the user's group (Chonnam National University). Established the tensile performance of a composite in which ultra-high-strength straight PE fibers (diameter 12 µm, length 18 mm, tensile strength 2700 MPa) were incorporated at 1.75 vol % into a cementless matrix based on blast-furnace slag + calcium hydroxide + sodium sulfate activator. The W/B 30 % mix (M30) achieved a compressive strength of 49.4 MPa, first-cracking strength of 4.8 MPa, tensile strength of 12.9 MPa, and strain capacity of 6.6 % — a tensile-to-compressive strength ratio of 26 %, more than twice that of ordinary concrete (~10 %).

## Key Contributions

- Secured state-of-the-art tensile performance in a cementless system at the time: simultaneously exceeded both the original AAS-ECC (Lee et al. 2012, tensile 4.7 MPa · strain 4.5 %) and PVA-ECC (4.5 MPa · 4 %) in strength and ductility. Achieved through ordinary mix design alone without special materials — the point of differentiation from Ranade et al.'s HSHDC (compressive 166 MPa).
- Quantified W/B sensitivity: M34 and M38 showed 29 % and 49 % lower tensile strength than M30, respectively — the increase in W/B degrades fiber-matrix bonding, reducing bridging performance.
- Effect of aggregate incorporation: adding 40 % fine aggregate by binder weight (M34-S) reduced tensile strength by 29 % and also lowered strain capacity — demonstrating the mechanism whereby the increase in matrix fracture toughness shrinks the margin for multiple cracking.
- Compressive-to-tensile strength ratio analysis: first-cracking strength averaged 8.10 % of compressive strength (similar to ordinary concrete), while the tensile strength ratio reached 20.7 % (16.3~26.2 % by mix), more than twice that of ordinary concrete.

## Methods

- Materials: GGBS (specific surface area 4320 cm²/g, density 2.92 g/cm³), Ca(OH)₂ 7.5 % + Na₂SO₄ 3 % (by binder mass), HRWRA, and PE fiber (density 0.91 g/cm³, ultra-high-modulus grade with elastic modulus around 96 GPa).
- 4 mixes: M30/M34/M38 (W/B variation) + M34-S (additional 40 % silica sand). Dry mixing 1 min → water mixing 1 min → HRWRA → fiber dispersion 4 min.
- Tests: dog-bone direct tension per JSCE recommendations (measurement section 80 mm, cross-section 30×13 mm) under displacement control at 0.1 mm/min with 2 LVDTs over 100 mm. Density (underwater weighing method), compressive strength (KS L 5105). Water curing at 23±2 °C for 28 days.

## Results

- All mixes showed clear strain hardening + multiple fine cracking followed by localized softening failure.
- Property ranges: density 1.9~2.1 g/cm³, compressive strength 36.9~49.4 MPa, first-cracking strength 2.7~4.8 MPa, tensile strength 6.5~12.9 MPa, strain capacity 4.7~6.6 %.
- Density measurements confirmed the absence of excess air voids (error versus theoretical values 0.05~1.87 %).
