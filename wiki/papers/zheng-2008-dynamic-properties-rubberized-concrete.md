## Summary
An enduring landmark paper of the CCR/CBM literature by Dr. L. Zheng's research team at Tongji University, in which the dynamic elastic modulus ($E_d$), dynamic shear modulus ($G_d$), dynamic Poisson's ratio ($\mu_d$), and material damping ratio (Damping Ratio $\zeta$) of rubberized concrete (RuC), produced by volume replacement of fine/coarse aggregate with waste tire rubber powder (particle size $0.5 \sim 2.0 mm$) and rubber chips (particle size $2 \sim 6 mm$) at 0, 15, 30, 45 vol %, were measured precisely using the ASTM C215 impact resonance method (longitudinal, transverse, and torsional vibration modes), and their correlation with static compressive strength was established. It was demonstrated that at a rubber replacement ratio of 45 vol % the material damping ratio reached **$\zeta = 2.95 \%$**, a **2.6-fold** increase over ordinary concrete ($\zeta = 1.15 \%$).

## Key Contributions
- **Precise quantification of the dynamic parameters of rubberized concrete based on the three ASTM C215 vibration modes**: mathematical description of the vibration attenuation mechanism induced by viscoelastic friction of the rubber particles.
- **Derivation of the damping ratio increase and dynamic modulus decay envelope as functions of the rubber replacement ratio ($r_v$)**: establishment of the linear prediction equation $\zeta = 1.15(1 + 0.035 r_v)$ ($R^2 = 0.98$).
- **Elucidation of aggregate size effects**: fine rubber powder exhibited less strength reduction than coarse rubber chips while its damping enhancement effect was superior by 25 %.
- **Establishment of design guidance for high-speed rail track slabs and seismic vibration-isolating foundations**: demonstration of a 6 dB reduction in the vibration acceleration level.

## Methods
- Mix proportions: OPC ($W/C = 0.45$), with rubber powder (RP) and rubber chip (RC) replacement ratios of 0, 15, 30, 45 vol %.
- Tests: $100 \times 100 \times 400 mm$ prismatic specimens were subjected to ASTM C215 impact hammer resonance testing with accelerometer FFT signal analysis; 28 d uniaxial compressive strength and static elastic modulus were also measured.
