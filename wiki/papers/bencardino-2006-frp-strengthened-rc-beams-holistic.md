## Summary

A Cement and Concrete Composites classic in which Prof. F. Bencardino (University of
Calabria) and Prof. R.N. Swamy (University of Sheffield) established four-point bending
tests on full-scale RC beams ($150 \times 250 \times 3000 mm$) covering shear-span ratios
($a/d = 6.9, 3.0$) and external mechanical end/intermediate anchorage systems (External
Anchorage System), together with a comprehensive holistic design model (Holistic Design
Model), in order to prevent premature end peeling and intermediate-crack-induced debonding
(Intermediate Crack Debonding) of reinforced concrete (RC) beams strengthened by externally
bonded carbon fiber reinforced polymer (CFRP) plates and to induce fully ductile failure.
The study demonstrated that applying the optimal anchorage system blocks brittle peeling
100 %, raises the ultimate flexural load from the unstrengthened beam's 54.0 kN to
**98.8 kN**, an increase of **83 %**, and dissipates strain energy up to CFRP rupture.

## Key Contributions

- **Completion of a fully ductile failure mechanism for FRP-strengthened RC beams combined
  with external anchorage**: brittle interfacial debonding suppressed and the CFRP tensile
  strain utilized up to $10100 \mu m/m$.
- **Clarification of flexure-shear interaction across shear-span ratios ($a/d = 6.9$ vs
  $3.0$)**: premature shear peeling prevented even in the deep-beam region.
- **Maximization of concrete compressive extreme-fiber strain**: plastic strains of
  compression-zone concrete stably maintained up to $3700 \sim 4200 \mu m/m$ at ultimate,
  versus $5000 \mu m/m$ for the unstrengthened beam.
- **Guidance for improving international design codes (ACI 440.2R, fib Bulletin 14)**:
  design-strength calculation formulas accounting for mechanical-anchor anchorage
  efficiency established.

## Methods

- Beam specimens: $150 \times 250 \times 3000 mm$ (tension reinforcement 2-$\phi 12$,
  $f_y = 540 MPa$; shear stirrups $\phi 8@100$).
- Strengthening material: Sika CarboDur S512 CFRP plate (thickness 1.2 mm, width 50 mm,
  $E = 165 GPa$, $f_{fu} = 2800 MPa$).
- Anchorage variables: Unplated (Control), Plated (CFRP alone), Plated + Anchorage 1 (end
  anchors), Plated + Anchorage 2 (end + intermediate anchors).
- Testing: four-point bending load tests ($a/d = 6.9$: span 2800 mm; $a/d = 3.0$: span
  1500 mm).
