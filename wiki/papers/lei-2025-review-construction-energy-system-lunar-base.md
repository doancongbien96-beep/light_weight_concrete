# Comprehensive Review of Supporting Energy System Construction Technologies and Thermal-Control Infrastructure for a Permanently Crewed Lunar Base: Vertical Solar Towers, Small Modular Reactors (SMR), Regolith Latent-Heat Storage, and Space Civil Engineering Construction Mechanisms

## Summary

To operate the life-support systems (ECLSS) and construction equipment of a permanently crewed lunar base without interruption under the harsh lunar environment — the 14-day long night ($-183\ ^\circ\text{C}$ extreme cold) and 14-day day ($+120\ ^\circ\text{C}$ heat), intense solar ultraviolet radiation from the absence of atmosphere and magnetic field, and space radiation — this paper comprehensively consolidates **the construction technologies for high-altitude vertical solar power towers in permanently illuminated regions (HIR) at the lunar south pole, megawatt-class small modular reactors (SMR/Kilopower), hydrogen-oxygen regenerative fuel cells (RFC), and latent/sensible heat thermal storage infrastructure based on local regolith (ISRU), together with thermal-control piping network construction schemes**, and systematically analyzes life-cycle reliability of the composite production-storage-distribution-heat-rejection grid and the space civil engineering construction process. This is the latest landmark review by Prof. Guoqing Zhang's team at Shenzhen University, published in Frontiers in Astronomy and Space Sciences in 2025.

## Key Contributions

1. **First comprehensive systematization of the four core architectures of lunar-base supporting energy system construction**:
   - Integrated comparison of construction technology and mass payload across photovoltaic-regenerative fuel cell (PV-RFC), small modular reactor (SMR), local regolith thermal energy storage (TES), and space wireless power transmission (WPT).
2. **Presentation of the construction mechanism for vertical solar towers at the lunar south pole**:
   - To overcome terrain-induced solar occlusion, lightweight inflatable/truss towers $20\sim 50\ \text{m}$ tall are erected by autonomous robotic construction, securing an annual sunlight fraction above 90 %.
3. **Completion of nighttime thermal control through ISRU regolith sensible/latent heat storage systems**:
   - Local regolith heated to high temperature ($>800\ ^\circ\text{C}$) for thermal storage establishes passive heat-buffering infrastructure that maintains the base interior at $+20\ ^\circ\text{C}$ through the 354-hour extreme night.
4. **Establishment of power-grid standards for future Artemis and ILRS international lunar research stations**:
   - Provides civil-engineering layout guidelines for microgrids distributing power to habitat modules, ISRU resource extraction plants, and launch/landing pads.

## Methods

- **Energy and construction system analysis framework (Sections 2~6 & Tables 1~5 & Figs. 1~12)**:
  - 1. Power production: vertical solar towers (GaAs multi-junction cells, efficiency 32 %), Kilopower reactors ($1\sim 10\ \text{kWe}$ Stirling engines).
  - 2. Energy storage: hydrogen-oxygen RFC (specific energy $450\ \text{Wh/kg}$), lithium-ion batteries, regolith sensible heat storage ($C_p = 800\ \text{J/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$).
  - 3. Thermal-control infrastructure: variable-conductance heat pipes (VCHP), regolith shielding covers, radiator panels.
- **Lunar environment database (Sections 2.1~2.3 & Figs. 3~6)**:
  - Sunlight analysis around Shackleton crater at the south pole and regolith thermophysical properties ($k = 0.001\sim 0.015\ \text{W/(m}\cdot\text{K)}$ under vacuum).

### 2. Interpretation of thermal control and regolith insulation infrastructure
- **Remarkable insulating capacity of vacuum-exposed regolith**: under the surface vacuum environment, the thermal conductivity of a regolith layer falls to $0.002\ \text{W/(m}\cdot\text{K)}$, so even a $0.5\ \text{m}$ cover prevents freezing of buried piping with 100 % reliability.
- **Civil shielding by SMR burial**: burying the small modular reactor at a regolith depth of 3 m fully blocks surface radiation exposure and secures safety standoff from habitable zones.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is the top-level landmark review origin of extraterrestrial energy self-sufficiency and space civil engineering, comprehensively consolidating the four core architectures of permanent lunar-base supporting energy systems and their thermal-control infrastructure construction technology.
