# Flexural Behavior of Extruded DFRCC Panel Composite Slab

## Summary

This paper evaluates the flexural behavior and crack control performance of composite slabs integrating stay-in-place ductile fiber-reinforced cementitious composite (DFRCC) formwork panels manufactured via extrusion as bottom tensile reinforcement with cast-in-place reinforced concrete (RC). Leveraging the 2D planar fiber alignment and superior flexural tensile capacity of extruded DFRCC panels ($f_t > 8.0\ \text{MPa}$, $f_t > 8.0 MPa$, flexural tensile strength $> 18\ \text{MPa}$, $> 18 MPa$), the system serves as unpropped formwork during construction while limiting service crack widths to below $0.05\ \text{mm}$ ($0.05 mm$) and enhancing ultimate flexural capacity.

## Key Contributions

- **Proposal of an extruded DFRCC permanent formwork composite slab system**: Formulated a composite section functioning as stay-in-place formwork without stripping while providing structural bottom tensile reinforcement.
- **Verification of interface shear bond integrity**: Proved complete composite action without interfacial delamination up to ultimate flexural failure through top shear keys and surface roughening on the DFRCC panels.
- **Quantification of crack control and deflection mitigation**: Demonstrated a 45 % increase in initial cracking load and a 35 % reduction in service-load deflections compared to conventional RC slabs.

## Methods

- **DFRCC panel specifications**: Thicknesses of 15 mm and 20 mm extruded panels, PVA/PE hybrid fiber reinforcement ($V_f = 1.8\%$), W/B = 0.26.
- **Composite slab specimens**: Full-scale slabs ($1000 \times 150 \times 3000\ \text{mm}$, $1000 \times 150 \times 3000 mm$) with 130 mm cast-in-place concrete atop DFRCC panels.
- **Flexural testing**: Four-point bending tests over a simply supported 2700 mm span with LVDT deflection tracking and electrical strain gauges.

## Results

- **Flexural performance comparison**:
  - Conventional RC slab: First-crack load $18.5\ \text{kN}$ ($18.5 kN$), ultimate flexural capacity $62.4\ \text{kN}$ ($62.4 kN$), maximum crack width $0.35\ \text{mm}$ ($0.35 mm$).
  - DFRCC composite slab: First-crack load $27.0\ \text{kN}$ ($27.0 kN$, 46 % increase), ultimate capacity $79.8\ \text{kN}$ ($79.8 kN$, 28 % increase), exhibiting distributed microcracking ($w < 0.04\ \text{mm}$, $w < 0.04 mm$) in the bottom DFRCC layer.
- Validated standard plastic section analysis (ACI 318, 318) for predicting composite slab flexural capacities under full interface bond.
