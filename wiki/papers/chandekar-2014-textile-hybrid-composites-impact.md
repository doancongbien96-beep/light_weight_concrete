## Summary
To maximize the impact damage resistance and fracture energy absorption capacity of aerospace and protective composite structures, Prof. B.V. Sankar's research team at the University of Florida characterized the low-velocity drop-weight impact behavior (impact energy 10 to 40 J) of hybrid composite panels, molded as woven epoxy composites ($100 \times 100 \times 3.2 mm$, fiber volume fraction $V_f = 50 \%$) that combine high-strength carbon fiber woven fabric (AS4 Carbon) with high-ductility glass fiber woven fabric (E-Glass) in various stacking layouts (layer-by-layer hybrid vs non-hybrid), through experiments and unit-cell 3D finite element analysis. The study demonstrated that a hybrid composite with E-Glass placed on the outer surfaces and Carbon laminated toward the interior improves the impact absorbed energy by **38 %** and reduces the back-face delamination area by **45 %** relative to an all-carbon composite.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishment of 3D elasticity and damage constitutive equations for the woven-fabric unit cell**: quantification of the anisotropic properties of E-Glass/epoxy ($E_a = 38.27 GPa$) and AS4 Carbon/epoxy ($E_a = 125.48 GPa$).
- **Identification of the impact energy absorption mechanism as a function of hybrid stacking sequence**: the surface glass fiber layers disperse local crushing damage while the interior carbon fiber layers carry flexural tension.
- **Derivation of contact force-displacement hysteresis envelopes by impact energy level (10, 20, 30, 40 J)**: a 22 % reduction in peak impact load together with an extended impact duration.
- **Precise measurement of the delamination area via ultrasonic C-scan nondestructive inspection**: design criteria proposed for non-perforating protective panels.

## Methods
- Composite panels: E-Glass woven fabric, AS4 Carbon woven fabric, epoxy resin (total thickness 3.2 mm, 8-ply layup, $V_f = 0.50$).
- Stacking configurations: All-Carbon, All-Glass, Hybrid-1 (two outer glass layers + four inner carbon layers + two outer glass layers), Hybrid-2 (alternating stacking).
- Impact testing: Instron Dynatup instrumented drop-weight tower (hemispherical indenter diameter 12.7 mm, mass 3.5 kg, impact energies of 10, 20, 30, and 40 J).
