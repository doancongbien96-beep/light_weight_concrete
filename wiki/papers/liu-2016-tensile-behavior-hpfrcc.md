## Summary

This study experimentally evaluated the effects of matrix binder composition (fly ash and silica fume replacement ratios), water-to-binder ratio ($W/B$), and PVA fiber volume fraction ($V_f = 1.0 \sim 2.5 \%$) on the direct tensile performance of high performance fiber reinforced cementitious composites (HPFRCC). Uniaxial direct tension tests on dogbone specimens precisely measured first-crack strength, ultimate tensile strength, tensile strain capacity, and multiple fine-crack spacing. At $W/B = 0.28$ with 50 wt % fly ash replacement, reduced matrix fracture toughness and optimized interfacial frictional bond acted in harmony, achieving a maximum tensile strain of $4.8 \%$ and an ultimate tensile strength of $5.6 MPa$.

## Key Contributions

- **Mapping of the tensile strain-hardening envelope versus binder composition**: identified the mechanism in which increasing fly ash replacement lowers matrix toughness while raising the complementary energy of fiber bridging.
- **Analysis of critical behavior by PVA fiber volume fraction**: demonstrated that stable multiple cracking forms above $V_f = 1.5 \%$ and that ductility saturates at $V_f = 2.0 \%$.
- **Verification of a quantitative crack-spacing and crack-width model**: confirmed strong agreement between mean crack spacing computed from Aveston-Cooper-Kelly (ACK) theory and measured values ($1.8 \sim 3.2 mm$).

## Methods

- **Materials and mix proportioning**: P.O 42.5 cement, Class F fly ash (replacement ratios 30 %, 50 %, 70 %), silica fume (0 %, 5 %), PVA fiber (REC15, diameter 39 µm, length 12 mm).
- **Test methods**: JSCE-compliant dogbone direct tension test (displacement control $0.5 mm/min$), dual-sided LVDT displacement measurement, 28 d compressive strength.

## Results

- Optimal mix (FA 50 %, $V_f = 2.0 \%$): first-crack strength $3.8 MPa$, ultimate tensile strength $5.6 MPa$, tensile strain capacity $4.8 \%$, compressive strength $48.2 MPa$.
- Fly-ash-free mix (OPC 100 %): matrix toughness so high that fibers ruptured; tensile strain capacity plunged to $0.8 \%$.
