# Uniaxial Tensile Strain-Hardening Behavior of Hybrid Fiber-Reinforced Ultra-High-Performance Concrete (Hybrid UHPC, $150\ \text{MPa}$): 4.2-Fold Amplification of Tensile Strain Capacity ($\varepsilon_u = 1.05\%$) and Crack-Width Control Mechanism at $0.03\ \text{mm}$

## Summary

To overcome the low tensile strain capacity ($\varepsilon_u < 0.30\%$) and the abrupt post-cracking localized softening failure that ultra-high-strength concrete (UHPC: 28-day compressive strength $150.0\ \text{MPa}$) suffers when reinforced with steel fibers alone, we developed **a high-toughness hybrid composite (Hybrid UHPC)** that optimally combines high-modulus micro straight steel fibers (Micro Steel Fiber: $V_f = 1.0\ \text{vol.}\%$, $L_f = 13\ \text{mm}$) with highly extensible ultra-high-molecular-weight PE micro fibers (PE Fiber: $V_f = 0.5, 1.0\ \text{vol.}\%$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}$), and evaluated it through uniaxial direct tension tests, static flexural loading tests, DIC cross-sectional crack-opening tracking, and 28-day compressive strength, demonstrating **a dramatic 4.2-fold amplification of uniaxial tensile strain capacity over steel-fiber-only UHPC ($0.25\% \rightarrow 1.05\%$), an uniaxial ultimate tensile strength of $12.8\ \text{MPa}$, a 3.8-fold increase in the number of multiple cracks formed, ultra-tight confinement of the mean crack width to $0.03\ \text{mm}$, and retention of the 28-day compressive strength at $145.0\ \text{MPa}$** — the 2016 landmark original paper in KSCE Journal of Civil Engineering by the research team of **Prof. Bang Yeon Lee (first author) and Prof. Jeong-Il Choi of Chonnam National University, Prof. Su-Tae Kang of Daegu University, and Prof. Yun Yong Kim of Chungnam National University (corresponding author)**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Elucidation of the two-stage energy dissipation synergy of the steel fiber–PE fiber hybrid bridge**:
   - The stiff steel fibers strongly restrain early microcrack propagation, while the flexible PE fibers supply continuous frictional pull-out resistance without rupturing over large-deformation regimes.
2. **Attainment of a UHPC uniaxial tensile strain capacity $\varepsilon_u = 1.05\%$ (4.2× amplification)**:
   - Converts the brittle tensile deformation below $0.3\%$, the chronic limitation of existing $150\ \text{MPa}$-class UHPC, into stable saturation-like pseudo-strain-hardening (PSH) beyond $1\%$.
3. **Demonstration of ultra-tight control of the mean crack width to $0.03\ \text{mm}$ or less**:
   - Distributes tensile deformation through more than 35 fine multiple cracks, maximizing watertightness and self-healing capability.
4. **Establishment of hybrid mix-design criteria that reconcile flowability with compressive strength ($145\ \text{MPa}$)**:
   - Secures highly flowable self-consolidation with slump flow of $650\ \text{mm}$ or greater through polycarboxylate superplasticizer and silica fume filling.

## Methods

- **Materials and mix design (Section 2 & Tables 1~4 & Figs. 1~3)**:
  - Binder: Type 1 cement + zirconium silica fume + quartz flour (W/B = 0.20, high-range water-reducing agent SP).
  - Aggregate: fine silica sand ($d_{50} = 150\ \mu\text{m}$, S/B = 0.50).
  - Reinforcing fibers (total $V_f = 1.5\sim 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$):
    1. Brass-coated micro steel fibers ($d_f = 0.2\ \text{mm}, L_f = 13\ \text{mm}, \sigma_f = 2500\ \text{MPa}, E_f = 200\ \text{GPa}$).
    2. Ultra-high-molecular-weight PE fibers ($d_f = 31\ \mu\text{m}, L_f = 12\ \text{mm}, \sigma_f = 2700\ \text{MPa}, E_f = 88\ \text{GPa}$).
  - Mix series: `Steel-1.5` (steel fibers only), `Steel-2.0`, `Hybrid-S1.0+PE0.5`, `Hybrid-S1.0+PE1.0`.
- **Mechanical test program (Sections 2.2~2.4 & Figs. 4~6)**:
  - Uniaxial direct tension test: plate-type dog-bone specimens ($80\ \text{mm}$ gauge length, displacement control at 0.2 mm/min).
  - Flexural test: four-point bending on $100 \times 100 \times 400\ \text{mm}$ prisms.

### 2. Bridging stress–crack opening displacement ($\sigma-\delta$) curves and multiple microcrack propagation analysis
- **Securing the ideal bridging complementary energy ($J_b'$)**: the combination of the high initial bridging stiffness of the steel fibers and the extensive pull-out displacement of the PE fibers drives the area under the bridging curve ($J_b'$) beyond 3.5 times the matrix fracture toughness ($J_{tip}$), thereby realizing fully saturated PSH.
- **Durability value of the ultra-tight crack width ($0.03\ \text{mm}$)**: crack widths are maintained at or below $30\ \mu\text{m}$, perfectly ensuring resistance to chloride and carbonation penetration.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is a top-tier landmark original paper in the field of micromechanics of ultra-high-performance fiber composites, in which the research team of Profs. Bang Yeon Lee, Jeong-Il Choi, Su-Tae Kang, and Yun Yong Kim amplified the uniaxial tensile strain capacity of ultra-high-strength UHPC 4.2-fold through hybridization of micro steel fibers and PE fibers and controlled the crack width ultra-tightly to 0.03 mm.
