# Behavior of Fixed-End RC Beams Strengthened with HPFRCC: Ductility and Moment Redistribution

## Summary

To prevent premature shear/flexural failure at hogging negative moment supports and maximize moment redistribution across plastic hinges in continuous and fixed-end reinforced concrete beams under seismic or overload conditions, Dr. N. Ghods and Professor M. K. Sharbatdar at Semnan University tested full-scale fixed-end beams (section 250 × 300 mm, 2600 mm clear span) made of high-strength concrete (HSC: fc = 51 MPa) and high-performance fiber-reinforced cementitious composites (HPFRCC: fc = 68, 75 MPa) reinforced with micro-steel fibers ($V_f = 1.0, 2.0\ \text{vol}\%$, tensile strength 1050 MPa) under monotonic point loading. The HPFRCC 2.0 % beam (HF2) achieved a **42.5 %** increase in ultimate load, **3.1-fold** amplification in energy dissipation capacity, and a moment redistribution ratio of **28.5 %** relative to the plain HSC control (NN), realizing ductile flexural failure without conventional shear stirrups.

## Key Contributions

- **Formulation of moment redistribution in fixed-end beam plastic hinges**: Fiber bridging in HPFRCC formed stable plastic hinges at support zones, smoothly redistributing 28.5 % of the load to mid-span positive moment regions.
- **Substitution of transverse shear reinforcement via steel fibers ($V_f = 1.0\%$ vs $2.0\%$)**: The HPFRCC matrix acted as internal shear reinforcement, restraining diagonal shear crack widths below 0.2 mm.
- **Amplification of energy absorption and displacement ductility ($\mu = 4.8$)**: Enhanced displacement ductility index 2.3-fold over brittle HSC failure modes.
- **Plastic design guidelines for continuous and frame structures**: Established mixture design criteria alleviating rebar congestion at support joints.

## Methods

- Specimen geometry: Section 250 × 300 mm, total length 3200 mm, clear span 2600 mm (tested under 1000 kN hydraulic actuator).
- Material formulations (Table 1):
  - **NN**: High-strength concrete control (HSC, fc = 51 MPa, ft = 4.2 MPa).
  - **HF1**: HPFRCC (steel fibers $V_f = 1.0\%$, fc = 68 MPa, ft = 6.8 MPa).
  - **HF2**: HPFRCC (steel fibers $V_f = 2.0\%$, fc = 75 MPa, ft = 9.2 MPa).
- Steel fibers: Straight high-strength steel fibers (length 50 mm, diameter 1 mm, aspect ratio 50, tensile strength 1050 MPa).
- Instrumentation: LVDTs for mid-span and support deflections, strain gages on steel bars and concrete.
