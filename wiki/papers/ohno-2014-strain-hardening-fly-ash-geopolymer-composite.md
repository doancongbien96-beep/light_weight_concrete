# First Development of a Fly Ash-Based Strain-Hardening Geopolymer Composite (SHGC): Ultimate Tensile Ductility of $4.3\%$ and the DIC-Based Mean Crack Width $45\ \mu\text{m}$ Restraint Mechanism

## Summary

To drastically reduce carbon emissions by excluding Portland cement at $100\%$ while still realizing the ultra-high-ductility multiple fine cracking behavior of conventional cement-based ECC, this study designed for the first time a **strain-hardening geopolymer composite (SHGC: Strain-Hardening Geopolymer Composite) incorporating 2.0 vol.% PVA fibers into a low-calcium Class F fly ash geopolymer matrix**, and precisely measured the uniaxial direct tensile behavior under curing temperature conditions ($20\ ^\circ\text{C}, 60\ ^\circ\text{C}, 80\ ^\circ\text{C}$) together with **the strain fields and fine crack width evolution captured by non-contact Digital Image Correlation (DIC)** — thereby demonstrating perfect crack restraint performance of pseudo strain-hardening (PSH) with an ultimate tensile strength of $4.6\ \text{MPa}$ and an ultimate tensile strain of $4.3\%$ under $60\ ^\circ\text{C}$ curing, plus a mean crack width of $45\ \mu\text{m}$ and a maximum crack width of $117\ \mu\text{m}$ even at $4.5\%$ strain, in an **immortal landmark origin paper of Construction and Building Materials 2014** by **Prof. Victor C. Li of the University of Michigan and Dr. Motohiro Ohno**.

## Key Contributions

1. **World's first proof of feasibility of cement-free fly ash geopolymer-based ultra-high-ductility strain-hardening (SHGC)**:
   - Overcame brittle fracture in an alkali-activated fly ash N-A-S-H gel matrix and demonstrated the vast tensile elongation of $4.3\%$ with pseudo strain-hardening (PSH).
2. **Precise visualization of the strain-hardening microcrack development mechanism through Digital Image Correlation (DIC)**:
   - Quantified the opening widths and spacing of more than 20 microcracks generated during tensile deformation via non-contact DIC strain field analysis at $30\ \mu\text{m}$ pixel resolution.
3. **Demonstration of mean crack width control at $45\ \mu\text{m}$ even in the ultimate tensile state ($\varepsilon = 4.5\%$)**:
   - Even at the enormous tensile elongation state of $4.5\%$, the mean crack width was strictly limited to $45\ \mu\text{m}$ and the maximum crack width to $117\ \mu\text{m}$, proving suppression of harmful ion penetration and self-healing potential.
4. **Establishment of the effect of curing temperature on geopolymer polymerization degree and fiber interfacial friction**:
   - Proposed the optimal $60\ ^\circ\text{C}$ heat treatment window that overcomes both the under-reaction vulnerability at room temperature ($20\ ^\circ\text{C}$) and the excessive brittle fiber rupture at high temperature ($80\ ^\circ\text{C}$).

## Methods

- **Materials and SHGC mixture design (Section 2.1 & Tables 1–2)**:
  - Binder: Class F fly ash 100 wt.%.
  - Alkali activator: liquid sodium silicate ($\text{SiO}_2/\text{Na}_2\text{O} = 2.0$) + 10M $\text{NaOH}$ solution.
  - Aggregate: silica sand ($d_{50} = 110\ \mu\text{m}$, S/FA = 0.30, W/GP solids ratio = 0.32).
  - Fiber: Kuraray K-II REC15 PVA fiber ($V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.}\%$, $d_f = 39\ \mu\text{m}$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}$, $\sigma_f = 1600\ \text{MPa}$, 1.2% oil coating).
- **Three curing conditions (Section 2.2 & Fig. 1)**:
  - Group A ($20\ ^\circ\text{C}$): 28 days of ambient curing.
  - Group B ($60\ ^\circ\text{C}$): 24-hour heating at $60\ ^\circ\text{C}$ followed by 27 days of ambient storage.
  - Group C ($80\ ^\circ\text{C}$): 24-hour heating at $80\ ^\circ\text{C}$ followed by 27 days of ambient storage.
- **Uniaxial tension and DIC crack analysis (Sections 2.3–2.4 & Figs. 2–4)**:
  - Specimens: dogbone tensile specimens ($80 \times 30 \times 13\ \text{mm}$, tension at 0.5 mm/min).
  - DIC system: surface speckle pattern imaging at 1-second intervals $\rightarrow$ Vic-2D software strain field and crack width integration.

### 2. Interpretation of the micromechanics PSH criterion and the DIC crack restraint mechanism
- **Micromechanical energy criterion $J_b'/J_{tip} \ge 1.0$ satisfied**: the $60\ ^\circ\text{C}$ heat curing forms an adequately strong N-A-S-H gel network, suppressing the matrix fracture toughness ($J_{tip}$) from growing excessively large while maximizing the frictional fiber pullout energy ($J_b'$) of the PVA fibers, thereby inducing stable steady-state flat crack propagation.
- **DIC-proven crack width self-restraint**: while the tensile strain increased from $1\% \rightarrow 4.5\%$, individual crack widths did not widen; instead new microcracks formed continuously, holding the mean crack width strictly at $45\ \mu\text{m}$.

- **Overall conclusion**: this paper is the historic top-authority landmark origin paper of the EGC/geopolymer composite field, in which Prof. Victor C. Li proved for the first time the design feasibility of a cement-free fly ash geopolymer-based strain-hardening composite (SHGC) and elucidated, through DIC analysis, the $4.3\%$ tensile ductility and the $45\ \mu\text{m}$ ultra-fine crack restraint mechanism.
