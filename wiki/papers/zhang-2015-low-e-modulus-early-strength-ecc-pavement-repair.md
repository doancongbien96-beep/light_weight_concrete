## Summary
To simultaneously realize the ultra-early strength development required for emergency section repair of airport runway and highway concrete pavements (traffic opening within 4 hours) and the low elastic modulus characteristics needed to prevent thermal shrinkage cracking, the research team of Professor Victor Li at the University of Michigan elucidated the mix design mechanism and mechanical properties of an ultra-early-strength low elastic modulus composite (Low-E Early-Strength ECC) combining calcium sulfoaluminate (CSA) cement, rubber powder, and high-ductility PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$). Within only 4 hours of casting the composite achieved a compressive strength of 24.5 MPa and a flexural strength of 6.8 MPa, while lowering the elastic modulus to 14.5 GPa and preventing reflective cracking 100 %.

## Key Contributions
- **Formulation of a low elastic modulus ECC allowing 4-hour ultra-early traffic opening**: rapid ettringite crystallization of CSA coupled with PVA fiber bridging.
- **Thermal stress reduction of 50 % through a low elastic modulus ($E = 14.5 GPa$)**: source-level blocking of reflective cracking induced by the stiffness mismatch with the substrate concrete.
- **Securement of a tensile strain capacity of 3.8 %**: prevention of debonding-type failure at pavement joints and patching areas.
- **Establishment of guidelines for emergency airport runway recovery**: demonstration of the feasibility of permitting aircraft landings immediately after a 4-hour nighttime closure work.

## Methods
- Binder: rapid-hardening CSA cement 80 % + fly ash 20 %, with citric acid (0.5 wt %) as setting retarder and rubber powder (10 vol %).
- Fibers: Kuraray REC15 PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 39 µm, length 12 mm).
- Testing: compressive strength at ages of 2 h, 4 h, 1 d, and 28 d, four-point flexural testing, dog-bone direct tensile testing, and dynamic elastic modulus measurement.

## Results
- Mechanical property development data of Low-E ECC at early curing ages:

| Age | Compressive strength (MPa) | Flexural modulus of rupture MOR (MPa) | Uniaxial tensile strength (MPa) | Ultimate tensile strain capacity (%) | Elastic modulus $E$ (GPa) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 2 h | 14.2 | 4.20 | 2.85 | 2.50 % | 10.5 |
| **4 h (traffic opening)** | **24.5** | **6.80** | **4.20** | **3.80 %** | **14.5 (Low-E)** |
| 1 day (24 h) | 38.5 | 9.50 | 5.10 | 4.20 % | 16.8 |
