## Summary
A study by Professor Victor Li's research team at the University of Michigan clarifying through single fiber pullout tests and micromechanics numerical analysis the chemical bond energy ($G_d$), frictional shear stress ($\tau_0$), slip-hardening coefficient ($\beta$), and snubbing coefficient ($f$) of single ultra-high molecular weight PE fibers and PVA fibers treated with ultra-fine silica fume packing and silane surface modification, in order to prevent premature fiber rupture in high-strength cement matrices of around 100 MPa compressive strength and realize ultra-ductile tensile strain hardening. Through optimal silica fume packing, the interfacial frictional stress of hydrophobic PE fibers was driven to **1.85 MPa** and the slip-hardening coefficient to **0.82**, achieving a tensile strain capacity of **8.5 %** without fiber rupture in a high-strength matrix.

## Key Contributions
- **Dedicated fiber interface micromechanics model for ultra-high-strength matrices ($f_c > 100 MPa$) completed**: boundary line established between the sharp rise of $\tau_0$ with matrix strength and the fiber rupture limit.
- **Interfacial slip-hardening mechanism of PE fibers ($\beta = 0.82$) clarified**: micro fibrillation of the fiber surface during pullout slip induces additional mechanical friction interlocking.
- **Optimal design ratio of chemical bond $G_d$ to frictional stress $\tau_0$ presented**: conditions $G_d \le 1.5 J/m^2$ and $\tau_0 \le 2.0 MPa$ derived.
- **Guidelines established for optimizing high-strength strain-hardening composite mixes**: stable multiple fine cracking induction guaranteed.

## Methods
- Matrix: OPC + silica fume (15 %) + fine slag (20 %), $W/B = 0.20$, 28 d compressive strength 105 MPa.
- Fibers: Spectra PE fibers ($d_f = 12 \mu m, L_f = 12 mm, f_t = 3000 MPa$) and PVA fibers ($d_f = 39 \mu m, L_f = 12 mm$).
- Tests: precision servo micro single fiber pullout tester ($0.1 mm/min$, embedded depth $L_e = 1 \sim 6 mm$, pullout angles $\theta = 0°, 30°, 60°$).

## Results
- Measured single fiber interface parameters by fiber type and embedded angle:

| Fiber type | Embedded angle ($\theta$) | Chemical bond $G_d$ ($J/m^2$) | Frictional shear stress $\tau_0$ (MPa) | Slip-hardening coeff. $\beta$ | Snubbing coeff. $f$ | Pullout failure mode |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| PE fiber | 0° (orthogonal) | 0.05 | **1.85** | **0.82** | - | **full pullout** |
| PE fiber | 30° (inclined) | 0.08 | **2.45** | **0.95** | 0.82 | **full pullout (high energy dissipation)** |
| PVA fiber | 0° (orthogonal) | 2.85 | 3.20 | 0.15 | - | premature rupture in 45 % of fibers |
