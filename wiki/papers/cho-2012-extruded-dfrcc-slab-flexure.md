# Flexural behavior and ductility enhancement of RC composite slabs with extruded DFRCC panels

## Summary

To eliminate formwork (half-precast rationalization) and control tensile-face cracking while
boosting ductility, highly ductile fiber-reinforced cement composites (DFRCC) were extruded
into thin permanent-formwork panels (20 mm thick) with three integral mechanical ribs, then
topped with 160 mm of cast concrete — three one-way full-scale RC composite slabs (600 mm
wide, 180 mm deep, 4000 mm span) tested in four-point bending plus panel-unit flexure
(co-authored by Bang Yeon Lee, Chonnam National University). The extruded DFRCC panel alone
showed outstanding strain-hardening and multiple cracking: flexural strength 37.0 MPa,
deflection ductility ratio $\delta/\delta_i = 9.01$. At the structural level the composite
slabs improved maximum moment 21 %, maximum curvature 42 %, and curvature ductility ratio
170 % over ordinary RC slabs (RC-0). Notably, the DFRCC-R specimen with half the tensile
reinforcement ($\rho_s = 0.26\ \%$) still secured maximum capacity equal to or better than
the ordinary RC slab ($\rho_s = 0.47\ \%$, D13 bars): 52.5 kN versus 51.3 kN.

## Key Contributions

1. **Ultra-high-toughness flexural hardening of extruded DFRCC panels**: shear-flow alignment
   during extrusion orients PVA fibers longitudinally; after first-cracking strength
   $f_i = 29.1\ \text{MPa}$, panel-unit flexure sustained stable hardening to 37.0 MPa.
   Twelve saturated fine cracks formed before peak with deflection ductility
   $\delta/\delta_i = 9.01$ — brittle failure fully excluded.
2. **Full composite action through mechanical interlocking of three ribs**: without shear
   studs or chemical adhesives, the three continuous trapezoidal ribs formed during extrusion
   mechanically interlock the topping concrete; no delamination occurred even at extreme
   flexure with $a/d \approx 8.4$.
3. **Proved a rebar-saving eco-friendly high-ductility slab system**: with the DFRCC panel
   sharing tensile stress directly, main reinforcement dropped 45 % (D13 → D10); the DFRCC-R
   slab delivered 1.02x the maximum flexural capacity and 1.44x the curvature ductility of
   ordinary RC — safety and economy together.
4. **Precise tensile-face crack-width control**: ordinary RC slabs opened single central cracks
   by millimeters after yield; slabs on DFRCC panels dispersed fine multiple cracks even at
   displacements above 30 mm, suppressing harmful width growth through ultimate load.

## Methods

- **Extruded DFRCC materials and mix (mass ratios to cement)**:
  - OPC (density $3.15\ \text{g/cm}^3$, fineness $363\ \text{m}^2/\text{kg}$) 1.0.
  - Silica powder (density $2.66\ \text{g/cm}^3$, fineness $379\ \text{m}^2/\text{kg}$,
    $\text{SiO}_2$ 95.5 %) 1.1.
  - Special DFRCC composite powder (BFS, sepiolite, $\text{Mg(OH)}_2$, $\text{CaCO}_3$, CSA,
    $\text{Al(OH)}_3$, CW150) 0.88.
  - Water 0.30, SP 0.0061, viscosity agent (HPMC) 0.018.
  - Fibers: oil-coated hydrophilic PVA (density $1.3\ \text{g/cm}^3$, diameter 39 µm, length
    8 mm, tensile strength 1700 MPa, E 29.4 GPa, elongation 3–113 %) at 2.0 vol.%.
- **Manufacture**: dry mix → wet knead → vacuum extrusion (20 mm thick, 600 mm wide, 4000 mm
  long, three ribs integral) → 50 °C steam curing for 3 days.
- **Slab specimen design**:
  - Section $600\ \text{mm} \times 180\ \text{mm}$, overall 4000 mm, clear span 3700 mm.
  - Topping concrete: design strength $f'_c = 24\ \text{MPa}$.
  - Main rebar: RC-0 (D13 @150 mm, $\rho_s = 0.47\ \%$, $f_y = 412\ \text{MPa}$), DFRCC-P
    (D13 @150 mm, $\rho_s = 0.47\ \%$), DFRCC-R (D10 @150 mm, $\rho_s = 0.26\ \%$,
    $f_y = 412\ \text{MPa}$).
  - Transverse steel: D10 @400 mm throughout. Cover 20 mm.
- **Equipment and instrumentation**:
  - Panel unit: $100\ \text{mm} \times 400\ \text{mm} \times 10\ \text{mm}$ specimens,
    four-point flexure (300 mm span, UTM displacement control, two LVDTs at midspan).
  - Slabs: four-point bending (700 mm load-point spacing), 1000 kN actuator, LVDTs at midspan
    and supports, concrete and rebar strain gauges top and bottom.

## Results

### 1. Panel-unit flexural behavior

| Item | First-crack $f_i$ (MPa) | First deflection $\delta_i$ (mm) | Max strength $f_b$ (MPa) | Max deflection $\delta$ (mm) | Deflection ductility ($\delta/\delta_i$) | Stiffness (kN/mm) | Crack count |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DFRCC panel** | 29.1 | 0.675 | 37.0 | 6.11 | **9.01** | 42.1 | 12 |

### 2. Four-point bending results for composite slabs

| Specimen | Rebar ratio $\rho_s$ (%) | Yield moment (kN·m) | Yield deflection (mm) | Max moment (kN·m) | Max deflection (mm) | Deflection ductility | Curvature ductility | Final failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RC-0 (control)** | 0.47 (D13) | 31.6 | 28.4 | 34.6 | 93.5 | 3.29 | 4.95 | top crushing + major crack over-opening |
| **DFRCC-P** | 0.47 (D13) | 35.8 | 21.4 | **41.8 (+21 %)** | **110 (+18 %)** | **5.15** | **13.4 (+170 %)** | top crushing + bottom fine multiple cracking |
| **DFRCC-R** | 0.26 (D10) | 32.1 | 28.0 | **36.2 (+5 %)** | **102 (+9 %)** | **3.64** | **7.13 (+44 %)** | top crushing + rebar yielding, ductile no-delamination |

- **Strength and stiffness**: DFRCC-P delayed first cracking 1.86x over RC-0 (13.1 kN vs
  7.03 kN) and reached a peak load of 62.3 kN (1.21x RC-0's 51.3 kN).
- **Ductility and mechanism**: curvature ductility 13.4 for DFRCC-P against RC-0's 4.95 —
  about 2.7x, proving excellent plastic energy dissipation under seismic and overload actions.
