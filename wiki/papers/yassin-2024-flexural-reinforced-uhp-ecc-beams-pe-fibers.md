## Summary
A 2024 study in which the research team of Professor Bang Yeon Lee at Chonnam National University characterized, through four-point bending tests and nonlinear section analysis, the flexural capacity envelope, deflection ductility index, load-deflection energy absorption ratio, and tensile crack distribution behavior as a function of tensile reinforcement ratio ($\rho_s = 0.8 \sim 2.5 \%$) when a cement composite of the 120 MPa-class ultra-high-strength, ultra-high-ductility grade (UHP-ECC, uniaxial tensile strain capacity $\epsilon_u > 8.0 \%$) was applied to full-scale reinforced concrete flexural members (R/UHP-ECC beams, $150 \times 250 \times 2200 mm$). Leveraging the ultra-high tensile bridging capacity of high-strength PE fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$), the study demonstrated increases of **48 %** in flexural capacity and **210 %** in deflection ductility over ordinary high-strength concrete (HSC) beams, with crack width controlled below **0.035 mm** up to failure.

## Key Contributions
- **Demonstration of the flexurally ductile failure mechanism of ultra-high-strength R/UHP-ECC beams**: formation of a fully ductile plastic hinge without spalling of the compression zone even at a high reinforcement ratio ($\rho = 2.5 \%$).
- **Maximization of crack-control performance**: maximum crack width fully restrained below $0.04 mm$ across the entire range from service load to the ultimate limit state.
- **3.2-fold improvement in flexural energy dissipation capacity**: verification of the performance of seismically designed flexural members resisting seismic and impact loads.
- **Establishment of a nonlinear section moment-curvature analysis model**: presentation of ACI 318 member design equations reflecting the UHP-ECC tensile strain-hardening envelope.

## Methods
- Eight full-scale beams ($150 \times 250 \times 2200 mm$, span of 1900 mm, tensile reinforcement of 2-D16, 2-D22, and 2-D25, $f_y = 500 MPa$).
- UHP-ECC: low-heat cement + silica fume + silica flour, PE fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, $f_c = 122.5 MPa$, $f_t = 11.5 MPa$, $\epsilon_u = 8.5 \%$).
- Testing: four-point flexural loading test (constant moment region of 600 mm), midspan LVDT deflection measurement, full-field DIC strain tensor and crack width mapping.
