## Summary
A Tongji University study clarifying the effects of the binder composite ratio of Class F fly ash and ground granulated blast furnace slag ($FA/GGBS = 4.0 \sim 1.0$) and alkali activator concentration on the matrix fracture toughness ($K_m$), single PVA fiber interfacial frictional stress ($\tau_0$), and direct tensile envelopes, in order to develop an eco-friendly geopolymer composite (Ambient-Cured PVA-EGC) that cures fully at room temperature ($23 °C$) without heat curing while exhibiting excellent uniaxial direct tensile strain-hardening performance. The GGBS 30 % + FA 70 % mix achieved a room-temperature 28 d compressive strength of **48.5 MPa**, a direct tensile strength of **5.2 MPa**, and an ultimate tensile strain capacity of **4.8 %**.

## Key Contributions
- **Micromechanics PSH strain-hardening criteria of PVA-EGC under ambient curing established**: field-cast feasibility without heat curing equipment demonstrated.
- **Ambient polymerization rate controlled through slag incorporation**: early (3 d) strength development and an appropriate matrix fracture toughness ($K_m = 0.38 MPa\sqrt{m}$) achieved simultaneously.
- **Single PVA fiber interface characteristics optimized**: frictional stress $\tau_0 = 1.55 MPa$ and PSH energy index $J_b'/J_{tip} = 5.2$ secured.
- **Foundation laid for commercialization of eco-friendly composite structural materials cutting embodied carbon by 68 %**: cementless field construction technology established.

## Methods
- Binder: Class F fly ash (70 wt %) + GGBS (30 wt %), combined activator of liquid sodium silicate and $NaOH 10 M$ ($W/B = 0.35$).
- Fiber: Kuraray REC15 PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 39 µm, length 12 mm).
- Curing: ambient air curing ($23 \pm 2 °C$, RH 60 %).
- Tests: dogbone direct tension test ($0.5 mm/min$, DIC strain), 28 d compressive strength (50 mm cubes), XRD and SEM.
