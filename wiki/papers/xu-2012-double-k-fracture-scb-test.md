## Summary
A pioneering study establishing a simple and precise technique for measuring the Double-K fracture model, a two-parameter fracture mechanics model developed to characterize the nonlinear fracture behavior of concrete (initial crack initiation toughness $K_{Ic}^{ini}$ and unstable propagation fracture toughness $K_{Ic}^{un}$), through the semi-circular bend (SCB) test. The geometry function of SCB specimens (stress intensity factor calibration function $Y_I(\alpha)$), which can be readily fabricated from drilled cores, was derived by finite element analysis, and an analysis procedure was established for automatically determining the crack initiation load ($P_{ini}$) and peak load ($P_{max}$) from the $P-CMOD$ curve obtained with a crack mouth opening displacement (CMOD) gauge.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishment of closed-form calculation formulas for Double-K fracture toughness of SCB specimens**: derivation of dimensionless stress intensity factor calibration expressions for radius $R$, thickness $B$, and notch length $a_0$.
- **Verification of the initiation toughness ($K_{Ic}^{ini}$) by acoustic emission (AE) and the nonlinear branching point of the $P-CMOD$ curve**: precise determination of the critical load at microcrack onset.
- **Verification of equivalence with conventional three-point bending (TPB) beam tests**: SCB test results from drilled cores agreed with large notched-beam TPB results within $\pm 5 \%$, establishing a diagnostic method for the residual fracture toughness of in-service structures.

## Methods
- **Specimens**: SCB specimens cut as half-discs from cylindrical cores of diameter $150 mm$ ($R = 75 mm, B = 50 mm, a_0/R = 0.2, 0.3, 0.4, 0.5$).
- **Test items**: three-point bending loading test (span $S = 0.8 \times 2R$), CMOD clip gauge instrumentation, acoustic emission (AE) monitoring.

## Results
- Normal-strength concrete ($f_c = 35 MPa$): $K_{Ic}^{ini} = 0.65 MPa\sqrt{m}$, $K_{Ic}^{un} = 1.38 MPa\sqrt{m}$.
- High-strength concrete ($f_c = 65 MPa$): $K_{Ic}^{ini} = 0.92 MPa\sqrt{m}$, $K_{Ic}^{un} = 1.85 MPa\sqrt{m}$.
