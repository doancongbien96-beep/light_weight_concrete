## Summary

To prevent delamination and brittle cracking of fireproof coatings on steel structures,
Prof. E.H. Yang's team (Nanyang Technological University, Singapore) developed a highly
fire-resistant ultra-high ductility composite (FR-UHDCC) — a foaming agent introduced into the
matrix to form porous micro voids, bridged by PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$) across three foam
dosages M1/M2/M3 — and characterized its uniaxial direct tension, thermal conductivity,
post-heating residual mechanics, and insulation performance in a Cement and Concrete
Composites landmark study. The optimal foam mix (FR-ECC-M2) achieved dry density **1050
kg/m³**, thermal conductivity **0.18 W/(m·K)**, direct tensile strength **1.15 MPa**, and a
world-class uniaxial tensile strain capacity of **8.34 %**.

## Key Contributions

- **Micromechanical design of an ultralightweight porous ductile fire-resistant composite
  (FR-UHDCC)** completed: foaming sharply lowers matrix fracture toughness $K_m$, delivering
  8.34 % tensile strain capacity.
- **Ultra-low thermal conductivity ($0.18 W/(m\cdot K)$) and lightweighting ($1050 kg/m^3$)
  simultaneously**: insulation 8x better than ordinary concrete.
- **Multiple fine-crack network demonstrated**: deformation distributed over more than 100
  fine cracks prevents coating delamination from differential thermal expansion 100 %.
- **Steel protection verified under ISO 834 two-hour standard fire curve**: at 25 mm coating
  thickness steel temperature was capped below $410 °C$.

## Methods

- Binder: Portland cement 0.3 + Class F fly ash 0.7, $W/B = 0.35$.
- Foaming agent: synthetic surfactant foam dilution (foam dosage: M1 low, M2 optimal, M3 high).
- Fiber: Kuraray REC15 PVA fiber ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 39 µm, length 12 mm, tensile
  strength 1600 MPa).
- Tests: dog-bone direct tension ($0.5 mm/min$, DIC strain), Hot Disk thermal conductivity,
  ISO 834 standard time-temperature heating.
