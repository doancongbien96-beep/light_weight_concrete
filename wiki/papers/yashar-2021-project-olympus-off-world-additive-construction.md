## Summary
A latest landmark in the space civil engineering and architecture field presenting the system architecture of Project Olympus, a development project for an autonomous additive manufacturing system for permanent lunar infrastructure (landing pads, roads, protective shielding walls, and habitat domes) jointly carried out by large-scale construction 3D printing company ICON together with architects BIG (Bjarke Ingels Group) and SEArch+ as part of NASA's Artemis lunar exploration program, along with gantry/robotics mobile 3D printer design, a process for materializing lunar in-situ regolith via thermoplastic/melt sintering, and ground full-scale additive output verification results. Using 100 % lunar in-situ resources (ISRU), construction technology was completed for landing pads and habitation facilities that fully shield against ultra-hot rocket plume impingement ($1500 °C$) and impact dust ejection.

## Key Contributions
- **Project Olympus three core lunar infrastructure structure architectures completed**: (1) rocket launch/landing pad with blast shield; (2) trunk roadways connecting the lunar base; (3) multipurpose protective dome habitat modules.
- **Multi-axis autonomous mobile 3D printing robotics platform dedicated to the lunar environment developed**: lightweight gantry/cable robot system designed to operate under 1/6 gravity and vacuum.
- **Lunar regolith thermoplastic polymer compounding and laser/microwave melt solidification demonstrated**: space concrete properties of at least 45 MPa compressive strength secured.
- **Ground sub-scale continuous additive output of landing pad and shielding wall succeeded**: autonomous unmanned construction scenario fully verified inside a ground chamber.

## Methods
- Materials: geopolymer and melt composites based on NASA JSC-1A and LMS-1 lunar simulant.
- 3D printing system: customized space additive manufacturing robot based on the ICON Vulcan.
- Test verification: rocket engine combustion flame exposure tests ($1500 °C$ hot gas jet impingement), vacuum thermal cycling, three-point bending, and compressive strength.

## Results
- Design specifications and property data for Olympus lunar infrastructure structures:

| Structure | Construction method | Primary feedstock | Required strength (MPa) | Protective performance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| Landing pad panels | 3D additive tiling | regolith melt sinter | compression $> 50.0$ | undamaged under $1500 °C$ rocket flame plume |
| Blast shielding wall | honeycomb hollow 3D shell | regolith composite | compression $> 35.0$ | blocks high-velocity dust ejection by 99.9 % |
| Habitat module dome | multilayer catenary vault | regolith geopolymer | compression $> 40.0$ | shields annual space radiation to at most 50 mSv |
