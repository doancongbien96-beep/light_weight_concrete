# Three-Dimensional Micromechanics Design and Inclined Pullout Mechanics of High-Strength, High-Ductility Concrete (HSHDC, $f_c = 165\ \text{MPa}$): PE Fiber Snubbing ($f=0.85$), Uniaxial Tensile Strength $14.5\ \text{MPa}$, Tensile Strain Capacity $\varepsilon_u = 3.5\%$, an Immortal Landmark

## Summary

To overcome the high matrix brittleness and premature fiber rupture that ultra-high-strength concrete ($f_c > 150\ \text{MPa}$) inevitably carries and to achieve ultra-high strength and tensile strain-hardening ductility simultaneously, **single-fiber orthogonal and three-dimensional inclined pullout tests of ultra-high-molecular-weight PE microfibers ($d_f = 38\ \mu\text{m}, \sigma_f = 3000\ \text{MPa}$) were performed within a $165.0\ \text{MPa}$-class ultra-high-strength cement matrix (inclination angles $\phi = 0^\circ, 14^\circ, 27^\circ, 37^\circ, 45^\circ$)**, deriving chemical bonding ($G_d$), frictional bonding ($\tau_0$), slip-hardening coefficient ($\beta = 0.08$), snubbing friction coefficient ($f = 0.85$), and matrix fracture toughness ($K_m = 0.82\ \text{MPa}\cdot\text{m}^{1/2}$); by **perfectly satisfying the two micromechanics PSH criteria ($J_b'/J_{tip} = 3.65 \ge 3.0, \sigma_0/\sigma_{fc} = 1.48 \ge 1.40$), a compressive strength of $165.0\ \text{MPa}$, a uniaxial direct tensile strength of $14.5\ \text{MPa}$, a uniaxial ultimate tensile strain capacity of $\varepsilon_u = 3.50\%$, and more than 30 fine multiple cracks ($w \le 35\ \mu\text{m}$) were realized simultaneously**, making this the ACI Materials Journal 2013 highest-authority immortal landmark original paper of **Prof. Ravi Ranade and University Professor Victor C. Li of the University of Michigan, USA, together with the US Army ERDC research team of William Heard**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Establishment of a three-dimensional inclined pullout micromechanics model dedicated to an ultra-high-strength matrix ($165\ \text{MPa}$)**:
   - Quantified the snubbing coefficient ($f = 0.85$) as a function of inclination angle $\phi$ and the local bending stress concentration at the fiber exit point from the matrix, deriving complete fiber-bridging curves ($\sigma-\delta$) without fiber rupture.
2. **World-first theoretical and experimental demonstration of the physical compatibility of ultra-high strength and high tensile ductility**:
   - By controlling the interfacial frictional slip hardening ($\beta > 0$) of the PE fibers to match the matrix crack-tip toughness $J_{tip} = 17.5\ \text{J/m}^2$, a complementary bridging energy of $J_b' = 64.0\ \text{J/m}^2$ was secured.
3. **Precise measurement of interfacial physical quantities through more than 50 single-fiber micro-pullout experiments**:
   - Demonstrated an ideal interfacial state of $\tau_0 = 3.80\ \text{MPa}, G_d = 0.0\ \text{J/m}^2$ (purely mechanical friction).
4. **Completion of design criteria for military protection, nuclear containment domes, and key seismic members of super-tall buildings**:
   - Established the design principles of a steel-reinforcement-free, highly tough composite resisting massive blast impact and seismic lateral loads.

## Methods

- **Micromechanics single-fiber test program (Section 2 & Tables 1~3 & Figs. 1~5)**:
  - Matrix: Type I cement + silica fume (25 wt.%) + silica flour + fine silica sand ($W/B = 0.20$).
  - Reinforcing fiber: Spectra 1000 ultra-high-strength PE fiber ($L_f = 12.7\ \text{mm}, d_f = 38\ \mu\text{m}, \sigma_f = 3000\ \text{MPa}, E_f = 117\ \text{GPa}$).
  - Single-fiber pullout: 50 specimens fabricated (30 orthogonal, 20 inclined: $\phi = 14^\circ, 27^\circ, 37^\circ, 45^\circ$).
- **Composite mechanical evaluation (Sections 3~4 & Figs. 6~10)**:
  - Uniaxial tension: dogbone specimens (100 mm gauge length, 0.5 mm/min displacement control).
  - Four-point bending test, 50 mm cube compressive strength, wedge-splitting notched-beam fracture toughness test.

### 2. Bridging constitutive curve ($\sigma-\delta$) and fracture toughness margin analysis
- **Peak bridging stress of $\sigma_0 = 15.2\ \text{MPa}$ achieved**: a strength margin of $1.55$ times over the first-cracking stress $\sigma_{fc} = 9.8\ \text{MPa}$ was secured, inducing stable crack propagation.
- **Perfect satisfaction of $J_b'/J_{tip} = 3.65$**: brittle fracture suppressed at its source, with continuous saturated multiple cracking expressed over the enormous strain-capacity range of $3.5\%$.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is the historic supreme landmark original paper of the high-strength, high-ductility concrete (HSHDC) field, in which Prof. Victor C. Li and Prof. Ravi Ranade established the three-dimensional inclined pullout micromechanics model in an ultra-high-strength matrix ($165\ \text{MPa}$) and simultaneously achieved a uniaxial tensile strength of 14.5 MPa and a tensile strain capacity of 3.5 %.
