# Flexural and Shear Behaviors of Ultra-High Performance Engineered Cementitious Composite (UHP-ECC) Beams: Non-Stirrup Ductile Flexural Failure and Shear Reinforcement Elimination

## Summary

Applying ultra-high-performance engineered cementitious composites (UHP-ECC, 2.0 vol.% PE fibers) possessing both ultra-high compressive strength (120 MPa) and high tensile strain capacity (8.0 % elongation at 16.5 MPa tensile strength) to reinforced structural members, Professors Ke-Quan Yu of Tongji University and Yao Ding of Chongqing University investigated twelve full-scale beams ($150\ \text{mm} \times 250\ \text{mm} \times 1500\ \text{mm}$) under four-point bending with DIC strain and crack mapping. The primary experimental parameters were the longitudinal tensile reinforcement ratio ($\rho_s = 0.54 \%, 1.05 \%, 1.62 \%, 2.15 \%$) and the presence or absence of shear stirrups, compared against conventional concrete (OPCC 35 MPa) RC beams. The findings showed that, whereas plain RC beams without stirrups suffered catastrophic brittle shear failure, the UHP-ECC beams exhibited **complete ductile flexural yielding failure even with 100 % elimination of shear stirrups (non-stirrup)** due to the matrix tensile bridging capacity (16.5 MPa) and shear resistance (18.5 MPa). Furthermore, at equivalent reinforcement ratios, flexural load capacities were enhanced by **50 ~ 110 %**, midspan deflection capacities increased by **2.5 ~ 4.0 times**, and rebar bond splitting was suppressed.

## Key Contributions

1. **Demonstration of ductile flexural failure in non-stirrup UHP-ECC beams with 100 % elimination of shear reinforcement**:
   - The tensile strain-hardening capability of the UHP-ECC matrix bridged diagonal tension cracks, preventing brittle shear failure and enabling flexural yielding up to ultimate deflections even in beams lacking stirrups.
2. **Substantial enhancement in member flexural capacity and deformation ductility**:
   - At a high reinforcement ratio of $\rho_s = 2.15 \%$, the ultimate flexural moment of the UHP-ECC beam increased by **over 85 %** compared to the RC beam, with ultimate drift ratios exceeding **4.5 %**, verifying seismic performance.
3. **Maximization of rebar-matrix deformation compatibility and suppression of bond splitting failure**:
   - High-density multiple microcracks below 50 µm distributed uniformly along longitudinal rebars, relieving stress concentrations and eliminating cover spalling and bond splitting.
4. **Alleviation of rebar congestion and lightweight structural design paradigm**:
   - Established a design approach that eliminates shear stirrups and reduces longitudinal steel reinforcement by over 40 % while maintaining structural safety in flexure and shear.

## Methods

- **Materials and mechanical properties**:
  - UHP-ECC: CEM I 52.5 R cement + GGBS + limestone powder + silica fume + micro-silica sand ($d_{50} = 120\ \mu\text{m}$), W/B = 0.14, PE fiber $2.0\ \text{vol.\%}$ ($L_f = 12\ \text{mm}, d_f = 24\ \mu\text{m}$, tensile strength 3000 MPa, elastic modulus 100 GPa).
  - 28-day material properties: UHP-ECC compressive strength **119.6 MPa**, uniaxial tensile strength **16.5 MPa**, uniaxial tensile strain capacity **8.0 %**.
  - OPCC (normal concrete): 28-day compressive strength 34.8 MPa, tensile strength 3.2 MPa.
  - Reinforcing steel: Longitudinal deformed bars D10, D14, D18, D20 (yield strength $f_y = 510\sim 610\ \text{MPa}$), stirrup D6 ($f_{yv} = 595\ \text{MPa}$).
- **Structural beam configurations and testing**:
  - Dimensions: width $b = 150\ \text{mm}$, height $h = 250\ \text{mm}$, total length $L = 1500\ \text{mm}$, clear span $L_0 = 1200\ \text{mm}$, shear span-to-depth ratio $a/d = 2.22$.
  - Variables (twelve beams total):
    - Beam type: UHP-ECC beams (with/without stirrups) vs conventional RC beams (with/without stirrups).
    - Longitudinal reinforcement ratio $\rho_s$: 0.54 % (2D10), 1.05 % (2D14), 1.62 % (2D18), 2.15 % (2D20).
  - Loading: Four-point bending test (MTS 1000 kN, displacement control at $1.0\ \text{mm/min}$, midspan LVDTs, 2D DIC surface strain analysis).

## Results

### 1. Flexural and shear structural performance of beam specimens by stirrup configuration and reinforcement ratio (Table 8 & Figures)

| **RC-NS-1** | OPCC (35 MPa) | 1.05 % | **Non-stirrup** | — (brittle) | **68.2 ± 3.0** | **6.2 ± 0.5** | **Brittle shear failure (diagonal tension)** |

| **RU-NS-1** | **UHP-ECC (120 MPa)** | **1.05 %** | **Non-stirrup** | **132.0 ± 4.0** | **168.0 ± 4.8 (+146 %)** | **36.2 ± 1.8 (+484 %)** | **Ductile flexural failure (no shear failure)** |

### 2. Structural mechanisms and DIC strain analysis
- **Shear carrying mechanism**: The shear bridging capacity of the UHP-ECC matrix ($V_{ecc} = \tau_u b d \approx 18.5\ \text{MPa} \times b d$) exceeded the required shear demand ($V_s$), suppressing flexural shear failure without shear reinforcement.
- **Crack control and ductility factor**: Deflection ductility indices ($\mu = \Delta_u / \Delta_y$) reached **4.5 ~ 6.0**, dissipating more than twice the plastic deformation energy compared to regular RC beams (2.0~2.5).

- **Overall conclusion**: Applying UHP-ECC to structural members enabled the complete removal of shear stirrups while providing flexural capacity and ductility for non-stirrup reinforced structural systems.
