## Summary
A 2025 latest study by Professor Jian-Guo Dai's research team at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University clarifying heavy-metal leaching immobilization ($Pb, Cr, Cd$), single PE fiber interfacial frictional stress ($\tau_0$), and uniaxial direct tensile strain-hardening performance of a high-strength ultra-high-ductility geopolymer composite (RM-EGC) in which strongly alkaline hazardous bauxite red mud (RM), an aluminum smelting by-product, replaces the precursor at a high content of 30 ~ 50 wt % and is combined with slag/silica fume, in order to resolve the massive environmental contamination problem of red mud and upcycle it into an ultra-high-performance structural material. Under the condition of 40 wt % RM replacement, a 28 d compressive strength of **82.5 MPa**, a direct tensile strength of **8.5 MPa**, and a tensile strain capacity of **8.8 %** were achieved while heavy-metal leachate concentrations were immobilized 100 % below drinking-water safety limits.

## Key Contributions
- **Activator consumption cut by 40 % using the intrinsic alkalinity of the strongly alkaline red mud**: sodium hydroxide and aluminate components of the RM participate directly in the reaction.
- **Mix achieving high strength (82.5 MPa) and ultra-high ductility (8.8 %) simultaneously established**: synergy of dense N-C-A-S-H gel networks and slip-hardening bridging by PE fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$).
- **TCLP standard heavy-metal leaching rate immobilized 99.5 % demonstrated**: complete trapping of heavy metal ions within a dense three-dimensional zeolite-phase lattice.
- **Foundation laid for commercialization of an eco-friendly composite cutting embodied carbon emissions by 78 %**: 100 % recycling of industrial by-products achieved.

## Methods
- Binder: finely ground red mud (RM 30, 40, 50 wt %) + GGBS 40 % + silica fume 10 % + fly ash 10 %, low-concentration sodium silicate solution ($W/B = 0.24$).
- Fiber: UHMWPE fiber ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 12 µm, length 12 mm, tensile strength 3000 MPa).
- Tests: dogbone direct tension test ($0.5 mm/min$, DIC strain), 28 d compressive strength, EPA TCLP heavy metal leaching test (ICP-OES), XRD/MIP microstructure.
