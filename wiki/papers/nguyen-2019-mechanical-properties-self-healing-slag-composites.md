# Activator-specific mechanical properties and the self-healing mechanism achieving $100\%$ closure of $41\ \mu\text{m}$ microcracks in eco-friendly ultra-high-ductile ($\varepsilon_u = 7.55\%$) alkali-activated slag composites (PE-AAS)

## Summary

This Composite Structures (2019) landmark origin paper by Prof. Bang Yeon Lee of Chonnam National University (corresponding author), Dr. Choi J.-I., and Prof. Kim H.-K. of Chosun University establishes how the chemical composition of the alkaline activator governs both mechanical ductility and self-healing performance in an ultra-high-ductile composite obtained by reinforcing an eco-friendly cement-free alkali-activated slag (AAS) matrix using no portland cement at all with 1.5 vol.% ultra-high-molecular-weight PE fiber. To this end, **three alkaline activators (`PE-S-Ca`: $\text{Ca(OH)}_2+\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3$, `PE-S-Na`: $\text{NaOH}$, `PE-S-NaS`: $\text{Na}_2\text{SiO}_3+\text{NaOH}$)** were compared through 28-day uniaxial direct tensile testing, followed by induction of $2\%$ tensile pre-damage and **36 days of water-immersed self-healing curing**, with precise evaluation of the optical-microscopy crack-width reduction ratio ($W_{rc}$), the impact resonant-frequency (RF) dynamic-stiffness recovery rate, and SEM-EDS and XRD microstructural phase analysis, demonstrating **tensile strength of $6.82\ \text{MPa}$ and ultimate tensile strain of $7.55\%$ for the `PE-S-Ca` mix together with $100\%$ complete closure of microcracks up to $41\ \mu\text{m}$ and $89.5\%$ resonant-frequency stiffness recovery**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Manifestation of world-class tensile ductility ($7.55\%$) in a cement-free slag-based composite**:
   - Applying the combined $\text{Ca(OH)}_2+\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3$ activator gently moderates the slag hydration rate, achieving a 28-day ultimate tensile strain of $7.55\%$ and a tensile strength of $6.82\ \text{MPa}$.
2. **Quantification of the self-healing crack-closure threshold by alkaline activator type**:
   - The `PE-S-Ca` mix completely closed microcracks up to $41\ \mu\text{m}$ within 36 days, and the `PE-S-Na` mix those up to $33\ \mu\text{m}$, at a $100\%$ closure rate, while even $50\sim 60\ \mu\text{m}$ cracks exhibited high closure rates above $60\%$.
3. **Demonstration of dynamic stiffness recovery ($89.5\%$) through impact resonant-frequency (RF) non-destructive testing**:
   - Deposition of healing products inside the microcracks fully restored the composite natural frequency that cracking had degraded, confirming structural stiffness restorability beyond that of cement-based ECC.
4. **Crystallographic elucidation of hydrotalcite (Mg-Al LDH) and C-(N)-A-S-H gel healing**:
   - Beyond calcite ($\text{CaCO}_3$), hydrotalcite layered double hydroxides formed by reaction of the slag's $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ and $\text{Al}^{3+}$ ions were proven to bind the crack gaps densely.

## Methods

- **Materials and alkali-activated slag mixes (Section 2.1 & Tables 1~3)**:
  - Binder: ground granulated blast-furnace slag powder (GGBS, Blaine fineness $435\ \text{m}^2/\text{kg}$, $\text{CaO} = 41.5\%$, $\text{SiO}_2 = 33.5\%$, $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 = 14.5\%$, $\text{MgO} = 6.5\%$).
  - Aggregate: silica sand ($d_{50} = 150\ \mu\text{m}$, S/B = 0.36).
  - Fiber: Spectra PE fiber ($V_f = 1.5\ \text{vol.}\%$, $d_f = 31\ \mu\text{m}$, $L_f = 12\ \text{mm}$, $\sigma_f = 2700\ \text{MPa}$, $E_f = 88\ \text{GPa}$).
  - Three alkaline activators:
    1. `PE-S-Ca`: $10.0\ \text{wt.}\%\ \text{Ca(OH)}_2 + 3.0\ \text{wt.}\%\ \text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3$ (solid powder-type mild activation).
    2. `PE-S-Na`: $3.0\ \text{wt.}\%\ \text{NaOH}$ aqueous solution.
    3. `PE-S-NaS`: $\text{Na}_2\text{SiO}_3$ ($\text{Ms} = 1.0$, $4.0\ \text{wt.}\%\ \text{Na}_2\text{O}$).
- **Damage induction and self-healing tests (Section 2.2 & Figs. 1~2)**:
  - Damage application: dogbone tensile specimens loaded to $2\%$ uniaxial tensile strain $\rightarrow$ unloaded, then initial crack widths ($W_i \le 60\ \mu\text{m}$) measured by optical microscopy.
  - Healing environment: water immersion at $20\ ^\circ\text{C}$ (measurements at 4, 7, 14, 28, and 36 days).
  - Resonant-frequency (RF) non-destructive testing: longitudinal steel-sphere impact $\rightarrow$ accelerometer FFT analysis.

### 2. Interpretation of healing-product formation and micro-bridging restoration mechanisms

- **Mild reaction rate of the $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$ activator and perfect self-healing**: The $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$ activator suitably moderates early-stage slag dissolution to optimize interfacial frictional bond with the PE fibers, inducing the ultimate ductility of $7.55\%$, and supplies abundant $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ ions during water immersion, sealing $41\ \mu\text{m}$ microcracks 100% completely with calcium carbonate and hydrotalcite crystals.
- **Resonant-frequency stiffness recovery**: The healing precipitates pack the microcrack gaps rigidly and restore stress-transfer continuity, recovering the elastic state to $89.5\%$ of its level before damage-induced failure.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is the world-leading authoritative eco-friendly self-healing construction-materials landmark origin study in which Prof. Bang Yeon Lee's team at Chonnam National University achieved $7.55\%$ ultra-high ductility through activator optimization in an eco-friendly cement-free alkali-activated slag composite and demonstrated $100\%$ self-healing of cracks up to $41\ \mu\text{m}$ together with $89.5\%$ dynamic-stiffness recovery.
