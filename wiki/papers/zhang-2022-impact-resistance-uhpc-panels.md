## Summary
To maximize the high-velocity projectile protection capability of protective bunkers and critical infrastructure, this study by Professor C. Wu's research team at Griffith University, Australia established the penetration depth, frontal crater diameter, rear-face scabbing limit thickness, and perforation resistance of ultra-high performance fiber-reinforced concrete (UHPC, $f_c = 155 MPa$, steel fiber $V_f = 2.0 \sim 3.0 \%$) panels ($500 \times 500 \times 50 \sim 100 mm$) under impact loads of high-velocity armor-piercing projectiles (12.7 mm AP projectile, $v = 850 m/s$), using live-fire gas gun tests and three-dimensional finite element impact simulation (LS-DYNA). The study demonstrated that steel fiber 2.5 vol % reinforced UHPC panels reduced the penetration depth by **72 %** compared with normal concrete, and that at a thickness of 75 mm they completely prevented 100 % of the rear-face spall ejection from the $850 m/s$ projectile.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishment of a local projectile impact damage law for UHPC under $850 m/s$ ultra-high-velocity projectile impact**: penetration depth reduced to $H = 22.5 mm$.
- **Quantification of the rear-face scabbing limit thickness ($h_{sc}$) as a function of the steel fiber volume fraction ($V_f = 1.5 \sim 3.0 \%$)**: $h_{sc}$ thinned by 66 % from 220 mm to 75 mm.
- **Completion of parameter sets for the LS-DYNA RHT and Johnson-Holmquist concrete damage models**: impact simulation error held within 5 %.
- **Proposal of slim design criteria for defense protective panels and nuclear power plant containment shielding walls**: realization of a 60 % reduction in the self-weight of protective structures.

## Methods
- Materials: UHPC ($f_c = 155 MPa$, micro straight steel fibers $L_f = 13 mm, d_f = 0.2 mm, V_f = 2.5 \%$).
- Panel thicknesses: $t = 50, 75, 100 mm$ ($500 \times 500 mm$).
- Tests: 12.7 mm armor-piercing projectile (mass 48 g, striking velocity $850 \pm 15 m/s$); high-speed laser velocimeter; 3D optical laser scanner.

## Results
- Impact damage data of UHPC panels at various thicknesses under 12.7 mm armor-piercing projectile impacts:

| Material | Panel thickness (mm) | Impact velocity ($m/s$) | Penetration depth (mm) | Frontal crater diameter (mm) | Rear-face scabbing diameter (mm) | Perforated |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Normal concrete (NC) | 100 mm | 850 | Perforated | 320 | 450 (large fragmentation) | Complete perforation failure |
| UHPC (Steel 2.5 %) | 50 mm | 850 | 28.5 | 110 | 185 (minor scabbing) | Non-perforated (rear-face damage) |
| **UHPC (Steel 2.5 %)** | **75 mm** | **850** | **22.5** | **95** | **0.0 (no damage)** | **Non-perforated (rear face completely undamaged)** |
| **UHPC (Steel 2.5 %)** | **100 mm** | **850** | **18.5** | **88** | **0.0 (no damage)** | **Non-perforated (complete protection)** |
