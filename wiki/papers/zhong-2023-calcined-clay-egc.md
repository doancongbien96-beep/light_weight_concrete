# High-Ductile EGC Prepared from Calcined Natural Clay

## Summary

Natural clay from construction waste (Nanning clay) was calcined at 700 °C for 3 h to secure reactivity, and EGC was prepared with GGBFS and PVA fiber. The effects of the slag replacement ratio (0/10/20 %) and fiber content (0.5~2.5 vol %) on tensile performance were evaluated, and crack behavior was quantified in real time with DIC. A practical pathway for EGC is presented using low-cost, widely distributed raw materials.

## Key Contributions

1. Achievement of tensile strain-hardening in calcined-clay-based EGC: at the optimum mix (slag 10 % + PVA 2.5 %), an ultimate tensile strength of 3.61 MPa, tensile strain of 6.59 %, and deformation energy of 199.25 kJ/m3.
2. Slag optimum at 10 %: combined densification of C-A-S-H (slag) + N-A-S-H (clay) gels strengthened interfacial bonding. At 20 %, excess Ca induced Ca(OH)2 hydration and swelling cracks, reducing strength and ductility simultaneously.
3. Quantitative relation between fiber content and cracking characteristics (Table 4): as fiber content rose from 0.5 to 2.5 %, the crack count increased from 3 to 56 and average crack spacing decreased from 33.3 to 1.8 mm (slag 10 % mix). Saturation of multiple cracking was confirmed.
4. DIC-based crack quantification methodology: the number of principal strain peaks matched the measured crack count. Maximum principal strain M (a crack width proxy) and coefficient of variation V (crack distribution uniformity) were introduced as indicators: M decreased and V decreased as fiber content increased.
5. Workability trade-off: adding 2.5 % PVA sharply reduced relative slump from 3.5 to 0.5, and slag 10 % partially recovered it.

## Methods

Natural clay (XRD: quartz 48.4 %, illite 31.4 %, kaolinite 16.7 %) was sieved to 75 μm and calcined at 700 °C, converting kaolinite completely to metakaolin. Activator: 8 M NaOH + Na2SiO3 (modulus 1.3). Direct tension on 15×50×260 mm specimens (gauge length 100 mm) at 0.5 mm/min. DIC used a Phantom VEO-410L high-speed camera (5000 fps, resolution 0.22 mm/pixel) with GOM software.

## Results

- First-cracking strength rose from 0.70 MPa without slag to 1.34 MPa at 10 % (matrix densification) and fell to 1.10 MPa at 20 %.
- Strain stabilized at fiber contents of 1.5 % and above (around 5 %), suggesting the existence of a critical fiber content.
- The slag 20 % mixes showed a localization tendency, with reduced crack count and increased spacing.
