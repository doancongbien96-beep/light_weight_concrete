# Engineering Properties of Steel Fiber-Reinforced Geopolymer Concrete

## Summary

This experimental study from NIT Calicut characterized the compressive strength, splitting tensile strength, modulus of rupture (flexural strength), elastic modulus, and Poisson's ratio of fly ash-based geopolymer concrete (GPC) reinforced with 0~1 vol % crimped steel fibers according to Indian IS standards, proposing empirical regression models based on the fiber reinforcing index $F = (l_f/d_f) V_f$. In M40 grade mixtures, incorporating 1 % steel fibers produced an 8.51 % gain in compressive strength, while splitting tensile strength (+61.63 %), elastic modulus (+64.92 %), and Poisson's ratio (+50 %) improved markedly.

## Key Contributions

- **Four design equations based on fiber reinforcing index $F = (l_f/d_f) V_f$**: Compressive strength $f_{cf} = 45.37 + 3.852 V_f$, splitting tensile strength $f_{ct} = 0.338 F\sqrt{f_c} + 2.764$, modulus of rupture $f_{cr} = 0.218 F\sqrt{f_c} + 5.054$, and elastic modulus $E_c (\times 10^4\ \text{MPa}) = 0.28 F\sqrt{f_c} + 2.149$ ($10^4\ \text{MPa}$ / 4 MPa), with prediction errors within ~1.06 %, 0.003 %, 0.002 %, and 0.002 %, respectively.
- **Code comparison at $V_f = 0$ extrapolation**: GPC exhibited $f_{cr} = 0.75\sqrt{f_c}$ (similar to the IS 456 OPC value of 0.7) and $E_c = 3190\sqrt{f_c}$ (markedly lower than the IS 456 OPC coefficient of 5000, attributed to lower aggregate volume fractions in GPC).
- **Confirmation of FRC behavioral mechanisms in geopolymer systems**: Consistent with OPC steel fiber concrete literature (Khaloo & Kim 1996, Song & Hwang 2004), fibers predominantly enhanced tensile and flexural properties rather than compressive strength.

## Methods

- Raw materials (kg/m³): Mettur thermal power plant Class F fly ash 639, fine aggregate 285, coarse aggregate (maximum aggregate size 20 mm) 975, sodium silicate solution 180, 14 M sodium hydroxide solution 72, extra water 53, naphthalene superplasticizer Conplast SP430 7.67.
- Activator: $\text{Na}_2\text{SiO}_3/\text{NaOH}$ mass ratio 2.5, activator-to-fly ash ratio 0.39 (following Rangan 2008 procedures). Alkaline solutions prepared 24 h prior to casting.
- Fibers: Crimped steel fibers, length 30 mm, diameter 0.45 mm, tensile strength 800 MPa, aspect ratio 66. Volume fractions $V_f = 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75$, and 1 %.
- Curing: Demolded after 3 d ambient resting, steam cured at 60 °C for 24 h.
- Testing: 150 mm cubes for compressive strength (IS 516-1959), 150×300 mm cylinders for splitting tension (IS 5816-1999), 100×100×500 mm prisms for three-point flexure (IS 516), and cylinders with LVDTs/compressometers for $E_c$ and Poisson's ratio. 15 specimens per property (75 total), with 3 specimens averaged per data point.

## Results

- Fresh concrete: Slump decreased from 123 mm ($V_f = 0$) to 77 mm (1 %), and Vee-Bee time increased from 7.9 to 20.3 s with increasing fiber content.
- Hardened properties (28 d): $f_c$ 45.37 $\rightarrow$ 49.23 MPa (+8.51 %), $f_{ct}$ 2.58 $\rightarrow$ 4.17 MPa (+61.63 %), $f_{cr}$ 5.00 $\rightarrow$ 6.20 MPa (+24.00 %), $E_c$ 2.15 $\rightarrow$ 3.55 $\times 10^4$ MPa (+64.92 %), Poisson's ratio 0.14 $\rightarrow$ 0.21 (+50.00 %).
- Property improvements grew monotonically with $V_f$; compressive strength gained +3.22 % at 0.25 % fibers before plateauing at +8.51 % for 1 % fibers.
- Poisson's ratio of 0.21 exceeded the standard plain concrete design value of 0.2.
