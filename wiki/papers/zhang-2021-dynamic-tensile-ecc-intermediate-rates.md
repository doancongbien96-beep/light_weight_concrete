## Summary
A study by Professor E.H. Yang's research team at Nanyang Technological University (NTU) clarifying through high-speed hydraulic tension tests the dynamic tensile strength increase, evolution of strain-hardening envelopes, tensile fracture energy absorption rate, and the rate sensitivity of single PVA fiber interfacial frictional stress ($\tau_0$) of ductile composites (PVA-ECC) in the intermediate strain-rate region ($\dot{\epsilon} = 10^{-4} \sim 10^{-1} s^{-1}$) arising during seismic ground motions and vehicle collisions. As the strain rate increased to $10^{-1} s^{-1}$, the direct tensile strength rose by 50 % from **4.8 MPa to 7.2 MPa** ($DIF_t = 1.50$) while a tensile strain capacity of **3.8 %** and a fracture energy of **$245 kJ/m^3$** were maintained.

## Key Contributions
- **Governing equation of PVA-ECC dynamic tension under intermediate strain rates established**: model $DIF_t = 1.0 + 0.11 \log_{10}(\dot{\epsilon}/\dot{\epsilon}_0)$ for the dynamic tensile strength increase factor over static loading formulated.
- **Viscous fluid bridging and interfacial friction amplification mechanism under dynamic loading clarified**: the Stefan viscous resistance of pore water drives the rise of dynamic interfacial bond.
- **45 % increase in impact energy absorption density demonstrated**: reliability secured for seismic and vehicle collision barrier applications.
- **Multiple fine crack density preserved**: more than 35 finely dispersed cracks formed even under high-rate tension.

## Methods
- Mix: PVA-ECC (Kuraray REC15, $V_f = 2.0 \%$, $f_c = 46.5 MPa$, $W/B = 0.45$).
- Test equipment: high-speed hydraulic servo actuator, high-precision laser displacement meters, 5000 fps high-speed camera DIC.
- Strain rates: $\dot{\epsilon} = 10^{-4}, 10^{-3}, 10^{-2}, 10^{-1} s^{-1}$.

| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
