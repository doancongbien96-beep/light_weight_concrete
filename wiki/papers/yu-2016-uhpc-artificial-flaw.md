## Summary
A CCR landmark paper by Professor K.Q. Yu of Tongji University and Professor Surendra Shah of Northwestern University that deliberately introduced artificial flaws (hollow glass beads and fine polymer particles, particle size $50 \sim 300 \mu m$) with controlled size and spatial distribution into the matrix to induce the micromechanical PSH energy condition, solving the fundamental problem that the extremely high fracture toughness ($K_m > 1.2 MPa\sqrt{m}$) of an ultra-high-strength matrix with compressive strength around 120 MPa causes premature fiber rupture and undermines tensile strain hardening. By introducing artificial flaws the matrix crack-tip fracture toughness was relaxed by **60 %**, demonstrating for the first time in the world the simultaneous achievement of ultra-high strength-ultra-high ductility at a 28 d compressive strength of **125 MPa**, a direct tensile strength of **14.2 MPa**, and a tensile strain capacity of **8.5 %**.

## Key Contributions
- **World's first establishment of a UHP-ECC micromechanics control technique via artificial flaw introduction**: $K_m$ relaxed from $1.2 \rightarrow 0.45 MPa\sqrt{m}$, securing a PSH energy index $J_b'/J_{tip} > 6.0$.
- **Simultaneous realization of ultra-high strength (125 MPa) and ultra-high ductility (8.5 %)**: brittle crushing under strengthening and localized tensile failure completely eliminated.
- **Optimal design envelope for flaw size ($a_0$) and volume fraction ($V_{flaw}$) derived**: highest tensile ductility when flaws of $150 \mu m$ are incorporated at 3.0 vol %.
- **Multiple fine crack density maximized**: a network of more than 110 fine cracks within the gauge length (mean crack width $18 \mu m$).

## Methods
- Matrix: low-heat cement + silica fume (25 %) + fine silica sand ($d_{50} = 100 \mu m$), $W/B = 0.16$.
- Artificial flaws: hollow glass beads (particle sizes 50, 150, 300 µm; incorporation rates 1 ~ 5 vol %).
- Fiber: ultra-high-strength Spectra PE fiber ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 12 µm, length 12 mm, $f_t = 3000 MPa$).
- Tests: dogbone direct tension test ($0.2 mm/min$, full-field DIC strain analysis), 28 d compressive strength (100 mm cubes), three-point flexural fracture toughness.
