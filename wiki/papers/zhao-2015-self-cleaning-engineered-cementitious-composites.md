## Summary
A landmark study in which the research team of Professor Victor Li at the University of Michigan investigated the Rhodamine B organic dye photodegradation efficiency, the UV-induced super-hydrophilicity contact angle evolution, the single PVA fiber interfacial properties, and the uniaxial direct tensile strain-hardening performance of composites incorporating nano titanium dioxide (nano-TiO2 anatase phase, particle size 15 nm, 1.0 ~ 5.0 wt %), developed as a smart self-cleaning high-ductility composite (Self-Cleaning ECC) that simultaneously provides photocatalytic oxidative decomposition of urban air pollutants ($NO_x, SO_x$) and stain prevention (self-cleaning) of building facade appearance. Under 3.0 wt % nano TiO2 incorporation, organic contaminants were degraded by **92 %** within 24 h of ultraviolet irradiation while a 28 d compressive strength of **48.5 MPa**, a direct tensile strength of **5.2 MPa**, and a tensile strain capacity of **4.5 %** were achieved.

## Key Contributions
- **First demonstration of functional integration of photocatalytic self-cleaning and high-ductility strain-hardening**: nano TiO2 addition preserved the PVA fiber bridging capacity without damage.
- **Rhodamine B organic contaminant photodegradation efficiency of 92 % achieved**: sustained self-cleaning action was verified under 24 h of UV irradiation ($1.0 mW/cm^2$).
- **Super-hydrophilic surface expression**: the water droplet contact angle decreased from $65°$ to $12°$, enabling automatic cleaning of surface contaminants during rainfall.
- **Proposal of a standard mix proportion for eco-friendly smart infrastructure cladding**: realization of facade materials combining air purification with zero maintenance cost.

## Methods
- Mix proportion: cement + fly ash ($FA/C = 1.2$) + anatase nano-TiO2 (1.0, 3.0, 5.0 wt %), with Kuraray REC15 PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$).
- Photocatalytic test: Rhodamine B solution was applied to the specimen surface, and spectrophotometer reflectance ($\Delta E^*$) was continuously measured under UV lamp irradiation.
- Mechanical tests: dogbone direct tension tests ($0.5 mm/min$, DIC strain), 28 d compressive strength, and surface contact angle measurements were performed.
