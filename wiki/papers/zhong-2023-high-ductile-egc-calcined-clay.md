## Summary
A 2023 study that established the micromechanical PSH strain-hardening criteria, the interfacial properties of single PE fibers, and the uniaxial direct tensile behavior of CC-EGC, an ambient-cured high-ductility geopolymer composite that employs low-cost natural calcined clay (Calcined Clay, kaolinite content 50 %, $d_{50} = 8.5 \mu m$) as a composite precursor together with fly ash/slag in place of high-purity refined metakaolin. Through the aluminosilicate nanogel cross-linking action of the calcined clay, the matrix fracture toughness was relaxed to $K_m = 0.32 MPa\sqrt{m}$, and the composite attained an ambient 28 d compressive strength of **58.5 MPa**, a direct tensile strength of **7.8 MPa**, and a tensile strain capacity of **8.5 %**.

## Key Contributions
- **Completion of an ambient-cured ultra-high-ductility geopolymer mix design (CC-EGC) based on natural calcined clay**: a tensile strain capacity of 8.5 % achieved without heat curing.
- **Optimization of the clay calcination temperature ($750 °C$) and the replacement ratio (30 wt %)**: densification of the fine N-A-S-H gel and establishment of fiber-slip interfacial equilibrium.
- **Demonstration of the slip-hardening bridging of single PE fibers ($\tau_0 = 1.85 MPa, \beta = 0.82$)**: securing a complementary energy index of $J_b'/J_{tip} = 12.5$.
- **Laying the groundwork for commercializing carbon-neutral infrastructure materials based on low-cost global feedstocks**: clinker-free environmental performance secured.

## Methods
- Binder: calcined clay (30 wt %) + F-grade fly ash (50 wt %) + GGBS (20 wt %), with a liquid sodium silicate combined activator ($W/B = 0.30$).
- Fiber: UHMWPE fiber ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 12 µm, length 12 mm, tensile strength 3000 MPa).
- Testing: dogbone direct tension test ($0.5 mm/min$, DIC strain measurement), 28 d compressive strength, XRD, FTIR, and 29Si MAS-NMR.
