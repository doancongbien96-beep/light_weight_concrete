## Summary
A study by Professor Victor Li's research team at the University of Michigan clarifying through electric furnace heating experiments and SEM/TGA analysis the phase transition/melting ($T_m = 145 °C$) of PE fibers, residual compressive strength, elastic modulus, and transition mechanisms of residual tensile behavior when ultra-high molecular weight polyethylene (UHMWPE) fiber reinforced ductile composites (PE-ECC, $f_c = 65 MPa$, direct tensile strength $8.5 MPa$, tensile strain capacity $8.0 \%$) are exposed to fire and high-temperature heating environments ($100 \sim 600 °C$). Beyond $150 °C$, tensile strain-hardening performance is lost due to PE fiber melting; however, it was demonstrated that the melted fine channels release internal matrix steam pressure completely, blocking spalling 100 % and retaining 60 % compressive strength even at $400 °C$.

## Key Contributions
- **Branching mechanism of tensile versus compressive behavior upon high-temperature melting of PE fibers ($145 °C$) clarified**: tensile ductility plunges while anti-spalling effect is maximized.
- **Quantitative models of residual compressive strength and elastic modulus by exposure temperature ($100 \sim 600 °C$) established**: strength retained at 85 % at $200 °C$ and 62 % at $400 °C$.
- **Steam devolatilization micro network formation proven**: fine channels of $12 \mu m$ diameter constrain internal steam pressure at high temperature to at most $0.2 MPa$.
- **Guidelines presented for diagnosing the residual capacity of fire-damaged structures**: limit-state assessment criteria established per heating temperature.

## Methods
- Mix: PE-ECC (Spectra PE fibers $V_f = 2.0 \%$, $W/B = 0.28$, $FA/C = 1.2$).
- Heating tests: electric furnace ramping ($5 °C/min$) to target temperatures of 100, 150, 200, 300, 400, and 600 °C, held 2 hours then slow cooled.
- Tests: dogbone direct tension test, 28 d compressive strength (100 mm cubes), dynamic elastic modulus, SEM interface observation.

## Results
- Measured residual mechanical property data of PE-ECC by heating temperature:

| Heating temperature ($°C$) | Compressive strength retention (%) | Elastic modulus retention (%) | Residual uniaxial tensile strength (MPa) | Residual tensile strain capacity (%) | Spalling |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Room temp ($20 °C$) | 100 % (65.2 MPa) | 100 % (24.5 GPa) | 8.50 | **8.20 % (ultra-ductile)** | none (normal) |
| $100 °C$ | 98.5 % | 92.0 % | 7.80 | **7.50 % (hardening retained)** | no spalling |
| $150 °C$ (melting point) | 92.0 % | 82.5 % | 3.50 | 0.85 % (softening transition) | no spalling |
| $200 °C$ | 85.0 % | 68.0 % | 2.10 | 0.05 % (brittle) | no spalling |
| $400 °C$ | 62.5 % | 38.0 % | 1.20 | 0.02 % (brittle) | no spalling |
