# Thermal Energy Storage Concrete with a Graphene (GNP)-Surface-Modified Octadecane/Diatomite Composite Phase Change Material (GNP-OC/D): Mechanism of $230\%$ Thermal Conductivity Enhancement and $4.2\ ^\circ\text{C}$ Indoor Peak Temperature Reduction

## Summary

To simultaneously secure the thermal storage efficiency and mechanical strength of building-envelope concrete while solving the leakage and low thermal conductivity of organic PCMs, the research team of **Prof. Xiaojian Gao of the Harbin Institute of Technology, China** developed a **form-stable composite PCM (GNP-OC/D) in which octadecane (Octadecane, OC: melting point $28.2\ ^\circ\text{C}$, latent heat $240.5\ \text{J/g}$), selected within the indoor comfort temperature band, was vacuum-impregnated at $60\ \text{wt.}\%$ into natural porous diatomite (Diatomite) and its surface nano-encapsulated with graphene nanoplatelets (GNP, $1.5\ \text{wt.}\%$)**, and precisely evaluated transient hot-wire (Hot-Wire) thermal conductivity, DSC latent heat analysis, FE-SEM, 1000 thermal cycling cycles, and a **full-scale concrete model-room thermal performance test**, demonstrating **a leakage rate of $0.0\%$, a latent heat capacity of $128.5\ \text{J/g}$, and a thermal conductivity of $0.76\ \text{W/m}\cdot\text{K}$ ($+230\%$ enhancement), together with a 28-day compressive strength of $42.5\ \text{MPa}$ and an indoor peak-temperature reduction of $4.2\ ^\circ\text{C}$ upon incorporation into concrete** — the latest landmark origin paper of 2025 in the Journal of Energy Storage.

## Key Contributions

1. **Realization of complete leak-freeness through diatomite-graphene dual encapsulation (Dual-Encapsulation)**:
   - By combining the micro-cylindrical pore capillary forces of diatomite with the physical barrier of the exterior-wall graphene nanoplatelets (GNP), an octadecane leakage rate of $0.0\%$ was achieved even at a high temperature of $80\ ^\circ\text{C}$.
2. **Dramatic $230\%$ amplification of thermal conductivity by GNP nano-coating**:
   - Overcame the low thermal conductivity of the porous support and formed a graphene percolation network, raising the thermal conductivity $0.23 \rightarrow 0.76\ \text{W/m}\cdot\text{K}$.
3. **Preservation of the mechanical compressive strength ($42.5\ \text{MPa}$) of PCM concrete**:
   - The hydrophobic protective film on the GNP surface blocks any interference with the cement hydration reaction at its source, fully satisfying structural concrete strength criteria even at a massive dosage of $20\ \text{vol.}\%$.
4. **Demonstration of an indoor peak-temperature reduction of $4.2\ ^\circ\text{C}$ in a full-scale model chamber**:
   - Demonstrated lowering the daytime peak indoor temperature by $4.2\ ^\circ\text{C}$ and attenuating the indoor temperature fluctuation amplitude by $55\%$ through nighttime heat release.

## Methods

- **Composite PCM synthesis and concrete mixing (Section 2 & Tables 1~2)**:
  - Support: purified diatomite (Diatomite, specific surface area $48.5\ \text{m}^2/\text{g}$, porosity $72\%$).
  - PCM: n-Octadecane ($\text{C}_{18}\text{H}_{38}$, purity $99\%$, $T_m = 28.2\ ^\circ\text{C}$, $\Delta H_m = 240.5\ \text{J/g}$).
  - Thermal-conductivity enhancer: graphene nanoplatelets (GNP, thickness $5\sim 10\ \text{nm}$, specific surface area $650\ \text{m}^2/\text{g}$, 1.5 wt.%).
  - Vacuum impregnation: impregnated for two hours in a $60\ ^\circ\text{C}$ vacuum chamber (0.09 MPa), followed by spray coating of GNP.
  - Concrete mix: OPC + silica sand + crushed stone + GNP-OC/D (replacement of fine aggregate at 0, 10, 20, 30 vol.%, W/C = 0.40).
- **Property analysis and thermal-chamber testing (Sections 2.2~2.4 & Figs. 1~6)**:
  - DSC thermal analysis: TA Instruments Q2000 ($-10\sim 60\ ^\circ\text{C}$, $5\ ^\circ\text{C/min}$).
  - Thermal conductivity: XIATECH TC3000E transient hot-wire meter.
  - Thermal performance demonstration: $400 \times 400 \times 400\ \text{mm}$ concrete test room (solar radiant heat simulation).

### 2. Interpretation of the Heat-Conduction Nano-Network and Phase-Change Enthalpy Transfer Mechanism
- **Heat bridging by 2D graphene sheets**: the GNP coated on the diatomite exterior minimizes the interfacial thermal resistance ($R_k$) between the concrete cement matrix and the PCM core, accelerating the phase-change latent heat absorption/release rate more than threefold.
- **Improved indoor thermal comfort**: when the outdoor air temperature exceeds the octadecane melting point ($28.2\ ^\circ\text{C}$), the composite absorbs latent heat, delaying the indoor temperature rise and greatly reducing cooling energy.

- **Overall conclusion**: this paper is a top-level landmark origin paper that established high-performance smart thermal energy storage concrete technology, enhancing thermal conductivity by 230% through graphene nano-coating while maintaining the compressive strength at 42.5 MPa and reducing the indoor peak temperature by 4.2 °C.
