## Summary

A 2024 Journal of Energy Storage paper in which Dr. M. B. Belete's research team at Debre Markos University, Ethiopia precisely established the physical, mechanical, thermal, and economic characteristics of a thermal-storage mortar obtained by incorporating 3, 5, 7, and 10 wt % of a shape-stable phase change material (ss-PCM: phase-change temperature **28 °C**, latent heat capacity **210 kJ/kg**, thermal conductivity **18 W/(m·°C)**) — formed by vacuum-combining an inorganic hydrate-salt mixture (sodium sulfate decahydrate $Na_2SO_4\cdot 10H_2O$ 45 % + calcium chloride hexahydrate $CaCl_2\cdot 6H_2O$ 44 % + surfactant 3 %) with expanded graphite (Graphite 8 %) — into cement mortar (cement:sand 1:3) for indoor temperature control and heating/cooling energy savings in buildings. The study demonstrated that with 5 wt % ss-PCM incorporation the mortar retains a compressive strength of **22.5 MPa** while lowering the indoor peak temperature by **3.8 °C** and saving **28 %** of building cooling energy.

## Key Contributions
- **Synthesis of a high-efficiency inorganic hydrate/graphite shape-stable ss-PCM**: combining the high latent heat of $210 kJ/kg$ with the high thermal conductivity of graphite ($18 W/(m\cdot K)$).
- **Correlation established between mortar mechanical properties and ss-PCM content (0 ~ 10 wt %)**: 5 wt % satisfies structural masonry/finishing criteria.
- **Indoor temperature buffering demonstrated through full-scale chamber thermal performance testing**: daily indoor temperature fluctuation range reduced by 42 %.
- **Life-cycle economic analysis**: simple payback period of 3.2 years and an annual $CO_2$ emission reduction of 1.4 t.

## Methods
- Raw materials: OPC, standard sand (1:3 mass ratio), ss-PCM (3, 5, 7, 10 wt % replacement of cement), $W/B = 0.50$.
- ss-PCM properties: phase-change temperature 28 °C, latent heat 210 kJ/kg, density $0.45 g/cm^3$, thermal conductivity 18 W/(m·°C), specific heat $3.2 kJ/(kg\cdot °C)$.
- Tests: 28 d compressive strength (50 mm cubes), flexural strength ($40 \times 40 \times 160 mm$), KD2 Pro thermal-conductivity meter, temperature monitoring of a full-scale model chamber ($400 \times 400 \times 400 mm$).
