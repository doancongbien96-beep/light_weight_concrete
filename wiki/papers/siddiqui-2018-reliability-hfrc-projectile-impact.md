## Summary
This study probabilistically evaluated the high-velocity projectile impact resistance and failure probability of hybrid fiber-reinforced concrete (HFRC) slabs incorporating hooked steel fibers and polypropylene (PP) fibers in combination, by coupling Monte Carlo simulation (MCS) with the First Order Reliability Method (FORM). Under steel projectile impact ($v = 100 \sim 200 m/s$), the front-face penetration depth ($x$), rear-face scabbing limit thickness, and crater (spalling cone) diameter were measured and reliability indices ($\beta$) were derived. Owing to the synergy between the high-toughness bridging of steel fibers and the micro-crack dispersion effect of PP fibers, it was demonstrated that the risk of rear-face scabbing is reduced by more than 75 % compared with ordinary concrete.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishment of limit state functions for projectile impact on HFRC slabs**: nonlinear reliability analysis models for front-face penetration and rear-face scabbing limit thickness.
- **Demonstration of the impact-resistance synergy of hybrid fibers (steel + PP)**: combining steel fibers (1.0 vol %) with PP fibers (0.5 vol %) increased the slab perforation threshold velocity by 28 % compared with steel fiber alone.
- **Failure mode transition analysis**: a transition from brittle perforation punching failure to a localized deformation absorption mode through three-dimensional fiber bridging was confirmed.

## Methods
- **Mix proportioning**: $W/B = 0.35$, compressive strength $65 MPa$, steel fiber (length 30 mm, diameter 0.5 mm), PP fiber (length 12 mm, diameter 30 µm).
- **Impact test**: pneumatic gas gun system, conical steel projectile with 20 mm diameter (mass 100 g, impact velocity $100 \sim 200 m/s$).
- **Specimens**: $400 \times 400 \times 75 mm$ slabs.

## Results
- Rear-face scabbing threshold slab thickness: ordinary concrete $110 mm \rightarrow$ HFRC $55 mm$ (thickness reducible by 50 %).
- Reliability analysis produced design thickness charts satisfying the target failure probability ($P_f < 10^{-4}$).
