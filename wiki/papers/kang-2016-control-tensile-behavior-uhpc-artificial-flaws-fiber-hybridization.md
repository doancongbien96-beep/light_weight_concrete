# Control of Direct Tensile Strain-Hardening of Ultra-High-Performance Concrete (UHPC) Through Artificial Flaws and Steel-PE Fiber Hybridization: Tight Crack Width Restriction to $0.04\ \text{mm}$ and 3.5-Fold Ductility Enhancement

## Summary

To overcome the brittle single-crack localization and sharp tensile softening of ultra-high-performance concrete (UHPC: compressive strength $150.0\ \text{MPa}$) while imparting microcrack control for long-term durability and self-healing, Professor Bang Yeon Lee (corresponding author) at Chonnam National University, Professor Su-Tae Kang at Daegu University, and Professor Burak Felekoglu at Dokuz Eylul University developed modified UHPC composites. The material incorporates spherical expanded polystyrene (PS) beads (diameters $1.0\sim 2.0\ \text{mm}$, content $0.5\sim 1.0\ \text{vol.}\%$) to introduce uniform artificial flaws, combined with hybrid reinforcement of straight brass-coated micro-steel fibers and high-ductility PE microfibers (total $V_f = 1.5\ \text{vol.}\%$). Uniaxial direct tension tests, 2D-DIC full-field strain tracking, crack width statistics, and 28 d compressive strength tests demonstrated a **3.5-fold increase in direct tensile strain capacity ($0.25\% \rightarrow 0.88\%$), a 4-fold increase in crack count (8 to 32 cracks), tight crack width restriction below $0.04\ \text{mm}$, and retention of $142.0\ \text{MPa}$ compressive strength compared to mono-steel UHPC**.

## Key Contributions

1. **First demonstration of micro-multiple cracking control in UHPC via artificial flaws**:
   - Uniformly distributed matrix flaws narrowed the statistical variation of first cracking strength, driving sequential multiple microcracking across the entire gauge length.
2. **Identification of complementary bridging synergy between steel and PE fibers**:
   - High-modulus micro-steel fibers maintained initial cracking strength and stiffness, while flexible PE microfibers sustained large-deformation pullout bridging energy.
3. **Tight crack width control below $0.04\ \text{mm}$ enabling complete autogenous self-healing**:
   - Subdivided the wide single crack ($w > 0.20\ \text{mm}$) of standard UHPC into over 30 microcracks with widths under $40\ \mu\text{m}$, creating conditions for 100 % autogenous healing upon moisture contact.
4. **3.5-fold ductility amplification while maintaining high compressive strength ($142\ \text{MPa}$)**:
   - Successfully converted brittle tensile failure into ductile strain-hardening at a moderate total fiber volume fraction of $1.5\ \text{vol.}\%$.

## Methods

- **Materials and mixture design (Section 2 & Tables 1–4 & Figs. 1–3)**:
  - Binders: Type 1 Portland cement + zirconium silica fume + quartz powder (W/B = 0.20, polycarboxylate superplasticizer).
  - Artificial flaws: Spherical expanded polystyrene (PS) beads ($d = 1.0\sim 2.0\ \text{mm}$, $V_{bead} = 0.5, 1.0\ \text{vol.}\%$).
  - Reinforcing fibers (total $V_f = 1.5\ \text{vol.}\%$):
    1. Straight brass-coated steel fibers ($d_f = 0.2\ \text{mm}, L_f = 13\ \text{mm}, \sigma_f = 2500\ \text{MPa}$).
    2. High-strength PE microfibers ($d_f = 31\ \mu\text{m}, L_f = 12\ \text{mm}, \sigma_f = 2700\ \text{MPa}$).
  - Series: `Steel1.5` (mono-steel 1.5%), `Hybrid` (Steel 1.0% + PE 0.5%), `Hybrid-Bead0.5`, `Hybrid-Bead1.0`.
- **Direct tension testing and image analysis (Sections 2.2–2.4 & Figs. 4–6)**:
  - Uniaxial direct tension: Dogbone specimens (80 mm gauge length, 0.2 mm/min).
  - Instrumentation: 2D-DIC full-field strain and crack opening tracking, LVDTs, 50 mm cube compressive strength.

### 2. Flaw control and crack width restriction mechanics
- **Stress redistribution via engineered flaws**: PS beads ($1\sim 2\ \text{mm}$) relieved severe localized stress concentrations at crack tips in the high-strength matrix, preventing runaway localized failure and inducing 32 multiple microcracks.
- **Autogenous healing potential of $0.04\ \text{mm}$ cracks**: Constraining crack widths below $40\ \mu\text{m}$ enabled secondary hydration of unhydrated clinker to achieve 100 % complete crack closure within 28 d.

- **Overall conclusion**: This landmark paper demonstrated that incorporating artificial PS bead flaws and hybridizing steel with PE fibers amplifies UHPC tensile strain capacity by 3.5-fold while restricting average crack widths to 0.04 mm.
