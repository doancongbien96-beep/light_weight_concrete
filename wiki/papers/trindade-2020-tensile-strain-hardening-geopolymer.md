## Summary
This study micromechanically evaluated the uniaxial direct tensile behavior of strain-hardening geopolymer composites (SHGC), obtained by incorporating PVA fibers and high-strength PE fibers into metakaolin and slag (GGBS)-based geopolymer matrices, under ambient curing conditions. Single-fiber pullout tests measured the interfacial fracture energy ($G_d$) and frictional shear stress ($\tau_0$), from which the PSH strength and energy indices were computed. Leveraging the low matrix fracture toughness ($K_m \approx 0.52 MPa\sqrt{m}$) of the metakaolin-slag matrix, it was demonstrated that PE-SHGC attains ultra-high-ductility multiple microcracking behavior — a tensile strength of 9.2 MPa and a tensile strain capacity of 6.5 % — even under ambient curing.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishment of a micromechanics model for ambient-cured metakaolin-slag SHGC**: defined ambient-cure binder mix proportions satisfying the $J_b'/J_{tip} > 5.5$ condition.
- **Quantification of the difference in geopolymer interfacial bond between PVA and PE fibers**: in the alkaline environment PVA fibers undergo some fiber rupture due to strong hydrogen bonding, whereas PE fibers realize fully slip-dominated pullout and achieve $6 \% \sim 7 \%$ strain capacity.
- **Analysis of multiple microcrack saturation characteristics**: crack spacing controlled to $2.0 \sim 2.8 mm$ and the mean crack width kept at or below $30 \mu m$.

## Methods
- **Mix proportions**: metakaolin 60 % + GGBS 40 %, activated by a potassium hydroxide ($KOH$) + potassium silicate ($K_2SiO_3$) combined alkaline solution.
- **Fibers**: PE fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 12 µm, strength 3000 MPa) and PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, REC15).
- **Test items**: uniaxial dogbone direct tension test, single-fiber pullout test, and three-point bending test for matrix fracture toughness.

## Results
- PE-SHGC: 28 d compressive strength $62.5 MPa$, tensile strength $9.20 MPa$, tensile strain capacity $6.48 \%$.
- PVA-SHGC: 28 d compressive strength $54.0 MPa$, tensile strength $5.10 MPa$, tensile strain capacity $3.85 \%$.
