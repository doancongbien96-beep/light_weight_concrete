# Residual Mechanical Behavior and Fire Spalling Mitigation Mechanisms of Expanded Glass Granule (EGG) Lightweight Engineered Geopolymer Composites (LEGC) Exposed to Elevated Temperatures up to $800\ ^\circ\text{C}$

## Summary

To reduce carbon dioxide emissions by 80 %, reduce structural dead weight, and maximize structural fire safety, Professor Abdulkadir Çevik and co-workers at the University of Gaziantep developed **eco-friendly lightweight engineered geopolymer composites (LEGC: dry density $1703\sim 1883\ \text{kg/m}^3$)** and cement-based lightweight ECC (LECC) by completely replacing silica sand with recycled expanded glass granule aggregates (EGG, particle size $0.1\sim 0.25\ \text{mm}$, bulk density $450\ \text{kg/m}^3$). Investigating precursor combinations (GGBS, Class-F fly ash, and Slag-FA 50:50 blend) with PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.\%}$) exposed to ambient temperature ($25\ ^\circ\text{C}$) and **elevated temperatures ($200\ ^\circ\text{C}, 400\ ^\circ\text{C}, 600\ ^\circ\text{C}, 800\ ^\circ\text{C}$) for 1 h**, the authors evaluated mass loss, residual compressive strength, uniaxial tensile stress-strain response, 4-point flexural deflection-hardening, and spalling resistance. At ambient temperature, Slag-FA hybrid LEGC (LEGC-S-FA) exhibited a 28 d compressive strength of **$48.5\ \text{MPa}$**, an ultimate tensile strength of **$4.6\ \text{MPa}$**, and an elongation of **$4.8\ \%$**. Under $800\ ^\circ\text{C}$ fire exposure, porous EGG aggregate networks and PVA fiber melting ($230\ ^\circ\text{C}$) released internal vapor pressure, achieving **0.0 % explosive spalling** and maintaining a **high residual compressive strength of 22.5 MPa (45 % retention)** via 3D N-A-S-H thermal sintering.

## Key Contributions

1. **Lightweighting ($1750\ \text{kg/m}^3$) and 4.8 % tensile ductility via expanded glass granules (EGG)**:
   - Reduced density by 18 % relative to conventional ECC while the spherical EGG particles promoted uniform 3D fiber dispersion to achieve $4.8\%$ tensile strain capacity with tight microcracks ($w < 45\ \mu\text{m}$).
2. **100 % spalling prevention under $800\ ^\circ\text{C}$ exposure**:
   - Prevented catastrophic explosive spalling in dense matrices by relieving vapor pressure through EGG internal pores and interconnected channels created by vaporized PVA fibers.
3. **Superior high-temperature strength retention of LEGC over cementitious LECC**:
   - Whereas cement-based LECC lost 85 % of its compressive strength at $800\ ^\circ\text{C}$ ($42.5 \rightarrow 6.5\ \text{MPa}$) due to C-S-H decomposition, LEGC-FA/Slag retained 22.5 MPa through thermal stability of inorganic aluminosilicate networks.
4. **Precursor-dependent (Slag vs FA) phase transition and microstructure evolution**:
   - Slag-based C-A-S-H underwent recrystallization above $600\ ^\circ\text{C}$, while fly ash N-A-S-H experienced ceramic sintering that densified the matrix at high temperatures.

## Methods

- **Raw materials and mixture proportions (Tables 1–3, mass ratios)**:
  - Precursors: GGBS ($d_{50} = 12.5\ \mu\text{m}$), Class-F Fly Ash ($d_{50} = 15.8\ \mu\text{m}$), OPC (for LECC comparison).
  - Activator: $12\ \text{M}\ \text{NaOH}$ solution + liquid sodium silicate ($\text{Na}_2\text{O}\cdot 3.1\text{SiO}_2$, specific gravity 1.373, $\text{Na}_2\text{SiO}_3/\text{NaOH} = 2.5$).
  - Aggregate: Expanded glass granules (EGG, $d = 0.1\sim 0.25\ \text{mm}$, bulk density $450\ \text{kg/m}^3$, crushing strength $3.1\ \text{MPa}$, aggregate-to-binder ratio = 0.36).
  - Fibers: Kuraray PVA fibers ($L_f = 12\ \text{mm}, d_f = 39\ \mu\text{m}$, tensile strength 1620 MPa, modulus 42.8 GPa, $V_f = 2.0\ \text{vol.\%}$).
  - 4 mix groups: LEGC-S (100 % Slag), LEGC-FA (100 % FA), LEGC-S-FA (50 % Slag + 50 % FA), LECC (100 % OPC).
- **Heating and testing protocols (Section 2.3 & Figure 3)**:
  - Electric furnace: $5\ ^\circ\text{C/min}$ heating to target temperatures (200, 400, 600, 800 °C), 1 h isothermal soaking followed by slow cooling.
  - Uniaxial tension: JSCE dogbone specimens ($330\ \text{mm} \times 60\ \text{mm} \times 13\ \text{mm}$, $0.2\ \text{mm/min}$).
  - Compression: ASTM C109 ($70\ \text{mm}$ cubes).
  - Flexure: $355\ \text{mm} \times 75\ \text{mm} \times 50\ \text{mm}$ prisms in 4-point bending.

### 2. High-temperature mechanisms and fire safety
- **Strength gain at 200 °C**: Steam generated during moisture evaporation triggered hydrothermal reactions that activated unreacted slag and fly ash, raising compressive strength by 8 %.
- **PVA fiber escape channels**: Above 230 °C, PVA fiber vaporization created an interconnected network of $39\ \mu\text{m}$ channels that fully relieved internal vapor pressure.

- **Overall conclusion**: This work demonstrated that coupling expanded glass granules with geopolymer binders delivers lightweight composites ($1750\ \text{kg/m}^3$) with 4.8 % tensile ductility, 46 % compressive strength retention at 800 °C, and 100 % spalling prevention.
