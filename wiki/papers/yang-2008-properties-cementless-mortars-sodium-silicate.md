## Summary
Toward practical application of eco-friendly cementless ground granulated blast-furnace slag mortars (AAS mortar) that use no Portland cement at all, this original paper on cementless alkali-activated binders by professors Keun-Hyeok Yang, Jin-Kyu Song, and Bang Yeon Lee precisely established experimentally how the alkali equivalent of a liquid sodium silicate activator ($Na_2O = 2.0 \sim 6.0 wt \%$), the $SiO_2/Na_2O$ molar ratio ($Ms = 0.5 \sim 2.0$), and various curing conditions (room-temperature air, water, $60 °C$ steam curing) affect the rate of compressive strength development, drying shrinkage, and the crystalline phases of C-A-S-H hydration reaction products. It demonstrated that under the condition of alkali equivalent $Na_2O = 4.0 \%$ and $Ms = 1.0$, a high strength of **62.5 MPa** at 28 d was developed.

## Key Contributions
- **Derivation of the optimum alkalinity of sodium silicate activated AAS mortar ($Ms = 1.0, Na_2O = 4.0 \%$)**: attainment of an ideal equilibrium between initial dissolution and polycondensation reactions.
- **Establishment of strength development envelopes by curing condition**: under $60 °C$ steam curing, early strength equal to 80 % of the 28-day strength (48 MPa) developed within 1 day.
- **Presentation of drying shrinkage control measures**: reduction of the water-to-binder ratio ($W/B = 0.35$) suppressed the autogenous shrinkage characteristic of AAS by 35 %.
- **Presentation of a standard for manufacturing eco-friendly cementless secondary products**: $CO_2$ emissions cut by 75 % relative to cement.

## Methods
- Binder: ground granulated blast-furnace slag powder (GGBS, specific surface area $4350 cm^2/g$).
- Activator: mixed aqueous solution of sodium hydroxide ($NaOH$) and sodium silicate ($Na_2SiO_3$) ($Ms = 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0$, $Na_2O = 2, 4, 6 \%$), $W/B = 0.35, 0.45$.
- Testing: KS F 2405 compressive strength (50 mm cubes), drying shrinkage strain (comparator), XRD crystalline phase analysis, SEM microstructure.
