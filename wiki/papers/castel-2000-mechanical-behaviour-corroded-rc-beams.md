## Summary

A Materials and Structures classic in which the team of Prof. A. Castel and Prof. R. François at the University of Toulouse, France characterized the local rebar cross-section loss (Pitting Corrosion), longitudinal corrosion crack widths in the cover concrete, flexural stiffness degradation, and ultimate-load behaviour of full-scale reinforced concrete beams ($150 \times 280 \times 3000 mm$, 28-day compressive strength 45 MPa) that underwent long-term natural corrosion under sustained loading (Natural Corrosion under Sustained Loading) for 14 years in a salt-water spray chamber replicating a real marine chloride environment. Unlike artificial electrochemically accelerated corrosion, corrosion attack under natural exposure concentrates at localized pitting sites: the study demonstrated a 25 % degradation of service-load flexural stiffness at a rebar section-loss rate of 10 %, with yield load decreasing in direct proportion to the residual rebar cross-sectional area.

## Key Contributions

- **First elucidation worldwide of the mechanical behaviour of full-scale beams naturally corroded under sustained service load for 14 years**: overcame the unrealistic uniform corrosion of artificial electro-accelerated testing and captured localized pitting-corrosion behaviour.
- **Established a bilinear correlation between rebar corrosion and flexural stiffness degradation**: quantified the reduction of the second moment of area before and after crack initiation.
- **Elucidated the mechanism of localized premature yielding driven by pitting corrosion (pitting factor $R = 3.5$)**: stress concentration at localized pits relative to the mean section-loss rate causes early rebar fracture.
- **Established assessment guidelines for the residual service life and flexural capacity of existing RC structures**: provided foundations for Eurocode and fib repair design criteria.

## Methods

- Beam specimens: $150 \times 280 \times 3000 mm$ (tension reinforcement 2-D16 Type 500, compression reinforcement 2-D8, shear stirrups D6@100).
- Concrete mix: OPC 400 kg/m³, coarse aggregate 1220 kg/m³, sand 820 kg/m³, water 200 kg/m³ ($W/C = 0.50$, 28 d $f_c = 45 MPa$, $E = 32 GPa$).
- Exposure conditions: sustained flexural service loading ($M_{ser} = 13.5 kN\cdot m$) in a 35 g/L NaCl salt-spray environment for 14 years.
- Testing: three-point bending test (span 2800 mm); rebar pitting depths measured at 0.1 mm precision.
