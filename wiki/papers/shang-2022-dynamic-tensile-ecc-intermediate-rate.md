## Summary
A study that used high-speed servo-hydraulic tension testing to establish the direct tensile strain-hardening behavior and fine crack propagation characteristics of high-ductility cement composites (PVA-ECC and PE-ECC) under the intermediate strain rates ($\dot{\epsilon} = 10^{-3} \sim 10^{-1} s^{-1}$) generated during seismic events and vehicle impacts. It precisely analyzed the rising trend of initial cracking strength and ultimate tensile strength ($DIF = 1.25 \sim 1.45$) at intermediate strain rates compared with the static condition ($10^{-5} s^{-1}$), the retention of tensile strain capacity, and the change in crack density. In the intermediate rate regime, PVA-ECC was confirmed to retain its ductility stably through enhanced interfacial frictional resistance, with seismic energy dissipation capacity improved by more than 40 %.

## Key Contributions
- **Quantified tensile performance over the seismic strain rate band ($\dot{\epsilon} = 10^{-2} s^{-1}$)**: established dynamic tensile strength and strain indices applicable to seismic design.
- **Demonstrated a positive correlation between crack formation density and strain rate**: as the rate increases, crack spacing narrows from $3.2 mm$ to $2.1 mm$ and the number of multiple cracks increases by 35 %.
- **Proposed a tensile energy absorption density model as a function of strain rate**: derived an energy absorption equation of the form $g(\dot{\epsilon}) = E_0 (1 + \alpha \ln(\dot{\epsilon}/\dot{\epsilon}_0))$.

## Methods
- **Materials**: PVA-ECC ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, $f_c = 52 MPa$) and PE-ECC ($V_f = 1.5 \%$, $f_c = 68 MPa$).
- **Test equipment**: high-speed servo-hydraulic tension testing machine, displacement velocities $0.05 mm/s \sim 50 mm/s$.
- **Instrumentation**: non-contact optical DIC strain analysis and ultra-high-speed camera observation of crack propagation.

## Results
- PVA-ECC:
  - $\dot{\epsilon} = 10^{-5} s^{-1}$: tensile strength $4.65 MPa$, strain capacity $4.50 \%$.
  - $\dot{\epsilon} = 10^{-1} s^{-1}$: tensile strength $6.42 MPa$ ($DIF = 1.38$), strain capacity $4.20 \%$, energy absorption capacity $245 kJ/m3$.
- PE-ECC:
  - $\dot{\epsilon} = 10^{-1} s^{-1}$: tensile strength $9.85 MPa$ ($DIF = 1.42$), strain capacity $6.80 \%$.
