# Mechanical Properties of SHCC According to Type and Content of Shrinkage Reducing Agent (SRA)

## Summary

As part of research to develop sprayable SHCC for infill joints between existing RC frames and seismic retrofit elements, this study experimentally investigated the effects of two hydration-heat-suppressing shrinkage reducing agent (SRA) types (powder-type strontium phase change material and liquid-type silicofluoride) and dosage rates on length change and compressive, tensile, and flexural behaviors of SHCC (W/B 39.2 %, PVA fiber 2.2 vol%). SRA incorporation significantly reduced early-age shrinkage without degrading compressive strength, while enhancing tensile and flexural strength, deformation capacity, and multiple microcrack distribution. The findings demonstrate that mitigating shrinkage-induced matrix micro-damage in rich-mix (high paste volume) SHCC is an effective, low-cost route to improving composite mechanical properties. The publication year is estimated from the PDF creation date (2015-09) as journal details are not printed in the text.

## Key Contributions

1. **Application of two SRA types with distinct shrinkage mitigation mechanisms to SHCC**: Powder-type strontium latent heat material (phase transition temperature 88 °C, latent heat capacity 82.0 cal/g) controlled thermal shock from hydration heat; liquid-type silicofluoride endothermically hydrolyzed into soluble silica and fluoride ions forming insoluble metal fluorides ($\text{CaF}_2$), offsetting hydration heat while providing micro-filler densification.
2. **Reduction in length change**: At 4 days, powder SRA at 3.4 % (SHCC-HR-34) and liquid SRA at 5.0 % (SHCC-SWP-50) reduced shrinkage by 49 % and 44 %, respectively (3.70 mm and 4.17 mm vs 7.46 mm in control), with early-age reductions persisting long-term.
3. **Preservation of compressive strength with increased ductility**: Compressive strengths were maintained (28 d strengths of 49.9~53.7 MPa vs 50 MPa target), while flexural strengths increased to 11.3 MPa (+15 % in SHCC-HR-34) and 12.8 MPa (+30 % in SHCC-SWP-50).
4. **Clarification of enhancement mechanisms**: Attributed to (i) mitigation of early-age matrix damage from reduced shrinkage and (ii) improved fiber dispersion and fiber-matrix interfacial properties due to slight air content increases (7.5 to 7.9~8.0 %). Liquid SRA provided higher tensile strength due to micro-filler and bond enhancements from precipitated fluorides.

## Methods

- Mix proportions (W/B 39.2 %, $V_f$ 2.2 %, target compressive strength 50 MPa): Water 347, Cement 498, Fly ash 387, calcium carbonate filler 603, PVA fiber 29 kg/m³ (density 1.3 g/cm³). SRA dosages: powder type 3.77/7.54, liquid type 5.9/11.8 (g in mix batch). Mix designations: SHCC-N (Control), SHCC-HR-17 / SHCC-HR-34 (powder 1.7/3.4 %), SHCC-SWP-25 / SHCC-SWP-50 (liquid 2.5/5.0 %) based on optimal dosages (3.4, 5.0 %) from previous studies. PVA fibers: diameter 39 μm, length 12 mm, aspect ratio 307, tensile strength 1,600 MPa, E = 40 GPa.
- Length change: Mold dimensions (outer 110 × 1060 × 30 mm / inner 50 × 1000 × 20 mm) with silicone base and perforated plate ($\phi 5\ \text{mm}$ @ 5 mm spacing), evaluating curvature-based length change on 20 mm thick specimens at 4, 7, and 14 days.
- Mechanical testing: Compression on φ100 × 200 mm cylinders (KS F 2403/2405 under 2000 kN load control); flexure on 100 × 100 × 400 mm prisms (KS F 2408); direct tension on JSCE-E-531 dumbbell specimens (cross-section 30 × 30 mm, gauge length 100 mm).
- Flexural toughness: ASTM C1018 toughness indices ($I_5, I_{10}, I_{20}$) extended to higher deflections beyond the standard $I_{20}$ limit (10.5 mm).

## Results

- Length change (mm / %):

| Mix | 4 d | 7 d | 14 d |
|---|---|---|---|
| SHCC-C (Control) | 7.46/1.19 | 13.35/2.14 | 15.23/2.44 |
| SHCC-SWP-50 (Liquid 5 %) | 4.17/0.67 | 11.49/1.84 | 13.45/2.15 |
| SHCC-HR-34 (Powder 3.4 %) | 3.70/0.59 | 12.57/2.01 | 14.28/2.28 |

- Compressive strength (3/7/28 d): SHCC-N 29.2/40.5/52.0, HR-17 28.9/42.6/52.4, HR-34 29.4/39.8/53.7, SWP-25 29.9/43.7/52.0, SWP-50 29.9/40.6/49.9 MPa. Elastic modulus 21.0~23.2 GPa, Poisson's ratio 0.23~0.26, slump flow 170~220 mm, air content 7.5~8.0 %.
- Tensile behavior: Pseudo-strain-hardening observed across all mixes. HR-34 and SWP-50 exhibited significantly higher tensile strength and strain capacity than SHCC-N. Beyond 1.75 % strain, SHCC-N localized while SRA mixes sustained continuous crack multiplication, with liquid SRA exceeding powder SRA at strains > 2 %.
- Flexural behavior: Initial stiffness remained comparable. Flexural strength reached 11.3 MPa for HR-34 (+15 %) and 12.8 MPa for SWP-50 (+30 %) over SHCC-N, with enhanced post-peak ductility in SRA mixes.
