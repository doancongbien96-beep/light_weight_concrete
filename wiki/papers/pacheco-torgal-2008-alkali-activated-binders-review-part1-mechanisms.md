# Comprehensive Review of Alkali-Activated Binders (AAB) and Geopolymers (Part 1): Historical Development, Chemical Reaction Mechanisms, N-A-S-H / C-A-S-H Gel Microstructure, and an Immortal Textbook of $80\%$ Carbon Emission Reduction

## Summary

To fundamentally reduce the $7\%$ share of global $\text{CO}_2$ emissions generated in conventional ordinary Portland cement (OPC) manufacturing and to recycle industrial by-products (blast furnace slag, coal fly ash, calcined metakaolin, mining waste) as eco-friendly structural binders, this paper comprehensively consolidates **the 70-year historical development lineage of alkali-activated binders (Alkali-Activated Binders: AAB) and geopolymers (Geopolymers) (Purdon's early findings in 1940, Glukhovsky's soil silicate theory in the 1950s, Davidovits' geopolymer naming in the 1970s), the kinetics of Si-O-Al network polycondensation reactions, alkali activator chemical species ($\text{NaOH}, \text{KOH}, \text{Na}_2\text{SiO}_3$), the thermodynamic microstructure of the principal hydration gel phases N-A-S-H (calcium-free three-dimensional zeolite-like phase) and C-A-S-H (low-calcium tobermorite-like phase), the optimal mix molar ratios ($\text{Na}_2\text{O}/\text{SiO}_2 = 0.25, \text{SiO}_2/\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 = 3.3, \text{H}_2\text{O}/\text{Na}_2\text{O} = 10\sim 14$), and the greenhouse gas $80\%$ reduction effect** — the most-cited immortal landmark classic review paper in the world, published in Construction and Building Materials in 2008 by **the research team of University Chair Professor Fernando Pacheco-Torgal and Prof. Said Jalali at the University of Minho, Portugal**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Perfect establishment of the historical lineage and chemical terminology system of alkali-activated binders (historical origin document)**:
   - Clearly distinguishes the previously confused definitions and reaction domains of alkali-activated slag (AAS), geopolymers, and inorganic polymers according to precursor calcium content.
2. **Formulation of the three-stage alkali dissolution–rearrangement–polycondensation reaction mechanism**:
   - Systematizes the dissociation of silicate ($\text{SiO}_4, \text{AlO}_4$) tetrahedra under strongly alkaline ($\text{pH} > 13.5$) conditions, the formation of monomer/oligomer complexes, and the kinetics of network three-dimensional polycondensation gelation.
3. **Contrast of the microstructure and degradation characteristics of N-A-S-H gel versus C-A-S-H gel**:
   - Establishes that calcium-free systems (fly ash/metakaolin) form a three-dimensional N-A-S-H gel with outstanding heat resistance ($>800\ ^\circ\text{C}$) and acid resistance, while high-calcium systems (slag) form a high-strength layered C-A-S-H gel.
4. **Establishment of the global standard guideline for cement-zero ($0\%$) carbon-neutral concrete**:
   - Serves as the foundational textbook for thousands of studies worldwide on geopolymers and cement-free binders.

## Methods

- **Reaction mechanisms and chemical modeling (Sections 2~5 & Figs. 1~8)**:
  - Three-stage reaction model: $1)\ \text{Al-Si}$ alkaline hydrolytic dissolution of minerals $\rightarrow 2)$ bridging monomer transfer and condensation oligomerization $\rightarrow 3)$ polycondensation of multi-three-dimensional aluminosilicate polymer networks.
  - Derivation of optimal molar ratios: $\text{Na}_2\text{O}/\text{SiO}_2 = 0.20\sim 0.30$, $\text{SiO}_2/\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 = 3.0\sim 4.0$, $\text{H}_2\text{O}/\text{Na}_2\text{O} = 10.0\sim 14.0$.
- **Comparative database by precursor material (Sections 5~7 & Tables 1~4 & Figs. 9~14)**:
  - Metakaolin ($\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 \cdot 2\text{SiO}_2$), low-calcium Class F fly ash, blast furnace slag (GGBS), mine waste powders.

### 2. Interpretation of water content ($\text{H}_2\text{O}/\text{Na}_2\text{O}$) and alkali activator effects
- **Destructive effect of surplus water**: when $\text{H}_2\text{O}/\text{Na}_2\text{O} > 25$, gel polycondensation is hindered and large capillary pores form, dropping the compressive strength by more than $80\%$.
- **Optimal activator ratio**: at $\text{Na}_2\text{O}/\text{SiO}_2 = 0.25$ the degree of gel polymerization is maximized, completing a dense continuous network structure.

- **Overall conclusion**: This review paper is the classic review that became the greatest immortal landmark textbook of global cement-free eco-friendly construction materials engineering, in which Prof. Pacheco-Torgal's research team comprehensively consolidated the 70-year history of alkali-activated binders and geopolymers, their chemical reaction kinetics, the N-A-S-H / C-A-S-H gel microstructure, and their eco-friendly carbon neutrality.
