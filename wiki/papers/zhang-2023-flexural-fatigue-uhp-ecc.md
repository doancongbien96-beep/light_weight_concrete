## Summary
To extend the long-term dynamic fatigue life of bridge deck joints and medium-traffic pavements, this study by Professor Christopher Leung's research team at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology established the flexural fatigue behavior (stress ratio $S = 0.65 \sim 0.90$, $R = 0.1$, frequency 5 Hz) of ultra-high performance strain-hardening cementitious composites (UHP-ECC, $f_c = 130 MPa$, static flexural modulus of rupture $f_r = 28.5 MPa$, PE fiber $V_f = 2.0 \%$), the stiffness degradation histories, the evolution of the dissipated energy area, and Weibull statistical fatigue life prediction envelopes. The study demonstrated that UHP-ECC recorded a **$2 \times 10^6$ cycle run-out limit** even under a high fatigue stress level of $S = 0.75$, improving the fatigue limit stress ratio by more than **25 %** compared with normal steel-fiber UHPC.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishment of a flexural fatigue ($S-N-P_f$) life prediction envelope for UHP-ECC**: derivation of a fatigue limit stress ratio $S_e = 0.73$ at a failure probability of 5 %.
- **Complete restraint of crack widths below $20 \mu m$ under repeated fatigue loading**: blocking the ingress of harmful substances even after millions of fatigue cycles.
- **Demonstration of the fatigue wear resistance of the PE fiber interface**: the slip-hardening mechanism suppressed interfacial damage during repeated shear friction.
- **Proposal of maintenance-free fatigue design criteria for long-span bridge decks**: establishment of design equations guaranteeing a 100-year fatigue life.

## Methods
- Mix proportions: UHP-ECC ($f_c = 130 MPa$, PE fiber $V_f = 2.0 \%$, $f_r = 28.5 MPa$).
- Fatigue test: $100 \times 100 \times 400 mm$ beams, four-point flexural fatigue testing machine (span 300 mm, 5 Hz sine wave, $S = 0.65, 0.70, 0.75, 0.80, 0.85, 0.90$).
- Instrumentation: continuous mid-span deflection measurement with laser displacement transducers; SEM analysis of PE fibers on fracture surfaces.
