## Summary

A landmark ASCE JMCE paper by the research team of Prof. A.A. Almusallam at King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM) that used electrochemical accelerated corrosion testing (current density $2 mA/cm^2$) and four-point flexural loading tests to establish how reinforcement corrosion level (mass loss ratio 0 ~ 25 %) affects the flexural crack initiation load, ultimate flexural capacity, deflection ductility, and failure mode of one-way RC slabs ($700 \times 300 \times 100 mm$, $f_c = 30 MPa$) in chloride-laden environments. Where reinforcement section loss is **5 % or less**, expansion of the corrosion products raises interfacial friction and flexural strength increases slightly; **beyond 5 %**, longitudinal splitting cracks in the cover concrete and bond loss slash ultimate flexural capacity, and at 25 % corrosion more than **60 %** of flexural capacity is lost.

## Key Contributions
- **Four-stage transition of flexural failure mode with reinforcement corrosion level**: stage 1 undamaged ductile flexure $\rightarrow$ stage 2 strength retained under minor corrosion $\rightarrow$ stage 3 cover spalling and bond-slip failure $\rightarrow$ stage 4 brittle rebar-fracture failure.
- **Derivation of the critical corrosion limit ($5 \%$ mass loss)**: beyond $5 \%$, rebar-concrete bond stress is lost abruptly.
- **Quantification of the relation between corrosion level ($C_r$) and ultimate flexural moment decay**: in the range $C_r > 5 \%$, the decay law $M_u = M_0 (1 - 0.028 C_r)$ was established.
- **A standard for assessing residual capacity of RC slabs and bridge decks**: structural safety diagnosis criteria established through non-destructive corrosion current measurement.

## Methods
- Slab specimens: $700 \times 300 \times 100 mm$ (tension reinforcement 3-D10, $f_y = 480 MPa$, effective depth $d = 75 mm$, cover thickness 20 mm).
- Accelerated corrosion: immersion in 5 % NaCl solution with DC application at $2.0 mA/cm^2$ (target corrosion levels 0, 2.5, 5.0, 10.0, 15.0, 25.0 % weight loss).
- Testing: simply supported four-point bending test (span 600 mm), midspan deflection by LVDT, precise measurement of rebar mass loss by chemical cleaning (ASTM G1).
