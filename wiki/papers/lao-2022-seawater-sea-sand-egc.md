# Development of High-Strength, High-Ductility Seawater Sea-Sand EGC (SS-EGC)

## Summary

This study by Prof. Jian-Guo Dai's group at Hong Kong PolyU developed the world's first seawater sea-sand geopolymer EGC (SS-EGC). The material achieved compressive strengths over 140 MPa and tensile strain capacity around 8 %. Although seawater slightly retards reaction, it increases hydrotalcite phases, raising nanoindentation modulus and fiber bond to enhance tensile strength.

## Key Contributions

- **First development of SS-EGC**: Across FA/GGBS 8:2 (F8S2) and 2:8 (F2S8), achieved compressive strength 84.5~145.1 MPa, strain capacity 6.9~8.7 %, and tensile strength 9.0~11.9 MPa. Seawater caused minor compressive strength drop of -1.5~-5.0 % (due to $\text{MgCl}_2$ reacting to form M-S-H gel).
- **Dual mechanism of seawater ions**: TGA verified increased Cl-hydrotalcite; nanoindentation deconvolution showed +17~20 % ultra-high-density (UHD) phase (~45 GPa, C-(N)-A-S-H + hydrotalcite). Higher matrix modulus elevated fiber bond and tensile strength.
- **DIC cracking behavior**: F2S8-S showed average crack width 59.4 µm with reduced dispersion, favoring self-healing.
- **Embodied carbon**: Relative to cementitious SS-ECC (1204 kg CO₂/m³), SS-EGC emitted 526 kg CO₂/m³ (43.7 %), shifting carbon burden from cement (92.9 %) to activators (72 %) and retarders (12.3 %).

## Methods

- Mix: Precursors FA + GGBS + silica fume (total 1.000), borax, $\text{Na}_2\text{SiO}_3$ + waterglass ($\text{Na}_2\text{O}$/precursor 6.5 %, $\text{SiO}_2/\text{Na}_2\text{O}$ 1.3), W/P 0.25, S/P 0.3 (sea-sand max 1.18 mm), UHMWPE fibers ($\varnothing 24\ \mu\text{m}$, ∅24 µm, length 18 mm) at 2 vol %.
- Curing: Demolded at 24 h, plastic wrapped, cured at 23 °C for 28 d (28 days).
- Testing: Compression per ASTM C109 (50 mm cubes), tension per JSCE (dog-bone, 80 mm LVDTs, DIC 3 s intervals), calorimetry (72 h), TGA (60 °C to 900 °C at 10 °C/min), nanoindentation (Oliver-Pharr, 11×11 grid with 484 points).

## Results

- Compressive strength: F8S2-F 89.0 / F8S2-S 84.5 / F2S8-F 145.1 / F2S8-S 143.0 MPa.
- Tensile: Strain capacity 8.7/8.1/8.6/6.9 %, tensile strength 8.1/9.0/11.2/11.9 MPa.
- UHD phase: Seawater increased UHD phase by +20 % in F8S2 and +17 % in F2S8; LD/HD phases dropped -10~-16 %.
- Embodied carbon: SS-EGC 526.2 kg CO₂/m³ vs SS-ECC 1204.1 kg CO₂/m³.
