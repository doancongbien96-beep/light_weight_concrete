# Structural Characteristics of Reinforced Large-Diameter PHC (R-LDPHC) Piles

## Summary

This paper compares the shear and flexural characteristics of reinforced large-diameter PHC (R-LDPHC, 700~1200 mm) piles incorporating infilled concrete with longitudinal and transverse reinforcement against conventional LDPHC piles through experiments and layered sectional analysis (Chungnam National University, Yun Yong Kim corresponding author; Chonnam National University, Bang Yeon Lee co-author). Reinforcement increased flexural strength by +231 % and improved shear strength by 3.28-fold—with infilled concrete and transverse rebar contributing 23.2 % and 76.8 %, respectively, to the 1748 kN shear capacity increment.

## Key Contributions

1. **231 % flexural strength gain and analytical validation**: Infilled concrete and longitudinal rebar increased flexural strength by 3.31-fold (231 % increase). Non-linear layered sectional predictions matched experimental values with an average error of 10.5 %.
2. **3.28-fold shear strength gain and quantitative apportionment**: Of the 1748 kN shear increment, transverse rebar provided 76.8 % and infilled core concrete contributed 23.2 %, controlling flexure-shear crack widths and preventing shear failure after initial cracking.
3. **Improved crack morphology**: Transverse rebar promoted symmetric crack distribution and minimized crack widths, securing ductile behavior.
4. **Completion of the PHC pile series**: Completes the ICP/LDPHC series spanning Bang (2013, KCI Korean) $\rightarrow$ Bang et al. (2014, SEM English) $\rightarrow$ this paper (Computers and Concrete, large-diameter extension).

## Methods

- **LDPHC body**: 80 MPa compressive strength concrete, diameter 1000 mm, thickness 130 mm. 19 prestressing steel strands (∅11.2 mm, tensile strength 1450 MPa) tensioned to 70 % of yield strength. Hexagonal transverse rebar ∅16 mm (yield 400 MPa) at 150 mm spacing.
- **Reinforcement**: Longitudinal rebar D29 (yield 400 MPa) in hollow core, fixed with ∅10 mm stirrups. High-fluidity infilled concrete (slump 210 mm, compressive strength 27 MPa, KS F 4009, OPC + crushed sand + maximum coarse aggregate 19 mm).
- **Fabrication**: Step 1 transverse rebar and prestressing cage $\rightarrow$ Step 2 infilled concrete casting and longitudinal rebar installation.
- **Testing and analysis**: Shear and flexural structural testing; theoretical strength prediction using non-linear layered sectional approach compared with tests.

## Results

- Flexural strength: R-LDPHC increased by 231 % over LDPHC (analysis-experiment error 10.5 %).
- Shear strength: 3.28-fold increase (transverse rebar contributing 76.8 %, infilled concrete contributing 23.2 %).
