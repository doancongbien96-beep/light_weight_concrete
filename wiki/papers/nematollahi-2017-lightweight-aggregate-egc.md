# Green Lightweight Fly Ash-Based EGC Using Lightweight Aggregates

## Summary

Development of lightweight EGCs that fully replace OPC with fly ash geopolymer and fully substitute micro-silica sand with lightweight aggregates (expanded perlite, hollow ceramic microspheres, expanded recycled glass). Density, strength, thermal conductivity, and tensile ductility were evaluated, demonstrating satisfaction of structural lightweight-concrete requirements (density <1850 kg/m3, compressive strength >17 MPa) along with environmental superiority over ECC.

## Key Contributions

1. Lightweight-concrete grade achieved in all mixes: density 1586~1833 kg/m3 (up to 24 % lower than ECC M45), compressive strength 43.4~56.8 MPa. Even EGC-S using micro-silica sand alone came to 1828 kg/m3 — the low specific gravity of fly ash itself (2.45 vs cement 3.15) inherently contributes to lightening.
2. Tensile ductility retained: despite lightweight aggregate substitution, tensile strain capacity of 3.5~3.7 % was equivalent across all mixes. The mechanism interpretation is the core contribution — in PSH index terms σ_0/σ_fc remains around 1.3~1.5 in all mixes, so strain capacities are likewise similar. In the lightweight-aggregate mixes σ_0 and σ_fc (J_tip) drop together, keeping the balance of the energy index J′_b/J_tip maintained.
3. Aggregate shape effect elucidated: spherical aggregates (recycled glass, ceramic spheres) reduce crack-path tortuosity, lowering matrix toughness and first-crack strength by about 30 %, while their smooth surfaces reduce fiber–matrix frictional bonding → ultimate tensile strength reduced by up to 32 %.
4. Thermal conductivity reduced by 38~49 %: the multicellular structure of recycled glass was most effective (at the level of 0.934 W/mK).
5. MSI analysis: CO2 emissions down 52 % versus ECC M45, embodied energy down 17 % (the activator and heat curing remain the residual burdens).

## Methods

Class F fly ash (Gladstone), 8 M NaOH + D-grade sodium silicate (Na2SiO3/NaOH 2.5), PVA (RECS15/8, oil-coating 1.2 %) at 2 vol %. Activator/fly ash 0.45, sand/fly ash 0.30 (volume-equivalent substitution). Heat curing at 60 °C for 24 h, then tested at 3 days. Direct-tension coupons of 400×75×10 mm (gauge length 80 mm); thermal conductivity via TCi analyzer (MTPS method).

## Results

- Saturated multiple cracking observed (crack spacing 2~3 mm), with estimated crack widths under load below ~100 μm.
- First-crack strength by aggregate shape: EGC-S·EGC-P (irregular) > EGC-G·EGC-M (spherical).
- Ductility equivalence explained via the Kanda-Li PSH index framework — an empirical demonstration case of [[pseudo-strain-hardening-criteria]].
