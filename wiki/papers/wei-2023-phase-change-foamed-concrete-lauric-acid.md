## Summary
A study clarifying the mechanical compressive strength, thermal conductivity, thermal charging rate, and fine pore structure of thermal-energy-storage foamed concrete incorporating a shape-stabilized composite phase change material (LA/EP ss-PCM), made by vacuum-impregnating porous expanded perlite (EP) with lauric acid (LA, phase change temperature $43 °C$, latent heat $\Delta H = 178 J/g$) at 10 ~ 30 wt % into foamed concrete (density $600 \sim 900 kg/m^3$) to combine the ultralight insulation of lightweight foamed concrete with high-latent-heat storage performance. An ultra-low thermal conductivity ($0.125 W/(m\cdot K)$) and an effective latent heat of **24.5 J/g** were simultaneously secured, and building energy consumption was demonstrated to fall by **38 %**.

## Key Contributions
- **Fabrication of the lauric acid/expanded perlite (LA/EP) shape-stabilized ss-PCM completed**: stable impregnation of high-content lauric acid at 65 wt % achieved without liquid leakage.
- **Realization of an ultralight integrated insulation-storage foamed concrete**: dry density $750 kg/m^3$, thermal conductivity $0.125 W/(m\cdot K)$, and compressive strength 5.2 MPa attained.
- **Demonstration of nano-densification of pore walls**: perlite particles reinforce bubble interfaces, suppressing bubble coalescence and rupture.
- **Foundation provided for commercialization as building exterior-wall insulation and roof thermal storage blocks**: a non-combustible inorganic insulation without fire risk realized.

## Methods
- ss-PCM fabrication: adsorption of molten lauric acid onto expanded perlite in a vacuum impregnation apparatus.
- Foamed concrete: cement + fly ash + animal-protein foaming agent (water-to-foam dilution ratio 1:30), LA/EP composite (10, 20, 30 wt %).
- Tests: Hot Disk TPS thermal conductivity, DSC latent heat, 28 d compressive strength (100 mm cubes), full-scale wall chamber thermal performance test.
