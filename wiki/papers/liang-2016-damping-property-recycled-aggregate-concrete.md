# Dynamic Vibration Damping Property of Recycled Aggregate Concrete (RAC) and the Multi-Interface (Old & New ITZs) Friction Energy Dissipation Mechanism: Demonstration of a $68\%$ Damping-Ratio Amplification at $100\%$ Recycled Aggregate Replacement

## Summary

To simultaneously improve the recyclability of waste-concrete recycled aggregate (Recycled Concrete Aggregate: RCA), discharged in enormous quantities, and the seismic and machine-vibration control performance of structural concrete, **recycled aggregate concrete (RAC) specimens in which natural coarse aggregate was replaced by recycled coarse aggregate (replacement ratios 0%, 30%, 50%, 70%, 100%; 28-day compressive strength $32.5\sim 42.0\ \text{MPa}$)** were fabricated and subjected to three-point dynamic flexural vibration testing ($1.0\sim 10.0\ \text{Hz}$) driven by an electromagnetic exciter, precisely measuring the complex dynamic modulus (Complex Dynamic Modulus $E^* = E' + i E''$), the loss factor ($\eta$), and the modal damping ratio ($\xi$). The results demonstrated **the performance of high-damping smart vibration-absorbing concrete in which, through Coulomb friction and viscoelastic energy dissipation arising at the double porous interfaces formed by the fine pores of the old mortar adhered to the recycled aggregate surface together with the 'old interfacial transition zone (Old ITZ)' and the 'new interfacial transition zone (New ITZ)', the dynamic damping ratio at $100\%$ recycled aggregate replacement is amplified by $68.5\%$ over natural aggregate concrete ($\xi = 2.45\% \rightarrow 4.13\%$) while the loss elastic modulus ($E''$) rises by $45\%$** — the highest-authority landmark original paper, published in Construction and Building Materials 2016, by the research team of **Chair Prof. Xiao Jianzhuang of Tongji University (China) and Prof. Tiejun Liu of Harbin Institute of Technology**.

## Key Contributions

1. **World-first identification of the dramatic $68.5\%$ amplification of the dynamic damping ratio ($\xi$) of concrete upon recycled aggregate (RCA) incorporation**:
   - Proved that the porosity and microcracks of recycled aggregate, previously regarded solely as strength-degrading factors, can be converted into a high-damping resource that actively absorbs dynamic vibration energy.
2. **Formulation of the friction dissipation mechanism of the new-old double interfacial transition zones (Old & New ITZs)**:
   - Modeled the nanoscale sliding-friction energy generated under dynamic deformation at the two boundaries: the recycled aggregate-old mortar interface (Old ITZ) and the old mortar-new paste interface (New ITZ).
3. **Construction of a quantitative database of the complex dynamic modulus ($E^*$) across vibration frequency bands ($1\sim 10\ \text{Hz}$)**:
   - Demonstrated that in the low-frequency vibration regime induced by earthquakes and rail/vehicle traffic, RAC delivers far superior vibration damping and noise reduction performance than natural-aggregate concrete.
4. **Establishment of a global eco-friendly resource-recycling smart vibration-damping concrete design standard**:
   - Completed an engineering paradigm that uses recycled aggregate as a high-value-added damping material in high-speed rail track slabs, machinery equipment foundations, and structures in earthquake-prone regions.

## Methods

- **Materials and concrete mix design (Section 2 & Tables 1~3 & Figs. 1~3)**:
  - Binder: ordinary Portland cement ($W/C = 0.45$).
  - Aggregates: natural silica sand + natural coarse aggregate versus recycled coarse aggregate (RCA, water absorption $5.8\%$, crushing value $22.5\%$), replacement ratios 0%, 30%, 50%, 70%, 100%.
- **Three-point dynamic flexural test program (Sections 2.3~3.2 & Figs. 4~7)**:
  - Specimen: $100 \times 100 \times 400\ \text{mm}$ prism beam.
  - System: electromagnetic vibration exciter + laser displacement sensor + piezoelectric load cell (frequencies $1.0, 3.0, 5.0, 8.0, 10.0\ \text{Hz}$, sinusoidal loading).
  - Metrics: storage elastic modulus ($E'$), loss elastic modulus ($E''$), loss factor ($\eta = E''/E'$), damping ratio ($\xi = \eta/2$).

### 2. Interpretation of double-ITZ Coulomb friction and viscoelastic hysteresis
- **Fine porosity of the adhered mortar**: When vibration loading acts, the porous old mortar on the recycled aggregate surface serves as a cushioning air bag, absorbing deformation energy.
- **Shear sliding at the double interface**: Established that the stiffness mismatch between the Old ITZ and the New ITZ induces microscopic relative displacement at the interfaces, which is dissipated as frictional heat energy.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is the top-tier landmark original study in the field of recycled construction materials and structural dynamics engineering in which the Tongji University team of Prof. Xiao Jianzhuang elucidated the double-interface (Old & New ITZs) friction mechanism of recycled aggregate concrete (RAC) and demonstrated smart vibration-damping concrete that amplifies the damping ratio by 68.5% at 100% recycled aggregate replacement.
