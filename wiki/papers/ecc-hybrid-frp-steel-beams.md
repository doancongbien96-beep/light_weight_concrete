# Flexural Behavior of ECC-Concrete Hybrid Composite Beams Reinforced with FRP and Steel Bars

## Summary

To address excessive crack widths and insufficient deflection ductility caused by the low elastic modulus of non-corrosive basalt fiber-reinforced polymer (BFRP) bars in reinforced concrete members, this study investigated the flexural crack propagation, load-deflection envelopes, rebar stress redistribution, and ductility indices of two-layer ECC-concrete composite beams incorporating a tensile PVA-ECC layer and hybrid BFRP-steel reinforcement via four-point bending tests and nonlinear sectional analysis. The results demonstrated that the tensile ECC layer relieved stress concentrations around the BFRP bars, controlling crack widths to below $0.05 mm$ and improving the deflection ductility index $\mu_\Delta$ by 3.2 times.

## Key Contributions

- **Establishment of flexural mechanisms in ECC-concrete two-layer hybrid sections**: The tensile ECC layer ($h_{ecc}/h = 0.25 \sim 0.50$) dispersed brittle concrete cracking into multiple microcracks, suppressing premature rupture of BFRP bars.
- **Verification of BFRP-steel hybrid reinforcement synergy**: Steel bars yielded to form a plastic hinge while BFRP bars sustained secondary stiffness, achieving a stable two-stage load envelope.
- **220 % enhancement in deflection ductility index ($\mu_\Delta$)**: Transformed the brittle failure mode of regular BFRP-reinforced concrete beams into fully ductile flexural failure.
- **Development of nonlinear moment-curvature ($M-\phi$) design formulations**: Established ultimate flexural capacity prediction models conforming to AASHTO and ACI 440 guidelines.

## Methods

- 10 full-scale composite beams ($150 \times 250 \times 2200 mm$, span 1900 mm).
- Matrix: Top normal concrete ($f_c = 42 MPa$) + bottom PVA-ECC layer ($t = 0, 60, 120 mm$, $f_c = 48 MPa$, $\epsilon_u = 3.5 \%$).
- Reinforcement: Tensile BFRP bars (diameters 12, 16 mm, $f_u = 1150 MPa$, $E = 48 GPa$) + deformed steel bars (HRB400, $f_y = 430 MPa$).
- Four-point bending tests, full-field DIC strain measurement, LVDT deflection transducers, and fiber-optic strain sensing.
