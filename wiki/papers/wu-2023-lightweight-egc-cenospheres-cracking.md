## Summary
A study by Professor Jian-Guo Dai's research team at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University clarifying the density-strength correlation, single PE fiber interface characteristics, direct tensile strain hardening, and fine cracking behavior of structural lightweight high-ductility geopolymer composites (LW-EGC) in which hollow ceramic aluminosilicate cenospheres (apparent density $750 kg/m^3$, median particle size 120 µm) replace the fine aggregate as hollow filler (0 ~ 100 vol %), to radically lighten structural self-weight while still exhibiting ultra-ductile tensile strain-hardening performance. Under a dry density of **1450 kg/m³** (40 % lighter than ordinary concrete), a 28 d compressive strength of **42.5 MPa**, a direct tensile strength of **5.8 MPa**, and an ultimate tensile strain capacity of **8.5 %** were achieved.

## Key Contributions
- **Structural ultra-lightweight high-ductility geopolymer (LW-EGC) mix completed**: tensile strain capacity of 8.5 % realized at a density of $1450 kg/m^3$.
- **Matrix toughness relaxation mechanism of hollow cenosphere spheres quantified**: gentle crack deflection around spherical particles achieved $K_m = 0.26 MPa\sqrt{m}$.
- **Slip-hardening bridging coefficient ($\beta = 0.85$) of single PE fibers secured**: interfacial frictional stress $\tau_0 = 1.75 MPa$ maintained, realizing a PSH index $J_b'/J_{tip} = 12.8$.
- **Innovation in modular construction and long-span bridge deck lightweighting**: structural self-weight cut by 40 %, reducing foundation costs by 30 %.

## Methods
- Binder: slag 60 % + fly ash 40 %, liquid sodium silicate activator ($W/B = 0.32$).
- Aggregate: hollow cenospheres (replacement ratios 0, 30, 60, 100 vol %).
- Fiber: UHMWPE fiber ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 12 µm, length 12 mm).
- Tests: dogbone direct tension test ($0.5 mm/min$, full-field DIC strain analysis), 28 d compressive strength, three-point bending test.
