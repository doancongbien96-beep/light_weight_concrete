# A Decade of Engineered Cementitious Composites (ECC) R&D Distilled: A Review of Micromechanics-Based PSH Design Theory, Processing (Casting, Extrusion, Spraying), and Full-Scale Structural Applications

## Summary

To consolidate the past decade of research and development on engineered cementitious composites (ECC) — invented in the early 1990s to overcome the limitations of brittle concrete — together with the technologies for applying it to actual infrastructure structures, this paper organizes the core theory of **micromechanics-based pseudo-strain-hardening (PSH) strength/energy design criteria ($\sigma_0 > \sigma_{fc}$, $J_b' > J_{tip}$), uniaxial tensile strain capacity ($3\sim 5\%$) achieved at a low fiber volume fraction below 2 %, and intrinsic crack-width restraint ($w < 60\ \mu\text{m}$)**, and comprehensively surveys the **flexible processing technologies of self-compacting casting, extrusion molding, and sprayed ECC construction along with real large-project track records including jointless link slabs for bridge decks, seismic damping elements for super-high-rise buildings, and dam repair**, in the Journal of Advanced Concrete Technology (JACT) 2003 most authoritative landmark inaugural review by **Prof. Victor C. Li, endowed chair professor at the University of Michigan**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Full establishment of the micromechanics-based ECC materials design strategy**:
   - By quantitatively modeling the three constituent elements — fiber, matrix, and interface — it establishes an analytical design framework that guarantees strain-hardening at $V_f \le 2.0\%$ without random trial-and-error.
2. **Elucidation of steady-state cracking and intrinsic crack-width self-control**:
   - It elucidates the intrinsic durability mechanism in which the crack width stays at or below $60\ \mu\text{m}$ even as stress increases and only new microcracks keep multiplying.
3. **Systematization of diverse field-tailored construction processes (Processing)**:
   - It presents mix proportioning and rheological guidelines for self-compacting casting with maximized flowability, extruded panel forming, and shotcrete spray repair methods.
4. **First disclosure of successful global full-scale infrastructure applications**:
   - It perfectly demonstrates practical engineering applicability through the jointless link slab of the Mihara Bridge in Japan, the seismic coupling beams of the Roppongi Hills super-high-rise, dam lining repair, and more.

## Methods

- **Scope of the reviewed literature and research framework (Sections 2~5 & Tables 1~3)**:
  - Distills roughly 60 core ECC studies worldwide from 1992–2003.
  - Key areas covered:
    1. Micromechanics design principles ($J_b'/J_{tip} \ge 1$, $\sigma_0/\sigma_{fc} \ge 1$).
    2. Mechanical property spectrum (tensile ductility $3\sim 5\%$, compressive strength $40\sim 80\ \text{MPa}$, flexural MOR $10\sim 15\ \text{MPa}$).
    3. Processing and forming technologies (Self-compacting, Extruded, Sprayed).
    4. Durability (freeze-thaw 300 cycles $RDM > 100\%$, self-healing watertightness).
    5. Structural applications (Link slabs, Bridge decks, Retrofit layers).

## Results

### 2. Mechanism Analysis of Link-Slab and Seismic Damper Applications
- **Elimination of bridge deck expansion joints (Link Slab)**: replacing the expansion joints over piers with ECC link slabs absorbs repeated flexural rotation deformation as microcracks of $50\ \mu\text{m}$ or less, cutting off deck water leakage and lower pier corrosion at the source.
- **Seismic energy-absorbing dampers**: dissipate the massive shear plastic-deformation energy of an earthquake through multi-microcrack shear friction, reducing damage to primary column and beam members to zero.

- **Overall conclusion**: This paper is a historic, immortal classic of a review in which Prof. Victor C. Li consolidated a decade of ECC research and development and its global full-scale structural application achievements, changing the design paradigm of modern civil and building composite materials.
