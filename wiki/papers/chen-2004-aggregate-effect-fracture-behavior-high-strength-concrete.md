## Summary
A landmark CBM paper in which Prof. B. Chen's research team at Hunan University, China, used notched-beam fracture tests and X-ray crack tracing to determine how the maximum coarse aggregate size ($d_{max} = 10, 15, 20 mm$) and aggregate volume fraction (30 to 50 %) govern the fracture characteristics of high strength concrete (HSC, $f_c = 75 \sim 95 MPa$), namely their influence on the fracture energy ($G_F$), critical stress intensity factor ($K_{IC}$), fracture process zone (FPZ) width, and crack deflection path of three-point notched flexural beams. The study demonstrated that as the aggregate maximum size $d_{max}$ increases from 10 mm to 20 mm, the fracture energy $G_F$ rises sharply by **60 %**, from **92.5 N/m to 148.2 N/m**, while the fracture toughness $K_{IC}$ climbs from **1.45 to 2.15 MPa·m¹/²**.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishment of a quantitative correlation between HSC aggregate maximum size ($d_{max}$) and fracture energy ($G_F$)**: coarser aggregate enlarges the crack deflection and aggregate interlock bridging area.
- **Three-dimensional visualization of the fracture process zone (FPZ) via X-ray transmission inspection**: first experimental measurement showing the microcrack damage zone width expanding from 12 mm to 28 mm with increasing $d_{max}$.
- **Elucidation of the interfacial transition zone (ITZ) strengthening mechanism by ultrafine GGBS powder (specific surface area $6000 cm^2/g$)**: control of the combined fracture modes of aggregate splitting itself and ITZ debonding.
- **Proposal of an optimum aggregate size design criterion for mitigating the brittleness of high-strength structural concrete**: securing shear and torsional ductility of high-strength members.

## Methods
- Raw materials: OPC (Shaofeng 52.5R), ultrafine slag powder ($6000 cm^2/g$), silica fume, river sand ($FM = 2.85$).
- Aggregate variable: crushed limestone coarse aggregate ($d_{max} = 10, 15, 20 mm$, specific gravity 2.39).
- Specimens: $100 \times 100 \times 515 mm$ notched beam (span 400 mm, notch depth $a_0 = 30 mm$).
- Testing: three-point flexural fracture test (MTS servo testing machine, CMOD control at $0.02 mm/min$), X-ray crack path inspection.
