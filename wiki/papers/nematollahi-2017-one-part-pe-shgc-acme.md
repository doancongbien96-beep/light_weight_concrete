## Summary
To solve the problem of handling hazardous, highly corrosive liquid alkaline activators and realize a one-part binder-free high-ductility geopolymer composite (One-Part PE-SHGC) constructed by simply pouring water like ordinary cement, the research team of Dr. B. Nematollahi at Swinburne University of Technology elucidated the ambient-temperature curing ($23 °C$) mechanical properties, single-fiber pullout bridging characteristics, and uniaxial direct-tension strain-hardening performance of composites in which solid sodium silicate powder and sodium hydroxide powder were pre-blended into the dry binder and ultra-high-molecular-weight PE fiber ($V_f = 2.0 \%$) reinforcement was incorporated. Excellent ductility was achieved: 28 d ambient compressive strength of 38.5 MPa, direct tensile strength of 4.8 MPa, and a tensile strain capacity of **4.2 %**.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishment of ambient-curing technology for just-add-water one-part high-ductility geopolymer (One-Part SHGC)**: a dramatic improvement in field-casting applicability and construction safety.
- **Demonstration of consistency between solid activator dissolution rate and PE fiber bridging capacity**: securing $J_b'/J_{tip} = 4.5$ induces stable multiple fine cracking.
- **Realization of a tensile strain capacity of 4.2 %**: ductility surpassing Portland-cement-based conventional PVA-ECC attained under ambient-curing conditions.
- **Embodied carbon cut by 65 %**: provides a foundation for commercializing low-carbon construction materials using industrial by-product fly ash/slag.

## Methods
- Binder: Class F fly ash 70 % + GGBS 30 % + solid sodium silicate powder ($Na_2O 14.7 \%$, $SiO_2 29.4 \%$) + anhydrous solid $NaOH$, $W/B = 0.33$.
- Fiber: Spectra PE fiber ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 38 µm, length 12.7 mm, tensile strength 2600 MPa, elastic modulus 75 GPa).
- Tests: dogbone direct tension test ($0.5 mm/min$, LVDT measurement), 28 d compressive strength (50 mm cubes), mini-slump flow.
