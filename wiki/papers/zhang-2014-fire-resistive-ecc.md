## Summary
A study by Professor Victor Li's research team at the University of Michigan clarifying, after high-temperature exposure ($200 \sim 800 °C$), the thermal conductivity, residual compressive strength, uniaxial direct tensile behavior, and thermal shock resistance of a composite incorporating hollow ceramic cenospheres (20 ~ 50 vol %) as lightweight insulating fine aggregate and reinforced with PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$), in order to realize a fire-resistive ductile composite (FR-ECC) that prevents explosive spalling of structures under fire and high-temperature heating environments while maintaining residual tensile ductility after fires. A dry density of **1350 kg/m³** and a room-temperature thermal conductivity of **0.25 W/(m·K)** were achieved, and it was demonstrated that even after heating at $400 °C$, a residual tensile strain capacity of **1.85 %** was maintained without spalling.

## Key Contributions
- **Micromechanics mix design of an ultra-lightweight fire-resistant ductile composite (FR-ECC) completed**: fusion of the insulation of hollow cenosphere spheres with the bridging force of PVA fibers.
- **Ultra-low thermal conductivity ($0.25 W/(m\cdot K)$) realized**: insulation performance improved sixfold over ordinary concrete ($1.5 W/(m\cdot K)$).
- **High-temperature explosive spalling blocked 100 % completely**: dual mechanism of melt channels formed by PVA fibers at $230 °C$ and the porous steam buffering action of cenospheres.
- **Preservation of residual tensile strain hardening after $400 °C$ exposure demonstrated**: risk of sudden post-fire brittle collapse eliminated.

## Methods
- Binder: cement + fly ash ($FA/C = 1.5$) + cenospheres (20, 35, 50 vol %), $W/B = 0.35$.
- Fiber: Kuraray REC15 PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 39 µm, length 12 mm).
- Heating tests: electric furnace heating ($200, 400, 600, 800 °C$, held 2 hours then slow cooled).
- Tests: Hot Disk thermal conductivity analyzer, dogbone direct tension test, 28 d compressive strength, SEM microstructure.

## Results
- Mechanical and thermophysical property data of FR-ECC by heating temperature:

| Heating temperature ($°C$) | Dry density ($kg/m^3$) | Thermal conductivity ($W/(m\cdot K)$) | 28 d compressive strength (MPa) | Residual uniaxial tensile strength (MPa) | Residual tensile strain capacity (%) | Spalling |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Room temp ($20 °C$) | 1350 | **0.25** | 38.5 | 4.85 | **3.80 %** | none (normal) |
| $200 °C$ | 1320 | 0.23 | 36.0 | 4.20 | **3.10 %** | **no spalling** |
| $400 °C$ | 1280 | 0.20 | 25.4 | 2.85 | **1.85 %** | **no spalling (ductility retained)** |
| $600 °C$ | 1240 | 0.18 | 14.2 | 1.15 | 0.20 % | no spalling |
| $800 °C$ | 1200 | 0.16 | 6.5 | 0.45 | 0.05 % | no spalling |
