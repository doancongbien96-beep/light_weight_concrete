## Summary

To minimize the cost of transporting construction materials from Earth when building a permanent crewed base (Lunar Base) on the lunar surface, Prof. L.S. Bell's research team at the University of Houston quantitatively compared and evaluated the physico-mechanical properties, process energy requirements, and resource efficiency of seven major indigenous construction materials drawn from in-situ resources — lunar basalt (Basalt), glassy regolith (Lunar Glass), sulfur concrete (Sulfur Concrete), hydraulic concrete (Lunar Concrete), and refined metals (Iron, Aluminum) — in this classic ASCE Journal of Aerospace Engineering paper. The team presented modular arch/dome base structural designs applying cast basalt (Cast Basalt, compressive strength **620 MPa**, elastic modulus **110 GPa**) together with a regolith shielding layer of 3 m or more as the optimal alternative.

## Key Contributions

- **Construction of a mechanics and thermodynamics database for the seven major indigenous lunar construction materials**: comparison of cast basalt, lunar glass, hydraulic concrete, sulfur concrete, and sintered regolith.
- **Quantified manufacturing process energy consumption per material ($kWh/kg$)**: cast basalt cuts energy consumption by 85 % relative to metal refining.
- **Establishment of radiation (GCR/SPE) shielding and micrometeoroid impact protection design criteria**: a 3 m lunar-soil cover blocks the annual radiation dose below $0.05 Sv$.
- **Presentation of a modular arch-vault (Arch-Vault) lunar habitat base building standard**: structural optimization resisting the tensile force from internal pressurization (1 atm = 101.3 kPa).

## Methods

- Analysis targets: lunar basalt, highland anorthosite, and regolith based on Apollo 11/12/15 lunar samples.
- Comparison items: compressive strength, tensile strength, elastic modulus, density, melting point, coefficient of thermal expansion, and manufacturing energy.
- Structural design: a circular dome 15 m in diameter and three-span continuous arch habitat modules.
