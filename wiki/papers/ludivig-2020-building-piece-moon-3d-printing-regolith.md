## Summary

An IAC 2020 conference paper in which the research team of the European Space Agency (ESA) European Astronaut Centre (EAC Spaceship EAC) experimentally demonstrated, for the construction of a permanent base on the lunar surface, the technical feasibility, layering resolution, mechanical compressive/flexural strength, and thermal stability under a high-vacuum thermal environment of a material-extrusion and binder-jetting 3D printing process that combines lunar regolith simulant (EAC-1A), an in-situ lunar resource (ISRU), with a liquid photocuring/thermosetting polymer binder. It realized high-content regolith layering above 80 wt %, securing excellent structural strength of 28 d compressive strength **32.5 MPa** and flexural strength **6.8 MPa**, and demonstrated an 85 % saving in Earth transportation mass.

## Key Contributions

- **Development of an ESA-1A high-content (80 ~ 85 wt %) lunar regolith 3D printing ink formulation**: establishment of mixing and dispersion technology with low-viscosity thermoplastic/photocuring polymers.
- **Success in high-precision unmanned autonomous layered printing**: shaping of high-resolution lunar wall modules and connecting blocks with a nozzle diameter of 1.5 mm and a layer thickness of 0.8 mm.
- **Demonstration of thermal-vacuum durability on the lunar surface**: a strength retention rate of 90 % confirmed after cycles between a vacuum chamber ($10^{-6} mbar$) and cryogenic/ultra-high temperatures ($-130 \sim +100 °C$).
- **Provision of foundational data for building ESA Moon Village infrastructure**: establishment of construction protocols for landing pad tiles and equipment shielding walls.

## Methods

- Raw materials: ESA EAC-1A lunar in-situ soil simulant ($SiO_2 46.5 \%$, $Al_2O_3 16.8 \%$, $CaO 11.2 \%$, $FeO 12.5 \%$, particle size $5 \sim 150 \mu m$).
- Printing systems: custom screw-extrusion 3D printer and photocuring DLP layering system.
- Tests: ASTM C109 compressive strength (40 mm cubes), 3-point flexural test, thermogravimetric analysis (TGA), and vacuum outgassing test.

## Results

- Mechanics and space-environment property data of the 3D printed regolith composite:

| Property item | EAC-1A 3D printed block | Design criterion for lunar surface structures |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Density ($kg/m^3$) | 1850 | $1500 \sim 2000$ |
| Uniaxial compressive strength (MPa) | **32.5** | $> 20.0$ |
| Flexural tensile strength (MPa) | **6.8** | $> 3.0$ |
| Elastic modulus (GPa) | **14.2** | $> 10.0$ |
