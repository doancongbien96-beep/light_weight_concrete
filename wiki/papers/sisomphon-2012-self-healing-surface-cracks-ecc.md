## Summary
To achieve rapid and permanent self-healing of surface cracks in cementitious mortar, this study applied a self-healing ECC composite layer to the surface, combining a calcium sulfoaluminate (CSA) expansive agent with a crystalline growth-promoting admixture (Crystalline Admixture). By jointly inducing controlled expansive crystal growth of ettringite and calcium carbonate ($CaCO_3$) precipitation, it demonstrated that relatively large surface cracks with widths up to $w = 150 \sim 300 \mu m$ could be completely closed within 14 d under underwater and wet-dry cyclic environments, blocking more than 99 % of the water permeability.

## Key Contributions
- **Establishment of a two-stage ettringite-calcium carbonate crystallization self-healing mechanism**:
  1. Early stage: needle-like ettringite crystal networks formed by hydration of the CSA expansive agent bridge the crack gap.
  2. Later stage: reaction of leached $Ca^{2+}$ with carbonates precipitates high-density calcite crystals.
- **Large expansion of the healable critical crack width**: beyond the conventional self-healing limit ($50 \sim 100 \mu m$), over 90 % closure was achieved even for cracks up to $250 \mu m$.
- **Quantification of watertightness and flexural stiffness recovery**: after 28 d of healing, full recovery of water-permeation resistance and 80 % restoration of reloading flexural stiffness.

## Methods
- **Material mix**: cement + fly ash (40 %), CSA expansive agent (10 wt %), crystalline admixture (1.5 wt %), PVA fibers ($V_f = 2.0 \%$).
- **Crack introduction**: controlled introduction of $50, 100, 150, 250 \mu m$ crack widths via three-point flexural loading.
- **Healing environments**: (1) full underwater immersion, (2) wet-dry cycling ($12 h$ water immersion + $12 h$ air drying).
- **Analysis**: environmental scanning electron microscopy (ESEM-EDS), specimen water-permeability testing, and optical image closure-ratio analysis.

## Results
- $150 \mu m$ crack: complete crack closure after 14 d of underwater healing, reaching a 28 d permeability coefficient of $k < 10^{-11} m/s$.
- Even under wet-dry cycling, accelerated uptake of atmospheric $CO_2$ successfully sealed 95 % of cracks of $200 \mu m$ or less within 28 d.
