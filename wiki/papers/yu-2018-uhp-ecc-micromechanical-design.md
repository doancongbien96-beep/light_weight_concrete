## Summary
A representative paper completing an eco-friendly ultra-high performance ductile composite (Green UHP-ECC) that simultaneously exhibits a compressive strength of 100 ~ 120 MPa, a direct tensile strength of 12 ~ 15 MPa, and a tensile strain capacity of 8.0 ~ 10.0 % by combining micromechanics design theory with high-volume fly ash (HVFA) binder technology. Replacing fly ash at 50 wt % to match the hydrophobic interfacial friction slip characteristics of UHMWPE fibers controlled the matrix fracture toughness ($K_m$) to $0.55 MPa\sqrt{m}$. Thereby securing an energy performance index $J_b'/J_{tip} > 10.0$, it demonstrated world-class ductility reaching a tensile strain of 10.0 %.

## Key Contributions
- **PSH micromechanics design map for UHP-ECC established**: interface parameter boundary conditions derived that eliminate brittle failure in the ultra-high-strength regime and realize $8 \sim 10 \%$ ductility.
- **$CO_2$ reduction and rheology optimization through composite fly ash substitution**: self-consolidating flowability secured, maximizing uniform fiber dispersion and two-dimensional planar orientation.
- **World-record tensile energy absorption density ($1.1 MJ/m3$) achieved**: core advanced material established for high-strength blast-resistant and seismic extreme structures.

## Methods
- **Mix proportions**: cement + Class F fly ash (50 wt %) + silica fume (10 wt %), $W/B = 0.18$, UHMWPE fiber ($V_f = 2.0 \%$, diameter 12 µm, length 18 mm).
- **Test program**: uniaxial dogbone direct tension test, single fiber pullout test, 28 d compressive strength, four-point bending test.

## Results
- 28 d properties: compressive strength $112.5 MPa$, ultimate tensile strength $13.8 MPa$, tensile strain capacity $9.85 \%$, mean crack width $22 \mu m$, and more than 95 cracks.
- Flexural modulus of rupture $28.5 MPa$ and deflection ductility ratio of 15.2 achieved.
