## Summary
To resolve the thermal-conductivity lag problem of building thermal energy storage concrete, this study investigated a hybrid thermal storage concrete in which a form-stable paraffin composite (Form-Stable PCM), blended with highly thermally conductive nanofillers such as porous expanded graphite (EG), multi-walled carbon nanotubes (MWCNT), and copper nanoparticles (Cu NPs) at 1-3 wt %, is embedded in concrete; it identified the mechanisms of thermal conductivity enhancement, latent heat charging/discharging rates, thermal stress mitigation, and mechanical compressive strength. Introducing the expanded graphite-MWCNT hybrid filler increased the thermal conductivity from $0.48 W/(m\cdot K)$ to **1.85 W/(m·K)**, a 3.8-fold increase, and shortened the heat charging time by **62 %**.

## Key Contributions
- **Design of a multi-scale thermally conductive filler network dedicated to form-stable PCM concrete**: three-dimensional bridging of 2D EG sheets and 1D MWCNTs minimized contact thermal resistance.
- **Large enhancement of thermal conductivity by 3.8-fold**: fully overcame the internal heat storage delay of the matrix during phase change.
- **Demonstrated stability over 1000 consecutive thermal cycles**: leakage rate below 0.05 % and latent heat retention of 98.8 %.
- **Retention of a 28 d compressive strength of 38 MPa**: realized integral thermal storage construction of load-bearing walls and slabs.

## Methods
- PCM: paraffin ($T_m = 26 °C$, $\Delta H = 165 J/g$) / expanded graphite composite (PCM content 70 wt %).
- Thermally conductive fillers: MWCNT ($d = 10 \sim 20 nm$, $L = 10 \mu m$) and Cu nanoparticles ($d = 50 nm$).
- Tests: Hot Disk TPS thermal conductivity analyzer, DSC latent heat analysis, uniaxial compressive strength (100 mm cubes), and full-scale exterior wall heat transfer simulation testing.

## Results
- Thermophysical property data of thermal storage concrete by type of thermally conductive filler:

| Mix ID | Thermal conductive filler combination | 28 d compressive strength (MPa) | Thermal conductivity ($W/(m\cdot K)$) | Effective latent heat $\Delta H$ ($J/g$) | Full heat charging time (min) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Control PCM-Conc | No addition | 32.5 | 0.48 | 28.5 | 155 min |
| PCM-EG-2% | Expanded graphite 2 % | 35.0 | 1.15 | 27.8 | 85 min |
| PCM-MWCNT-1% | MWCNT 1 % | 36.5 | 1.25 | 27.5 | 78 min |
| **PCM-HYB (EG+CNT)** | **EG 2 % + MWCNT 1 %** | **38.2** | **1.85** | **26.9** | **58 min (62 % reduction)** |
